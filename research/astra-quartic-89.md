# Masked quartic minimization on the actual paired faces

A masked fourth-moment comparison on the early window gives a height estimate on the **comparison row itself**. On that row the actual companion pays \(o(1)\) above any admissible reciprocal-tower fraction \(K/L\) of the point cap and on every deletion in (2). There is an exact opposite-probability test for the least fourth moment. Its sharp witness can approach any chosen opposite optimizer, with exterior shortfalls proportional to the cube of the optimizing primary density. The square-order subfield gives a genuine obstruction on the same adaptive sparse mask. An unconditional upper of the required size on all prime faces remains open.

## The surviving sites of a quartic comparison row

Let \(n=(p-1)/2\), let \(a\) be the true larger mark, \(\gamma=\sqrt n\,a\ge1+\delta\), and use the delayed pins of [round 88](astra-persistent-88.md). Write \(P=\{i\ne0:r_i>\delta/8\}\) for the physical mask, \(h=(Z_b)_+\) on the exterior and zero at the root. On \(P\), \(h\ge(2d-1)r\). The tilted record gives \(b\le D/13\). A common choice of length has \(|P|/n\ll_\delta1/(d\log D)\) and the inverse lower of round 88. The following statements hold at that length and at any of its growing alternatives.

Set \(u_i=q_i/a\) and write
\[
 T_P(q)=\sum_{i\in P}u_i^4,\qquad Q_P(q)=\gamma^4 n T_P(q).
\]
Suppose a single actual optimizer \(q\) satisfies \(T_P(q)\ll K^2\). Take \(L\ge K\ge2\) with \(\log_2^*L\le(12/13-\xi)\log_2^*p\), for fixed \(0<\xi<12/13\). For this same row,
\[
 dV_P(q)=o(\sqrt n/L),\qquad
 V_P(q)=(n/d)\sum_P(1+h_i)q_i^2.                                      \tag{1}
\]
Moreover, uniformly over all \(A\) of at most \(L^4/K^2\) sites, put \(B=P\cap(A\cup\{u_i\ge K/L\})\). Then
\[
             q(h1_B)=o(1),\qquad q(r1_B)=o(1/d).                       \tag{2}
\]
The fixed deficit and order-\(d\) companion price survive on \(P\setminus A\) below the relative density \(K/L\). One can simultaneously retain \(nq_i>c_\delta d\sqrt{\log D}\) at the length with the counting saving, by the low-density subtraction of round 88.

For (1), the bounded early approximation of \(h\) with precision \(KL\) has supremum \(U=O(1+\log\log p)\) and normalized \(L^2\) error \(o(1/(KL))\). The product costs at most an additive constant in tower height, leaving a relative margin. Interpolate the masked collision with the total mass and apply Cauchy–Schwarz to the error as in round 88 (7). This gives
\[
 dV_P(q)\le(1+U)Q_P(q)^{1/3}+o(1/(KL))\,Q_P(q)^{1/2}=o(\sqrt n/L).
\]
All fixed products of \(K,L,U\) are \(n^{o(1)}\). The remaining part of the pure inverse norm costs at most \(1+1/d\).

For (2), the high set \(H=P\cap\{u_i\ge K/L\}\) has \(n\sum_H q_i^2\ll (L/K)^2T_P(q)\ll L^2\) and \(|H|\le L/(aK)\). Also \(n\sum_{P\cap A}q_i^2\le\gamma^2\sqrt{|A|T_P(q)}\ll L^2\). Thus the collision on \(B\) is \(O(L^2)\) and \(|B|\ll L\sqrt n/K+L^4/K^2\). On this set the normalized second norm of \(h\) is at most \(U\sqrt{|B|/n}+o(1/L)=o(1/L)\). Cauchy–Schwarz and the paired pin prove (2). The height paid on the remaining sites follows from the fixed-gap opposite pairing.

## An exact opposite-law criterion and its cubic contact

This finite criterion holds for every exterior mask \(P\), including a data-dependent one. Write \(\mathcal F_-\) for the probabilities in the sector opposite the primary row, \(\beta=1/(na)\) for their maximum root, \(M=1-a\) and \(T_*=\min_{\mathcal Q_a}T_P\). For a scalar \(c>0\) consider \(w\in\mathcal F_-\) with \(w_i\ge c\) for \(i\notin P,\ i\ne0\). Then
\[
 \boxed{\quad T_*^{1/4}=
 \sup_{c,w}
 \frac{[c M/a-(\beta-w_0)]_+}
      {\left(\sum_P(c-w_i)_+^{4/3}\right)^{3/4}}.\quad}               \tag{3}
\]
Take a zero denominator as zero. When \(T_*>0\) the supremum is attained. It can be attained arbitrarily close in total variation to **any specified opposite optimizer** at a correspondingly small floor. For even \(P\) both the minimizing primary row and an attaining test may be chosen even when the opposite optimizer approached is even.

Indeed \(\sum_{i\ne0}u_iw_i=\beta-w_0\) and \(\sum_{i\ne0}u_i=M/a\). Taking the positive part of the shortfall and applying Hölder bounds the ratio by \(T_P(q)^{1/4}\). Conversely, choose a quartic minimizer \(q_*\), let \(z_i=(q_{*,i}/a)^3 1_P(i)\), and fix an opposite optimizer \(w^*\) with exterior zero set \(S\). The first variation makes \(q_*\) a minimizer of the linear \(z\)-cost. The [complementary moment equations and their finite LP dual](astra-capacity-84.md) give an opposite-sector potential \(U\), constants allowed, with \(U_i\le z_i\) on \(S\) and equality on the positive exterior support of \(q_*\). On \(S\), \(U_i>0\) precisely where \(z_i>0\), with \(U_i=z_i\) there. Subtract a sufficiently large multiple of \(w^*\) to make \(U\le0\) on the remaining exterior and at the root. This preserves its values on \(S\). Choose \(\kappa>\max(0,\max_i U_i)\), set \(N=\sum_i(\kappa-U_i)\), \(w=(\kappa-U)/N\), \(c=\kappa/N\). Then \(w\) is an actual opposite probability and
\[
 (c-w_i)_+=z_i/N\quad(i\ne0),\qquad
 cM/a-(\beta-w_0)=T_*/N.                                              \tag{4}
\]
The denominator in (3) is \(T_*^{3/4}/N\). On uncharged positive primary sites \(w_i=c\). Strict convexity in the masked variables makes \(q_*|_P\) common to all quartic minimizers.

For any other opposite optimizer \(v\), put \(w_\tau=(1-\tau)v+\tau w\), \(c_\tau=\tau c\), \(0<\tau\le1\). The numerator scales by \(\tau\). Every strict shortfall in (4) occurs where \(q_*>0\), hence \(v_i=0\) there. At the remaining sites the floor still holds. This scales all shortfalls by exactly \(\tau\) and retains equality in (3). Reflection averaging gives the even version, for instance by averaging the dual potential against an even minimizing row.

Thus the desired prime upper \(T_*\ll K^2\) is equivalent to a bound \(O(K^{1/2})\) for these normalized shortfall ratios, even if the tested probability is restricted to an arbitrarily small neighborhood of one assigned opposite maximizer. The root loss \(\beta-w_0\) and the exterior floor \(c\) both enter the ratio.

There is a prime-only restriction on **all** sharp witnesses when \(T_*>0\). Equality in the shortfall bound gives \(w_i\le c\) on every positive exterior coordinate of any minimizing row, with \(w_i=c\) on its uncharged coordinates. Equality in Hölder gives \((c-w_i)_+=\lambda u_i^3 1_P(i)\) for some \(\lambda>0\). The prime [affine support theorem](parallel-low-energy-7.md) gives \(m(m+1)\ge n\) for \(m=|\operatorname{supp}q_*|\). Thus at least \((\sqrt{1+4n}-3)/2\) exterior coordinates have \(w_i\le c\). For every \(1/(1+\delta)<\eta<1\), at least
\[
 \left(1-\frac1{\eta(1+\delta)}\right)\sqrt n-O(1)
\]
of these satisfy \(c-\lambda\eta^3\le w_i\le c\) and \(q_{*,i}>0\). At most \(1/(a\eta)\) sites have \(u_i>\eta\) by mass. In the construction (4) one may take \(\kappa=1\), since \(U_i\le z_i\le1\) on \(S\) and \(U\le0\) elsewhere. The resulting probability is nonnegative, has \(N>0\) by its repaired root, and \(\lambda=c\). These shallow contacts constrain the positions of an exact sharp witness; their count by itself does not bound its ratio.

## The adaptive obstruction on the square-order control

Write \(N=v^2\) for a field order with \(v\) an odd prime and \(n=(N-1)/2\). The [unique larger optimum](square-endpoint-uniqueness.md) is \(q=a1_C\) on the subfield annihilator \(C\), with \(|C|=(v+1)/2\), \(a=1/|C|\) and \(\gamma\to\sqrt2\). Fix \(0<\delta<\sqrt2-1\). The same early terminal selection and approximation apply in this family. The [growing geometric closure](parallel-depth-window-30.md) applies over both families and the [zero-floor polynomial bound](vanishing-floor-38.md) uses the field order \(N\). The tilted-energy selection, pin and geometric sum over lengths in round 88 then use only the exact finite identities. One can select the same kind of sparse physical mask with \(d|P|/n\ll_\delta1/\log D(N)\).

At **every** growing length of the early companion in round 88,
\[
 V_P(q)\gg_\delta\sqrt n,\qquad
 T_*=|C\cap P|,\qquad
 \frac{|C\cap P|}{d^2L^2}\longrightarrow\infty
 \quad\left(\log_2^*L\le(12/13-\xi)\log_2^*N\right).                    \tag{5}
\]
For the first bound, \(q_i^2=a q_i\) on the full support and the true complementary pin gives \(q(h1_P)\gg_\delta d\). For the last bound put \(F=C\cap P\). It has \(|F|\le |C|=O(\sqrt n)\), so the early bounded approximation of \(h\) at precision \(L\) gives \(\|h1_F\|_{2,n}=o(1/L)\). But
\[
 c_\delta d\le q(h1_P)=a\sum_F h_i
       \le\gamma\sqrt{|F|}\,\|h1_F\|_{2,n}.
\]
This proves (5) uniformly. Removing \(O(d^2L^2)\) arbitrarily chosen sites costs only \(o(d)\) height by the same estimate. The ratio in (3) on this very mask is exactly \(|C\cap P|^{1/4}\), attained by a genuine opposite law with equal strict shortfalls on the entire set \(C\cap P\). The full-face minimum violates the round-88 fourth-moment hypothesis for every admitted tower in this field-extension control. No prime counterexample follows from (5).

There is an explicit opposite interpolation behind this obstruction. Write \(\Pi_-=(I-J-K_{\rm Jac})/2\) for the nonconstant minus projector. The constant minus witness in the square endpoint proof vanishes on \(C\setminus\{0\}\), giving \(K_{{\rm Jac},i0}=v/n\) at each such site. Since \(C\) is a subgroup, the compression \((\Pi_-)_{C,C}\) has diagonal \(1/2\) and off-diagonal \(-1/(v-1)\). Thus it is exactly \(\lambda(I-J_C)\), for \(\lambda=(v+1)/(2(v-1))\) and \(J_C\) the averaging projector on \(C\). For every \(F\subset C\setminus\{0\}\), set \(y=1_F-|F|e_0\). The actual opposite field \(U=\Pi_-y/\lambda\) interpolates \(y\) everywhere on \(C\). By the [strict complementary support partition](rank-partitions.md), some opposite optimizer is positive everywhere outside \(C\), since the primary optimizer is unique. Subtract a sufficiently large multiple to make \(U<0\) strictly outside \(C\), and form the probability in (4). At \(F=C\cap P\) this realizes the uniform shortfalls attaining (3) using a single projection of the physical mask with its root correction. Its exterior contact set \(\{i\ne0:w_i\le c\}=C\setminus\{0\}\) has size asymptotic to \(\sqrt{n/2}\). The all-witness contact lower at primes excludes exactly this feature of the square construction.
