## Result

The requested certification is **not established by the available records**. The pure-seed calculation gives an outward physical price bound below \(0.528\), but no signed \(P_{5000}\)-price bound for the actual remainder
\[
E_h=D_h-D^{\mathrm J},\qquad h=601,602,
\]
has been produced. The available \(4.8\)-dimensional negative-type representation bound for \(V_h-S\) is not itself a physical packet-price estimate.

The relevant family and limits remain:

- primes \(p\equiv1\pmod 4\), with \(n=(p-1)/2\);
- the original closed cold square \(Q\);
- the actual marked floor \(t_0=n\), with bulk floors \(t_i\) on \(Q\);
- physical frequency coordinates, translated pair \(P_{5000}\), including both boundary pairs;
- first \(p\to\infty\) through eligible primes, with \(h=601,602\) fixed.

The existing pure-seed result is
\[
\|KD^{\mathrm J}\|_{P_{5000}}<0.528
\]
eventually and uniformly on \(Q\). This does not imply
\[
\|K(D_h-D^{\mathrm J})\|_{P_{5000}}<0.472.
\]

## Exact signed remainder decomposition

Write
\[
V_h=S+R_h,\qquad S=2\sqrt n\,Ke_0,
\]
and, off the marked coordinate,
\[
d_t(z)=\frac{z}{\sqrt{4t+z^2}}.
\]
The derivative diagonal is \(D_h=\operatorname{diag}d_{t_i}(V_{h,i})\), while \(D^{\mathrm J}\) uses \(d_{t_i}(S_i)\), with the actual marked coefficient at \(t_0=n\).

Expanding before taking moduli gives
\[
d_t(S+R)-d_t(S)
=L_t(S,R)+H_t(S,R),
\]
where the exact linear predictor is
\[
L_t(S,R)=d_t'(S)R
=\frac{4t\,S}{(4t+S^2)^{3/2}}\,R.
\]

Equivalently, using
\[
d_t(z)=\frac1{2\sqrt t}
\sum_{\ell\ge0}(-1)^\ell
\frac{\binom{2\ell}{\ell}}{4^\ell}
\left(\frac{z^2}{4t}\right)^\ell z,
\]
the signed linear term is
\[
L_t(S,R)=\frac1{2\sqrt t}
\sum_{\ell\ge0}(-1)^\ell
\frac{\binom{2\ell}{\ell}}{4^\ell}
\frac{(2\ell+1)S^{2\ell}R}{(4t)^\ell}.
\]
Thus every coefficient of \(S^{2\ell}R\) must be collected with its sign before applying the physical price.

The nonlinear remainder is exactly
\[
H_t(S,R)=\frac1{2\sqrt t}
\sum_{\ell\ge0}(-1)^\ell
\frac{\binom{2\ell}{\ell}}{4^\ell}
\frac{
\displaystyle\sum_{j=2}^{2\ell+1}
\binom{2\ell+1}{j}S^{2\ell+1-j}R^j
}{(4t)^\ell}.
\]

## Explicit analytic tail

The existing endpoint bounds give, uniformly for the frozen rows and the convex segments used in the secant argument,
\[
|S|\le s:=2.828428,\qquad |R_h|\le b:=1.971573.
\]
Let
\[
N:=4\inf_{Q}\Re t.
\]
For the cold range \(t\ge28\), \(N\ge112\), and
\[
\rho_R:=\frac{(s+b)^2}{N}
\le \frac{4.8^2}{112}<0.206.
\]

Taking absolute values only after separating the signed linear term gives the explicit scalar majorant
\[
|H_t(S,R)|
\le
\frac1{2\sqrt{\inf_Q\Re t}}
\sum_{\ell\ge0}
\frac{\binom{2\ell}{\ell}}{4^\ell}
\frac{
(s+b)^{2\ell+1}-s^{2\ell+1}
-(2\ell+1)s^{2\ell}b
}{N^\ell}.
\tag{1}
\]
This is convergent because \(\rho_R<1\). If orders \(0,\ldots,L\) are retained in the signed computation, an outward tail is
\[
\operatorname{Tail}_{L}
\le
\frac1{2\sqrt{\inf_Q\Re t}}
\sum_{\ell>L}
\frac{\binom{2\ell}{\ell}}{4^\ell}
\frac{
(s+b)^{2\ell+1}-s^{2\ell+1}
-(2\ell+1)s^{2\ell}b
}{N^\ell}.
\tag{2}
\]
This is the required analytic remainder after the exact linear predictor. Numerically, the first omitted nonlinear terms are small at the scalar level: the \(\ell=1\) contribution to (1) is approximately \(3.0\times10^{-2}\), and the \(\ell=2\) contribution is approximately \(4.9\times10^{-3}\). However, these scalar bounds do **not** yet price the resulting physical packets.

At the marked coordinate, the same decomposition must use \(t_0=n\) and the actual marked derivative. The marked correction cannot be replaced by the bulk coefficient; it must be included as a separate signed boundary column before taking moduli.

## Why this does not certify \(0.472\)

The missing estimate is the physical price of the signed coefficient family generated by
\[
K\,L_{t_i}(S_i,R_{h,i})
\quad\text{and}\quad
K\,H_{t_i}(S_i,R_{h,i}),
\qquad h=601,602.
\]

The records establish only that \(R_h=V_h-S\) admits a negative-type representation of total dimensional cost below \(4.8\), and that the full fixed-depth kernels have position entries \(O(n^{-1/2})\). Neither statement controls the translated physical packet price:

1. A negative-type dimension budget is a counting/Mellin representation bound, not a \(P_{5000}\) price.
2. The position-entry \(O(n^{-1/2})\) estimate does not control a physical packet whose translated mass can be \(O(1)\).
3. The positive comparison from Round 131 is obstructed, but that obstruction applies only to that particular nonnegative comparison; it does not settle the signed residual.
4. The existing pure-seed price is \(<0.528\), leaving a required remainder margin of \(0.472\), but no outward signed coefficient table for \(R_{601}\) or \(R_{602}\) is available.

Therefore the precise current conclusion is:

\[
\boxed{\text{The proposed direct signed subtraction is analytically well-defined, but the }0.472
\text{ physical bound remains unproved.}}
\]

No certified refutation has been obtained either.

## Reproducible outward proof test

For each \(h\in\{601,602\}\):

1. Construct the actual signed representation of \(R_h=V_h-S\), including bad lifts, the marked coordinate, and both physical boundary pairs.
2. Form the signed coefficients of \(L_t(S,R_h)\) through order \(2L+1\), using the displayed coefficient
   \[
   (-1)^\ell
   \frac{\binom{2\ell}{\ell}}{4^\ell}
   \frac{2\ell+1}{2\sqrt t(4t)^\ell}
   \]
   on \(S^{2\ell}R_h\).
3. Add all contributions sharing the same Mellin weight.
4. Only then fold \(k\) and \(-k\) and take \(2|c_k|\).
5. Price the resulting base and translated columns with the fixed rational \(P_{5000}\) list, including the actual \(t_0=n\) column.
6. Add the outward tail (2), translated through the multiplier bound for the remaining weights.
7. Add the separately priced marked and bad-lift boundary corrections.
8. Verify, with rational/Arb outward intervals, that
   \[
   \|K E_h\|_{P_{5000}}\le 0.472-\delta
   \]
   for a single \(\delta>0\), uniformly on \(Q\), for all sufficiently large eligible primes.

A finite check at one or finitely many primes would not prove the stated asymptotic claim.
