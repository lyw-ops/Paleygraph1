## The arithmetic orthogonal form after a second activation

Let \(N\) be the twisted Legendre object, \(M=N\star N^\chi\) and \(R=\mathcal T(M)\) the independent \(Sp_8\) standard. For each odd integer \(h\), set
\[
 X_h=L^{\star h}\star R,\qquad P_h=\mathcal T(X_h),
\]

The even number of negative factors gives positive inertia before applying \(\mathcal T\). The lower bound on generic rank from the two weight lists excludes a punctual input. Arithmetically \(L^\chi=L^\vee, R^\chi=R^\vee=R\), and \(\mathcal T(X)^\vee=\mathcal T(X^\vee)^\chi\) by inversion of \(\chi(1-a)\). Thus \(P_h^\vee=P_h\) and \(P_h^\chi=P_{-h}\).
