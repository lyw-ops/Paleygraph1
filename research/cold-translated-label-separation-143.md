# Cross-order label separation on the actual translated Jacobi column

This is a Round 143 derivation for the leading marked mixed block of the actual Jacobi seed and Legendre center. Take one canonical translated Jacobi input \(X_\xi\), with disjoint base and translated boundary pairs, and all full-lift phases. The real mark is \(t_0=n\). The two bulk expressions before restoring the physical lift and mark errors are \(K[Y K(C_1X_\xi)]\) and \(K[C_1 K(YX_\xi)]\), as in the [Round 142 weight table](cold-translated-leading-weight-table-142.md).

Work with single choices of the full lifts. A lift of \(X_\xi\) or \(C_1\) is an invertible Jacobi convolution line, with the translated spatial Kummer character included only in the former. A Legendre lift in \(Y\) is simple. In the order \(Y\) then seed, the inner convolution with \(X_\xi\) preserves simplicity. The transport acts by the ordinary rank-one twist on the nonpunctual generic local system, and the next convolution by the unshifted Jacobi line and outer transport also preserve simplicity. Thus each generic output constituent of this order is simple.

For \(Y\) then seed the two possible folded counts at base weight magnitude one are \(h_1=3\) and \(h_1=4\), with ranks eight and six, respectively. For seed then \(Y\) the complete geometric output of each phase has \(h_1=1\) or \(h_1=2\), with ranks six and eight, respectively. The folded multiplicity counts are additive and nonnegative on geometric constituents. This follows from the two exact weight fibers in the relevant Mellin category, or from its direct sum weight decompositions: for each fixed twist, each occurrence with \(|w|=1\) is counted. A simple label appearing on both sides would require at least three such counts in one complete seed-then-\(Y\) phase. This is impossible even if its convolution before the final transport decomposes.

It follows that the two full orders have disjoint geometric labels in the leading block after the standard lift comparison, uniformly for every disjoint added pair. Arithmetic equality would imply geometric equality. The conclusion uses the summed count of two fixed base twists and needs no global simplicity result for the seed-then-\(Y\) order. It leaves equalities among phases of a single order open. The exact actual finite-field action also contains marked and exceptional lift repairs of translated physical price \(O_{\Lambda}(n^{-1/2})\). The claim here concerns the generic main terms of the leading block.
