# Source-dependent outflow on the actual Paley faces

Let $G=\widehat{\mathbb F_p^{\times2}}$ have even order $n=(p-1)/2$. Fix complementary optimal rows $\mu_s$, normalized as probabilities, with marks $q_s=\gamma_s/\sqrt n$, $q_+q_-=1/n$. Their off-root supports are disjoint. Write $D_s$ for the forbidden Fourier positions, $S_s$ for the root and the zeros of an opposite exposing optimum, and $Z_s(i)=sY_t(i)=s\sqrt n v_t(i)$ for the oriented cold score. All changes below use a single pair of output rows across the thresholds of the [joint objective](astra-tail-53.md) $M_t$. The terminal energy $E_t=\|v_t\|^2$ is common to all pairs.

## When source selection cannot move any mass

Let $f\le\mu_s$ be a nonnegative outgoing subprobability with $f(0)=0$. At each donating site route its mass according to the same probability $P$ on **displacements**, allowing a different $f,P$ on each face and at each time. Assume that $h=P*f-f$ is the difference between two actual laws on that face. Let $H$ be the subgroup generated by the support of $P$. Every donating coset of $H$ must be disjoint from the entire positive support of **each** opposite optimum $\nu$, including its root.

In fact $C(a)=\sum_j f(j)\nu(j-a)$ is nonnegative, $C(0)=0$ by exposure and the excluded root, and $P*C=C$. The last assertion follows since $h\in V_s$ has mean zero and every translate of $\nu$ belongs to $\mathbb R\mathbf1+V_{-s}$. Fourier modes fixed by convolution are precisely the characters trivial on $H$, by equality in the triangle inequality at multiplier one. Thus $C=0$ on $H$, and nonnegativity gives the claim. In particular **every generating displacement law forces $f=0$**. A directed generator step suffices. This is an obstruction at every individual time in the actual growing window, even with donor selection depending arbitrarily on the score, support and opposite optimizer. The matrix that sends $f$ and retains the rest need not preserve either full sector class. It yields zero improvement over its own reference pair, whose charged value need not have positive normalized excess.

For a generating law one can also quantify source dependence of a general admissible flow. With $T$ the convolution by $P$, solve $(I-T^*)\psi=1-n\nu$ at mean zero. The inverse on mean-zero functions exists and preserves $V_{-s}$, so $\psi\in V_{-s}$. For arbitrary feasible edges $F_{ji}$ directed from $j$ to $i$, write $f_j=\sum_i F_{ji}$, $F=\sum_j f_j$, keeping the no-root bulk budget. Then
$$
 \sum_{ji}F_{ji}\,[\psi(i)-(T^*\psi)(j)]=F.
$$
Indeed the divergence pairs to zero with $\psi$, and every donor is a zero of $\nu$. If $Q_j$ is its destination distribution, the left side is the donor-weighted sum of $E_{Q_j}\psi-E_{j+P}\psi$. This gives an exact directed-edge test, including for site-dependent routes. For instance a dictionary with supremum of the displayed edge weight strictly below 1 cannot move positive mass. No uniform bound on the oscillation of this inverse is used.

## The order-two displacement and a constructive charged trial

Take the fixed displacement $d=n/2$. An actual target on a given face can be reached from the chosen reference in one step along $d$ if and only if every pair $\{i,i+d\}$ retains its total mass. Indeed its change then has $h_{i+d}=-h_i$, and sending $(-h_i)_+$ from $i$ uses at most the reference probability at $i$. The balanced pair with the root is fixed entrywise. If either site in another pair is excluded by exposure, that pair is also fixed. For the other pairs choose representatives $i$, write $x_i=h_i$, and put $W_i=\mu_s(i)+\mu_s(i+d)$. The exact tests are
$$
 \max(0,W_i-q_s)\le \mu_s(i)+x_i\le\min(q_s,W_i),
 \qquad
 \sum_i x_i\, i(a)=0\quad(a\in D_s,\ d(a)=-1).
$$
Here $i(a)$ is character evaluation. The factor $1-d(a)$ cancels at the remaining forbidden Fourier positions and equals 2 at the displayed ones. Real and imaginary parts can be imposed separately. The root, sector, exposure and probability budgets are exact. This quotient need not contain the full face at a general prime.

There is a direct upper and lower estimate for the best common charged value in this dictionary. Temporarily drop the displayed Fourier equations *only to produce a trial*. In every movable pair, fill the lower-score site as much as the cap allows. Ties may retain the reference. Call the resulting common pair $G_t$, with changes $y_s$. Its survival probabilities are simultaneously smallest at **every** threshold on each side under just the pair masses, exposure, root and caps. This follows by treating separately intervals that split the pair; in all others the survival is fixed. Thus $M_t(G_t)$ is a sharp lower bound for this relaxed problem and a lower bound for the actual restricted problem.

For an upper bound project $y_s$ orthogonally to the real kernel of the displayed Fourier equations to obtain $z_s$. One may project into the even kernel if all inputs are even and ties respect inversion. Take the largest $0\le\lambda\le1$ for which each $\mu_s(i)+\lambda z_{s,i}$ meets its displayed budget on both faces. The resulting pair $L$ is feasible. Define
$$
 R=\sum_{s,i}|Z_s(i)-Z_s(i+d)|\,|y_{s,i}-z_{s,i}|.
$$
Writing $B$ for the baseline pair and $\mathcal M$ for its dictionary minimum, one has
$$
 M_t(G_t)-E_t/4\ \le\ \mathcal M-E_t/4\ \le\ M_t(L)-E_t/4
 \ \le\ (1-\lambda)M_t(B)+\lambda[M_t(G_t)+R]-E_t/4.
$$
For the last bound, compare $L$ to the feasible-for-the-relaxation mixture of $B,G_t$. Correct its masses within each pair at scalar $W_1$ cost at most $\lambda R$ summed across signs. The integrated maximum changes by at most that sum, and is convex under mixture by its survival formula. This works even if the unscaled projected row is signed. Direct evaluation of $L$ can improve the last bound. If $\lambda=0$ the reference is recovered. These tests require no unknown face singular-value lower bound or separate threshold completion.

At $p=97$ **every even point of the full optimal faces** is accessible by this one order-two displacement from every other even point. Indeed both free plus directions at anchors 10 and 21 in the [full cyclotomic face](astra-face-54.md) satisfy the pair cancellation exactly. The even minus face is a singleton. Evenization preserves the complete laws of the cold scores, hence the restricted and unrestricted minima agree at every depth for an even reference. Every profile with subgroup index 1, 2, 3 or 6 is instead blocked on the plus side by the opposite-support test above. The order-two routing attains the true [previously certified](astra-face-54.md) charged minimum per step in $[.047068071,.047068072]$ at $t=32$.

At that time the [round-58 plus perturbation](astra-jointface-58.md) can thus be routed with just displacement 24 and improves the common objective by more than .003649205. Holding the true minus row fixed, the lowest-score filling gives $M_t(G_t)<2.504908000$, whereas the exact minimum exceeds 2.561786507. Thus the Fourier correction cannot be dropped even if one true exposed support, root, point caps, every order-two pair mass, and a common row at all thresholds are fixed on the variable side. ## A growing-window obstruction for fully conditioned antipodal movement

Let $\mu,\mu'$ be *any two* optimal rows on the same larger-theta face with $\alpha=\sqrt n\mu_0\ge1$. Allow an arbitrary coupling between them, including retained mass. Write $g_u=\sum_i\phi_u(i)/(2\sqrt n)$, $b_u=\sqrt{4+v_u(0)^2}-|v_u(0)|$, $A_t=\sum_{u<t}(1-g_u)^2$, $B_t=\sum_{u<t}(2-b_u)$. The [exact cold mean identity](astra-critical-44.md) gives $E_{\mu}Z_s=2t(\alpha-1)-\alpha B_t+E_t/4+A_t+H_t(\mu)$, with $H_t(\mu)\ge0$. Adding it for the two marginals gives
$$
 E|Y_t(i)+Y_t(j)|\ \ge\ E_t/2+2A_t+4t(\alpha-1)-2\alpha B_t\ \ge\ 4-o(1)
$$
uniformly for each $1\le t\le D(p)$. Indeed $A_t\ge(1-1/\sqrt n)^2$, $B_t=o(1)$ in the marked window, and $E_t=\sum_j(\phi_{t-1}(j)-2g_{t-1}/\sqrt n)^2\ge(2-2\sqrt{D/n})^2$ there, by the root injection and $g_{t-1}^2\le D$. Optimum marks are bounded by the Jacobi estimate. For arbitrary moves on both faces, averaging their two respective lower bounds retains $E_t/2+2A_t-o(1)$ by $\alpha+1/\alpha\ge2$. The obstruction operates on true endpoint marginals, beyond the [uniform-marginal sector-map constraint](astra-jointface-58.md).

If only the moved portion must be antipodal on the whole history, the seed already gives a mass restriction. Write the current and target as $r+f,r+g$ with common retained $r$, and couple equal moving masses $f,g$. If the unnormalized seed cost of this coupling is $e=\int|Y_1(i)+Y_1(j)|$, then
$$
 \|r\|_1\ \ge\ \frac{2(\gamma_s-1/\sqrt n)-e/2}{2\sqrt{p/n}} .
$$
Both complete seed means equal $2(\gamma_s-1/\sqrt n)$, and $Z_s\le2\sqrt{p/n}$ by the Jacobi bound. Adding the two expectations and subtracting the moving contribution proves the inequality. A vanishing-error partial plan must retain at least $\gamma_s/\sqrt2-o(1)$ of the original mass on either side. Whole-history reflection within one face and oriented cross-sign quantile coupling have different marginals.
