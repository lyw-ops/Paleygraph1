# A mixed seed unit with two genuine nonseed factors

Retain \(R=V_{601}-V_{600}\), \(V_s=S+W_s=V_{600}+sR\), both lifts, the original square and the physical \(t_0=n=(p-1)/2\) of [Round 170](cold-linear-remainder-seed-harmonic-170.md). Use its \(\varepsilon,M,N,s_n,c_l,b_l,a_l\) and the rational enclosures of Rounds 168 and 170. In addition to the pure unit and one-remainder first harmonic, collect the exactly two-remainder seed unit in the true bulk even Taylor source.

At order \(l\ge1\), there are \(\binom{2l}{2}\) placements of two \(W_s\)'s. The \(2l-2\) seed factors have Laurent unit \(c_{l-1}s_n^{2l-2}\). Thus these contributions share the same vector \(R^2\int_0^1(1-s)W_s^2\,ds\) before the first \(K\). Relative to its leading coefficient \(-(3/2)(4t)^{-3/2}\) at \(l=1\), the scalar is
\[
 \Xi_2(t)=\sum_{k\ge0}(-1)^k f^{(2)}_k y^k,\qquad
 y=s_n^2/(4t),\qquad
 f^{(2)}_k=\tfrac43(k+1)^2 a_{k+1}.
\]
Indeed, writing \(l=k+1\), the order-\(l\) relative coefficient is \((2/3)b_l\binom{2l}{2}c_{l-1}\), which equals \((4/3)l^2a_l\). In particular the first two coefficients are \(1,15/4\). This is the same actual bulk packet under every frozen \((KD_{602})^jK\), with no commuting of a multiplier through \(K\). The physical mark and bad-character corrections retain their separate vanishing first-source charges.

On the original square let \(Y_0,Y_1,\Delta,\rho\) be as in Round 168. Set
\[
 U_2=1-f^{(2)}_1Y_0+f^{(2)}_2Y_1^2-f^{(2)}_3Y_0^3+f^{(2)}_4Y_1^4
      +\tfrac43\Delta\,\frac{4+2\rho}{(1-\rho)^4}<0.635 .
\]
This bounds \(|\Xi_2|\) eventually. At the real center the alternating series is positive with decreasing terms, since the successive coefficient ratio is at most \(4\) and \(Y_1<0.127\). The final term comes from the derivative bound using \(f^{(2)}_k\le(4/3)(k+1)^2\) on \(|y|\le\rho\).

Each selected unit word in the old paired first-moment budget has at least four charges for the two increments and the two nonseed factors, regardless of whether the two nonseed factors occupy the same pair. Before taking the unsigned price the branch debit at order \(l\) is therefore at least
\[
 \varepsilon^2\,4\binom{2l}{2}c_{l-1}s_n^{2l-2}D_s^2 ,
\]
where \(D_s\) is the actual nonseed dimension mass. This debit is disjoint from the pure and exactly-one-nonseed debits. After branch removal all remaining word charges still have nonnegative coefficients in \(D_s\), so substitute \(D_s=M-s_n\). On the reassembled packet the unit costs no seed moment. The two \(R\)'s and the two \(W_s\)'s retain the four moments, and the post-\(K\) factor 2 cancels the integral mass \(1/2\).

Thus in Round 170's last price bracket add \(6(M-s_-)^2 U_2/N\) and subtract
\[
 \sum_{l=1}^{4}
 \frac{4 b_l \binom{2l}{2} c_{l-1}8^{l-1}(M-s_+)^2}{N^l}.
\]
The first four positive debits suffice. The attached exact rational comparison, using the same square-root lower bounds, yields leading full price below \(0.00008311056\). With the vanishing repairs included the full post-\(K\) price is eventually below \(0.00008312\), and its first genuine \(KD_{602}\) step at rate \(1.066\) is below \(0.00008861\) on both lifts. These are source and fixed-step bounds. The scalar has no outer-power decay, so a prime-uniform distributed tail and nonlinear continuation remain open.
