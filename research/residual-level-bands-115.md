# Two bands in the simultaneous small trimmed-moment branch

Use the actual fixed-floor critical cap-rate late repair and residual branch of [Round 114](reciprocal-input-marks-114.md). In particular \(A\to0\), \(\mathbb E_\rho W^2\to0\), \(\beta_*=c_-/\mu\), \(W=1-\beta_* z\), \(h(0)=0\), and \(gg_-=t/n\) off the mark. The fixed threshold \(\eta>0\) from that branch satisfies \(\mathbb E_n[hz1_{\{g<\eta\}}]\le-c n^{-1/2}\). A provisional extension of the round's independent review localizes this cancellation and identifies the actual omitted mark's sign.

The exact reciprocal product gives \(\mathbb E_n(h/g)=(n/t)\mathbb E_n(hg_-)=O_t(n^{1/4})\). Thus for fixed \(a>0\), \(\mathbb E_n[h1_{\{g<a n^{-3/4}\}}]=O_t(a n^{-1/2})\). The strip \(-b<z<0\) contributes at most \(b\mathbb E_n h=O(b n^{-1/2})\) in absolute value. Restrict first to the negative sign and then choose small fixed \(a,b>0\). The remaining integral still satisfies
\[
 \mathbb E_n[hz1_{\{a n^{-3/4}\le g<\eta,\ z\le -b\}}]\le-c' n^{-1/2}.
\]
The positive favored repair law meanwhile concentrates at \(z=1/\beta_*\). The value \(\beta_*\) must be at least \(1-o(1)\), since \(|z|\le1\). The two statements concern the same sequence of actual fields and the same repair window.

The omitted opposite input mark in the full reciprocal moment contributes positively in this branch. In the normalization multiplying the moment by \(n\), its main term for \(P(x)=x^2\) is exactly
\[
 n c_- \mathbb E_n(h^2 g z)=q^2\mathbb E_\omega[h^2(1-W)]=q^2\mathbb E_\omega h^2+o(1)\gtrsim1,
\]
by Cauchy–Schwarz under \(\rho\). The same holds for \(P_A\) since \(|P_A(h)|\ll h^2\), \(A\to0\), and \(\mathbb E_\omega h^4\ge(\mathbb E_\omega h^2)^2\). The opposite mark can therefore settle a critical coefficient in the full moment on this branch. A prime-uniform upper \(o(n^{-1})\) for the corresponding anchored contribution would actually contradict it. The formulas above establish the requirement but do not supply that upper.
