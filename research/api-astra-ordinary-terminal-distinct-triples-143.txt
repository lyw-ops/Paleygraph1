## Result: inconclusive; the prime-specific upper bound remains open

For the actual ordinary family
\[
p\to\infty,\qquad p\equiv1\pmod 4,\qquad n=\frac{p-1}{2},
\]
with the Jacobi kernel \(K\), physical coordinate \(0\), zero-start orbit, fixed floor \(\tau>0\), and \(T=T(p)\to\infty\) chosen so that \(T+1\) lies in the simultaneous marked window, the available arguments do **not** prove
\[
\mathscr D_{F,T}=o(T^2)
\]
for a selectable true opposite optimizer \(F\) on either sign.

Here
\[
H=\sqrt n\,g_{T-1},\qquad
G=\sqrt n\,g_T,
\]
and, for an actual opposite mean-one optimizer \(F\), with
\[
q_*=F_0,\qquad \lambda=F-q_*e_0,
\]
the exact distinct-index statistic is
\[
\boxed{
\mathscr D_{F,T}
=
\frac1n
\sum_{\substack{i,j,k\ne0\\ i,j,k\ {\rm distinct}}}
F_iH_jH_kK_{ij}K_{ik}.
}
\]

The physical mark is retained throughout; it is removed only after the weighted contribution of the source coordinate has been bounded by the marked-window estimate.

### What is proved

The exact quadratic comparison gives
\[
\mathbb E\!\left[\lambda(KH^\circ)^2\right]
=
\mathscr D_{F,T}+O_\tau(T),
\]
where \(H^\circ=1_{\{j\ne0\}}H\), and all expectations are normalized by \(1/n\). The \(O_\tau(T)\) term includes:

- the diagonal \(j=k\);
- the terms with \(i=j\) or \(i=k\);
- the physical source \(j=0\) or \(k=0\);
- the Jacobi diagonal contribution.

The source removal is valid because, in the actual growing marked window,
\[
|H_0|\max_i|K_{i0}|=O_{\tau,B}(T^{-2B}).
\]

Moreover, the terminal identity implies that if an ordinary prime gap persisted on one sign, namely
\[
\frac{q_*}{\sqrt n}\ge 1+\eta,
\]
then for every actual opposite optimizer and every admissible growing \(T\),
\[
\mathbb E(\lambda G)\ge c_{\tau,\eta}T.
\]
By Cauchy–Schwarz this forces
\[
\mathscr D_{F,T}\ge c'_{\tau,\eta}T^2
\]
eventually. Thus the distinct-index statistic is genuinely the obstruction: coincidence terms, the physical mark, and the diagonal cannot supply the required quadratic mass.

The desired sufficient estimate would be, for at least one selectable true optimizer \(F\) on each sign,
\[
\boxed{\mathscr D_{F,T}=o(T^2).}
\]
Equivalently, the weaker first-moment target
\[
\boxed{
\frac{\sqrt n}{T}\,
\mathbb E\!\left[1_{\{i\ne0\}}F_i g_T(i)\right]=o(1)
}
\]
would suffice. The terminal identity also gives the equivalent signed form
\[
\frac{\sqrt n}{T}\,s\,\mathbb E(F\Delta v)=o(1),
\qquad
\Delta v=v_{T+1}-v_1,
\]
up to \(O_\tau(T^{-1/2})\) and marked-window errors.

### Why the present bounds do not settle it

The spectral estimate only gives
\[
\mathbb E[\lambda(KH)^2]
\le \|\lambda\|_\infty\,\mathbb E H^2
\ll \sqrt p\,T,
\]
since \(F_i\le 1+\sqrt p\) and \(\mathbb E H^2\ll_\tau T\). This is much too large when \(T\) is the available growing depth, which is only of iterated-logarithmic size in \(p\).

Entrywise Jacobi bounds are also insufficient. Using
\[
|K_{ij}|\le \frac{\sqrt p}{n}
\]
destroys the signed cancellation and gives no \(o(T^2)\) estimate. The existing Fejér/autocorrelation arguments control difference multiplicities of supercritical or high-site sets, but they do not control the weighted signed kernel
\[
(i,j,k)\longmapsto F_iH_jH_kK_{ij}K_{ik}
\]
for the actual nonlinear terminal field \(H\).

The true opposite-face constraints,
\[
F\ge0,\qquad \mathbb EF=1,\qquad
KF=-s(F-\mathbf1),\qquad F_0=q_*,
\]
have not yet been combined with the actual \(H\)-dependent quadratic form in a way that produces a prime-uniform upper bound. The optimizer may concentrate its second moment on the actual terminal high set; the existing concentration argument only shows that such concentration is necessary under a prime gap, not that arithmetic forbids it.

The order of limits therefore remains:

1. Fix \(\tau>0\) and the relaxation/trajectory parameters.
2. Let \(p\to\infty\) through ordinary primes \(p\equiv1\pmod4\), selecting \(T(p)\to\infty\) with \(T(p)+1\) inside the marked window.
3. Prove the signed upper bound or first-moment bound uniformly along that choice.
4. Only then take \(\tau\downarrow0\).

No such prime-uniform estimate is currently proved.

## Ranked next tests

### 1. Exact-face quadratic minimization — highest priority

For each actual \(H\), minimize
\[
F\longmapsto
\frac1n\sum_{\substack{i,j,k\ne0\\i,j,k\ {\rm distinct}}}
F_iH_jH_kK_{ij}K_{ik}
\]
over the genuine opposite optimal face
\[
F\ge0,\quad \mathbb EF=1,\quad KF=-s(F-\mathbf1),\quad F_0=q_*.
\]

This is linear in \(F\) once \(H\) is fixed, so it is a finite LP over the true face. Its dual should be written with the physical coordinate and all distinct-index corrections retained. The first useful outcome would be an exact dual certificate showing
\[
\min_{F\ {\rm on\ the\ face}}\mathscr D_{F,T}
\le o(T^2),
\]
or, conversely, an explicit dual obstruction proving that every face point has quadratic cost.

This test directly exploits the selectable optimizer and avoids imposing an artificial support model.

### 2. Prime-specific character expansion of the signed triple form

Expand the actual \(H_jH_k\) and the two Jacobi factors in multiplicative characters, retaining:

- the true prime family \(p\equiv1\pmod4\);
- the physical mark \(0\);
- the exact opposite-face equations;
- the growing depth \(T(p)\);
- the normalized \(1/n\) norm.

The target is a cancellation estimate for
\[
\mathscr D_{F,T}
=
\frac1n\sum_{i,j,k\ {\rm distinct}}
F_iH_jH_kK_{ij}K_{ik},
\]
not an absolute-value estimate. A successful route would need a prime-uniform bound on the relevant character sums with coefficient growth controlled at the actual marked depth. Fixed-complexity trace estimates are inadequate unless their conductor/rank growth and the floor \(\tau\) are tracked through the whole limit.

At present, the first precise missing arithmetic estimate is therefore:

\[
\boxed{
\text{For ordinary }p\equiv1\pmod4,\text{ bound the exact face-weighted signed triple sum }
\mathscr D_{F,T}
\text{ by }o(T^2).
}
\]

No actual prime subsequence obstruction has been found, but no argument excludes one.
