### Checked consequence: the actual overflow has an exact stationary-factor bridge, but no arithmetic gap follows

Assume the hypotheses of the cited real-cap comparison: fixed \(t,M>0\), \(n=(p-1)/2\to\infty\), \(\alpha\le Bn^{-1/2}\), and \(|\gamma|\ge\delta\). Let \(S=S_*\) be the localized aligned overflow set, let \(\epsilon\in\{\pm1\}\) be its common sign, and put
\[
q:=\epsilon\tau\,1_S\ge0,\qquad y:=\epsilon Y\quad\text{on }S.
\]
Thus \(q\le L\), \(y\ge\lambda\sqrt n\), and
\[
\mathbb E_n q\le \mathbb E_n|\tau|
 \le \frac{\alpha}{2A}\le \frac{B}{2A\sqrt n},
\qquad
\mathbb E_n(qy)\ge c_0,
\]
where \(c_0=3(\cosh\delta-1)/8\) may be used after the localization in the reference.

Let
\[
f=f_\epsilon:=\frac{b+\epsilon Y}{2},\qquad \nu=\mathbb E_n b.
\]
The exact reciprocal stationary equations give
\[
Kf=\epsilon\left(f-\frac{\nu}{2}\mathbf1\right).
\]
Since \(y=\epsilon Y>0\) on \(S\), \(f=(b+y)/2\) there. Consequently,
\[
\boxed{\;
\epsilon\langle Kq,f\rangle_{2,n}
=\langle q,y\rangle_{2,n}
-\left\langle q,\frac{\nu-(b-y)}2\right\rangle_{2,n}.
\;}
\tag{A}
\]
On \(S\),
\[
0\le b-y=\sqrt{Y^2+4t}-|Y|\le 2\sqrt t.
\]
Therefore, eventually (for \(n>t\)),
\[
\frac{\nu-2\sqrt t}{2}\,\mathbb E_nq
\le
\langle q,y\rangle_{2,n}
-\epsilon\langle Kq,f\rangle_{2,n}
\le
\frac{\nu}{2}\,\mathbb E_nq,
\]
and hence
\[
\boxed{\;
\left|
\epsilon\langle Kq,f\rangle_{2,n}
-\langle q,y\rangle_{2,n}
\right|
\le \frac{\nu B}{4A\sqrt n}.
\;}
\tag{B}
\]
In particular,
\[
\epsilon\langle Kq,f\rangle_{2,n}\ge c_0-O_{t,M,B}(n^{-1/2}).
\]
Thus the actual aligned overflow is not merely a prescribed sparse vector: it has asymptotically the same positive pairing with the exact stationary factor \(f\) as with the true source \(y\).

### Exact cap identity on the localized overflow

The capped equation gives \(KU=a-\mu\mathbf1\). On \(S\), the enlarged-threshold relation is
\[
U=\epsilon(A+q),\qquad a=A,\qquad A=\sqrt{M^2+4t},
\]
up to the excluded marked coordinate. Since \(KU=a-\mu\mathbf1\) is an exact global identity, it can be paired with the localized \(q\) without any error:
\[
\epsilon\langle Kq,U\rangle_{2,n}
=\langle q,a-\mu\rangle_{2,n}
=(A-\mu)\mathbb E_nq.
\]
Splitting \(U\) over \(S\) and \(S^c\) gives
\[
\boxed{\;
\langle Kq,(A+q)1_S\rangle_{2,n}
=
(A-\mu)\mathbb E_nq
-\epsilon\langle Kq,U1_{S^c}\rangle_{2,n}.
\;}
\tag{C}
\]
This is the requested combination of the cap equation with the actual aligned overflow. It identifies the precise missing term: the exterior transport
\[
\mathcal R_S:=\epsilon\langle Kq,U1_{S^c}\rangle_{2,n}.
\]

The available cap-rate assumptions do not make \(\mathcal R_S=o(1)\). Indeed,
\[
|\mathcal R_S|
\le \|Kq\|_{2,n}\,\|U1_{S^c}\|_{2,n}
\le \sqrt{\mathbb E_nq^2}\,\|U1_{S^c}\|_{2,n}
\le \sqrt B\,n^{-1/4}\|U1_{S^c}\|_{2,n}.
\]
Even the natural bound \(\|U1_{S^c}\|_{2,n}=O(n^{1/4})\) only gives \(O(1)\), not a vanishing error. Thus (C) cannot presently be converted into a positive restricted Rayleigh gap.

### Why this does not prove a prime-specific gap

For the one-sign high-source test \(h=g1_{H_\epsilon}\), the prior estimate gives only
\[
\frac{\epsilon\langle h,Kh\rangle_{2,n}}{\|h\|_{2,n}^2}
\ge 1-C'\eta-o(1).
\]
Identity (A) shows that the aligned actual overflow reinforces the same near-unit stationary direction; it does not force a loss. In fact, any proposed prime-specific estimate of the form
\[
\epsilon\langle Kq,f\rangle_{2,n}
\le \langle q,y\rangle_{2,n}-\rho_\eta
\]
with fixed \(\rho_\eta>0\) is impossible: (B) contradicts it for all sufficiently large primes.

The prescribed weighted critical-set counterexamples remain relevant here: support size \(O(\sqrt n)\), bounded peak, and one-sign alignment alone do not yield a uniform arithmetic gap. The reciprocal factor \(f\) supplies the additional exact equation, but (A) shows that this equation is compatible with saturation rather than exclusion. The genuinely missing nonlinear/arithmetic condition is a bound such as
\[
|\mathcal R_S|=o(1)
\quad\text{or a direct positive lower bound for}\quad
\|h\|_{2,n}^2-\epsilon\langle h,Kh\rangle_{2,n}
\]
on the *actual* sign class \(H_\epsilon\), not on arbitrary prescribed weighted sets.

### Status and next tests

**Status: inconclusive, with a quantified failed mechanism.** The actual stationary factor yields the new exact bridge (A)–(C), but it does not produce a gap beyond \(C\eta\). The separate critical cap rate \(\alpha=O(n^{-1/2})\) remains required; these identities do not generate it.

1. **Highest-priority test:** prove an exterior-transport estimate
   \[
   \langle Kq,U1_{S^c}\rangle_{2,n}=o(1)
   \]
   or a signed substitute exploiting the actual complementarity of \(U\). This would turn (C) into a genuine restricted estimate.

2. **Independent arithmetic test:** establish a prime-specific gap directly for
   \[
   h=g1_{H_\epsilon},\qquad |H_\epsilon|\le B_0\sqrt n/\eta,
   \]
   using both \(h-f_\epsilon\) and the stationary sign constraint. A bound based only on support, \(\ell^\infty\), or a prescribed weighted rectangle is insufficient.
