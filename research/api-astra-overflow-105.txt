### New genuine structural constraint

Let \(P=I-J_0\), where \(J_0x=\mathbb E_n(x)\mathbf 1\). For the actual Jacobi operator,
\[
K^*=K,\qquad K\mathbf 1=0,\qquad K^2=P.
\]
Write
\[
g=b-2\sqrt t\,\mathbf 1,\qquad Y=Kb=Kg,\qquad G=K\tau,
\]
and let \(R\) be a receiving subset of nonmark sites satisfying, for some \(\varepsilon\in\{\pm1\}\),
\[
G_j\ge d,\qquad \varepsilon Y_j\ge \lambda\sqrt n\qquad(j\in R).
\]
Thus \(R\) is the fixed-sign portion of the genuine high stationary receiving set. Put
\[
\rho=\frac{|R|}{n},\qquad u=\mathbf 1_R,
\]
and use normalized inner products \(\langle x,y\rangle=\mathbb E_n(x_iy_i)\).

Define
\[
A=\langle u,G\rangle,\qquad B_R=\langle u,\varepsilon Y\rangle,\qquad
H=\langle G,\varepsilon Y\rangle.
\]
The actual stationary equations and the sign assumptions give
\[
A\ge d\rho,\qquad B_R\ge \lambda\sqrt n\,\rho. \tag{A}
\]

#### 1. Exact Fourier-sector cap-defect obstruction

Since \(G=K\tau\), reciprocity gives
\[
A=\langle Ku,\tau\rangle.
\]
Moreover,
\[
\|Ku\|_2^2=\langle u,K^2u\rangle
=\langle u,Pu\rangle=\rho(1-\rho). \tag{B}
\]
Because \(\tau\) is supported on the genuine capped overflow set and
\[
\|\tau\|_2^2\le \alpha,
\]
Cauchy–Schwarz applied to (A)–(B) yields
\[
d^2\rho^2
\le A^2
\le \rho(1-\rho)\alpha.
\]
Hence every such fixed-sign rectangle necessarily satisfies
\[
\boxed{\alpha\ge \frac{d^2\rho}{1-\rho}.} \tag{C}
\]

This is a genuine actual-\(K\) constraint, not an arbitrary-set entry estimate. In particular, if the rectangle construction supplies
\[
|R|\ge r_0\sqrt n
\]
and one assumes the conditional critical rate
\[
\alpha\le \frac{B_0}{\sqrt n},
\]
then (C) forces
\[
B_0\ge d^2r_0+o(1). \tag{D}
\]
Therefore the signed rectangle is excluded whenever the cap-defect constant is smaller than \(d^2r_0\). For an unspecified constant \(B_0\), this does not settle the target, but it gives a quantified necessary lower constant for the cap defect.

There is also a stronger localization statement. If \(S=\operatorname{supp}\tau\) is the genuine sending set, then
\[
A=\langle (Ku)|_S,\tau\rangle,
\]
so
\[
\boxed{\|(Ku)|_S\|_2^2\ge \frac{A^2}{\alpha}
\ge \frac{d^2\rho^2}{\alpha}.} \tag{E}
\]
At the critical scales \(\rho\asymp n^{-1/2}\), \(\alpha\asymp n^{-1/2}\), the Fourier transform \(K\mathbf 1_R\) must place a fixed positive fraction of its exact sector energy
\[
\|Ku\|_2^2=\rho(1-\rho)
\]
inside the genuine capped sending set \(S\). Thus the sending set cannot merely be a large overflow support: it must capture a quantitatively prescribed portion of the Jacobi Fourier energy of the actual receiving set.

#### 2. Reciprocity forces a three-vector feasibility condition

The same equations give the exact identity
\[
H=\langle K\tau,\,\varepsilon Kg\rangle
=\varepsilon\langle \tau,Pg\rangle. \tag{F}
\]
The Gram determinant of \(u,G,\varepsilon Y\) therefore implies
\[
\boxed{
\left|H-\frac{AB_R}{\rho}\right|
\le
\left[
\left(\|G\|_2^2-\frac{A^2}{\rho}\right)
\left(\|Y\|_2^2-\frac{B_R^2}{\rho}\right)
\right]^{1/2}.
} \tag{G}
\]
Here
\[
\|G\|_2^2=\|K\tau\|_2^2\le \|\tau\|_2^2\le\alpha.
\]
This uses both actual updates (\(Y=Kg\), \(G=K\tau\)) and exact Fourier-sector feasibility (\(K^2=P\)); it is unavailable for the vertex sign matrix.

At the critical rectangle scales,
\[
\rho\asymp n^{-1/2},\quad A\asymp n^{-1/2},\quad B_R\asymp 1,
\]
so \(AB_R/\rho\) is order one. Consequently, unless one of the residual factors in (G) is large, the rectangle forces the global reciprocity pairing
\[
\varepsilon\langle \tau,Pg\rangle
\]
to be order one with a prescribed sign. This is a substantial additional requirement: the positive contribution from the fixed-sign high receiving set cannot be canceled arbitrarily by the rest of the stationary configuration.

### Status and remaining gap

The argument supports a restricted structural statement:

* If \(\alpha\le B_0/\sqrt n\), a fixed-sign high receiving rectangle of density \(\rho\asymp n^{-1/2}\) requires \(B_0\ge d^2r_0+o(1)\).
* Its genuine sending set must capture the Fourier-sector energy in (E).
* The actual overflow and stationary source must satisfy the reciprocity constraint (G).

This does **not** prove the required cap-defect rate, nor does it exclude the rectangle for arbitrary \(B_0\). The unresolved estimate is either:

1. a prime-uniform bound showing that \(K\mathbf 1_R\) cannot concentrate the amount in (E) on the genuine capped sending set \(S\); or  
2. a genuine nonlinear estimate forcing
   \[
   \varepsilon\langle \tau,Pg\rangle=o(1),
   \]
   which would contradict (G) when the residual terms are \(o(1)\).

### Ranked next tests

1. Prove a Jacobi-sector concentration bound for \(R\) equal to the actual fixed-sign high-\(Y\) set and \(S\) equal to the capped overflow support, rather than arbitrary sets.

2. Estimate \(\langle\tau,Pg\rangle\) directly from the capped update \(U=K\Phi_t(C)\), retaining the sign relation between \(\tau\) and \(U\); an \(o(1)\) bound would rule out the critical rectangle.
