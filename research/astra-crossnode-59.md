# Signed arithmetic collection across a pair of Picard vertices

At the real floor \(t=2031/128\), a tagged inner \(C_2\) leaf produces an actual destructive collision with the direct outer \(m^2\) source. Its saving is between 75 and 77 billionths per Jacobi sign in the pre-activation weight measure. Collecting **every** arity-one outer character on this leaf with **every** direct outer character still leaves an escaping two-level packet majorant. Its certified lower mass exceeds 21 by height 2114. Separately, the inner \(C_4\) and two quadratic Legendre families have no new collisions with any direct old character or any purely Jacobi outer multiplier on an inner source, in any Taylor degree. These results concern the four fixed distinguished coefficients at the round-58 floor. The common marked continuation below the floor and the prime theta limit remain open.

Write \(A,B\) for the characters of the two determinant-one Legendre standards, so \(Y=(A+B)/2\). Let \(P\) be \(H_t(V)=\sqrt{4t+V^2}\) with the usual four distinguished pre-\(K\) amendments in arity zero, and let \(D=H'_t(V)\). All coefficients below are after full arithmetic collection. The raw \(G\) determinants in the positive Jacobi coordinate are \((-m)^{e_i}\). [Four-seed normalization and limiting arithmetic group](astra-ranknext-58.md)

At each ordinary inner level extract the two \(m^{\pm2}\) coefficients, each \(u=[m^2]P\). They make exactly \(2u C_2\). The actual all-lift identity gives \(K(2u C_2)=u(A+B)-4u C_1/\sqrt q\). The latter Jacobi correction has vanishing first-packet cost. Retain the signed \(u(A+B)\) through the adjacent outer vertex. On disjoint pairs of levels, pool all the old characters in \(P+uD(A+B)\) before taking moduli. All other branches retain the round-58 collection and unsigned propagation. Equivalently, at even heights remove the tagged part of the preceding odd-height measure from its use in the unary channel only, and substitute the signed expression above for the direct and tagged unary terms. ## The whole unary channel still escapes

Let \(U_1\) be a symmetric upper pre-activation weight measure for the fully collected unary kernel and \(D_Y=\delta_0+(\delta_{-2}+\delta_2)/2\). Write \(a=[m]D\) and \(a_0=169624/10^6<a\). Pooling the remaining tagged terms with it and with the other direct terms loses at most twice their unsigned cost, by the reverse triangle inequality applied separately at each arithmetic label. A pre-projection upper bound for the loss at each boundary weight is
\[
 4u_{\max}\,\big[ (U_1-a_0(\delta_{-1}+\delta_1))*D_Y\big],
 \qquad u_{\max}=30611/10^9 .
\]
The factor \(4u_{\max}\) pays twice the original tagged field \(2uY\). Both full twists give the same summed measure. The estimate also covers new collisions within the tagged source. It applies to pooling any subset of the described coincidences, since the leading constructive terms can be included or omitted without reducing their combined charge; the other terms still pay twice their full cost.

Use the round-58 degree-28 upper unary kernel. At each required weight add the ordinary tail at input dimension \(M+1\), by extracting arity one at auxiliary radius one. Use the round-58 lower kernels for the fixed limiting coefficients, arities through nine and shifts through 18. ## A full-degree exclusion for the three other leading sources

Let \(T(f)\) denote the generic sheaf after the first \(K\) on a simple arity-zero constituent \(f\). For any surviving source here \(T(f)\) is negative. If its dimension is \(d\), the two full twist lists have total absolute weight \(2d\). This follows from the boundary rank formula and equality of generic rank and convolution dimension for a negative output. Quadratic twisting may interchange the two lists. For \(f=m^4,\operatorname{Sym}^2 A,AB\), let \(N(h)\) be the absolute sum on the lists of \(m^h T(f)\).

| \(f\) | \(d\) | \(N(-1),N(0),N(1)\) |
|---|---:|---|
| \(m^4\) | 4 | 14, 8, 14 |
| \(\operatorname{Sym}^2 A\) | 4 | 12, 8, 16 |
| \(AB\) | 8 | 24, 16, 24 |

The two shifts translate the lists by opposite signed offsets, so \(N\) is convex on the real line and its unique integer minimum is \(N(0)=2d\). If \(m^kT(g)=m^jT(f)\) up to the quadratic twist, \(T(g)\) must itself attain \(2d\). After absorbing a possible twist, this forces \(k=j\). Convolution by that Jacobi line can then be inverted. On the open generic locus \(K\) tensors by an invertible ordinary line. Middle extension recovers the simple input, hence \(g=f\) up to the simultaneous quadratic twist. Those paired lifts already have equal coefficients and agree in sign. This handles all source degrees and arbitrary integral outer Jacobi powers. A second generic \(K\) preserves the same distinction on surviving simple inputs. [Two-twist lists](astra-ranknext-58.md#a-two-node-noncollision-test) · [Boundary rank rule](astra-feedback-52.md#reading-rank-from-two-mellin-characters)

A direct competitor must lie in the old representation ring with dimension four or eight. The old derived product is \(SL_2^2\times SL_3^2\times SL_4^4\times SL_5^4\times SL_6^2\times SL_7^2\), with one common Jacobi quotient. In dimension four the possibilities are either \(SL_2\) symmetric cube, the product of both \(SL_2\) standards, or any of the eight rank-four standards and duals. In dimension eight they are either \(SL_2\) seventh symmetric power, a standard times the other symmetric cube, either \(SL_3\) adjoint, or an \(SL_2\) standard times any rank-four standard or dual. Completeness follows from the Weyl dimension formula: for \(SL_3\) the irreducible dimensions at most eight are \(1,3,6,8\); for \(SL_4\) they are \(1,4,6\); for ranks five through seven only the trivial and the standard or dual have dimension at most eight. Product dimensions multiply. For each of the resulting 11 and 22 shapes, equality of the first-list sums fixes the only possible Jacobi exponent. The checker compares both entire lists there and finds no match for any of the three \(T(f)\). This also handles their arbitrary Jacobi translates, since a direct competitor has a free Jacobi exponent already. The result leaves outer multipliers with a nontrivial derived representation available.
