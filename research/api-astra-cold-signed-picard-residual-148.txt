## Result

The proposed \(0.074\) bound is **not established** by the supplied work. The precise failure is that every currently checked estimate for
\[
R_h:=K\,\operatorname{diag}\!\bigl(d'_{t_i}(S_i)Y_{h,i}\bigr),
\qquad h=601,602,
\]
takes absolute values before the residual is regrouped by actual arithmetic labels.

The relevant setup remains:

- the original closed cold square centered at \(2031/128\), of half-width \(2^{-17}\);
- the genuine height rows \(h=601,602\);
- \(t_0=n\) at the exceptional physical mark and \(t_i=t\) off the mark;
- the actual fully marked finite-field operators, with both boundary-pair lifts retained;
- the translated \(P_{5000}\) packet norm;
- the limit taken uniformly on the square first, followed by sufficiently large eligible primes in the original prime family, so that the compact-lift and marked-diagonal repairs are \(O_{P_{5000}}(n^{-1/2})\).

The positive estimates currently available are
\[
\|R_h\|_{P_{5000}}<0.260
\]
from the separate center/residual calculation, and
\[
\left\|K\operatorname{diag}\!\bigl(d'_{t_i}(S_i)
(\mathcal C_{{\rm oth},i}+Y_{h,i})\bigr)\right\|_{P_{5000}}<0.408
\]
for the first-omitted piece. Neither estimate proves, nor disproves,
\[
\|R_h\|_{P_{5000}}<0.074.
\]

The \(0.074\) figure is only the residual budget obtained if the fixed-other-center contribution is charged separately at \(<0.150\) against a joint \(0.224\) target. That separation is not itself a signed estimate: the \(0.150\) and \(0.260\) bounds arise from different positive envelope constructions and cannot be subtracted or combined without a new common decomposition.

## Why the existing obstructions do not settle the signed question

The checked positive witnesses are not obstructions to the actual residual:

1. The first-omitted retained envelope has a positive witness exceeding \(0.356\) on the first twelve weights. This rules out repricing that particular positive homogeneous envelope, not the signed operator \(R_h\).

2. The direct positive packet has a two-step witness exceeding \(1.073296\), and the resummed positive comparison has a witness exceeding \(0.570\). These comparisons discard cancellations between distinct actual arithmetic objects.

3. The selected-pool calculation through the \(\beta^2\) block separates several named higher-center contributions, but it does not provide a signed old Jacobi/Legendre projection of the actual \(Y_h\). In particular, it does not identify the residual with the old labels after the physical mark, both lifts, and the final \(K\)-transport are restored.

Thus the current evidence supports only the statement:

> A positive repricing of the existing first-omitted envelope cannot reach the required price, while the actual signed residual remains unresolved.

The same-row alternative
\[
\|A_{601}^2-B^2\|_{P_{5000}}<0.580184
\]
is likewise still open. The height-transfer estimate
\[
\|A_{602}-A_{601}\|_{P_{5000}}<0.017
\]
does not supply the missing signed square estimate.

## Remaining gap

A valid \(0.074\) result must construct, for each \(h=601,602\), an actual signed decomposition of \(Y_h\) into old Jacobi/Legendre labels—after one or two actual Picard steps—with:

- both full lifts;
- the exceptional physical mark \(t_0=n\);
- both boundary-pair contributions;
- the exact finite-prime \(K\)-transport;
- a uniform tail estimate on the original square;
- and an eventual \(O_{P_{5000}}(n^{-1/2})\) bound for compact and marked repairs.

The finite coefficient check must be followed by a uniform analytic-tail and physical-repair argument. A finite retained-label calculation alone would not settle the asymptotic prime-family claim.

## Ranked next tests

### 1. Actual signed old-label projection of \(Y_h\) — highest priority

For \(h=601,602\), expand one and then two actual Picard steps for \(Y_h\) in the old Jacobi/Legendre label basis. Keep the two full lifts and the exceptional \(t_0=n\) mark separate until after the signed label collection. For each retained label:

1. compute the exact signed coefficient before taking modulus;
2. transport it through \(K\,d'_t(S)\);
3. form the induced signed \(P_{5000}\) column map;
4. include the actual marked and compact-lift corrections;
5. bound all omitted labels and analytic orders uniformly on the closed square.

**Pass criterion:** an eventual physical bound
\[
\sup_{h\in\{601,602\}}\|R_h\|_{P_{5000}}<0.074.
\]

A failure of this test is informative only if the failed label family is named and its signed coefficient or transport contribution is rigorously bounded away from the required budget.

### 2. Direct same-row signed square test — second priority

At \(h=601\), collect the signed difference
\[
A_{601}^2-B^2
\]
before applying positive packet majorants. Separate terms according to whether the two derivative factors carry matching old labels, mismatched lifts, or the exceptional marked coordinate. Use the known
\[
\|B\|_{P_{5000}}<0.634,\qquad
\|B^2\|_{P_{5000}}<0.402,
\]
but do not charge the residual by the triangle inequality until after all available label cancellations have been collected.

**Pass criterion:**
\[
\|A_{601}^2-B^2\|_{P_{5000}}<0.580184
\]
uniformly on the square and eventually over the eligible primes.

This test is independent of proving the one-step \(0.074\) residual estimate and could establish the stated same-row alternative. Neither test is currently completed by the supplied records.
