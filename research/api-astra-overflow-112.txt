## Fresh result: exact marked/bulk split of the mixed cubic

I use the fixed-floor critical-cap hypotheses from R109–R112, with the limiting order **fixed \(t,M,\beta\) first, then \(p\to\infty\)** along the prime subsequence. Let
\[
\pi_y=\frac{h_y}{nm},\qquad \omega_y=\frac{g_y}{n\mathbb E_n g},
\]
where \(h=\bar h\) is the actual late repair average and \(g=f_\epsilon/\sqrt n\) is the true favored factor. The marked repair has
\[
h_0=0,\qquad \pi_0=0,\qquad \omega_0\asymp 1.
\]

Define
\[
F_\pi(x):=\sum_a\pi_a k(x-a).
\]
Then, directly from the definition of \(T_3\),
\[
\sum_{a,b,c}\pi_a\pi_b\omega_cT_3(a,b,c)
   =\mathbb E_x F_\pi(x)^2F_\omega(x),                     \tag{2}
\]
where \(F_\omega(x)=\sum_c\omega_ck(x-c)\).

The marked part \(c=0\) is exactly
\[
S_{\rm mark}
 :=\omega_0\!\!\sum_{\substack{a,b\ne0\\a\ne b}}
       \pi_a\pi_b T_3(a,b,0).                              \tag{3}
\]
Since \(\pi_0=0\),
\[
\sum_{a,b}\pi_a\pi_bT_3(a,b,0)
   =\mathbb E_x\,k(x)F_\pi(x)^2.                            \tag{4}
\]
The only terms removed in passing from (4) to (3) are \(a=b\). Their total contribution is \(O(n^{-1}p^{-1/2})\): indeed
\[
\sum_a\pi_a^2=O(n^{-1}),
\]
and the translated repeated-index Jacobi estimate gives
\[
T_3(a,a,0)=O(p^{-1/2})
\]
for \(a\ne0\). Therefore
\[
\boxed{
S_{\rm mark}
=\omega_0\,\mathbb E_x\!\left[k(x)F_\pi(x)^2\right]
 +O(n^{-1}p^{-1/2}).
}                                                             \tag{5}
\]

The complementary genuine-bulk contribution is
\[
S_{\rm bulk}
 :=\sum_{\substack{a,b,c\\ c\ne0,\;a,b,c\ {\rm distinct}}}
       \pi_a\pi_b\omega_cT_3(a,b,c),                         \tag{6}
\]
and hence the required distinct mixed cubic is
\[
\boxed{
S_{\rm mark}+S_{\rm bulk}
=\sum_{a,b,c\ {\rm distinct}}\pi_a\pi_b\omega_cT_3(a,b,c).
}                                                             \tag{7}
\]

The R112 argument gives, with the persistent repair sign,
\[
\epsilon(S_{\rm mark}+S_{\rm bulk})\gtrsim p^{-1/2}.           \tag{8}
\]
Consequently, either a prime-uniform estimate
\[
S_{\rm mark}=o(p^{-1/2})
\quad\text{and}\quad
S_{\rm bulk}=o(p^{-1/2})                                    \tag{9}
\]
would exclude the repair obstruction, or one must show that the two terms have incompatible signs or magnitudes.

## Relation with the marked linear Jacobi average

The marked scalar in the first branch is
\[
L_\pi:=\sum_y\pi_y k(y)=F_\pi(0).
\]
Thus (5) isolates the precise missing relation:
\[
\boxed{
\mathbb E_x[k(x)F_\pi(x)^2]
\ \text{versus}\ F_\pi(0).
}                                                             \tag{10}
\]

No relation of the form
\[
\mathbb E_x[k(x)F_\pi(x)^2]
   =C\,F_\pi(0)+o(p^{-1/2})                                  \tag{11}
\]
follows from the current hypotheses. The available norm estimates only give
\[
\left|\mathbb E_x[k(x)F_\pi(x)^2]\right|
 \le \|k\|_\infty\,\|F_\pi\|_2^2
 \ll \|F_\pi\|_2^2
 \ll n^{-1/2},                                               \tag{12}
\]
because \(F_\pi=(\sqrt p\,m)^{-1}Kh\), \(\sqrt p\,m\asymp1\), and
\(\|Kh\|_2^2\asymp n^{-1/2}\). Since \(p\asymp n\), (12) is exactly the critical \(p^{-1/2}\) scale, not a saving.

In particular, the marked linear alternative
\[
\epsilon L_\pi\ge c_1
\]
does not by itself force the marked cubic (5) to be positive, nor does the bulk alternative
\[
\langle1_{G\setminus(C\cup\{0\})}b,e\rangle
\le-c_2n^{-1/2}
\]
control the sign of \(S_{\rm bulk}\). The true remaining arithmetic statement is therefore a **mixed covariance estimate**
\[
\mathbb E_x[k(x)F_\pi(x)^2]
   +\omega_0^{-1}S_{\rm bulk}
   =o(p^{-1/2}),                                             \tag{13}
\]
under the actual-repair restrictions on \(\pi,\omega\). A bound for arbitrary square-supported weights would not suffice.

## Fourier form of the remaining prime-bound

On the dual of the multiplicative-square group \(G\), write \(\widehat k(\chi)\) for the Fourier transform of \(k\). With normalized Fourier conventions,
\[
\mathbb E_x[k(x)F_\pi(x)^2]
 =
 \sum_{\chi,\psi}
 \widehat k(\chi)\widehat k(\psi)
 \widehat k((\chi\psi)^{-1})
 \widehat\pi(\chi)\widehat\pi(\psi).                          \tag{14}
\]
Meanwhile
\[
L_\pi=F_\pi(0)=\sum_\chi \widehat k(\chi)\widehat\pi(\chi).    \tag{15}
\]
Thus the desired mark-versus-cubic estimate would require cancellation in the quadratic Fourier form (14) that is invisible in the linear form (15). The Jacobi/Katz estimate controls individual translated \(T_3\)'s only at \(O(p^{-1/2})\), which is insufficient after summation because the actual weights have total mass one.

### Status

- **Exact dichotomy:** established in (7)–(8), with the marked contribution genuinely isolated and collision error \(O(n^{-1}p^{-1/2})\).
- **New checked limitation:** the direct norm bound is only \(O(n^{-1/2})=O(p^{-1/2})\); it cannot exclude either branch.
- **Remaining gap:** prove cancellation in (14), or prove an incompatible sign relation between (5) and (6) using the actual repair origin of \(\pi,\omega\).

   \]
