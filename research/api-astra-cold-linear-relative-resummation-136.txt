### 1. Exact signed linearization

At a nonmarked coordinate, write the Jacobi seed in the good Mellin field as
\[
S=\frac{s_n}{2}(L+L^{-1}),\qquad s_n=2\sqrt{p/n}.
\]
For
\[
d_t(z)=\frac{z}{\sqrt{4t+z^2}},
\]
the analytic derivative at the seed is
\[
d_t'(S)=\frac{4t}{(4t+S^2)^{3/2}}.
\]
Its Laurent coefficient at exponent \(r\in2\mathbb Z\) is
\[
a_r(t)
=
\sum_{\substack{\ell\ge |r|/2\\ \ell\equiv r/2\;(\mathrm{mod}\,1)}}
\frac{(-1)^\ell}{2\sqrt t}
\frac{\binom{2\ell+1}{\ell}}{4^\ell}
\left(\frac{s_n}{2}\right)^{2\ell}
\binom{2\ell}{\ell-r/2}
(4t)^{-\ell}.
\]
Equivalently, before summing over \(\ell\), the order-\(2\ell\) contribution is
\[
a_{\ell,r}(t)
=
\frac{(-1)^\ell}{2\sqrt t}
\frac{\binom{2\ell+1}{\ell}}{4^\ell}
\left(\frac{s_n}{2}\right)^{2\ell}
\binom{2\ell}{\ell-r/2}
(4t)^{-\ell}.
\]

If the actual nonseed row has the Laurent-field expansion
\[
C_h=\sum_M c_{h,M}M,
\]
then the exact linear-in-\(C_h\) contribution is
\[
E_h^{(1)}
=
\sum_{r,M} a_r(t)\,c_{h,M}\,L^rM.
\]
The required signed collection is therefore over identical pairs \((r,M)\), not merely over equal numerical boundary weights. Its physical price would be
\[
\mathcal L_h
=
\left\|
K\sum_{r,M}a_r(t)c_{h,M}L^rM
\right\|_{P_{5000}},
\qquad h=601,602,
\]
with the actual marked coordinate \(t_0=n\) restored separately.

The supplied records provide neither the coefficients \(c_{601,M}\), \(c_{602,M}\), nor their field identities. The positive envelope \(\beta\) is insufficient: it destroys precisely the signed coincidence information needed in this formula.
