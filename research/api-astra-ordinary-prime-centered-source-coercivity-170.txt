## Result

For the actual ordinary-prime family, the available Jacobi identities give a genuine **partial source-weighted gain**, but not the required coercivity of the true quotient or of \(C_+(A)\).

I keep the following data fixed throughout:

- \(p\to\infty\) through ordinary primes, with \(n=p\);
- physical mark at \(0\), with marked mass \(q=q(p)\asymp \sqrt p\);
- original sector \(\epsilon\in\{\pm1\}\), and
  \[
  P_\epsilon=\frac{I+\epsilon K-\Pi}{2};
  \]
- \(U'\) an actual independent set of genuine zero coordinates of the optimizer;
- \(w\ge 0\) the actual complementary interpolant on \(U'\);
- \(A=\{i\in U':w_i>0\}\), \(B=U'\setminus A\);
- all norms and Gram matrices in counting normalization.

The centered physical Gram and source are
\[
G_0=E_{U'}^*\left(P_\epsilon-
 \frac{P_\epsilon e_0e_0^*P_\epsilon}{(P_\epsilon)_{00}}\right)E_{U'},
\qquad
c_0=\frac1{\sqrt p}
\left(\mathbf 1_{U'}+\frac{q-1}{(P_\epsilon)_{00}}\,b_0\right),
\]
where \(b_0=E_{U'}^*P_\epsilon e_0\). The exact complementary relation is
\[
G_0w=0,\qquad c_0^*w=0.
\]

The actual quotient cost after eliminating \(B\) is
\[
C_{\mathrm q}(A)
=\frac1p\left[
c_{0,B}^*G_{0,BB}^{-1}c_{0,B}
+d_A^*Q_A^\dagger d_A\right],
\]
where
\[
Q_A=G_{0,AA}-G_{0,AB}G_{0,BB}^{-1}G_{0,BA},
\qquad
d_A=c_{0,A}-G_{0,AB}G_{0,BB}^{-1}c_{0,B}.
\]

### 1. Actual signed Jacobi control on the positive support

For real \(z\), put \(D_w=\operatorname{diag}(w)\). Since \(G_0w=0\), the exact weighted difference identity is
\[
z^*D_wG_0D_wz
=
-\sum_{i<j}(G_0)_{ij}w_iw_j(z_i-z_j)^2.
\]

For distinct actual unmarked columns,
\[
(G_0)_{ij}=\frac{\epsilon}{2}K_{ij}+O(p^{-1}),
\]
with the \(O(p^{-1})\) uniform over the adaptive actual support. Consequently, on
\[
A_H=\{i\in A:w_i\ge H\},
\]
the replacement of the exact centered form by the signed Jacobi form satisfies
\[
\left|
z^*D_wG_0D_wz
+
\frac{\epsilon}{2}\sum_{i<j}K_{ij}w_iw_j(z_i-z_j)^2
\right|
\ll \frac1H\|D_wz\|_2^2.
\]

This is genuine arithmetic control: it uses the actual prime Jacobi matrix, the actual \(w\), the actual marked sector, and the actual positive support. It is not a positivity-only model.

However, it is not a lower coercive estimate. The Jacobi difference form is signed, and the global Jacobi operator has nontrivial positive and negative spectrum. Thus this identity does not imply
\[
z^*D_wG_0D_wz\gg \|D_wz\|_2^2
\]
on the relevant quotient. The missing issue is cancellation between Jacobi edges, especially through the low-weight region \(A\setminus A_H\) and the zero block \(B\).

### 2. A genuine source-weighted partial gain

Assume the actual zero block satisfies
\[
b=|B|\le (1-\eta)\frac{p}{\sqrt p}=(1-\eta)\sqrt p
\]
for some fixed \(\eta>0\). The physical entry estimates give
\[
(G_0)_{ii}=\frac12+O(p^{-1}),
\qquad
|(G_0)_{ij}|\le \frac{\sqrt p}{2p}+O(p^{-1}),
\qquad
|(c_0)_i|\ll p^{-1/2}.
\]
Gershgorin therefore gives
\[
G_{0,BB}\ge \frac{\eta}{4}I_B
\]
for sufficiently large primes. Hence
\[
D_B:=c_{0,B}^*G_{0,BB}^{-1}c_{0,B}
\ll_\eta \frac bp.
\]
In particular, under \(b\ll\sqrt p\),
\[
D_B=O_\eta(p^{-1/2})=o(1).
\]

The Schur-transfer source
\[
q_A:=c_{0,A}-d_A
=G_{0,AB}G_{0,BB}^{-1}c_{0,B}
\]
satisfies
\[
\|q_A\|_2^2\le D_B.
\]
Therefore, for every \(s>0\), if \(\mathbf 1_{[s,\infty)}(Q_A)\) denotes the spectral projector of \(Q_A\) on eigenvalues at least \(s\), then
\[
\left\langle d_A,\,
Q_A^\dagger \mathbf 1_{[s,\infty)}(Q_A)d_A
\right\rangle
\le \frac{\|d_A\|_2^2}{s}
\ll \frac{|A|/p+b/p}{s}.
\]

Taking the growing admissible window
\[
1\ll T=T(p)=o(\sqrt p),
\qquad s=T^{-1/2},
\]
gives
\[
\left\langle d_A,\,
Q_A^\dagger \mathbf 1_{[T^{-1/2},\infty)}(Q_A)d_A
\right\rangle
\ll \frac{|A|/p+b/p}{ }\,\sqrt T.
\]
Since \(|A|\le p\) and \(T\to\infty\),
\[
\frac1p
\left\langle d_A,\,
Q_A^\dagger \mathbf 1_{[T^{-1/2},\infty)}(Q_A)d_A
\right\rangle
=O(\sqrt T/p)
\]
in the normalized quotient convention, and more generally the unnormalized source cost is \(O(\sqrt T)\), hence \(o(T)\).

Thus the portion of the genuine quotient supported on eigenvalues
\[
\lambda\ge T^{-1/2}
\]
is already negligible compared with the target \(T\). The same conclusion applies to the zero-block debit \(D_B\).

This is a real prime-uniform partial gain, provided the displayed actual cardinality condition on \(B\) holds.

## Precise remaining gap

The argument does **not** control the low spectrum
\[
0<\lambda<T^{-1/2}
\]
of the true \(Q_A\). On this subspace,
\[
d_A^*Q_A^\dagger d_A
\le T^{1/2}\|P_{(0,T^{-1/2})}(Q_A)d_A\|_2^2
\]
is too weak: the trivial source bound permits a contribution larger than \(T\), and the rank estimate for the low spectral space does not control the source projection.

The actual Jacobi signed form also does not close this gap. It identifies the relevant arithmetic operator, but no prime-uniform lower bound has been proved for its quadratic form on the centered optimizer-compatible subspace. In particular, the following missing estimate would suffice:
\[
\|P_{(0,T^{-1/2})}(Q_A)d_A\|_2^2
=o(T^{1/2})
\]
uniformly along every fixed-gap sequence, or a stronger source-weighted Jacobi inequality of the form
\[
\langle d_A,Q_A^\dagger d_A\rangle
=o(T).
\]

A second unresolved hypothesis is the actual support condition
\[
|B|\ll \sqrt p
\]
or an appropriate bound on the combined low-weight/zero set. The present calculation proves the gain conditionally on that true optimizer support regime; it does not force the regime for every fixed-gap ordinary-prime sequence.

Therefore the proposed fixed-gap exclusion is **not proved**.

## Ranked next tests

1. **Source projection onto the low Jacobi spectrum — highest priority.**  
   For the actual adaptive \(A,B,w\), estimate
   \[
   \|P_{(0,T^{-1/2})}(Q_A)d_A\|_2
   \]
   using the explicit source
   \[
   c_0=p^{-1/2}\left(\mathbf1+\frac{q-1}{d}b_0\right),
   \]
   rather than its entrywise bound. A bound exploiting cancellation with \(w\), \(c_0^*w=0\), would directly close the only uncontrolled spectral range.

2. **Prove an actual support-size/low-weight bound.**  
   Establish, for every fixed gap and admissible \(T=o(\sqrt p)\), that the genuine optimizer satisfies
   \[
   |B|+\bigl|\{i\in A:0<w_i<H\}\bigr|
   \le (1-\eta)\sqrt p
   \]
   for some fixed \(H,\eta>0\), or derive an equivalent weighted estimate. This would make the Gershgorin transfer and high-weight signed Jacobi comparison available on the full relevant support.
