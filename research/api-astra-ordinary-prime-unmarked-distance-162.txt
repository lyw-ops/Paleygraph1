## Result: precise failure of the generic projection/trial-vector route

I did not find an actual-prime estimate of the required strength. The obstruction is precise: the complementary optimizer gives a particular solution of the wrong equation, not an orthogonal-distance witness, and the available pointwise Jacobi-kernel bounds give no \(T\)-scale coercivity on the adaptive support.

Fix an eligible ordinary prime
\[
p\equiv1\pmod 4,\qquad n=(p-1)/2,
\]
a physical sign \(\epsilon\in\{+1,-1\}\), and the actual least-bulk-energy optimizer \(F=qe_0+E_Sx\), where \(S\subset\{1,\dots,n-1\}\) is its genuine positive bulk support. All norms and inner products below are counting norms. Put
\[
U=(S\cup\{0\})^c,\qquad W=U\cup\{0\}=S^c,
\]
and
\[
P=\frac12(I-\epsilon K-\Pi),\qquad J=I-P=\frac12(I+\epsilon K+\Pi).
\]

### 1. Exact distance variational formula

Let \(A=JE_U\). Since \(J\) is a projection and \(J\mathbf1=\mathbf1\), the residual from projecting \(\mathbf1\) onto \(\operatorname{Ran}(A)\) lies in
\[
\mathcal Q:=\operatorname{Ran}(J)\cap E_W\mathbb R^W.
\]
Consequently
\[
\operatorname{dist}(\mathbf1,\operatorname{Ran}(JE_U))^2
=
\sup_{0\ne y\in\mathcal Q}
\frac{|\langle \mathbf1,y\rangle|^2}{\|y\|_2^2}.
\tag{1}
\]

This gives the correct direction for a trial-vector argument: a proposed \(y\) must be both

1. supported on the genuine \(W=S^c\), including the physical mark \(0\), and
2. \(J\)-invariant.

A vector merely supported on \(W\) is not enough.

Equivalently, if \(z\) is supported on \(W\) and satisfies
\[
(Jz)_U=J_{UW}z_W=0,
\tag{2}
\]
then \(y=Jz\in\mathcal Q\), and
\[
\operatorname{dist}(\mathbf1,\operatorname{Ran}(JE_U))^2
\ge
\frac{|\langle\mathbf1,z\rangle|^2}{\langle z,Jz\rangle}.
\tag{3}
\]
Thus (2), or an explicit controlled transfer to (2), is indispensable.

### 2. Why the complementary optimizer is not such a witness

For every true complementary optimizer \(R\), exact complementarity gives
\[
R_i=0\quad(i\in S),
\]
so \(R\) is indeed supported on \(W\). However,
\[
PR=R-\mathbf1,\qquad JR=\mathbf1.
\]
Therefore
\[
(JR)_U=\mathbf1_U,
\]
and hence
\[
(JE_U)^*R=\mathbf1_U\ne0.
\tag{4}
\]

Thus \(R\) is a particular solution of
\[
J_{UW}r_W=\mathbf1_U,
\]
whereas an orthogonal-distance witness must solve the homogeneous equation
\[
J_{UW}z_W=0.
\]
Using \(R\) as a lower-bound witness reverses the required direction. The vector \(R-\mathbf1\) lies in the \(P\)-sector, but it is not supported on \(W\) and has no nonzero pairing with the target \(\mathbf1\) in the required \(J\)-sector.

This is the concrete failure of the natural complementary-row argument.

### 3. Exact Schur-complement identity

Writing
\[
H=E_U^*JE_U,
\]
the exact identity from the marked exterior decomposition is
\[
d_{p,\epsilon}^2
:=
\operatorname{dist}(\mathbf1,\operatorname{Ran}(JE_U))^2
=
n-\langle \mathbf1_U,H^\dagger\mathbf1_U\rangle.
\tag{5}
\]
With \(q=F_0\) and \(x=F|_S\), it simplifies to
\[
d_{p,\epsilon}^2
=
\frac{n^2}{q^2+\|x\|_2^2}.
\tag{6}
\]

Hence, for one admissible \(T=T(p)\to\infty\) in the simultaneous marked window,
\[
d_{p,\epsilon}^2=\omega(n/T)
\]
is equivalent, since \(q^2=O(n)\), to
\[
\|x\|_2^2=o(nT).
\tag{7}
\]

The known fixed-gap implication goes in the opposite direction:
\[
\|x\|_2^2\ge c\,nT,
\]
and therefore only yields
\[
d_{p,\epsilon}^2\le C\,\frac nT.
\tag{8}
\]
This is compatible with the desired contradiction but does not provide it.

### 4. What the physical Jacobi kernel currently supplies

On the genuine \(U\),
\[
H
=
\frac12\left(I_U+\epsilon K_{UU}+\frac1n\mathbf1_U\mathbf1_U^*\right),
\tag{9}
\]
and the cross block is
\[
J_{UW}
=
\frac12\left(\epsilon K_{UW}+\frac1n\mathbf1_U\mathbf1_W^*\right).
\tag{10}
\]

The physical diagonal is exact:
\[
K_{ii}=-\frac1n,
\qquad
J_{ii}=\frac12+\frac{1-\epsilon}{2n}.
\]
The pointwise Jacobi estimate
\[
|K_{ij}|\le \frac{\sqrt p}{n}
\]
only gives, for example,
\[
\|K_{UW}\|_{\mathrm{HS}}^2
\le
\frac{p}{n^2}|U||W|,
\]
which is \(O(n)\) when both sets have macroscopic size. It gives neither a lower bound for the Schur complement in (5) nor a \(T\)-dependent control of the adaptive compression.

The relaxed projection model confirms that projection algebra, exact reciprocity, optimization, and complementarity alone cannot imply the desired bound. That model is not an actual-prime counterexample: it lacks the circulant Jacobi kernel and its physical diagonal. It therefore establishes a limitation of the generic argument, not a refutation of the prime statement.

## Remaining gap

For both physical signs \(\epsilon=\pm1\), with the actual least-energy support \(S_{p,\epsilon}\), physical mark \(0\), and one fixed admissible function \(T(p)\to\infty\), the missing estimate is either

\[
\|x_{p,\epsilon}\|_2^2=o(nT(p)),
\]
or equivalently
\[
\operatorname{dist}(\mathbf1,\operatorname{Ran}(JE_U))^2
=\omega(n/T(p)).
\]

The currently available fixed-gap spectral window instead forces a source-weighted low-spectrum contribution of order \(T\), so no fixed-regularization or trace estimate can bridge this gap.

## Ranked next tests

### 1. Direct actual marked-return test — highest priority

For the actual \(W=S^c\), let
\[
D_{p,\epsilon}=E_W^*PE_W.
\]
For a fixed \(b>0\), with
\[
L(p)=\lfloor bT(p)\rfloor,
\]
test or prove, uniformly for \(p\to\infty\) through eligible primes and separately for both \(\epsilon=\pm1\),
\[
\sum_{j>L(p)}
\langle e_0,D_{p,\epsilon}^j e_0\rangle
=o(T(p)).
\tag{11}
\]

At each fixed prime the series is nonnegative and convergent, and the exact identity is
\[
\sum_{j>L}
\langle e_0,D_{p,\epsilon}^j e_0\rangle
=
\frac{\langle x,(I-C)^Lx\rangle}{q^2},
\]
where \(C=E_S^*PE_S\). This route directly targets the required contradiction and preserves the adaptive support and physical marking. A trace bound or a bound for a nonadaptive mask would not suffice.

### 2. Construct an exact \(J\)-sector witness on \(W\)

Search for \(z_{p,\epsilon}\) supported on the genuine \(W=S^c\) satisfying
\[
J_{UW}z_W=0,
\]
with
\[
\frac{|\langle\mathbf1,z\rangle|^2}{\langle z,Jz\rangle}
\ge L_p\,\frac n{T(p)},
\qquad L_p\to\infty.
\tag{12}
\]

Then (3) gives the desired lower bound. A near-mask \(z\) is not enough unless one explicitly solves or controls the leakage equation \(J_{UW}z_W\ne0\). The complementary optimizer \(R\) cannot be used directly because its leakage is exactly \(\mathbf1_U\), as in (4). This test would require a genuinely prime-sensitive use of the circulant Jacobi entries and the actual complementary zero set.
