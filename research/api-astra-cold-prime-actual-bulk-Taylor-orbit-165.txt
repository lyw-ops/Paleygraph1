## Result: inconclusive, with a precise failure of the available sufficient bound

I cannot presently prove a summable signed translated orbit for the actual bulk Taylor source. The obstruction is not a counterexample to the orbit itself; it is that the available estimates control the source only **before** the full outer frozen transport, while the translated norm of the transported multiplier is still larger than one.

### Fixed setting

- Prime family: the eligible cold primes \(p\to\infty\) for which the cited packet, sheet, and convolution estimates hold; write \(n=p\).
- Physical marking: \(t_0=n=p\), while \(t_i=t\) for \(i\ne0\).
- Both lifts are retained.
- Domain: the original complex square \(Q\), not a relaxed square.
- Frozen height: \(h=602\), with
  \[
  D=D_{602},\qquad B=KD,\qquad Z=(I-B)^{-1}K.
  \]
- Translated norm: \(P_{5000}\).
- Counting control: normalized \(\ell^2\) operator norm and entrywise/max-entry bounds.
- Order of limits: first sum or estimate the infinite orbit and Taylor series at each fixed eligible prime \(p\); only then take \(p\to\infty\).

Let \(R=V_{601}-V_{600}\), and let \(r^{\mathrm b}\) be the bulk Taylor remainder evaluated on the actual pair of rows. The actual remainder satisfies
\[
r=r^{\mathrm b}+\delta e_0,
\]
where \(\delta=O(\varepsilon^2)\) is the marked physical correction.

The bulk series is
\[
r^{\mathrm b}_i
=
R_i^2\int_0^1(1-s)(4t_i)^{-1/2}
\left(1+\frac{(V_{600,i}+sR_i)^2}{4t_i}\right)^{-3/2}\,ds.
\]

The leading \(l=0\) term has already been priced and is excluded from the new claim:
\[
\|Kr^{(0)}\|_{P_{5000}}<0.000031.
\]
The whole untransported bulk Taylor term has the established bound
\[
\|Kr^{\mathrm b}\|_{P_{5000}}<0.000115+O(p^{-1/2}).
\]

## What is controlled

The marked exception is controlled in the physical transport:
\[
\sum_{j\ge0}\|B^jK(r-r^{\mathrm b})\|_\infty
=O(\varepsilon^2p^{-0.455}).
\]
This uses the genuine height-602 max-entry estimate
\[
\sum_{j\ge0}\|(KD)^jK\|_{\max}=O(p^{-0.455}),
\]
which is valid for both lifts and the actual marked coordinate.

For the alternative transport involving \(K^2\),
\[
ZK(r-r^{\mathrm b})
=
(r-r^{\mathrm b})-\overline{(r-r^{\mathrm b})}
+
ZD\bigl((r-r^{\mathrm b})-\overline{(r-r^{\mathrm b})}\bigr),
\]
the centered non-atomic part is again \(O(\varepsilon^2p^{-0.455})\), but the direct atom
\[
\delta(e_0-\mathbf 1/n)
\]
has no decay in the stated translated norm. Thus the physical atom is harmless for the \(B^jK\)-orbit, but it is not harmless for the \(ZK\)-transport unless it is explicitly separated.

The frozen height-602 multiplier estimates are
\[
\|B\|_{P_{5000}}<1.066,
\qquad
\|B\|_{2\to2}<0.518,
\]
and
\[
\|B^jK\|_{\max}
\ll
\min\{p^{-1/2}(1.066)^j,\,(0.518)^j\}.
\]
The resulting entrywise sum is \(O(p^{-0.455})\).

## The precise gap

The required physical bulk orbit is
\[
\mathcal O_{\mathrm b}
=
\sum_{j\ge0}B^jK r^{\mathrm b}
=
Zr^{\mathrm b}.
\]
The alternative inverse-times-\(K^2\) expression is
\[
\widetilde{\mathcal O}_{\mathrm b}
=
\sum_{j\ge0}B^jK^2r^{\mathrm b}
=
ZKr^{\mathrm b}.
\]

The available \(P_{5000}\) estimate only gives, schematically,
\[
\|B^jK r^{\mathrm b}\|_{P_{5000}}
\lesssim
(1.066)^j\|Kr^{\mathrm b}\|_{P_{5000}},
\]
which is not summable. The counting estimate is summable, but it is not a translated-norm estimate for the full signed source. Combining it with the max-entry bound gives only
\[
\|B^jK r^{\mathrm b}\|_\infty
\le
\|B^jK\|_{\max}\sum_i|r^{\mathrm b}_i|.
\]
Since
\[
\sum_i|r^{\mathrm b}_i|
=
p\,\|r^{\mathrm b}\|_{1,p},
\]
the entrywise summation produces a factor \(p\):
\[
\sum_{j\ge0}\|B^jK r^{\mathrm b}\|_\infty
\lesssim
p^{1-0.455}\|r^{\mathrm b}\|_{1,p}.
\]
This is \(O(p^{0.545})\) for a merely \(O(1)\) normalized first norm of the source and is therefore useless for confinement. It does not become small merely because
\[
\|Kr^{\mathrm b}\|_{P_{5000}}<0.000115.
\]

The missing statement is a **source-local signed transport estimate** of the form
\[
\sum_{j\ge0}
\|B^jK r^{\mathrm b}_{\ge1}\|_{P_{5000}}
< C_{\mathrm{bulk}}\varepsilon^2,
\]
where \(r^{\mathrm b}_{\ge1}\) denotes the bulk Taylor series with the already priced \(l=0\) term removed, uniformly over \(Q\), both lifts, and all sufficiently large eligible primes. Nothing in the existing \(1.066\) multiplier bound proves this. Nor does the \(O(p^{-0.455})\) entrywise resolvent estimate, because it loses the normalized-to-unnormalized factor \(p\).

Consequently:

- the actual marked atom is controlled in the physical \(B^jK\)-orbit;
- the leading quadratic source is controlled before the outer inverse;
- the signed bulk tail after the outer inverse remains unproved;
- no nonlinear confinement theorem follows.

This is a failure of the current sufficient bound, not a refutation of the asymptotic target.

## Hypotheses that would suffice

Either of the following would close the transport part.

### H1. Source-local translated contraction

For the actual bulk source, prove directly
\[
\sum_{j\ge0}
\|B^jK r^{\mathrm b}_{\ge1}\|_{P_{5000}}
\le C\varepsilon^2
\]
with \(C\) independent of \(p\), both lifts, and \(t\in Q\).

A stronger but easier-to-check version is a decomposition into signed translated packets \(r_{\nu}\) with
\[
r^{\mathrm b}_{\ge1}=\sum_\nu r_\nu,
\qquad
\|B^jr_\nu\|_{P_{5000}}
\le C\rho^j w_\nu,
\quad \rho<1,
\quad \sum_\nu w_\nu=O(\varepsilon^2).
\]

### H2. Genuine moving starter

Alternatively, construct a moving physical starter \(V_{m(p)}\) with residual
\[
r_p=\|F_t(V_{m(p)})-V_{m(p)}\|_{2,p}
\]
and prove, uniformly on the original \(Q\),
\[
p^{1-\beta}r_p^2=o(1),
\]
together with the signed partial-sum and sheet hypotheses of the frozen-orbit transfer theorem. The existing exponent \(\beta=0.455\) would require
\[
r_p=o\!\left(p^{-0.2725}\right),
\]
while the stronger \(\beta=1/2\) threshold requires \(r_p=o(p^{-1/4})\). No such moving residual has yet been established for the actual prime family and physical marking.

## Ranked next tests

### 1. Highest priority: the first unpriced signed bulk coefficient

Take the \(l=1\) term in the exact bulk series:
\[
r^{\mathrm b,(1)}_i
=
-\frac{3R_i^2}{2\sqrt{4t_i}}
\int_0^1(1-s)
\frac{(V_{600,i}+sR_i)^2}{4t_i}\,ds.
\]

Separate its actual word types, including both lifts and the common base-pair characters, and test the **transported** quantities
\[
B^jK r^{\mathrm b,(1)}\quad\text{and}\quad B^jK^2r^{\mathrm b,(1)}
\]
in \(P_{5000}\), not merely \(Kr^{\mathrm b,(1)}\). The decisive output is a uniform ratio or packet majorant \(\rho<1\) after the first signed transport. If this fails already for \(l=1\), it identifies a named obstruction in the bulk iteration. If it succeeds, the same word ledger can likely be summed over \(l\) using \(M^2/N<1\).

### 2. Second priority: moving-starter residual test

Construct an actual \(V_{m(p)}\) on the original square, retaining \(t_0=p\) and both lifts, and measure
\[
r_p=\sup_{t\in Q}\|F_t(V_{m(p)})-V_{m(p)}\|_{2,p}.
\]
Then verify the two distinct finite-prefix conditions:

1. signed resolvent partial sums through the \(\beta\log p/|\log q|\) cutoff;
2. signed residual-prefix sums through the \(\beta\log p/(2|\log q|)\) cutoff.

The test passes only if the residual rate satisfies \(p^{1-\beta}r_p^2=o(1)\) and the corresponding starter remains on the physical sheets. This would bypass the unresolved full translated bulk orbit, but it is a genuinely new moving-starter theorem rather than a consequence of the fixed height-602 estimates.
