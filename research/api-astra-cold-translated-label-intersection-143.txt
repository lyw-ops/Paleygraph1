## Checked result

Work with the actual eligible odd-prime family \(q\) (and, where the cited construction permits it, the separately verified prime-square extensions), with
\[
n=(q-1)/2,\qquad t_0=n,
\]
the bulk floors \(t_i=t\) on the original cold square, the pure seed \(S=s_nC_1\) unshifted, the actual Legendre multiplier \(Y\) unshifted, and exactly one translated source \(X_\xi\). Both full lifts are retained, and the marked denominator is restored at the end. The translated pair is assumed genuinely disjoint from the base pair:
\[
\{\xi,\chi\xi\}\cap\{1,\chi\}=\varnothing .
\]

### 1. Folded rank coincidences are not arithmetic coincidences

The four phase rows have the following total generic dimensions, obtained from
\[
r=\sum_{k>0} k h_k+b,\qquad b=2:
\]

\[
\begin{array}{c|c|c}
\text{order and phase} & \sum k h_k & r\\ \hline
S\text{ then }Y,\ \text{same fiber} & 1+3=4 & 6\\
S\text{ then }Y,\ \text{different fibers} & 2+4=6 & 8\\
Y\text{ then }S,\ \text{same sign} & 3+1=4 & 6\\
Y\text{ then }S,\ \text{opposite sign} & 4 & 6
\end{array}
\]

Thus the first, third, and fourth rows have the same rank, and the second row has rank eight. This is only a folded inertia statement. It does not identify the corresponding arithmetic constituents.

### 2. Generic global arithmetic labels are separated

For the actual leading mixed block on the unshifted seed column, the two corrected orders have the form
\[
\frac{\alpha s_n}{4t}
 \left(F_4+\frac{D+D^\vee}{8}+\frac R4\right)
 +O_{P_{5000}}(n^{-1/2}).
\]

The rank-four field \(F_4\) and the newer fields \(D,D^\vee,R\) have distinct derived arithmetic types. The independence calculations cited in the record separate their \(SL_2^2\), \(SL_3^2\), and rank-four factors, with the common Jacobi exponent retained. Therefore no constituent of \(F_4\) is equal to a constituent of \(D\), \(D^\vee\), or \(R\), even when their folded ranks agree.

The same separation persists on the genuinely translated column: tensoring by either translated full-lift character \(\xi\) or \(\chi\xi\) changes the common Jacobi/twist coordinate but does not identify the independent derived factors. Consequently, under the stated disjointness hypothesis, there is **no generic arithmetic label pooling between the \(F_4\) sector and the \(D,D^\vee,R\) sectors**. In particular, the equal-rank rows do not produce opposite signed copies of a common global label.

This is the exact arithmetic separation currently supported by the supplied records.

### 3. There is one exact physical coincidence, but it is not yet a global label identity

For the selected degree-one inverse-line mixed block, the two physical orders are exactly equal after retaining the actual marking:
\[
H_{S,Y}(x)=H_{Y,S}(x).
\]
The equality follows from
\[
u\longmapsto x^2/u,
\]
which preserves the relevant quadratic-sign factor and exchanges the two mixed orders. It includes the constant marked corrections and both full-field square-selector branches.

At the collision \(x=-1\), the two local sections have arithmetic twists whose product is trivial at the collision. The local geometric vanishing-cycle inertia in both orders is
\[
\operatorname{Unip}(2).
\]
Thus the two orders have the same local inertia label for this selected inverse-line realization.

However, the supplied argument does **not** prove equality of the complete global arithmetic sheaf labels or of their full Frobenius character decompositions. It proves equality of the physical position-domain kernels and equality of the displayed local inertia contribution. The intrinsic local Frobenius matrix and the rest of the global nearby representation remain unspecified.

Hence the correct conclusion is:

- **Generic global derived labels:** separated between \(F_4\) and \(D,D^\vee,R\).
- **Selected inverse-line physical block:** exactly equal in the two orders, with common local inertia \(\operatorname{Unip}(2)\).
- **Full global arithmetic label of that coincident physical block:** not established.

### Error and limit statement

The physical lift and marking repairs are bounded by
\[
O_{P_{5000}}(n^{-1/2})
\]
relative to the canonical translated-column price, uniformly on the nominated cold square and for the fixed disjoint translated pair. This error is taken after forming the exact finite-field expressions and then sent to zero along the eligible-prime family:
\[
q\to\infty,\qquad n=(q-1)/2\to\infty.
\]
The exact physical equality of the two inverse-line orders holds before this limit; the \(O_{P_{5000}}(n^{-1/2})\) term concerns the comparison with the simplified leading arithmetic realization, not a failure of the order-exchange identity.

## Remaining gap

The argument does not classify all exceptional translated twists. The disjointness condition removes the obvious collisions
\[
\{\xi,\chi\xi\}\cap\{1,\chi\}\neq\varnothing,
\]
but the records do not prove that these are the only exceptional twists after determinant/Jacobi shifts and possible accidental identifications in the rank-four representation ring. Nor does the local vanishing-cycle calculation determine the complete global arithmetic label of the exactly equal inverse-line block.
