# A paired seed saving in the actual height correction

Use the [actual height change](cold-frozen-cross-reduction-138.md) with \(W=V_{602}-V_{601}\). On the original closed square, \(W\) has unshifted representation price below \(\varepsilon=0.011814\) and both endpoint rows have absolute dimension budget below \(M=4.8\). Both representations designate the same Jacobi seed of mass \(s_n\ge\sqrt8\) separately from the other centers and the actual Picard residual. Put \(N=4(2031/128-2^{-17})\) and \(x=M^2/N\).

For \(D_{602}-D_{601}\), telescope each bulk analytic term using one \(W\) and \(2\ell\) other factors from the two endpoints. Pair the latter factors in the [first-moment estimate of Round 128](cold-actual-quadratic-128.md#first-moment-price-of-the-actual-derivative-difference). On a seed pair the independent lifted weights are equiprobable \(\pm1\). Their mean absolute sum is one, compared with the separate triangle cost two. This deducts one \(s_n^2\) times the other masses per pair before the bound \(M\) is applied. Every other contribution is still charged by its unsigned upper. The resulting mass and first moment bounds are
\[
 m_0\le \frac{\varepsilon}{\sqrt N}(1-x)^{-3/2},\qquad
 m_1\le \frac{\varepsilon}{\sqrt N}(1+2x-12/N)(1-x)^{-5/2}.
\]
The bound uses actual endpoint representations throughout, so no dimension enlargement by \(\varepsilon\) is needed. The translated column cost for the derivative difference after \(K\) is at most \(m_0+2m_1+O_{5000}(\varepsilon n^{-1/2})\), including both physical boundary pairs and the two marked floors \(t_0=n\). Indeed two is the Lipschitz constant of the base price, and off-base weights incur only the total multiplier mass as in Round 128. The strict rational comparison
\[
 \frac{\varepsilon^2(3+3x-24/N)^2}{N(1-x)^5}<(0.017)^2
\]
therefore gives \(\|A_{602}-A_{601}\|_{P_{5000}}<0.017\) eventually through eligible primes on the whole square.

The two inherited products with this height difference cost less than \(0.017\cdot 0.528=0.008976\) and \(0.017\cdot1.048=0.017816\). Consequently an actual height-601 mixed anticommutator below \(0.268468\) suffices for the \(0.277444\) mixed budget. An actual height-601 square difference below \(0.703184\) suffices for the \(0.721\) full two-height budget. These sufficient estimates concern the frozen rows, with the same physical marking. A strict signed estimate at either new budget and a subsequent invariant-ball argument remain to be proved.
