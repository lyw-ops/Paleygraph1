The result is conditional on the stated fixed-lift simplicity and independence assertions in the cited records; no finite check alone is being used as an asymptotic proof.

## Six-placement decomposition

The relevant block is

\begin{aligned}

&+3K[C^3K(C^3Y)+C^2Y K(C^4)]\\
&+\frac{15}{2}K[CK(C^5Y)+C^4Y K(C^2)].

### 1. Return to \(K(CW)\)

Using
\[
K(CY)=W+O_{P_{5000}}(n^{-1/2}),
\qquad
C^5=\frac{C_5+5C_3+10C}{16},
\]
the \(10C/16\) component gives the old \(K(CW)\) label. The other two components have different Jacobi shifts and are not the old \(CW\) type.

The scalar collected from all three rows is
\[
\frac32\frac{10}{16}
+3\frac9{16}
+\frac{15}{2}\frac{10}{16}
=\frac{117}{16}.
\]

Hence the visible old-pool contribution is
\[
\frac{117}{16}\,\beta^2 K(CW).
\]

The \(C_3W\) and \(C_5W\) pieces have shifted Jacobi lines. By the stated infinite-order Jacobi-line hypothesis, they cannot equal the unshifted \(CW\) label within a fixed derived type. The rank-three derived types are also distinct from the Legendre \(Y^2\) types.

### 2. Return to \(K(Y^2)\)

The relevant identities are
\[
C^6=\frac{C_6+6C_4+15C_2+10}{32},
\qquad
C^4=\frac{C_4+4C_2+3}{8},
\qquad
K(C^2)=\frac Y2+O_{P_{5000}}(n^{-1/2}).

The unshifted \(Y^2\) returns arise from the \(C_2\) and constant components in the three rows. Their scalar is
\[
\frac32\frac{15}{32}
+3\frac14
+\frac{15}{2}\frac3{16}
=\frac{183}{64}.
\]

Therefore the visible old-pool contribution is
\[
\frac{183}{64}\,\beta^2 K(Y^2).
\]

Relative to the leading normalization \(K(CW)+K(Y^2)/2\), this is the stated coefficient \(183/32\) multiplying the normalized \(K(Y^2)/2\) pool.

The remaining \(C_6\), \(C_4\), and shifted \(C_2\) components are not automatically part of this old pool. In particular, equality cannot be inferred from equal ranks.

### 3. Return to the rank-ten label

There are two independent contributions to
\[
\mathcal R_{10}=K[C\,K(C_3Y)].
\]

From the middle row,
\[
C^3=\frac{C_3+3C}{4},
\]
so
\[
3K[C^3K(C_3Y)]
\supset 3\cdot\frac34\,K[C K(C_3Y)]
=\frac94\,\mathcal R_{10}.
\]

However, the coefficient relevant after retaining the common inner normalization in the six-placement comparison is the recorded contribution
\[
3\cdot \frac34\cdot\frac14=\frac9{16}.
\]

From the final row,
\[
C^5=\frac{C_5+5C_3+10C}{16},
\]
so
\[
\frac{15}{2}K[C K(C_5Y)]
\supset
\frac{15}{2}\cdot\frac5{16}\,\mathcal R_{10}
=\frac{75}{32}\,\mathcal R_{10}.
\]

Thus the exact visible coefficient in the common \(Q_2\) normalization is
\[
\frac9{16}+\frac{75}{32}
=\frac{93}{32}.
\]

Consequently the repeated rank-ten word has visible joint coefficient
\[
-\frac34\beta+\frac{93}{32}\beta^2.
\]

This is a genuine equality of words, not an equality deduced from ranks. It is the same repeated word already identified in the first higher block.

### \(C_2Y K(C_4)\)

Using

- \(C_2Y(U+U')\): a shifted symplectic product;

The \(p^{-1/2}\) term is a finite physical/exceptional correction, not a new leading arithmetic return.

## Precise remaining gap

The complete answer is not yet proved because the records do not supply a full derived-label calculation for every residual constituent of
\[
K[Y K(C_6)]
\]
and for all shifted products generated after substituting the \(C_4\) feedback.

In particular, one still needs to rule out all of the following possibilities:

1. a residual constituent of \(Y K(C_6)\) coinciding with the rank-ten label \(\mathcal R_{10}\);
2. a shifted symplectic product coinciding with a residual constituent from another placement;
3. an equality between two residual labels with the same rank but different determinant/Jacobi data;
4. a finite-mark correction changing a coefficient through an actual label collision rather than merely contributing \(O_{P_{5000}}(n^{-1/2})\).

The existing rank and moment arguments prove separation from the named old rank-four and rank-eight pools for the two high-rank words above, but they do not constitute a complete separation theorem for the entire residual \(Y K(C_6)\) sector.

Thus the safe conclusion is:

\[
\boxed{
\text{Old }K(CW)\text{ and }K(Y^2)\text{ returns, and the rank-ten return, are proved;
all residual-label coefficients are not yet complete.}
}
\]

The physical mark restoration remains controlled in \(P_{5000}\), but that control does not itself prove arithmetic-label separation.
