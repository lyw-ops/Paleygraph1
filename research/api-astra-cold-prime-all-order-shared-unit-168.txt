## Result

For the actual eligible prime family \(p\to\infty\) used in the cold construction (in particular \(p\equiv1\pmod 4\)), retain

\[
n=t_0=\frac{p-1}{2},\qquad R=V_{601}-V_{600},\qquad
V_s=V_{600}+sR,
\]

the original complex square, both Jacobi lifts, the physical marking at coordinate \(0\), and the translated price \(P_{5000}\). Let

\[
D_{602}=\operatorname{diag}\Phi'_{t}(V_{602}),\qquad
B=KD_{602}.
\]

The all-order pure-seed terms sharing the same actual \(R^2\) label have an explicit common scalar factor. This factor preserves the first-order cancellation, but it does **not** by itself produce a summable transported tail under the currently proved \(KD_{602}\) estimates.

### 1. All-order common scalar

At bulk coordinates the two lifts give

\[
S=s_n\frac{z+z^{-1}}2,\qquad
s_n=2\sqrt{\frac pn}.
\]

Consequently,

\[
[S^{2l}]_{\mathrm{Laurent}\,0}
=
s_n^{2l}\frac{\binom{2l}{l}}{4^l}.
\]

For the even Taylor source

\[
r^{\mathrm b}
=
\sum_{l\ge0}(-1)^l b_l(4t)^{-l-1/2}
R^2\int_0^1(1-s)V_s^{2l}\,ds,
\qquad
b_l=(2l+1)\frac{\binom{2l}{l}}{4^l},
\]

the pure-seed component \(V_s\rightsquigarrow S\) therefore contributes, relative to the \(l=0\) \(R^2\) source, the common factor

\[
\boxed{
\Xi_{\mathrm{all}}(t)
=
\sum_{l\ge0}(-1)^l
(2l+1)\frac{\binom{2l}{l}^{\,2}}{16^l}
\left(\frac{s_n^2}{4t}\right)^l
}.
\]

The first terms are

\[
\Xi_{\mathrm{all}}(t)
=
1-\frac{3s_n^2}{16t}
+\frac{45s_n^4}{256(4t)^2}
-\frac{350s_n^6}{4096(4t)^3}
+\cdots.
\]

Since \(U_n=s_n^2/2=2p/n\), the first correction is exactly

\[
1-\frac{3U_n}{8t},
\]

recovering the common factor \(\Xi(t)\) already obtained from the first two Taylor orders.

The series is absolutely convergent on the original square. Indeed, for sufficiently large \(p\),

\[
\left|\frac{s_n^2}{4t}\right|
\le 0.127
\]

uniformly on the square, while the coefficient asymptotic is \(O(l)\). Thus the pure-seed scalar may be summed before taking absolute values. The marked coordinate is not governed by this bulk scalar: replacing the bulk expression by the physical marked radical at \(t_0=n\) costs, after the first \(K\),

\[
O(n^{-1/2}\varepsilon^2),
\]

with the same charge as in the earlier two-order calculation. The bad-character and lift corrections retain their separate charges.

### 2. The first regular surviving label

After the unit branches are removed once, the first regular surviving label is the actual two-increment label

\[
R^2.
\]

Its pure-seed contribution is

\[
r_{\mathrm{reg}}^{\mathrm{seed}}
=
\Xi_{\mathrm{all}}(t)\,
\frac{R^2}{2\sqrt{4t}},
\]

up to the separately charged marked and bad-coordinate repairs. The \(R\)-factors retain their actual first-moment charges; no additional subtraction of their norm is legitimate after taking an unsigned norm.

For every fixed outer exponent \(j\),

\[
B^jK r_{\mathrm{reg}}^{\mathrm{seed}}
=
\Xi_{\mathrm{all}}(t)\,
B^jK\!\left(\frac{R^2}{2\sqrt{4t}}\right)
\]

on the bulk. Hence the all-order summation improves the **initial source price** but does not alter the transport operator \(B\).

This is the key obstruction: the scalar factor is attached before transport. It is not a multiplier that can be moved through \(K\) or through \(D_{602}\), and it gives no new factor depending on \(j\).

### 3. Why the present estimates do not prove a summable full transport

The available actual height-\(602\) estimates are

\[
\|B\|_{P_{5000}}<1.066,
\qquad
\|B\|_{2\to2}\le 0.518,
\]

together with the entrywise column estimate

\[
\|B^jK\|_{\max}
\ll
\min\{n^{-1/2}(1.066)^j,\,(0.518)^j\}.
\]

The interpolation of these two bounds gives

\[
\sum_{j\ge0}\|B^jK\|_{\max}
=O(n^{-0.455}),
\]

but only for a **single column** or a finite marked-column combination. The \(R^2\) source is a distributed word source, not a single physical column. Applying the entrywise estimate to it would require an \(\ell^1\)-type bound on the full source with the correct \(n\)-scaling; the currently available \(P_{5000}\) price and first-moment comparison do not provide that.

Conversely, the \(2\)-norm contraction \(0.518^j\) cannot be converted into a useful translated \(P_{5000}\) estimate for this physical packet. The pure-seed obstruction recorded previously shows that small frequency entries and counting contraction alone do not control the physical packet price.

Therefore the presently justified statement is

\[
\boxed{
\text{The all-order common pure-seed factor exists and improves the initial }R^2
\text{ source price, but no summable full transport }
\sum_{j\ge0}\|B^jK r_{\mathrm{reg}}^{\mathrm{seed}}\|_{P_{5000}}
\text{ follows.}
}
\]

The failure is not a refutation of summability on the actual geometry. It is a precise failure of the available sufficient bound: the scalar cancellation is source-side, whereas the missing estimate is a signed translated transport estimate for the distributed \(R^2\) channel.

### Order of limits

All formulas above are uniform on the original square for each sufficiently large eligible prime \(p\), with \(n=(p-1)/2\), and for both lifts. The valid order is:

1. sum the absolutely convergent pure-seed Laurent/Taylor series at fixed \(p\);
2. charge the physical mark and bad-character repairs;
3. analyze the outer transport \(j\to\infty\);
4. only then take \(p\to\infty\).

The current entrywise argument controls the \(j\)-sum for marked columns, but does not justify interchanging the \(j\)-sum with the \(p\to\infty\) limit for the full distributed \(R^2\) source.

## Evidence and hypotheses used

- Actual increment \(R=V_{601}-V_{600}\), not an abstract increment.
- Physical \(t_0=n=(p-1)/2\) at the marked coordinate.
- Bulk \(t\) elsewhere on the original square.
- Both lifts and the original square are retained.
- The scalar unit branch is removed once; regular branches and marked repairs remain charged.
- The common Jacobi seed is \(S=2\sqrt n\,Ke_0\) with \(s_n=2\sqrt{p/n}\).
- Existing paired first-moment savings justify the improved \(R^2\) source price.
- Existing \(KD_{602}\) bounds justify column summability, but not distributed-source transport.

## Remaining gap

A useful signed cold transfer now requires one of the following:

1. a translated operator estimate for \(B^jK\) restricted to the actual \(R^2\)-generated regular channel, with a rate \(<1\) in a compatible norm; or
2. a genuine moving starter/nonlinear bridge that avoids summing the full frozen translated response.

## Ranked next tests

### 1. Highest priority: signed partial sums on the actual \(R^2\) channel

For eligible primes \(p\), compute or rigorously majorize

\[
L_J(R^2)
=
\sum_{j=0}^{J}B^jK
\left(\frac{R^2}{2\sqrt{4t}}\right)
\]

using the actual two lifts, physical mark, and original square. Track the \(P_{5000}\) price before absolute values, with \(J\) through the natural cutoff \(J\asymp c\log n\). Test whether the signed partial sums remain uniformly bounded or contract after the all-order factor \(\Xi_{\mathrm{all}}\). This directly tests the missing transport statement rather than another source-only estimate.

### 2. Second priority: restricted nonlinear bridge

Prove a weighted estimate for \(B\) on the subspace generated by the actual regular \(R^2\) words, rather than on all translated packets. The target is a norm \(P^\ast\) satisfying

\[
\|B^jK r_{\mathrm{reg}}^{\mathrm{seed}}\|_{P_{5000}}
\le C\theta^j\|r_{\mathrm{reg}}^{\mathrm{seed}}\|_{P^\ast},
\qquad \theta<1,
\]

with the physical mark charged separately by \(O(n^{-1/2}\varepsilon^2)\). If this restricted estimate fails, the next viable route is a moving starter and nonlinear bridge rather than further same-label summation.
