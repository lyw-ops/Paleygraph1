## What was genuinely established

The valid signed collection is the following. Writing
\[
E_h=A_h-B_3,
\]
and expanding in the actual non-\(S,\alpha Y\) displacement
\[
\beta W+H_h,
\]
the common full Jacobi–Legendre label \(\lambda=(k,a,b)\) has pre-modulus coefficient
\[
\binom{j+l}{l}\beta^l\Gamma_{j+l}(\lambda)
\]
in \(H_h^jW^l\).

At fixed \(H_h\)-degree \(j\), two disjoint collections are valid:

\[
H_h^j:\qquad
\Gamma_j+\frac14\binom{j+2}{2}\beta^2\Gamma_{j+2},
\]
and
\[
H_h^jW:\qquad
(j+1)\beta\Gamma_{j+1}
+\frac34\binom{j+3}{3}\beta^3\Gamma_{j+3}.
\]

The coefficient \(1/4\) is the arithmetic unit from the four ordered dual pairings in \(W^2\). The coefficient \(3/4\) is the selected common-standard part of \(W^3\). These are disjoint summands before taking absolute values, and the same folded collection is valid for both physical lifts because the full label lists are symmetric under the two Legendre-standard exchange and Jacobi exponent sign exchange.

The checker then:

1. retains the exact common complex square;
2. retains \(t_0=n\) and the physical marking;
3. retains both lifts;
4. retains the full translated \(P_{5000}\) input class;
5. applies the old positive \(H_h^j\) comparison and exact \(W\)-measure after the signed merger;
6. preserves the original tail, residual leak, lost-path terms, and \(O_{5000}(n^{-1/2})\) physical repairs.

Hence the improvement is a legitimate signed estimate on the stated family, not merely a positive-price artifact.

## Why the source-local resolvent does not yet provide an alternative confinement

The prior translated-resolvent estimates give two different regimes.

At height \(250\), the translated price rate satisfies
\[
q<0.996682
\]
with the auxiliary off-base parameter \(\Lambda=1000\), and therefore the transported resolvent has entrywise \(O(n^{-1/2})\) bounds.

At the genuine height \(601\), the available bounds are instead
\[
q<1.048
\]
in the translated \(P_{5000}\) comparison and
\[
\|KD_{601}\|_{2\to2}<0.5145.
\]
Combining these yields the entrywise resolvent estimate
\[
\max_{i,x}\left|[(I-KD_{601})^{-1}K]_{ix}\right|
=O(n^{-0.467}).
\]

That estimate is not a \(P_{5000}\)-confinement estimate. An entrywise bound does not control the translated price norm uniformly, because a translated column may have many nonzero physical coordinates and the \(P_{5000}\) price sums them with a first-moment weight. A naive Cauchy–Schwarz conversion would introduce a factor comparable to
\[
\left(\sum_x P_{5000}(x)^{-1}\right)^{1/2},
\]
which is not bounded uniformly in the growing finite group. No weighted \(\ell^2\)-to-\(\ell^\infty\) embedding with the required \(P_{5000}\) price has been proved.

Moreover, the height-601 resolvent is linearized at the reference predictor \(V_{601}\). The supplied estimates do not prove that the true stationary diagonal differs from this reference on a support small enough to be absorbed by the resolvent perturbation criterion. Consequently they do not produce a nonlinear invariant ball for the true cold orbit.

The physical-coordinate issue is also not removable by the entrywise estimate: the two lift corrections and marked-coordinate restoration are only known to be \(O_{5000}(n^{-1/2})\) after the translated price is controlled. They cannot be inserted after an uncontrolled \(\ell^2\)-to-\(P_{5000}\) conversion.

## Precise status

- **Supported:** a real signed two-vertex improvement exists, uniformly on the original square and for the actual marked prime family.
- **Refuted:** the presently collected \(W^2/W^3\) common-label family is quantitatively sufficient for the \(0.51961239\) target.
- **Not refuted:** a larger signed determinant-shift collection or a genuinely weighted source-local confinement.
- **Open:** the actual signed contraction in the common \(P_{5000}\) domain, and any invariant uniform in eligible primes.

The failure is therefore a failure of the current sufficient bound, not a lower bound for the actual derivative or actual signed operator.
