# Independent rank-four groups for two inner sources

The \(C_4\) and individual Legendre symmetric-square sources in the [two-vertex calculation](astra-crossnode-59.md) can be described jointly with the old arithmetic group. The two lifts of the \(C_4\) source have independent \(Sp_4\) derived factors. The two Legendre symmetric-square lifts are arithmetic duals with one new \(SL_4\) factor and determinant \(L^{\star4}\) on the specified lift. Both conclusions hold uniformly through the eligible primes and prime squares. The new factors rule out any one-step linear cancellation of those inner sources with a direct old label, even for a non-Jacobi old outer multiplier.

## The pure fourth cosine

Let \(L\) be the normalized Jacobi convolution line and \(N=N_2\) the determinant-one Legendre object. Write \(\mathcal T\) for ordinary twisting by \(\chi(1-a)\) on the generic local system followed by middle extension. This is the geometric part of \(K\) on a source. The two \(C_4\) constituents are \(\mathcal T(L^{\star4})\) and its quadratic Mellin twist. Their identification with the balanced hypergeometric family \(N_4,N_4^\chi\) uses the exact trace and normalization of the Chebyshev columns. In the notation of that proof, \((\mathcal H_4)(4)\) before the ordinary twist is the fourth Jacobi convolution line with its stated pure perverse trace.

The hypergeometric calculation already gives arithmetic and geometric groups \(Sp_4^2\) for these two lifts. In particular the norm-one test gives the symplectic self-duality, their two quadratic twists have distinct local characters, and the unique interior singularity excludes a comparison by a translate at \(-1\). This is the existing Chebyshev-column theorem, applied here to the new joint group. All old simple derived factors have types \(A_1,\ldots,A_6\). Neither \(Sp_4\) factor has a simple quotient in common with any old factor. Goursat therefore makes the two \(Sp_4\)'s act independently, including relative to the common Jacobi torus. ## The individual quadratic Legendre source

Put \(M=\operatorname{Sym}^2_{\star}N\), with the symmetric square taken in the convolution category, and \(\mathcal D=\mathcal T(M)\). The Deligne weight lists for \(M\) are \((-4,0,4)\) at \(1\) and \((0,0,0)\) at \(\chi\), from the tensor-compatible fiber and the two rank-two Legendre lists. Thus the generic rank before the ordinary twist is four. Its convolution dimension is three, so its positive inertia at one has one invariant line. It is therefore one unipotent block of size four. After the ordinary twist the inertia at one is quadratic times that block, with zero invariant stalk. The unique interior singularity is again at one. The new Deligne lists are \((-4,0,0,0)\) at \(1\) and \((0,0,0,4)\) at \(\chi\). The single-block Theorem 17.1 gives geometric \(SL_4\) as derived group. Since \(M\) is arithmetically convolution-self-dual, \(\mathcal D^\vee=\mathcal D^\chi\) arithmetically. This uses \(\chi(1-a^{-1})=\chi(a)\chi(1-a)\) for eligible \(q\), and the compatibility of ordinary rank-one twisting with duality and inversion. Hence the two lifts form one derived factor. [Two-boundary rule and even-source closure](astra-feedback-52.md#reading-rank-from-two-mellin-characters) · [Katz, Theorems 16.1 and 17.1](https://web.math.princeton.edu/~nmk/mellin331.pdf)

The centered first list of \(\mathcal D\) is \((-3,1,1,1)\). The four older \(SL_4\) lists are \((-3/2,-3/2,3/2,3/2)\), \((-1/2,-1/2,-1/2,3/2)\), the zero list and \((-2,-1,-1,4)\). All differ from the new list and its negation. A possible rank-one convolution twist would only shift the whole list. The Goursat condition holds against all four old \(SL_4\) factors. The other old factors and the two \(Sp_4\)'s have different simple types. This proves the full new derived product independently of the old factors. ## The arithmetic determinant of \(\mathcal D\)

The two weight sums \(-4,4\) and the one-interior-singularity category identify the geometric determinant line with \(L^{\star4}\), by the same relation between boundary weights and rank-one hypergeometric parameters as in [round 55](rank-four-positive-55.md#geometric-independence-and-conditional-moments). To evaluate the arithmetic constant, use the normalized ordinary local system underlying \(\mathcal D\), of weight \(-1\). It has at zero a single trivial-inertia block of length four, at one a quadratic block of length four, and at infinity another quadratic block of length four. Its ordinary determinant is geometrically trivial. In the trivial-character fiber of \(N\) the two eigenvalues are \(q^{-1},q\). Indeed their weights are \(-2,2\) and the zero-boundary Legendre nodal fiber gives invariant eigenvalue \(q^{-1}\), since the square root of \(-1\) is in the field. Equivalently its unnormalized ordinary Legendre trace at zero is the split nodal value \(1\). The ordinary twist defining \(\mathcal D\) is unramified with value one at zero. The new invariant eigenvalue there is therefore \(q^{-2}\), from the negative extreme of the symmetric-square fiber. The four eigenvalues along the size-four monodromy chain are \(q^{-2},q^{-1},1,q\). Thus the ordinary determinant is the constant line with eigenvalue \(q^{-2}\).

Fix a full good character \(\alpha\ne1,\chi\). Compare \(\mathcal D\otimes\mathcal L_{\alpha(a)}\) in ordinary notation with four copies of \(\mathcal L_{\alpha(a)}\otimes\mathcal L_{\chi(1-a)}\). At \(0,1,\infty\) the multisets of tame semisimple characters agree and all are nontrivial. The ratios of the unramified determinants are \(q^{-2}\) at all three places. With differential \(da\), the local epsilon quotient is the unramified determinant ratio raised to \(1+\operatorname{ord}(da)\). The three exponents are \(1,1,-1\), so the global quotient is \(q^{-2}\). The ranks match, both cohomologies are concentrated in degree one of dimension four, and their common global and cohomological sign factors cancel. The reference eigenvalue in each rank-one Mellin cohomology is \(-J(\alpha,\chi)\). Hence the determinant of the normalized good Mellin fiber is \(q^{-2}J(\alpha,\chi)^4\), exactly the fiber of \(L^{\star4}\). This selects arithmetic scalar \(+1\). Eligible \(q\) have good characters already over the base. [Local and global epsilon factors](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1501.07807v2) · [The same reference-quotient method in round 32](astra-prime-phase-32.md)

For an exact finite normalization check, write \(n_q(a)=\ell(a)(\ell*\ell)(a)\) with \(\ell(a)=\chi(1-a)\). Then \(n_q/q\) is the perverse trace of \(N\). The raw \(N\star N\) trace is \(q^{-2}n_q*n_q\) at good characters, and the symmetric square subtracts the punctual unit. At the trivial Mellin character the compact trace of the symmetric square is \(1+2q^{-2}\) from the fiber eigenvalues \(q^{-2},1,q^2\) and the boundary \(q^{-4}\) replacement of the positive eigenvalue. Raw multiplication minus the unit gives \(4q^{-2}-1\). At the quadratic character all the weights are zero and the two traces agree. Inverting the trivial-character correction \(2-2q^{-2}\) gives the constant row \(2(q+1)/q^2\). The subsequent twist \(\ell\) kills the punctual unit, so the trace of \(\mathcal D\) is
\[
 \frac{\ell(a)}{q^2}\big[(n_q*n_q)(a)+2(q+1)\big].
\]
The [checker](two-node-groups-59-check.py) constructs the unnormalized row on four extensions of each base and recovers the determinant by exact cyclotomic Newton identities. The [records](two-node-groups-59-checks.jsonl) check every good character in the bases \(5,9,13\). The uniform determinant identity follows from the epsilon quotient.

## Consequences for the two-node test

In the Laurent root at each node, an old character is trivial on all three new derived factors. Tensoring one \(C_4\) lift or one quadratic Legendre lift with any old character leaves a nontrivial new factor. It cannot combine with a direct old label. The three factors are independent, and for the \(SL_4\) factor the standard and its dual are distinct. Thus none of these four individual lift terms can cancel a different lift at the linear outer vertex, even if the multiplier has a nontrivial old derived label. The next nonzero balanced return to old sectors can occur at quadratic degree in one new factor. The two averaged fields each have a quadratic unit of coefficient \(1/2\), from the two symplectic self-pairings in the first family and two ordered standard–dual pairings in the second. The mixed product has no unit. There is no classification here of the rank-eight \(\mathcal T(N\star N^\chi)\).
