## Result: formal higher-order block, but no verified pooling identity

I used the actual seed
\[
S=s_nC_1,\qquad s_n=2\sqrt{p/n},
\]
the actual \(Y\)-coefficient \(\alpha\), the original marked floor \(t_0=n\), and the unshifted \(C_1\) input. The physical operator is always the full \(K\), including its inverse line; no inverse-line term is discarded.

For
\[
d_t(z)=\frac{z}{2\sqrt t}\left(1+\frac{z^2}{4t}\right)^{-1/2},
\]
the relevant expansions are
\[
d_t(S)
 =\frac{S}{2\sqrt t}
 -\frac{S^3}{16t^{3/2}}
 +O(S^5t^{-5/2}),
\]
and
\[
d_t'(S)Y
 =\frac{Y}{2\sqrt t}
 -\frac{3S^2Y}{16t^{3/2}}
 +O(S^4Yt^{-5/2}).
\]

Define, with the actual coordinatewise \(t_i\),
\[
T_1=K\,\operatorname{diag}\!\left(\frac{S_i}{2\sqrt{t_i}}\right),
\qquad
U_1=K\,\operatorname{diag}\!\left(\frac{\alpha Y_i}{2\sqrt{t_i}}\right),
\]
\[
T_3=-K\,\operatorname{diag}\!\left(\frac{S_i^3}{16t_i^{3/2}}\right),
\qquad
U_3=-K\,\operatorname{diag}\!\left(\frac{3S_i^2\alpha Y_i}{16t_i^{3/2}}\right).
\]

The previously treated mixed block is
\[
(T_1U_1+U_1T_1)C_1.
\]

The first higher-order transported block having the same one-\(Y\) content is therefore exactly
\[
\boxed{
\mathcal H_4 C_1
=
(T_3U_1+U_1T_3+T_1U_3+U_3T_1)C_1 .
}
\tag{2}
\]

The signs and relative coefficients are fixed: the two cubic-seed placements have coefficient \(-1\), while the two quadratic-seed corrections to \(d_t'(S)Y\) have coefficient \(-3\), before the common powers of \(2\sqrt t\) are inserted.

For a common bulk denominator \(t\), equation (2) becomes
\[
\begin{aligned}
\mathcal H_4C_1
={}&-\frac{\alpha s_n^3}{64t^2}
 K\!\left[C_1^3\,K(C_1Y)\right]
-\frac{\alpha s_n^3}{64t^2}
 K\!\left[Y\,K(C_1^4)\right]\\
&-\frac{3\alpha s_n^3}{64t^2}
 K\!\left[C_1\,K(C_1^2Y)\right]
-\frac{3\alpha s_n^3}{64t^2}
 K\!\left[C_1^2Y\,K(C_1^2)\right],
\end{aligned}
\tag{3}
\]
up to the usual \(O_{P_{5000}}(n^{-1/2})\) marked-denominator corrections. Formula (3) retains all four whole-\(K\) orderings.

Using the known first feedback rows,
\[
K(C_1Y)=W+\frac{C_1}{p},
\qquad
K(C_1^2)=\frac Y2-\frac{C_1}{\sqrt p},
\]
the leading pieces of (3) reduce to the four new word types
\[
K(C_1^3W),\qquad
K(YK(C_1^4)),\qquad
K(C_1K(C_1^2Y)),\qquad
K(C_1^2Y^2).
\tag{4}
\]

The \(p^{-1}\) and \(p^{-1/2}\) pieces from the displayed feedback rows are lower-order physical corrections after the final \(K\), with the same \(O_{P_{5000}}(n^{-1/2})\) status as in the accepted mixed-column calculation.

## What can and cannot be identified

The Jacobi part alone gives
\[
C_1^3=\frac{C_3+3C_1}{4},
\qquad
C_1^4=\frac{C_4+4C_2+6}{8}.
\]
Thus the pure cubic-seed term necessarily contains a direct \(C_1\) component and a new \(C_3\) component, while the fourth-power term contains \(C_4,C_2\), and the constant line.

However, the available accepted feedback identities do **not** determine
\[
K(C_1^2Y),\qquad K(C_1^3W),\qquad K(YK(C_1^4)),
\]
or their subsequent \(K\)-transports. In particular, the known identities for \(K(C_1Y)\) and \(K(C_1^2)\) do not imply an identity for \(K(C_1^2Y)\). Consequently, there is presently no checked arithmetic-object equality showing that any of the four terms in (4) is identical to another term with an opposite signed coefficient.

The independent-group results cited for \(F_4,D,D^\vee,R\) rule out pooling with those already identified rank-four and mixed-Legendre labels. They do not classify the new \(C_3\), \(C_4\), \(C_1^2Y\), or \(C_1^3W\) sectors. Therefore the proposed all-order collection on the unshifted \(C_1\) column is **inconclusive**, not supported or refuted.

## Physical estimate status

A signed \(P_{5000}\) estimate for (2) is also not established. The positive pure-seed comparison from the resummed calculation cannot be reused as a signed estimate: it takes absolute values after separating nonseed words, and its \(0.570\) witness obstruction remains applicable. The present block requires either:

1. exact identification and cancellation among the four terms in (4), or  
2. a new signed transport bound for their distinct arithmetic types.

The marked-floor restoration is not the obstruction. Once the bulk calculation is available, the difference between \(t_0=n\) and the bulk denominator contributes only
\[
O_{P_{5000}}(n^{-1/2})
\]
relative to the selected block, uniformly on the fixed original closed square, with the physical zero coordinate and both boundary pairs retained.

The limits must be taken in this order:

1. fix the actual prime/prime-square family and the original closed square with \(t_0=n\);
2. perform the finite signed character calculation at each eligible prime;
3. obtain a uniform \(P_{5000}\) estimate for sufficiently large eligible primes;
4. only then pass to the asymptotic \(n\to\infty\), absorbing the marked and exceptional-lift terms as \(O(n^{-1/2})\).

No full square contraction, half-step confinement, or growing-depth estimate follows from (2).

## Ranked next tests

1. **Exact finite character test for the four new words.**  
   For small eligible prime powers, compute the full \(K\)-images of
   \[
   C_1^3W,\quad YK(C_1^4),\quad C_1K(C_1^2Y),\quad C_1^2Y^2
   \]
   with the original physical marking. Check equality of their complete Mellin labels, including inverse-line and exceptional-frequency components, not merely numerical trace values. This is the fastest decisive test for genuine pooling.

2. **Derive the next feedback rows and price them signed.**  
   Prove exact formulas for \(K(C_1^2Y)\) and \(K(C_1^4)\), then transport them through the remaining \(K\) while retaining the \(t_0=n\) marking. A successful result must give either an explicit opposite-sign identity in (4) or a uniform \(P_{5000}\) bound for \(\mathcal H_4\) that is separate from the positive analytic tail.
