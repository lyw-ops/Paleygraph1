## Result: \(\alpha=o(1)\) gives signed interaction control only with an additional stability or source-size hypothesis

Fix \(t,M>0\), let \(p=2n+1\) run through the Paley primes, and write
\[
A=\sqrt{M^2+4t}.
\]
By the supplied mark limit \(U_0/\sqrt n\to0\), the mark is eventually not in the overflow set, since its enlarged threshold is \(\sqrt{M^2+4n}\). Thus all nonzero \(\tau_i\) are bulk coordinates and
\[
\tau_i=\operatorname{sgn}(U_i)(|U_i|-A),\qquad |U_i|>A.
\]

A pointwise checked inequality is
\[
\tau_i^2\le (U_i^2-M^2)_+.
\]
Indeed, on overflow,
\[
\tau_i=\sqrt{M^2+L_i}-\sqrt{M^2+4t},\qquad L_i=U_i^2-M^2,
\]
and \(\tau_i\le\sqrt{L_i}\). Consequently
\[
\|\tau\|_{2,n}^2\le \alpha. \tag{A}
\]

### 1. A genuine one-sided transport reduction

On the overflow set,
\[
U_i=\operatorname{sgn}(U_i)(A+|\tau_i|),
\]
so exactly
\[
\tau_iU_i=A|\tau_i|+\tau_i^2.
\]
Therefore, for \(D_p=\|Y-U\|_{2,n}\),
\[
\begin{aligned}
\mathbb E_n(\tau_iY_i)
&=\mathbb E_n(\tau_iU_i)+\mathbb E_n\bigl(\tau_i(Y_i-U_i)\bigr)\\
&\le A\,\mathbb E_n|\tau_i|+\mathbb E_n\tau_i^2
   +\|\tau\|_{2,n}D_p\\
&\le A\sqrt{\alpha}+\alpha+\sqrt{\alpha}\,D_p. \tag{B}
\end{aligned}
\]
Hence the actual signed interaction is \(o(1)\) whenever \(\alpha=o(1)\) and
\[
\sup_p\|Y-U\|_{2,n}<\infty. \tag{C}
\]
This is a new sufficient reduction, but not a consequence of \(\alpha=o(1)\) alone.

There is a parallel source formulation. Put
\[
z=b-2\sqrt t\,\mathbf 1\ge0.
\]
Since \(K\mathbf1=0\), \(Y=Kz\), and \(K^2=I-J_0\),
\[
|\mathbb E_n(\tau_iY_i)|
=|\mathbb E_n((K\tau)_iz_i)|
\le \|\tau\|_{2,n}\,\|z\|_{2,n}
\le \sqrt\alpha\,\|z\|_{2,n}. \tag{D}
\]
Thus a uniform \(L^2\) bound on the true nonnegative source \(z\) also converts \(\alpha=o(1)\) into the desired vanishing interaction. Its mean is fixed,
\[
\mathbb E_n z_i=\nu-2\sqrt t,
\]
but this does **not** imply a uniform \(L^2\) bound: the source could concentrate on a vanishing set.

A standard sufficient route to (C) is a genuine stability estimate. If, away from the exceptional mark, both \(Y\) and \(C_M(U)\) stay in a fixed bounded interval \([-B,B]\), then \(\Phi_t\) is \(q\)-Lipschitz there with
\[
q=\frac{B}{\sqrt{4t+B^2}}<1.
\]
Using \(\|U-C_M(U)\|_{2,n}\le\sqrt\alpha\),
\[
\|Y-U\|_{2,n}
\le q\bigl(\|Y-U\|_{2,n}+\sqrt\alpha\bigr),
\]
and hence
\[
D_p\le \frac{q}{1-q}\sqrt\alpha. \tag{E}
\]
The mark has derivative tending to \(1\), so a bulk estimate alone does not automatically prove (E); this is precisely the remaining field-first stability issue.

### 2. Why the actual Paley arithmetic does not give \(\max K\tau=o(1)\) from \(\alpha=o(1)\)

For the genuine Paley matrix, apart from the exceptional/diagonal terms, the entries in a fixed row have common magnitude \(h_p\asymp n^{-1/2}\) and signs given by a quadratic character:
\[
K_{rj}=h_p\,\chi(r-j).
\]
The necessary arithmetic condition for vanishing signed transport is therefore
\[
\max_r h_p\left|\sum_{j\in S_p}\chi(r-j)\tau_j\right|=o(1), \tag{F}
\]
where \(S_p=\{j:\tau_j\ne0\}\). This is a weighted Paley character-sum condition on the **actual overflow set**.

There is no unconditional cancellation in (F) for adaptively chosen \(S_p,\tau_j\). To see the obstruction with correct units, fix a row \(r\), choose \(m_p=\lfloor c/h_p\rfloor\asymp\sqrt n\) indices for which \(\chi(r-j)\) has the same sign, and prescribe
\[
\tau_j=c_0\,\operatorname{sgn}\chi(r-j)
\]
on this set. Then
\[
(K\tau)_r=h_p m_p c_0+o(1)\asymp c_0,
\]
while, choosing the corresponding prescribed overflow height
\[
L_j=4t+2Ac_0+c_0^2,
\]
gives
\[
\alpha=\frac{m_p}{n}L_j=O(n^{-1/2})\longrightarrow0.
\]
Thus \(\alpha=o(1)\) is compatible with order-one \(\max K\tau\) for the actual Paley sign pattern.

This is an explicit **prescribed** obstruction, not a genuine capped fixed point: the displayed \(U\) and \(\tau\) need not satisfy \(U=K\Phi_t(C_M(U))\). Therefore it does not refute the desired theorem for the self-consistent capped row. It does show that Paley arithmetic alone, or the entrywise bound, cannot supply the theorem; one must prove that self-consistency prevents such row-adapted overflow.

### Remaining gap

The unresolved statement is consequently precise:

> For the genuine capped fixed point, does self-consistency force either (C), or the weighted character estimate (F), or directly \(\mathbb E_n\tau_iY_i=o(1)\)?

At present \(\alpha=o(1)\) supplies only (A). It does not by itself imply \(\kappa=\max K\tau=o(1)\), and the adaptive Paley obstruction prevents an unconditional character-sum argument.

### Ranked next tests

1. **Bulk stability test:** prove a fixed-floor bound on the true bulk source \(z=b-2\sqrt t\) in \(L^2\), with the mark separated explicitly. Then (D) immediately yields \(\mathbb E_n\tau Y=o(1)\).

2. **Overflow character test:** compute the field-first capped iterates and test whether their genuine overflow sets satisfy (F). The target is weighted discrepancy \(o(h_p^{-1})=o(\sqrt n)\), not merely \(\alpha=o(1)\).
