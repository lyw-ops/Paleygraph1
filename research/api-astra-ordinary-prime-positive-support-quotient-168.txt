## Result

For the actual ordinary-prime family, no prime-uniform \(o(T)\) bound is currently obtained for either the positive-support cost or the orthogonal quotient cost. The exact basis-invariant split is valid, but the available Jacobi mixing estimate controls only the dimension of a low-spectrum subspace, not the overlap of the actual centered source with that subspace.

Here the prime family is an ordinary-prime sequence \(p\to\infty\), with
\[
n=\frac{p-1}{2},
\]
restricted, if necessary, to a fixed physical sign \(\epsilon\) and fixed floor/marked window. The physical marking is the coordinate \(e_0\), and the relevant columns are the actual centered marked columns
\[
a_i=P_0e_i,\qquad
P_0=P_\epsilon-\frac{P_\epsilon e_0e_0^*P_\epsilon}{d},
\qquad d=(P_\epsilon)_{00}.
\]
All coefficient and matrix norms below are counting norms; the representer norm is the physical Euclidean norm.

Let \(U'\) be the actual independent zero-column extension, let
\[
A=\{i\in U':w_i>0\},\qquad B=U'\setminus A,
\]
where \(w\ge 0\) is the actual complementary interpolant satisfying
\[
Lw=-\frac r{\sqrt n}\mathbf 1_\Gamma,\qquad
\sum_{i\in U'}w_i=n-r.
\]
The centered Gram and source are
\[
G_0=(\langle a_i,a_j\rangle)_{i,j\in U'},\qquad
c_0=\frac1{\sqrt n}\left(\mathbf1_{U'}+\frac{q-1}{d}b_0\right),
\]
with \(G_0w=0\) and \(c_0^*w=0\).

---

## Exact orthogonalization on the actual positive support

The positive-support Gram is
\[
G_A=(G_0)_{AA}.
\]
Its kernel is exactly
\[
\ker G_A=\operatorname{span}\{w_A\},
\]
because \(w_A>0\) and the full centered Gram has kernel \(\mathbb Rw\).

Choose an orthonormal eigenbasis \(u_\ell\) of \(w_A^\perp\) with
\[
G_Au_\ell=\lambda_\ell u_\ell,\qquad \lambda_\ell>0.
\]
The physically orthonormalized marked columns are
\[
\phi_\ell
=\lambda_\ell^{-1/2}\sum_{i\in A}(u_\ell)_i\,a_i.
\]
Since \(c_{0,A}\perp w_A\), the minimal physical representer satisfying the \(A\)-constraints has squared norm
\[
\boxed{
C_+(A)
=
c_{0,A}^*G_A^\dagger c_{0,A}
=
\sum_{\lambda_\ell>0}
\frac{|u_\ell^*c_{0,A}|^2}{\lambda_\ell}.
}
\]
This is the genuine positive-support inverse cost. It is not altered by changing the independent extension \(U'\) while keeping the actual support \(A\).

The residual source on \(B\) is
\[
c'_B
=
c_{0,B}-G_{0,BA}G_A^\dagger c_{0,A},
\]
and the quotient Gram is
\[
S_B
=
G_{0,BB}-G_{0,BA}G_A^\dagger G_{0,AB}.
\]
The exact orthogonal quotient cost is
\[
\boxed{
C_{\mathrm q}(A)
=
(c'_B)^*S_B^{-1}c'_B.
}
\]
For nonempty \(B\), \(S_B\) is positive definite. The full centered marked cost splits as
\[
\boxed{
c_0^*G_0^\dagger c_0
=
C_+(A)+C_{\mathrm q}(A).
}
\]
Equivalently, \(C_{\mathrm q}(A)\) is the squared norm of the residual physical representer after quotienting the full marked span by the span of the genuine positive-support columns.

This is the requested basis-invariant split. The complementary coefficients \(w\) remain explicit: \(w_A\) is the kernel vector removed in the positive-support orthogonalization, and \(w\) remains the exact positive interpolant for the full centered system.

## What is actually proved on a small zero block

If the actual zero block satisfies
\[
|B|\le (1-\eta)\frac n{\sqrt p}
\]
for fixed \(\eta>0\), then the physical entry bounds and Gershgorin give
\[
G_{0,BB}\ge \frac{\eta}{4}I_B
\]
for all sufficiently large ordinary primes. Since
\[
|(c_0)_i|=O(n^{-1/2}),
\]
the direct zero-block debit obeys
\[
c_{0,B}^*G_{0,BB}^{-1}c_{0,B}
=
O_\eta\!\left(\frac{|B|}{n}\right)
=
O_\eta(n^{-1/2}).
\]
This is a genuine source-weighted estimate, but it concerns the elimination order \(B\) first. It does not by itself bound the positive-support cost \(C_+(A)\) or the quotient cost \(C_{\mathrm q}(A)\) after orthogonalizing on \(A\).

For the low-spectrum projector
\[
P_s=\mathbf1_{(0,s]}(G_{0,BB}),
\]
the actual Jacobi mixing estimate gives
\[
\operatorname{rank}P_s
\le
\frac{b^2/(4n)+O(b/\sqrt n)}{(1/2-s)^2},
\qquad b=|B|.
\]
At \(s=T^{-1/2}\),
\[
\operatorname{rank}P_s
\le
(1+O(T^{-1/2}))\frac{b^2}{n}
+O\!\left(\frac b{\sqrt n}\right).
\]

This is a checked rank bound on the actual adaptive block. It is not a source estimate.

Indeed, the relevant low-spectrum debit is
\[
C_{B,\mathrm{low}}
=
c_{0,B}^*P_sG_{0,BB}^{\dagger}P_sc_{0,B}
=
\int_{(0,s]}\lambda^{-1}\,d\mu_{c_{0,B}}(\lambda),
\]
where
\[
\mu_{c_{0,B}}(E)
=
\|1_E(G_{0,BB})c_{0,B}\|_2^2.
\]
A rank estimate controls the number of atoms/eigenvalues in this integral, but not the source measure \(\mu_{c_{0,B}}\). The actual source could, as far as the present estimates show, have substantial projection onto the low eigenspace. Thus
\[
\operatorname{rank}P_s\ll \frac{b^2}{n}
\]
does not imply
\[
C_{B,\mathrm{low}}=o(T).
\]

The same issue appears after positive-support orthogonalization: a small-dimensional quotient slow space can still contain the residual source \(c'_B\).

## Status for a fixed-gap sequence

Assume hypothetically that there is a fixed-gap sequence of ordinary primes \(p_j\), with fixed sign \(\epsilon\) and fixed floor, and with an admissible marked window \(T_j=T(p_j)\to\infty\). The order of limits is:

1. form the exact finite-\(p_j\) physical split using the actual \(e_0\), \(A_j\), \(B_j\), \(w_j\);
2. then let \(p_j\to\infty\), with \(T_j\to\infty\) inside the admissible window;
3. seek a uniform estimate \(C_+(A_j)+C_{\mathrm q}(A_j)=o(T_j)\).

The current evidence proves neither
\[
C_+(A_j)=o(T_j)
\]
nor
\[
C_{\mathrm q}(A_j)=o(T_j).
\]
It also does not prove a fixed-gap exclusion.

The precise failure is source alignment: the centered Jacobi mixing calculation supplies a low-rank upper bound, while the inverse costs require a source-weighted spectral bound. The relaxed positive-interpolant model shows that positivity of \(w\), correct mass scaling, and coarse Gram information alone do not prevent a large centered inverse cost. That model is not an arithmetic counterexample for the actual Jacobi matrices, so it refutes only the proposed sufficient inference, not the target statement.

## Remaining component and growing window

After the actual positive-support orthogonalization, the remaining unresolved quantity is
\[
\boxed{
C_{\mathrm q}(A)
=
(c'_B)^*
\left(
G_{0,BB}-G_{0,BA}G_A^\dagger G_{0,AB}
\right)^{-1}
c'_B.
}
\]
Equivalently, one must control the source-weighted low spectrum of the quotient Gram \(S_B\) at the admissible cutoff \(s=T_j^{-1/2}\), together with the positive-support cost from eigenvalues below that cutoff.

The existing \(B\)-block estimate gives an \(O_\eta(n^{-1/2})\) direct debit when \(b\ll n/\sqrt p\), and the high-spectrum transfer is harmless at \(s=T_j^{-1/2}\) provided the corresponding source debit is used. The unresolved part is the low-spectrum source overlap below that cutoff.

---

## Ranked next tests

### 1. Source-weighted quotient spectral measure — highest priority

For the actual \(A_j,B_j,w_j\), compute or bound
\[
\mu_j(E)
=
\left\|
1_E(S_{B_j})\,c'_{B_j}
\right\|_2^2,
\]
and prove, uniformly along every hypothetical fixed-gap sequence,
\[
\int_{(0,T_j^{-1/2}]}
\lambda^{-1}\,d\mu_j(\lambda)
=o(T_j).
\]
A sufficient dyadic form is
\[
\mu_j\bigl((2^{-m-1},2^{-m}]\bigr)
\ll
o(T_j)\,2^{-m}
\]
summed over \(2^{-m}\le T_j^{-1/2}\). This directly tests the actual source and quotient, rather than rank. It is the cleanest route to
\[
C_{\mathrm q}(A_j)=o(T_j).
\]

### 2. Adaptive-support coercivity or combined low-weight elimination

Prove a support-specific statement for
\[
C_j=B_j\cup\{i:0<w_{j,i}<H_j\}
\]
such as
\[
|C_j|\le (1-\eta)\frac n{\sqrt p}
\]
for some \(H_j\) compatible with the growing window, and then establish a lower coercivity estimate on the remaining high-weight signed Jacobi form after Schur elimination. This would control both the zero coefficients and the low positive weights in one actual support-dependent quotient.

The needed estimate must retain the signed squared-difference form
\[
z^*D_wG_0D_wz
=
-\sum_{i<j}(G_0)_{ij}w_iw_j(z_i-z_j)^2,
\]
including edges to low-weight coordinates. An unweighted rank or trace estimate is insufficient.

**Conclusion:** the exact orthogonal positive-support/quotient decomposition is checked and extension-invariant, but the requested prime-uniform inverse-cost bound remains inconclusive.
