# Separation of five named linear center terms under seed twists

Use the same physical nine-center predictor as in [Round 137](cold-linear-split-137.md), with fixed row \(\mathcal C\) from [Round 65](astra-joint-65.md), original closed cold square, actual mark \(t_0=n\) and heights 601 and 602. The raw multiplier \(d_t'(S)(\mathcal C-S)\) is expanded on good bulk full Mellin lifts. After collecting seed powers, every term with exactly one nonseed center field is a scalar times a convolution-line twist \(L^{\star k}\) of that field. The scalar carries the analytic coefficient and the appropriate lift weight. This observation concerns the bulk trace objects before the final application of \(K\).

## Which identities can occur at this vertex

The five named summands \(U,U',D,D^\vee,R\) in the predictor have exact underlying objects in the earlier analysis. With \(N\) the determinant-one Legendre object, \(\chi\) the quadratic character, and \(\mathcal T\) the ordinary twist by \(\chi(1-a)\) followed by middle extension, the two \(U\) objects come from \(\mathcal T(L^{\star4})\) and its quadratic Mellin twist. The \(D\) object is \(\mathcal T(\operatorname{Sym}^2_\star N)\) and its quadratic Mellin twist is the arithmetic dual, with determinant of the first object \(L^{\star4}\). The \(R\) object comes from \(\mathcal T(N\star N^\chi)\). Their compact normalization and exceptional feedback are in the [two rank-four group calculations](two-node-groups-59.md) and the [mixed Legendre calculation](astra-rankeight-60.md).

The joint connected derived product contains independent \(Sp_4,Sp_4,SL_4,Sp_8\) factors, trivial on the old center objects. Restrict the five field representations to this product. Their restrictions are, respectively, the standard on the first \(Sp_4\), the standard on the second \(Sp_4\), the standard and dual on the same \(SL_4\), and the standard on \(Sp_8\). The Jacobi line and scalar normalizations are trivial on this product. Objects attached to distinct factors therefore remain inequivalent after every Jacobi twist. The \(SL_4\) standard and dual are inequivalent even after twisting, because their restrictions have distinct highest weights. Every one of the five is separated from any old center constituent, whose restriction is trivial. This applies to the actual constituent labels on either lift, even if passing to the quadratic mate interchanges a pair. When the same constituent recurs on the two lifts it carries the same label and can be counted with its actual coefficient.

For a fixed constituent of dimension \(d>0\), a further identification with a different Jacobi exponent would imply \(L^{\star d(k-k')}\simeq 1\) by taking determinants. The Jacobi line has infinite order in this category, as detected by its nonzero boundary weight. Thus only equal exponents can identify repeated twists of this constituent. The earlier Laurent resummation already pools such repetitions in the scalar coefficient multiplying a fixed word.

Consequently at the degree-one center vertex there is no shared arithmetic representation between these five constituents and the other named center groups, and no shared label between two distinct members of the five apart from repetitions of a constituent already appearing through a lift. This applies to exact identifications of trace objects. It gives no lower bound for the physical packet operator and no assertion that values at an individual finite-field character are distinct. It also does not classify new label coincidences after final transport, when combining with a nontrivial input packet, or in the genuine Picard residual. The physical marked and bad-lift contributions are handled by the existing uniform repair rather than by identifying them with good-lift character values.

The direct follow-up is to classify which actual Picard residual objects can return to these derived types with the same Jacobi exponent. A separate mixed-block calculation would need to match representations through the intermediate \(K\). Degree one of the eight center terms alone gives no cancellation across the five separated labels.
