### Matching derived types

The matching is exact on the connected derived product
\[
Sp_4^{(U)}\times Sp_4^{(U')}\times SL_4^{(D)}\times Sp_8^{(R)}.
\]

- \(U\) and \(U'\) are the two independent \(Sp_4\) standards.
- \(D\) is the \(SL_4\) standard.
- \(D^\vee\) is its arithmetic dual, still a distinct \(SL_4\) label.
- \(R\) is the defining \(Sp_8\) representation.

No old-center constituent has any of these derived restrictions, and no two different members of this list become equal merely after a Jacobi twist. Thus the only same-label pooling is:
1. repetition of \(U\), \(U'\), \(D\), \(D^\vee\), or \(R\) itself; and
2. the explicit arithmetic duality \(D^\vee=D^\chi\), with its corresponding Jacobi shift.

### Jacobi exponents and determinant phases

The relevant exponent data are preserved as follows.

- \(U,U'\) arise from the two lifts of \(L^{\star4}\). Their \(Sp_4\) determinants are trivial.
- \(D\) has determinant
  \[
  \det D=L^{\star4}
  \]
  with arithmetic phase \(+1\).
- \(D^\vee\) is the arithmetic dual branch. In transported powers, the two standard branches carry the shifted cubic labels
  \[
  m^{-4}D,\qquad m^{4}D^\vee.
  \]
  More generally, the Pieri calculation gives shifts \(4j\) for the \(D\)-branch and \(4(j+1)\) for the dual branch.
- \(R\) is arithmetically self-dual, symplectic, and has determinant phase \(+1\). Its quadratic contraction produces no determinant shift.

Consequently, a residual can pool with a named linear field only when both its derived label and its Jacobi exponent agree. In particular, \(D\) and \(D^\vee\) cannot be pooled as though they were the same \(SL_4\) representation; they match only after retaining the appropriate duality and exponent shift.

- the residual leaves have coefficients of order \(10^{-9}\);

- the physical residual norm is still charged by the uniform \(0.260\) estimate;

Thus the result supports the proposed mission, but does not close the asymptotic invariant or establish genuine growing depth.
