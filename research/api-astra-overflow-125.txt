### New checked inequality and the remaining obstruction

Let \(q\) be the signed negative row at any fixed genuine ordinary depth, including the row obtained by starting from the projected cap Picard seed
\[
\widetilde b^0=(I-\epsilon K)a_m
\]
with
\[
m\ge (1/4+\kappa)\frac{\log n}{|\log q_M|},\qquad \kappa>0.
\]
Retain the true factors \(g,g_-\), the output mask through \(\Lambda^\pm\), and assume the established bounds
\[
\|q\|_{2,n}\le C_qn^{-1/4},\qquad
\|g\|_{2,n}+\|g_-\|_{2,n}\le C_gn^{-1/4},\qquad
\mu\le C_\mu n^{-1/2}.
\]

From the exact masked functional identity,
\[
T(q)
=\frac pn\mu\,\mathbb E_n\!\left[qH\right]
+O\!\left(n^{-1}\|q\|_{2,n}\right),
\qquad
H:=g\Lambda^- -g_-\Lambda^+,
\]
and \(0\le \Lambda^\pm\le1\), hence
\[
\|H\|_{2,n}
\le \|g\|_{2,n}+\|g_-\|_{2,n}
\le C_gn^{-1/4}.
\]
Therefore, uniformly over all such \(q\),
\[
\boxed{
T(q)\le
\frac pn\,\mu\,\|q\|_{2,n}\|H\|_{2,n}
+O(n^{-1}\|q\|_{2,n})
\le \frac{C}{n}+O(n^{-5/4}).
}
\tag{A}
\]
This is a genuine one-sided prime-uniform upper retaining both reciprocal factors and the removed output mark. It is not strong enough for the requested \(o(1/n)\) conclusion.

Indeed, (A) shows that the available norm information permits the critical size \(T(q)\asymp n^{-1}\). More precisely, since the remainder contributes only \(o(1/n)\),
\[
nT(q)=\frac pn\,n\mu\,\mathbb E_n(qH)+o(1),
\]
so the required upper \(T(q)=o(1/n)\) reduces to the arithmetic correlation condition
\[
\boxed{\mathbb E_n\!\left[q\,(g\Lambda^- -g_-\Lambda^+)\right]
=o(n^{-1/2})}
\tag{B}
\]
uniformly over the eligible primes. Cauchy–Schwarz gives only \(O(n^{-1/2})\), exactly the critical scale. Thus neither the true-factor norm bounds nor the output-mask bounds supply a power saving.

### Picard-height error does not remove this obstruction

For the projected cap Picard initializer, the capped contraction gives
\[
\|\widetilde b^0-z\|_{2,n}=O(q_M^m)
=O(n^{-1/4-\kappa}).
\]
Consequently, for the largest \(r=\lceil\sqrt n\rceil\) absolute coordinate errors,
\[
\sum_{i\in B_{\rm top}}|\widetilde b^0_i-z_i|
\le \sqrt r\,\Big(\sum_i|\widetilde b^0_i-z_i|^2\Big)^{1/2}
=O(n^{1/2-\kappa})
=o(\sqrt n).
\tag{C}
\]
The fixed-depth ordinary update preserves this localized threshold by the established Lipschitz/first-norm propagation. Hence the Picard seed can be transferred to the genuine ordinary row with
\[
n\bigl|T(\widetilde q)-T(q)\bigr|=o(1)
\]
at every fixed depth. This verifies that seed approximation is no longer the bottleneck. The unresolved issue is already present for the explicit Picard seed itself: proving (B).

### Precise obstruction to a Mellin-complexity shortcut

At logarithmic height \(m\asymp\log n\), the cap map is iterated \(m\) times before the ordinary repair begins. A fixed-degree Mellin or bounded-rank expansion is therefore unavailable without an additional approximation theorem whose rank grows slowly enough to preserve a prime-uniform cancellation estimate. The norm estimate \(O(n^{-1/4-\kappa})\) controls approximation error, but it supplies no cancellation for the main correlation in (B). In particular, replacing \(q\) by a low-complexity approximation only helps if one proves, for that approximation,
\[
\mathbb E_n(q_{\mathrm{approx}}g\Lambda^-)
-\mathbb E_n(q_{\mathrm{approx}}g_-\Lambda^+)
=o(n^{-1/2});
\]
boundedness of the individual terms does not imply cancellation between them.

Thus the proposed arithmetic estimate is presently **inconclusive**, with the exact missing power-saving condition given by (B). The all-prime goal remains open.

### Ranked next tests

1. **Joint masked Fourier/Mellin test.** Expand the explicit \(m\)-step seed and the two \(\Lambda\)-weights jointly, not separately, and estimate the resulting character sums with the output deletion retained. The target is a uniform \(o(n^{-1/2})\) bound for (B).

2. **Residual-correlation test.** Compute or bound
\[
R_m=\mathbb E_n\!\left[(\widetilde q_m-\widetilde q_{m-1})
(g\Lambda^- -g_-\Lambda^+)\right].
\]
If \(\sum_{r\ge m}|R_r|=o(n^{-1/2})\) uniformly in eligible primes, then a finite-height seed would inherit the desired upper; failure at the critical scale would quantify the actual residual obstruction.
