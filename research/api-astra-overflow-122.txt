## Result: a genuine two-step counting continuation bound, but no packet contraction

Let
\[
R_m=V_{m+1}-V_m,\qquad 
D^{\sec}_m=\int_0^1\Phi_t'\!\left(V_m+sR_m\right)\,ds,
\]
where the integral is taken on the actual common physical sheet along the segment joining \(V_m\) and \(V_{m+1}\). Then exactly
\[
R_{m+1}=K D^{\sec}_m R_m. \tag{A}
\]

This is the physical adjacent-secant operator; replacing it by \(D(V_m)\) is not legitimate without an error estimate.

### Quantitative secant perturbation

Assume the whole segment lies in a sheet-preserving domain on which
\[
\kappa_m=\sup_{s\in[0,1]}\|\Phi_t''(V_m+sR_m)\|_\infty<\infty.
\]
Then coordinatewise
\[
\|D^{\sec}_m-D(V_m)\|_\infty
\le \kappa_m\|R_m\|_\infty. \tag{B}
\]
Since \(K\) is an \(L^2\)-contraction,
\[
\|KD^{\sec}_m\|_{2\to2}
\le \|KD(V_m)\|_{2\to2}+\kappa_m\|R_m\|_\infty. \tag{C}
\]
Consequently, for any two consecutive physical secants satisfying the same hypotheses,
\[
\boxed{\;
\|R_{m+2}\|_{2,n}
\le
\bigl(\rho_m+\kappa_m a_m\bigr)
\bigl(\rho_{m+1}+\kappa_{m+1}a_{m+1}\bigr)
\|R_m\|_{2,n},
\;} \tag{D}
\]
where
\[
\rho_j=\|KD(V_j)\|_{2\to2},\qquad
a_j=\|R_j\|_\infty.
\]
This is a genuine scalar continuation estimate for the actual residuals, not an assumed uniform packet-mass estimate.

### Numerical implication at height 600

The supplied height-600 data give
\[
\rho_{600}<0.514,\qquad
a_{600}\le \|R_{600}\|_\pi<0.010955.
\]
Thus the first secant obeys
\[
\|KD^{\sec}_{600}\|_{2\to2}
\le 0.514+0.010955\,\kappa_{600}. \tag{E}
\]
In particular, if the actual segment curvature satisfies
\[
\kappa_{600}<86.3, \tag{F}
\]
then the single secant remains contractive. If both adjacent segments satisfy this bound with the same \(\kappa\), then
\[
\|R_{602}\|_{2,n}
\le (0.514+0.010955\kappa)^2\|R_{600}\|_{2,n}<\|R_{600}\|_{2,n}. \tag{G}
\]
For example, \(\kappa\le 10\) gives the explicit two-step factor
\[
(0.514+0.10955)^2<0.389.
\]
The threshold \(86.3\) is only an algebraic consequence of the certified residual price and the \(0.514\) linearized counting bound; it is not itself a verified curvature bound.

The marked coordinate is harmless at this level provided the segment stays on the marked sheet: its second derivative has the extra \(n^{-1/2}\) factor from the radicand \(4n+V_0^2\). The unresolved issue is the uniform bulk value of \(\kappa_m\) on the actual moving segment, not the secant identity.

## What this does not prove

It does **not** give residual packet contraction. The available absolute packet majorant at height 600 is
\[
q_{600}=1.046434,
\]
so taking absolute prices before composing two secants yields only
\[
q_{600}^2=1.095025\ldots>1.
\]
Therefore the existing packet estimate cannot certify contraction, even over two steps. The parity statement removes geometric punctual terms, but it does not bound the absolute coefficient/rank price of the surviving odd words. In particular, an odd signed word can have arbitrarily large absolute packet price while retaining zero punctual contribution.

The valid conclusion is therefore:

* **Counting resource:** conditional genuine two-step contraction (D), with a very permissive curvature threshold at height 600.
* **Packet resource:** no contraction established; the current absolute upper fails quantitatively because \(1.046434^2>1\).
* **Prime-uniform status:** not yet obtained, since neither a prime-uniform \(\kappa_m\) along all relevant physical segments nor a signed packet comparison has been supplied.

## Ranked next tests

1. **Direct secant-curvature replay.** On the actual complex square, evaluate
   \[
   \kappa_m=\sup_{s\in[0,1],\,x}
   |\Phi_t''(V_m(x)+sR_m(x))|
   \]
   with the marked and unmarked formulas kept separate. A bound \(\kappa_m<86.3\) at two consecutive heights would certify (G); a substantially smaller bound would give a useful scalar continuation resource through the height-600 region.

2. **Two-step signed packet replay.** Form the actual product
   \[
   K D^{\sec}_{m+1}K D^{\sec}_m
   \]
   before taking absolute packet prices, retain the odd-word signs and translated boundary fibers, and compute its induced price on the stored residual packet. The target is a bound \(<1\) on that **specific residual direction**, not on the full absolute operator. This is the minimal test capable of converting the parity information into genuine packet contraction.
