## Result

A signed two-step telescope exists for the **true \(R^2\) channel**, but the available estimates do not yet convert it into the required uniformly summed translated price. The telescope is exact for the frozen height-\(602\) operator and preserves the distinction between the genuine \(R^2\) source and the mixed-word debit.

Keep
\[
p\equiv1\pmod 4,\qquad n=\frac{p-1}{2},\qquad
R=V_{601}-V_{600},\qquad
B=KD_{602},
\]
with both lifts, the original complex square, and the physical mark \(t_0=n\). Write
\[
Z=(I-B)^{-1}K.
\]
On the bulk coordinates, the all-even seed resummation gives the actual \(R^2\) source
\[
q_{\mathrm{true}}^{\mathrm b}(t)
=
\Xi(t)\,\frac{R^2}{2\sqrt{4t}},
\]
where
\[
\Xi(t)=
\sum_{l\ge0}(-1)^l(2l+1)c_l^2
\left(\frac{s_n^2}{4t}\right)^l,
\qquad
s_n=2\sqrt{\frac pn},
\qquad
c_l=4^{-l}\binom{2l}{l}.
\]
The first terms are
\[
\Xi(t)=1-\frac34y+\frac{45}{64}y^2-\frac{175}{256}y^3+\cdots,
\qquad
y=\frac{s_n^2}{4t}.
\]
The previously established uniform bound on the original square is
\[
|\Xi(t)|<0.906
\]
for all sufficiently large eligible primes. This scalar applies only to the common actual \(R^2\) packet. The mixed-source terms retain their separate debit, including the positive pure-seed debit
\[
\varepsilon^2s_n^{2l}d_l,
\qquad
d_l=l+2(1-l)c_l,
\]
and the old mixed-word bound. No cancellation with those mixed terms is being asserted.

### Exact telescope

Since
\[
Z=(I-B)^{-1}K,
\]
we have the exact coboundary identity
\[
K=(I-B)Z.
\]
Therefore, for every \(J\ge0\) and every source vector \(q\),
\[
\sum_{j=0}^{J}B^jKq
=
\sum_{j=0}^{J}B^j(I-B)Zq
=
Zq-B^{J+1}Zq.
\tag{1}
\]
This applies in particular to \(q=q_{\mathrm{true}}^{\mathrm b}\), with no movement of \(K\) through a multiplier.

Equivalently, defining
\[
H_{\mathrm{true}}:=Zq_{\mathrm{true}}^{\mathrm b},
\]
the true \(R^2\) transport is the signed difference
\[
B^jKq_{\mathrm{true}}^{\mathrm b}
=
B^jH_{\mathrm{true}}-B^{j+1}H_{\mathrm{true}}.
\tag{2}
\]
Thus the natural moving signed starter is \(H_{\mathrm{true}}\), not the unsigned source norm.

The frozen \(2\to2\) estimate from height \(602\),
\[
\|B\|_{2\to2}\le 0.518,
\]
implies, for each fixed eligible prime,
\[
B^{J+1}H_{\mathrm{true}}\longrightarrow0
\quad(J\to\infty),
\]
provided \(H_{\mathrm{true}}\) is in the relevant second-norm space. Hence the signed infinite transport satisfies
\[
\sum_{j\ge0}B^jKq_{\mathrm{true}}^{\mathrm b}
=
Zq_{\mathrm{true}}^{\mathrm b}
=
H_{\mathrm{true}}
\tag{3}
\]
in the frozen linear problem.

This is a genuine signed telescope for the actual distributed \(R^2\) source.

## Physical mark and bulk decomposition

The scalar identity is valid on the bulk coordinates. Restoring the physical mark gives
\[
q_{\mathrm{true}}
=
q_{\mathrm{true}}^{\mathrm b}
+\delta_{\mathrm{mark}}e_0
+\delta_{\mathrm{bad}},
\]
where the prior estimates give a marked/bad-coordinate charge of order
\[
O(n^{-1/2}\varepsilon^2)
\]
after the first \(K\), with the stated physical \(t_0=n\) radicand. Consequently,
\[
\sum_{j=0}^{J}B^jKq_{\mathrm{true}}
=
Zq_{\mathrm{true}}^{\mathrm b}
+Z(\delta_{\mathrm{mark}}e_0+\delta_{\mathrm{bad}})
-B^{J+1}Zq_{\mathrm{true}}.
\tag{4}
\]
The frozen entrywise estimate controls the marked part after the appropriate \(K\)-smoothing at scale \(O(n^{-0.455})\) in the previously treated form. It does **not** presently give the required translated bound for the bulk quantity
\[
Zq_{\mathrm{true}}^{\mathrm b}.
\]

## What is proved and what is not

### Proved

For the genuine \(R^2\) channel:

1. The all-order seed resummation produces the scalar \(\Xi(t)\) on the original square.
2. The cancellation is attached to the same actual \(R^2\) packet under every frozen outer power \(B^jK\).
3. The exact signed identity (1) gives a two-step telescope.
4. The physical mark and bad-coordinate terms remain separate \(O(n^{-1/2}\varepsilon^2)\)-type repairs after the first \(K\).
5. The mixed-word source is not included in the telescope and must retain its independent debit.

### Not proved

The telescope does **not** imply the absolute translated summability
\[
\sum_{j\ge0}
\bigl\|B^jKq_{\mathrm{true}}\bigr\|_{P_{5000}}
<\infty
\]
with a useful uniform constant. Indeed, (2) only gives
\[
\|B^jKq_{\mathrm{true}}\|_{P_{5000}}
\le
\|B^jH_{\mathrm{true}}\|_{P_{5000}}
+
\|B^{j+1}H_{\mathrm{true}}\|_{P_{5000}},
\]
and the existing translated estimates do not bound \(H_{\mathrm{true}}=Zq_{\mathrm{true}}^{\mathrm b}\) in \(P_{5000}\).

The available entrywise estimate
\[
\|(KD_{602})^jK\|_{\max}
\ll
\min\{n^{-1/2}(1.066)^j,\;0.518^j\}
\]
controls frozen columns and their tails, but it is not a translated operator estimate on the distributed bulk source. Applying it directly would require a compatible first/counting norm bound for the full \(q_{\mathrm{true}}^{\mathrm b}\), which is exactly the missing step.

The nonlinear bridge is also unresolved: one must show that the moving starter
\[
H_{\mathrm{true}}=Zq_{\mathrm{true}}^{\mathrm b}
\]
remains inside the common physical coordinate disks and that its nonlinear correction can be charged without reintroducing an unsummed mixed-source term.

Therefore the current status is:

\[
\boxed{\text{Exact signed telescope: proved.}}
\]
\[
\boxed{\text{Uniform summed translated price or sufficient nonlinear bridge: open.}}
\]

The order of limits remains important: first obtain estimates uniform in sufficiently large eligible primes on the original square, then take \(J\to\infty\) in the frozen telescope. The present argument does not justify interchanging an absolute \(j\)-sum with the prime limit.

## Ranked next tests

### 1. Highest priority: translated bound for the moving starter

Prove directly, on the original square and both lifts, a bound of the form
\[
\|Zq_{\mathrm{true}}^{\mathrm b}\|_{P_{5000}}
\le C_{\mathrm{move}},
\]
with \(C_{\mathrm{move}}\) below the available common-domain margin. The calculation should retain the scalar factor \(\Xi(t)\), use
\[
|\Xi(t)|<0.906,
\]
and split the source into:

- the bulk true \(R^2\) packet;
- the physical marked correction;
- the bad-coordinate correction;
- the mixed-source debit, charged without cancellation.

A successful estimate would turn (3) into a sufficient moving-starter construction.

### 2. Secondary: quantify failure of absolute transport

Compute or bound
\[
\sum_{j=0}^{J}
\|B^jKq_{\mathrm{true}}^{\mathrm b}\|_{P_{5000}}
\]
using the two available rates \(1.066\) and \(0.518\), while preserving the paired \(R^2\) saving. If the resulting lower/upper comparison shows that the absolute sum cannot be made uniformly small, record the obstruction explicitly: the signed telescope remains valid, but absolute translated summability is the wrong target and must be replaced by the moving-starter bridge above.
