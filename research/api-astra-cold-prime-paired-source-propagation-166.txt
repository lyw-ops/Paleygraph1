## Result

For the actual eligible-prime family used in the cited estimates—sufficiently large eligible primes \(p\), with
\[
n=t_0=(p-1)/2,
\]
the physical mark at coordinate \(0\), both lifts, the original closed square \(Q\), and the actual radicand at the mark—the paired Jacobi calculation gives a genuine preinverse estimate
\[
\|Kr\|_{P_{5000}}<10^{-4}+O(n^{-1/2}),
\]
more precisely \(<0.000103\) eventually, for the actual even Taylor source
\[
r=\Phi_t(V_{600}+R)-\Phi_t(V_{600})-D_{600}R,
\qquad R=V_{601}-V_{600}.
\]

This is a valid improvement, but it does **not** presently yield either

1. a summable translated bound for
   \[
   (I-KD_{602})^{-1}Kr
   =\sum_{j\ge 0}(KD_{602})^jKr,
   \]
   or

2. a moving-starter/confinement proof for the nonlinear orbit.

The precise failure is that the paired Jacobi saving is attached to the two actual \(R\)-factors in the source. After multiplication by powers of \(KD_{602}\), no corresponding signed packet estimate has been proved for the newly inserted derivative factors.

---

## What is established

Put
\[
B=KD_{602}.
\]
The actual paired source argument gives, uniformly on \(Q\), for both lifts and the physical marked coordinate \(t_0=n\),
\[
\|Kr\|_{P_{5000}}
 \le
 \frac{\varepsilon^2}{\sqrt N}
 (2+x-12/N)(1-x)^{-5/2}
 +O(n^{-1/2})
 <0.000103,
\]
with
\[
\varepsilon=0.011,\qquad
N=4(2031/128-2^{-17}),\qquad
x=\frac{4.811^2}{N}<1.
\]

The subtraction \(12/N\) is a genuine positive first-moment saving from the common Jacobi seed in the two \(R\)-factors. It is not an unsigned cancellation and remains valid for the actual source before the outer inverse.

At height \(602\), the available frozen estimates are
\[
\|B\|_{P_{5000}}<1.066
\]
and
\[
\|B\|_{2\to2}<0.518.
\]
The latter is a normalized second-norm estimate on the closed square; the former is the translated physical price.

The entrywise column estimate
\[
\sum_{j\ge0}\|B^jK\|_{\max}=O(n^{-0.455})
\]
is also valid, but it is a **column estimate**. It cannot be applied directly to a bulk vector \(r\) without a source representation in the corresponding column/translated class.

---

## Failure of the first transport route

For the first requested inverse,
\[
(I-B)^{-1}Kr=\sum_{j\ge0}B^jKr,
\]
the only directly available translated estimate gives
\[
\|B^jKr\|_{P_{5000}}
\lesssim
0.000103\,(1.066)^j.
\]
The absolute translated series therefore diverges. This is not a numerical issue: the available multiplier price is strictly larger than one.

One can combine this with the counting estimate, but the resulting hybrid estimate does not close. The source bound is a bulk vector bound, not a single physical column bound. Its normalized second norm is at best \(O(\varepsilon^2)\), so conversion to a coordinate bound costs \(\sqrt n\):
\[
\|B^jKr\|_\infty
\lesssim
\sqrt n\,O(\varepsilon^2)(0.518)^j.
\]
Combining
\[
0.000103(1.066)^j
\quad\text{and}\quad
\sqrt n\,O(\varepsilon^2)(0.518)^j
\]
only gives a cutoff estimate with a possible polynomial loss in \(n\); it does not produce a uniformly bounded or summable translated orbit. In particular, the cutoff occurs at logarithmic depth, while the translated prefix grows like \(1.066^j\).

The missing statement would have to be a **source-local transported packet estimate**, not merely another application of the preinverse source estimate.

---

## Failure of the \(K^2r\) route

The second requested inverse is
\[
(I-B)^{-1}K^2r
=\sum_{j\ge0}B^jK^2r.
\]
Since
\[
K^2=I-P_1,
\]
this source contains the direct bulk term
\[
r-\Bigl(\frac1n\sum_i r_i\Bigr)\mathbf 1.
\]
Thus the \(K^2\)-route is not equivalent to the \(Kr\)-route. In particular, the direct coordinate/constant component cannot be priced using only the already established \(\|Kr\|_{P_{5000}}\) estimate.

The cited marked-atom estimate controls the difference between the actual and bulk source after transport at scale
\[
O(\varepsilon^2n^{-0.455})
\]
for the smoothed part, but it explicitly leaves a direct atom in the \(K^2\)-transport. No bound has been proved that absorbs this atom into a summable translated bulk orbit.

Therefore the \(K^2r\) expression has an additional, explicit obstruction: the direct \(I-P_1\) component must be priced separately before any outer Neumann summation.

---

## Consequence for a moving starter

The nonlinear propagation theorem requires, in addition to signed shifted-resolvent control, a residual condition of the form
\[
n^{1-\beta}\rho^2=o(1),
\qquad \beta\le \tfrac12,
\]
where \(\rho\) is the normalized second-norm size of the actual residual.

The bound
\[
\|Kr\|_{P_{5000}}<0.000103
\]
is a translated source price and supplies no \(n\)-decay for \(\rho\). Hence it does not verify
\[
n^{1-\beta}\rho^2=o(1).
\]
Nor does the frozen entrywise estimate supply a compatible growing-height residual estimate. Consequently, no moving-starter proof follows from the present data.

This is a failure of the proposed sufficient mechanism, not a refutation of the asymptotic target. The actual source may still have additional cancellation after outer transport, but that cancellation has not been identified or bounded.

---

## Ranked next tests

### 1. Highest priority: source-local signed outer packet

Construct the joint packet for
\[
B^jKr=(KD_{602})^jKr
\]
before taking absolute prices. Retain the two actual \(R\)-factors as a designated Jacobi pair and expand the \(j\) derivative factors jointly with them. The required output is a quantitative recurrence for the translated source price, for example
\[
S_{j+1}\le \rho S_j+\eta_j,
\qquad \rho<1,
\]
or a directly summable estimate
\[
\sum_{j\ge0}\|B^jKr\|_{P_{5000}}<\infty
\]
with a numerical margin sufficient for nonlinear confinement.

This test must preserve the actual mark \(t_0=n\), both lifts, and the physical source. Reapplying the \(0.000103\) preinverse estimate independently at each \(j\) will not suffice.

### 2. Second priority: quantitative moving-starter residual

Compute an actual normalized residual estimate after one outer step, separately for
\[
(I-B)^{-1}Kr
\quad\text{and}\quad
(I-B)^{-1}K^2r.
\]
The target is an \(n\)-dependent bound
\[
\rho_n=O(n^{-\gamma})
\]
with
\[
\gamma>\frac{1-\beta}{2},
\]
or an equivalent direct confinement estimate that bypasses the general \(n^{1-\beta}\rho_n^2\) criterion.

For the \(K^2\) case, the direct \(I-P_1\) atom must be isolated and priced explicitly; the existing marked-column \(O(n^{-0.455})\) estimate cannot be substituted for it.
