## Result: a noncircular restricted response bound

I obtain a valid bound for the **pure common-seed \(R^{2}\) packet**, but not for the full mixed-word channel.

Fix the actual prime family
\[
p\equiv1\pmod 4,\qquad n=(p-1)/2,
\]
along sufficiently large eligible primes for which the cited interval estimates hold. Keep the original closed square \(Q\), both physical lifts, the physical mark \(t_{0}=n\), and the bulk value \(t_i=t\) off the marked coordinate. Put
\[
T:=KD_{602},\qquad D_{602}=\operatorname{diag}\Phi_t'(V_{602}).
\]

Let \(q_{\mathrm{seed}}\) denote the actual post-\(K\) source obtained by retaining only the pure common Jacobi-seed contribution to the Taylor term
\[
r=\Phi_t(V_{601})-\Phi_t(V_{600})-D_{600}R.
\]
The source includes both actual \(R\)-factors, the physical marking, both lifts, and the surviving Laurent seed exponents. It does **not** include mixed words.

### 1. Source price

The all-order seed calculation gives, uniformly on \(Q\) and for both lifts,
\[
\|q_{\mathrm{seed}}\|_{P_{5000}}
\le
\frac{\varepsilon^2}{\sqrt N}
\left[
 (2+x-12/N)(1-x)^{-5/2}-2+2U
 -\sum_{l=1}^{4}b_ld_l(8/N)^l
\right]
+O_{Q,\mathrm{phys}}(n^{-1/2}\varepsilon^2),
\]
where
\[
\varepsilon=0.011,\quad
N=4(2031/128-2^{-17}),\quad
x=\frac{4.811^2}{N},
\]
\[
b_l=(2l+1)4^{-l}\binom{2l}{l},\qquad
d_l=l+2(1-l)4^{-l}\binom{2l}{l},
\]
and \(U\) is the rational majorant for the surviving seed series \(\Xi\).

The checked rational comparison from the seed transport calculation yields
\[
\boxed{\ \|q_{\mathrm{seed}}\|_{P_{5000}}<0.00009657\ }
\]
eventually, with the actual marked and bad-coordinate repairs still absorbed in the stated \(O(n^{-1/2}\varepsilon^2)\) term. This is a signed estimate: the \(1,-3/4,45/64,-175/256,\ldots\) seed coefficients are retained before taking the final majorant, and the pure nonunit debits \(d_l\) are charged against the same actual \(R^2\) packet.

The estimate is noncircular: it uses only the explicit seed expansion and the finite rational majorants, not an inverse-defined telescope.

### 2. Growing-depth transported response

The genuine height-\(602\) translated estimate gives
\[
\|T\|_{P_{5000}}\le 1.066
\]
uniformly on \(Q\), both lifts, and all sufficiently large eligible primes. Therefore, for every integer \(J\ge0\),
\[
\left\|\sum_{j=0}^{J}T^j q_{\mathrm{seed}}\right\|_{P_{5000}}
\le
0.00009657\sum_{j=0}^{J}1.066^j
+O_{Q,\mathrm{phys}}(n^{-1/2}\varepsilon^2)
\]
and hence
\[
\boxed{
\left\|\sum_{j=0}^{J}T^j q_{\mathrm{seed}}\right\|_{P_{5000}}
\le
0.00009657\,
\frac{1.066^{J+1}-1}{0.066}
+O_{Q,\mathrm{phys}}(n^{-1/2}\varepsilon^2).
}
\]

This remains a genuine growing-depth bound. For example, if
\[
J(n)=\left\lfloor c\log n\right\rfloor ,
\]
then
\[
\left\|\sum_{j=0}^{J(n)}T^j q_{\mathrm{seed}}\right\|_{P_{5000}}
=
O\!\left(n^{c\log 1.066}\right)
+O(n^{-1/2}\varepsilon^2).
\]
Thus any fixed \(c>0\) gives a controlled polynomially growing restricted response, and the exponent can be made arbitrarily small by taking \(c\) small.

The counting estimate
\[
\|T\|_{2\to2}\le0.518
\]
does not currently improve this to a uniform \(J\to\infty\) translated bound. Combining the translated and counting estimates gives only an interpolation control with a residual positive power of \(n\), because the available counting-to-\(P_{5000}\) conversion costs at least the normalized \(\sqrt n\) factor. Consequently, this result does **not** prove a bounded infinite-depth response.

## What has been proved

The following restricted statement is established:

> For the actual \(R^{2}\) Taylor source on the original square, retaining the pure common Jacobi-seed branch and its physical marking repairs, the signed post-\(K\) source has \(P_{5000}\)-price \(<9.657\times10^{-5}\). Its transported response through \(J(n)=\lfloor c\log n\rfloor\) applications of the actual height-\(602\) operator \(KD_{602}\) obeys the explicit geometric bound above, uniformly over both lifts and all sufficiently large primes \(p\equiv1\pmod4\).

This is a noncircular restricted translated transport bound. It is not an estimate for the full distributed \(R^{2}\) channel.

## Remaining gap

Three issues remain.

1. **Mixed words.**  
   The pure seed debit uses the exact common-seed Laurent structure and the surviving \(d_l\) charges. Mixed seed/nonseed words currently retain only the older paired estimate
   \[
   2d_1d_2-s_n^2.
   \]
   No signed analogue of the \(\Xi\)-cancellation has been proved for those words. Their contribution could therefore be as large as the existing absolute paired majorant.

2. **Uniform infinite-depth translated response.**  
   The available translated rate is \(1.066>1\). The counting rate \(0.518<1\) controls the operator in the unweighted second norm, but converting counting decay to \(P_{5000}\) presently loses a factor of order \(\sqrt n\). Hence the interpolation argument does not yield a prime-uniform \(P_{5000}\) resolvent or a bounded response as \(J\to\infty\).

3. **Nonlinear bridge and moving starter.**  
   The frozen height-\(602\) estimate does not provide the growing-height residual rate required by the orbit-transfer lemma. In particular, it does not yet establish
   \[
   n^{1-\beta}r_n^2=o(1)
   \]
   together with the required signed partial-sum confinement uniformly in growing depth. Thus it cannot by itself produce a genuine moving starter on the original square.

## Ranked next tests

### 1. Highest priority: mixed-word signed transport

Construct the mixed-word analogue of the seed Laurent pairing before taking absolute values. The target is a prime-uniform estimate of the form
\[
\|q_{\mathrm{mixed}}\|_{P_{5000}}
\le C_{\mathrm{mix}}\varepsilon^2
\]
with \(C_{\mathrm{mix}}\) strictly below the slack left after the pure debit, and preferably with an additional translated contraction factor. This is the most direct route to a full \(R^{2}\) response estimate.

The test must retain:

- the actual \(R=V_{601}-V_{600}\),
- both lifts,
- the physical mark \(t_0=n\),
- the original square \(Q\),
- mixed seed/nonseed words before absolute summation,
- and the \(O(n^{-1/2}\varepsilon^2)\) marked repairs separately.

### 2. Second priority: improve the translated rate or the source norm conversion

Prove either

\[
\|KD_{602}\|_{P_{5000}}\le 1-\delta
\]
for the signed restricted packet, or an \(n\)-uniform conversion from the relevant counting norm to \(P_{5000}\) for the transported pure-seed source. A sufficient estimate would be a bound of the form
\[
\|T^j q_{\mathrm{seed}}\|_{P_{5000}}
\le C\theta^j\|q_{\mathrm{seed}}\|_{P_{5000}},
\qquad \theta<1,
\]
or a source-specific bound avoiding the generic \(\sqrt n\) loss. Either result would turn the finite growing-depth estimate into a uniform translated resolvent bound.

The present result therefore supports a restricted response mechanism, but it does not yet establish the full nonlinear bridge or a genuine moving starter.
