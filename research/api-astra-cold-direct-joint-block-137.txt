## Result

**Inconclusive; the requested bound \(<0.721\) is not established.** The available certified estimates miss the target by a small but genuine amount, and the positive comparison obstruction does not refute the actual signed block.

Let
\[
A_h=K D_h,\qquad J=K D^{\mathrm J},\qquad R_h=K E_h=A_h-J,
\]
with \(h=601,602\). On the original closed square \(Q\), with the actual marked coordinate \(t_0=n\), both boundary pairs retained, and the physical translated norm \(P_{5000}\), the accepted resummation gives, for sufficiently large eligible primes,
\[
\|A_h\|_{P_{5000}}<0.666,\qquad
\|J\|_{P_{5000}}<0.528.
\]

The desired block is
\[
A_{602}A_{601}-J^2.
\]
Without a new joint signed identity, the available submultiplicative estimate is only
\[
\|A_{602}A_{601}-J^2\|_{P_{5000}}
\le
\|A_{602}A_{601}\|_{P_{5000}}+\|J^2\|_{P_{5000}}
<
0.666^2+0.528^2.
\]
Numerically,
\[
0.666^2+0.528^2
=0.443556+0.278784
=0.722340,
\]
which is above \(0.721\).

Equivalently, using
\[
A_{602}A_{601}-J^2
=R_{602}A_{601}+J R_{601},
\]
would require correlated control of the two signed remainders. The current estimate \(\|R_h\|_{P_{5000}}<0.666\) is an upper bound for each actual step, not the required joint signed transport estimate.

## What is genuinely supported

The resummed calculation does establish the following useful facts.

1. **Actual one-step signed collection.**  
   Pure seed powers are collected exactly before modulus. For the nonseed factors, the calculation uses the positive envelope \(\beta\), including the excess-zero charge and residual envelope. The resulting eventual actual prices are
   \[
   \|K E_h^{(1)}\|_{P_{5000}}<0.527,\qquad
   \|K(E_h-E_h^{(1)})\|_{P_{5000}}<0.142,
   \]
   and hence
   \[
   \|K E_h\|_{P_{5000}}<0.666.
   \]
   These include the analytic tail, exceptional-lift repair, actual marked corrections, and both boundary pairs.

2. **Pure Jacobi block.**  
   The all-order pure-seed estimate gives
   \[
   \|J\|_{P_{5000}}<0.528,
   \qquad
   \|J^2\|_{P_{5000}}<0.278784.
   \]

3. **The positive-comparison obstruction is not a counterexample.**  
   The positive transport witness with rate \(>0.570\) only applies after cancellation between distinct nonseed words has been discarded. It therefore does not lower-bound the actual signed operator
   \[
   A_{602}A_{601}-J^2.
   \]
   It shows only that repricing that particular comparison cannot reach the one-step target \(0.472\).

4. **Counting and fixed-height confinement are not the missing block estimate.**  
   The prior counting estimates give useful fixed-height contraction and outgoing control, but they do not provide a \(P_{5000}\)-priced joint block bound or a growing-depth invariant-ball argument.

## Remaining hypotheses and order of limits

The calculation is on the **original closed square**
\[
Q=\left\{a:\left|a-\frac{2031}{128}\right|\le 2^{-17}\right\},
\]
with the physical marking at \(t_0=n\), bulk coordinates using the actual \(t_i\), and the translated physical norm \(P_{5000}\). The prime family is the sufficiently large **eligible/good prime family** for which the Mellin lifts, exceptional repairs, and analytic-tail estimates hold.

The established estimates are eventual in that family: for every fixed frozen block \(h=601,602\), the finite retained computation is followed by the limit \(n\to\infty\), with the \(O_{5000}(n^{-1/2})\) marked and exceptional corrections then absorbed. This does **not** yet justify the second required limit in which Picard depth grows. A complete result would need uniform sheet confinement and invariant-ball control for all depths \(k\), after the large-\(n\) estimates have been made uniform.

## Quantitative size of the missing improvement

Using the current pure-seed bound \(0.528\), the actual factor would need to satisfy
\[
\|A_h\|_{P_{5000}}
<
\sqrt{0.721-0.528^2}
=
\sqrt{0.442216}
\approx 0.664993.
\]
Thus the displayed \(0.666\) bound is only about \(0.00101\) too weak at the factor level.

Alternatively, retaining \(0.666\) for the actual factor would require improving the pure-seed bound to
\[
\|J\|_{P_{5000}}
<
\sqrt{0.721-0.666^2}
=
\sqrt{0.277444}
\approx 0.526729.
\]

These are fallback numerical targets, not established estimates.

## Ranked next tests

1. **Joint signed two-step Laurent/word aggregation — highest priority.**  
   Expand
   \[
   A_{602}A_{601}-J^2
   \]
   before applying the positive envelope, retaining the common pure-seed powers and the translated pair through both steps. In particular, identify repeated actual nonseed words across the two heights and preserve their signs through the intermediate \(K\). A certified \(P_{5000}\) outward computation below \(0.721\), including the infinite tail and \(O_{5000}(n^{-1/2})\) marked repairs, would settle the frozen block.

2. **Sharpen one factor or isolate a signed cross-term — second priority.**  
   Either:
   - improve the actual translated factor bound from \(0.666\) to \(<0.664993\), or
   - improve the pure-seed factor from \(0.528\) to \(<0.526729\), or
   - directly bound the cross expression
     \[
     R_{602}A_{601}+J R_{601}
     \]
     below \(0.721\) while retaining cancellation between the two terms.  
   After such a bound, separately verify the half-step margin, the invariant-ball radius, and uniform growing-depth confinement in the order \(n\to\infty\) first and Picard depth \(k\to\infty\) second.

Thus the present record supports a **near miss for the frozen physical block**, not a proof or refutation of the \(0.721\) target.
