## Report: joint cold remainder remains unpriced

### New checked statement

Let \(D_s\) denote the marked seed multiplier and write, at the two actual predictor rows,
\[
D_h=D_s+E_h,\qquad h\in\{601,602\}.
\]
Then the genuine two-step derivative decomposes exactly as
\[
KD_{602}KD_{601}
=
KD_sKD_s
+\underbrace{KE_{602}KD_s+KD_sKE_{601}}_{\text{mixed}}
+\underbrace{KE_{602}KE_{601}}_{\text{nonlinear}}.
\tag{A}
\]
The accepted packet estimate gives
\[
\operatorname{rate}_\Lambda(KD_sKD_s)<0.287.
\]
Therefore a frozen packet contraction would follow from the joint, signed estimate
\[
\operatorname{rate}_\Lambda\!\left(
KE_{602}KD_s+KD_sKE_{601}+KE_{602}KE_{601}
\right)<0.713-\delta
\tag{B}
\]
for some \(\delta>0\), uniformly on the original closed square, with both marked conventions and both physical sheets included.

The present records do **not** establish (B).

### What is available

1. **Counting norm.** On the genuine seed-centered disks,
\[
\|KD_{602}KD_{601}\|_{2\to2}<0.2658,
\]
and the first and second secants have counting rates \(<0.515\) and \(<0.518\), respectively. This is stronger than needed in the normalized second norm, but it is not a translated packet estimate.

2. **Single-step translated pricing.** The frozen full derivatives have translated rates
\[
\operatorname{rate}_\Lambda(KD_h)<1.048
\]
at the verified fixed heights. The actual seed step has rate \(<0.535\), while the seed–seed two-step contribution has rate \(<0.287\).

3. **Signed position cancellation.** For the full actual two-step derivative on the physical mean-zero space, every position entry is \(O(n^{-1/2})\). The same signed local-system argument applies to the mixed degree-\((1,1)\) words and to higher odd words, after collecting the middle variable before taking absolute values. The marked correction is supported on the base averaging column and is \(O(n^{-1/2})\) after the outer transport.

4. **Analytic tails.** The dimension envelope \(M<4.811\), with \(M^2<N\), controls the odd expansions and their absolute tails. The outgoing secant calculation gives a genuine second-step coordinate bound, and the curvature estimates keep both transports on the common physical sheets.

These facts verify analyticity, sheet confinement through the recorded depth, and fixed-depth signed entry cancellation. They do not price the coefficient projection of (A) in \(P_\Lambda\).

### Precise failure of the current sufficient bound

An \(O(n^{-1/2})\) position-entry estimate cannot be converted into a bounded translated packet rate. For example, on \(n-1\) nonmarked coordinates, the matrix
\[
A_n(x,z)=n^{-1/2}\sigma_x\sigma_z
\]
with \(\sigma_x\in\{\pm1\}\) and \(\sum_x\sigma_x=0\) has every entry of size \(n^{-1/2}\), preserves the mean-zero subspace, and has counting operator norm \(O(1)\). Nevertheless, applying it to a one-column positive packet produces total absolute mass of order \(n^{1/2}\). Thus the already-proved entry bound, even together with the counting contraction, gives no uniform \(P_\Lambda\)-rate.

The same issue occurs in the nonlinear words. The local-system argument bounds a **signed sum over the intermediate coordinate** by \(O(kl\,p^{-1/2})\) times the product of dimensions. A packet estimate requires first projecting that signed sum onto the actual translated boundary fibers and then summing the resulting dimensions with the \(\pi\)- and \(5000\)-moment charges. No such projected coefficient bound is present in the records.

A separate failure is visible from the known positive rates. If one ignores signs and uses only
\[
\operatorname{rate}_\Lambda(KD_h)<1.048,\qquad
\operatorname{rate}_\Lambda(KD_s)<0.535,
\]
then the triangle estimate for the remainder in (A) is already larger than one. Even the formal bound
\[
(1.048)^2+(0.535)^2
\]
does not approach \(0.713\), and replacing the first term by the known seed–seed rate does not control the mixed terms. Hence “repricing” the existing positive majorant cannot close the target, consistently with the positive witness obstruction.

### Remaining hypotheses needed for closure

A sufficient new input would be a coherent signed packet representation for \(E_h\), with the two transports retained jointly, satisfying:

- for every input translated packet \(P\), the mixed output
  \[
  KE_{602}KD_sP+KD_sKE_{601}P
  \]
  has projected base price plus \(5000\)-weighted off-base first moment at most \(c_{\rm mix}P_\Lambda(P)\);

- the jointly collected nonlinear output \(KE_{602}KE_{601}P\) has rate \(c_{\rm nl}\);

- the estimates hold on both half-sheets and include the two marked/base boundary corrections;

- the explicit tail satisfies
  \[
  c_{\rm mix}+c_{\rm nl}\le 0.713-\delta.
  \]

The natural low-arity target is to compute the arities \((1,3)\), \((3,1)\), and \((3,3)\) after the intermediate signed convolution, rather than bounding the two factors separately. The existing odd-parity cancellation at \(y=1\) should be used before taking absolute values. Higher arities can then be charged by the geometric dimension ratio \(M^2/N<1\), but the tail constant must be recorded explicitly; the current \(O(\cdot)\) statements are insufficient for the \(0.713\) threshold.

### Conclusion

The mission is **inconclusive, with a precise obstruction to the presently available sufficient bounds**. The actual full block is strongly contractive in counting norm, and its position entries exhibit genuine signed cancellation. However, neither fact supplies the required translated packet projection. The positive majorant cannot be repriced, and the separate single-step rates cannot bound the mixed/nonlinear remainder below \(0.713\).

### Ranked decisive next tests

1. **Joint low-arity coefficient projection.** Compute, with exact rational interval arithmetic, the signed translated packet coefficients for \((1,3)\), \((3,1)\), and \((3,3)\), including both transports and both marked corrections. Record the resulting \(P_\Lambda\)-rate before absolute value and compare directly with \(0.713\).

2. **Rank–tail certificate.** If the low-arity projection is favorable, prove a uniform rank-and-tail inequality for all \((2r+1,2s+1)\) beyond that cutoff, with an explicit numerical tail and separate base/off-base charges. A failure of this certificate for the actual coherent residual would be a rigorous obstruction; failure of an absolute majorant alone would not be.
