## New quantitative bridge: the frozen marked row needs a stronger residual than Newton existence

Let \(V=V_a\) be a physical Picard predictor on the square \(Q\), with
\[
R=K\Phi_t(V)-V,\qquad r=\|R\|_{2,n},
\]
and let \(A=I-KD\), \(Z=A^{-1}K\). Assume
\[
\|A^{-1}\|_{2\to2}\le a,\qquad \max_{i,j}|Z_{ij}|\le b n^{-\beta},
\quad 0<\beta\le \tfrac12.
\]

The translated-Newton argument requires only
\[
n^{(1-\beta)/2}r\longrightarrow0. \tag{N}
\]
This makes the nonlinear stationary correction small because its coordinate bound is quadratic:
\[
\|Y-V-H\|_\infty
\le 2bCa^2 n^{1-\beta}r^2=o(1).
\]

However, the exact frozen-mark identity contains a *linear* transported residual:
\[
(1-\eta)P
 = Zb-\frac{2V-S}{2t\sqrt t}
 -\frac{A^{-1}R}{t\sqrt t}, \tag{1}
\]
where \(P=fZe_0\), \(S\) is the Jacobi seed, \(t\) is the fixed physical coordinate near \(2031/128\), and \(|\eta|=O(n^{-1/2})\) on the stated seed disks.

Thus a prime-uniform bound on the marked susceptibility requires, in addition to a bound on \(Zb\),
\[
\|A^{-1}R\|_\infty=o(1). \tag{M}
\]
The available operator estimate gives only
\[
\|A^{-1}R\|_\infty
\le \|A^{-1}R\|_2
\le a\sqrt n\,r. \tag{2}
\]
Using the translated entry estimate gives
\[
\|A^{-1}R\|_\infty
\le b n^{-\beta}\|R\|_1
\le b n^{1-\beta}r. \tag{3}
\]
Consequently, a sufficient residual condition for the frozen marked gap is
\[
r=o(n^{-(1-\beta)}), \tag{M'}
\]
which is strictly stronger than (N). At the best translated exponent \(\beta=\tfrac12\),
\[
\text{Newton existence: }r=o(n^{-1/4}),\qquad
\text{frozen-mark control via (3): }r=o(n^{-1/2}).
\]

This is a precise failure of the proposed residual bridge: the normalized quadratic residual scale does **not** by itself control the marked scalar continuation at the same predictor. It does not imply a branch singularity; it only shows that the existing certificate has an unclosed linear term.

### Conditional marked-gap statement

Suppose, uniformly on \(Q\),

1. \(\|Zb\|_\infty\le B\);
2. \(\|A^{-1}R\|_\infty\le \varepsilon_n\to0\);
3. \(\|V\|_\infty,\|S\|_\infty=O(1)\);
4. the Newton hypotheses hold, including \(n^{(1-\beta)/2}r\to0\);
5. the nominal corrected marked derivative has margin
   \[
   h\left\|\frac{P}{\sqrt t}\right\|_\infty
   \le \frac{2\cdot10^{-4}}{\sqrt3}-\delta
   \]
   for some fixed \(\delta>0\).

Then (1) yields
\[
\|P\|_\infty
\le \frac{B+O(n^{-3/2})+O(\varepsilon_n)}{1-|\eta|}
= B+o(1),
\]
and the Newton stationary correction is \(o(1)\) in coordinate norm. Therefore the frozen marked gap transfers to the stationary branch, provided the usual physical midpoint-secant uniqueness assumptions hold. The prime arithmetic task is consequently not merely to prove (N), but to prove either (M) directly or a cancellation improving (2)–(3).

## Finite-to-asymptotic bookkeeping

Let \(a_0\) be a fixed physical depth and suppose the genuine Picard increments satisfy
\[
\|V_{j+1}-V_j\|_{2,n}\le C_R q_R^{\,j-a_0},\qquad q_R<1,
\]
for all \(j\ge a_0\). Then at
\[
a(n)=a_0+
\left(\frac{1-\beta}{2|\log q_R|}+\epsilon\right)\log n
\]
one obtains (N). But this says nothing about (M) unless one also proves a transported-residual estimate. If the only available estimate is (3), the required depth is instead
\[
a(n)\ge a_0+
\left(\frac{1-\beta}{|\log q_R|}+\epsilon\right)\log n.
\]
Hence the marked continuation may require approximately twice the physical Picard depth demanded by stationary existence.

This depth \(a(n)\) is independent of the inverse-column truncation length. A column representation with
\[
h(n)=\frac{\omega}{2\log(\rho/q)}\log n+O(1)
\]
controls the translated inverse only if its packet, boundary-fiber, and tail hypotheses hold through \(h(n)\). There is no valid identification \(h=a\).

## First open hypothesis

The first genuinely missing hypothesis is therefore:

\[
\boxed{\quad
\sup_Q\|A_a^{-1}R_a\|_\infty=o(1)
\quad\text{at a moving physical depth }a=a(n),
\quad} \tag{H\(_{\rm mark}\)}
\]

simultaneously with the translated-column resources and the residual condition (N). A signed estimate for \(A_a^{-1}R_a\), or cancellation between this term and \(Zb\), would remove the apparent extra \(n^{(1-\beta)/2}\) loss.

This is prime-specific: general contraction supplies \(r_a\), but not the coordinate cancellation or negative-Mellin structure needed for \(A_a^{-1}R_a\).

## Ranked next tests

1. **Direct transported-residual test.** At growing physical depths compute or bound
   \[
   G_a=A_a^{-1}R_a
   \]
   in coordinate norm, not only \(\|R_a\|_{2,n}\). Test whether \(\|G_a\|_\infty\) decays at least \(o(1)\), and whether its marked coordinate cancels against \(Zb\).

2. **Combined signed-source test.** Apply the seed-subtracted identity to the full source
   \[
   Zb-\frac{A^{-1}R}{t\sqrt t}
   \]
   and seek a three-field/parity estimate at the same column cutoff used for \(Zb\). A successful \(O(1)\) bound with \(o(1)\) residual would establish the frozen mark gap without demanding \(r=o(n^{-(1-\beta)})\).
