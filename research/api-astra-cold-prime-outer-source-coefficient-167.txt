## Checked result: one genuine outer transport is available

Let
\[
n=t_0=\frac{p-1}{2},\qquad
R=V_{601}-V_{600},\qquad
V_s=V_{600}+sR,
\]
with both lifts retained, the original complex square, and the physical marked radicand \(4n+W_0^2\) at the marked coordinate. At all other coordinates the radicand is \(4t+W_i^2\).

Take the actual \(l=1\) Taylor coefficient in
\[
r^{\mathrm b}
 =\sum_{l\ge0}(-1)^l b_l(4t)^{-l-1/2}
 R^2\int_0^1(1-s)V_s^{2l}\,ds,
\qquad b_l=(2l+1)\binom{2l}{l}4^{-l}.
\]
Since \(b_1=3/2\), this coefficient is
\[
r_1^{\mathrm b}
=
-\frac32(4t)^{-3/2}
R^2\int_0^1(1-s)V_s^2\,ds.
\tag{1}
\]

The two \(R\)-factors are retained as actual increment factors. The two \(V_s\)-factors are the paired non-increment factors, with the common Jacobi seed retained independently on both lifts. The seed-pair first-moment saving is \(s_n^2\ge 8\). Hence the Round-165 estimate applies to the actual \(l=1\) coefficient, including the physical marked correction and bad-character repairs:
\[
\|K r_1\|_{P_{5000}}<0.000031
\tag{2}
\]
for all sufficiently large eligible primes. Here \(r_1\) denotes the actual coefficient, not merely the bulk formal coefficient; the difference at the marked coordinate is included in the \(O(n^{-1/2})\) repair.

### Correct first outer step

The physical outer transport is
\[
B_{602}K r_1,
\qquad
B_{602}=KD_{602},
\]
not \(K^2r_1\) and not \(ZK r_1\). The order is therefore
\[
(KD_{602})K r_1.
\tag{3}
\]

The actual translated multiplier estimate at height \(602\) gives
\[
\|KD_{602}\|_{P_{5000}}<1.066
\tag{4}
\]
on the same square, with the same physical marking and both lifts. Using submultiplicativity of the translated packet price,
\[
\|(KD_{602})K r_1\|_{P_{5000}}
\le
\|KD_{602}\|_{P_{5000}}\,
\|K r_1\|_{P_{5000}},
\]
and therefore
\[
\boxed{
\|(KD_{602})K r_1\|_{P_{5000}}
<1.066(0.000031)
<0.000033046.
}
\tag{5}
\]

Thus the actual \(l=1\) packet survives one genuine outer multiplication with a checked bound below \(3.31\cdot10^{-5}\). The coefficient sign in (1) is retained, and the paired Jacobi aggregation is performed before the outer multiplier is applied. This is a genuine transported actual coefficient, rather than a bound on an unrelated formal source.

The corresponding exact transported residue to be priced next is
\[
\boxed{
(KD_{602})^2K r_1
}
\tag{6}
\]
with \(r_1\) given by (1), retaining the two actual \(R\)-labels and the paired \(V_s,V_s\) labels.

## What this does not prove

It does **not** yet give a signed summable tail. The available translated estimate only gives
\[
\|(KD_{602})^jK r_1\|_{P_{5000}}
\lesssim
(1.066)^j\,0.000031,
\]
which is not summable in \(j\). The counting estimate
\[
\|KD_{602}\|_{2\to2}<0.518
\]
cannot presently be combined with (2) to obtain a uniform translated tail: converting a normalized counting estimate to a physical maximum estimate introduces the ambient \(\sqrt n\) factor, and no \(n^{-1/2}\) source price for the full transported \(l=1\) packet has been established.

There is also no new signed cancellation beyond the first paired source aggregation. Estimate (5) uses the positive induced translated price of \(KD_{602}\); it does not show that the successive packets \((KD_{602})^jK r_1\) cancel across \(j\).

## Hypotheses used

1. The genuine physical predictors \(V_{600},V_{601}\) and increment \(R=V_{601}-V_{600}\) exist on the original square.
2. Both lift choices are expanded independently.
3. The actual mark is \(t_0=n=(p-1)/2\); all other sites use \(t\).
4. The two \(R\)-factors in (1) retain their actual increment price \(\varepsilon=0.011\).
5. The two non-increment factors are paired through the common Jacobi seed, giving the squared seed mass \(s_n^2\ge8\).
6. The translated packet price is submultiplicative for the physical operator \(KD_{602}\).

## Ranked next tests

   Expand

Track whether the first outer \(D_{602}\) preserves the paired seed labels and whether a second pairing produces a genuine reduction below the naive factor \(1.066^2\). A bound with an effective rate \(<1\), or an \(n^{-1/2}\) gain after the first transport, would open the summable-tail route.

2. **Establish a counting-to-translated bridge for the transported \(l=1\) packet.**  
   Prove a bound of the form
   \[
   \|(KD_{602})^jK r_1\|_{\max}
   \le C\,n^{-\alpha}\rho^j,
   \qquad \rho<1,
   \]

The missing ingredient is an \(n^{-1/2}\)-scale physical source normalization after the first outer step; without it, the \(0.518\) counting contraction does not yield a translated summable tail.
