## Status: inconclusive, with an exact reduction to two genuinely arithmetic estimates

Fix the actual ordinary-prime family from Rounds 164–165, a sign \(\epsilon\), and a hypothetical fixed-gap sequence \(p\to\infty\) with the associated fixed floor. Write \(n=n(p)\), retain the actual adaptive independent zero-column set \(U'(p)\), the actual complementary coefficients \(w=w(p)\ge 0\), and choose the admissible marked time \(T=T(p)\to\infty\) in the prescribed order: first fix the gap/sign/floor, then let \(p\to\infty\) and select \(T(p)\) inside the marked window. All norms below are counting norms.

The physical coordinate is the actual marked coordinate \(0\). With
\[
P_\epsilon=\frac{I+\epsilon K-\Pi}{2},\qquad
d=(P_\epsilon)_{00}=\frac{|\Gamma|}{n},\qquad
b_0=E_{U'}^*P_\epsilon e_0,
\]
the centered operator and source are exactly
\[
G_0
 =E_{U'}^*\left(P_\epsilon-
     \frac{P_\epsilon e_0e_0^*P_\epsilon}{d}\right)E_{U'},
\]
and
\[
c_0=\frac1{\sqrt n}
 \left(\mathbf 1_{U'}+\frac{q-1}{d}b_0\right).
\]
The actual complementary row satisfies
\[
G_0w=0,\qquad
\mathbf 1_{U'}^*w=n-\frac nq,
\qquad
Lw=-\frac{n/q}{\sqrt n}\mathbf 1_\Gamma .
\]
Moreover \(\ker G_0=\mathbb Rw\) and \(c_0\perp w\).

### 1. Exact separation of the zero-coefficient and positive-kernel costs

Let
\[
A=\{i\in U':w_i>0\},\qquad B=\{i\in U':w_i=0\}.
\]
The \(B\)-block \(G_{0,BB}\) is positive definite whenever \(B\neq\varnothing\). Define
\[
E_B:=\langle c_{0,B},G_{0,BB}^{-1}c_{0,B}\rangle ,
\]
and
\[
Q_A=G_{0,AA}-G_{0,AB}G_{0,BB}^{-1}G_{0,BA},
\]
\[
d_A=c_{0,A}-G_{0,AB}G_{0,BB}^{-1}c_{0,B}.
\]
Then the centered inverse cost is exactly
\[
\boxed{
\langle c_0,G_0^\dagger c_0\rangle
   =E_B+\langle d_A,Q_A^\dagger d_A\rangle .
}
\]
Thus the two requested debits are genuinely distinct.

Because \(0\le G_0\le I\), the block-Schur inequality gives
\[
\bigl\|G_{0,AB}G_{0,BB}^{-1}c_{0,B}\bigr\|_2
 \le E_B^{1/2},
\]
hence
\[
\|d_A\|_2\le \|c_{0,A}\|_2+E_B^{1/2}.
\]
The entry cap from Round 165 gives \(c_{0,i}=O(n^{-1/2})\), so \(\|c_0\|_2=O(1)\) when \(|U'|\le n\). This is useful, but it does not control \(E_B\): a positive definite principal block can have arbitrarily small eigenvalues despite bounded diagonal entries.

### 2. Explicit low/high-weight and elimination split

Fix a cutoff \(H>0\) and write
\[
A_H=\{i\in A:w_i\ge H\},\qquad
A_L=\{i\in A:0<w_i<H\}.
\]
After the \(B\)-elimination, split \(Q_A\) according to \(A_H\cup A_L\). If \(A_H\neq\varnothing\), the \(A_L\)-block is positive definite. Put
\[
E_L:=\langle d_{A_L},Q_{A_LA_L}^{-1}d_{A_L}\rangle ,
\]
\[
e_H=d_{A_H}
 -Q_{A_HA_L}Q_{A_LA_L}^{-1}d_{A_L},
\]
and
\[
Q_H=
 Q_{A_HA_H}
 -Q_{A_HA_L}Q_{A_LA_L}^{-1}Q_{A_LA_H}.
\]
Then
\[
\boxed{
\langle d_A,Q_A^\dagger d_A\rangle
   =E_L+\langle e_H,Q_H^\dagger e_H\rangle .
}
\]
Again, this is exact. Also,
\[
\|e_H\|_2\le \|d_{A_H}\|_2+E_L^{1/2}.
\]

Consequently, a sufficient bound for the complete centered cost is
\[
E_B=o(T),\qquad
E_L=o(T),\qquad
\frac{(\|d_{A_H}\|_2+E_L^{1/2})^2}
     {\kappa_{p,H}}=o(T),
\]
where
\[
\kappa_{p,H}
=
\inf_{\substack{x\perp w_{A_H}\\x\ne0}}
 \frac{\langle x,Q_Hx\rangle}{\|x\|_2^2}.
\]
This is an actual-prime, source-compatible criterion. It is not presently proved.

### 3. What the signed Jacobi form does and does not give

For real \(z\), the exact kernel identity gives
\[
z^*D_wG_0D_wz
 =
-\sum_{i<j}(G_0)_{ij}w_iw_j(z_i-z_j)^2.
\]
On vectors supported on \(A_H\), the physical entries satisfy
\[
(G_0)_{ij}=\frac{\epsilon}{2}K_{ij}+O(n^{-1}),
\]
with \(K_{ij}\) the actual Jacobi-field entries for the given ordinary prime. Therefore
\[
z^*D_wG_0D_wz
 =
\mathcal J_{p,H}(z)
 +O\!\left(H^{-1}\|D_wz\|_2^2\right),
\]
where
\[
\mathcal J_{p,H}(z)
=
-\frac{\epsilon}{2}
 \sum_{i<j}K_{ij}w_iw_j(z_i-z_j)^2
\]
uses the actual adaptive support and the actual prime \(p\). The \(O(H^{-1})\) comparison follows from \(\sum_iw_i=n-n/q\) and the \(O(n^{-1})\) rank-correction entries.

However, this is not yet a lower bound for \(Q_H\). If \(C=B\cup A_L\), then
\[
Q_H=G_{0,A_HA_H}
   -G_{0,A_HC}G_{0,CC}^{-1}G_{0,CA_H}.
\]
Thus, for \(x_H=D_{w,A_H}z\),
\[
\langle x_H,Q_Hx_H\rangle
 =
\mathcal J_{p,H}(z)
 -\underbrace{
 \langle G_{0,CA_H}x_H,
 G_{0,CC}^{-1}G_{0,CA_H}x_H\rangle
 }_{\mathcal L_{p,H}(x_H)\ge0}
 +O\!\left(H^{-1}\|x_H\|_2^2\right).
\]
The term \(\mathcal L_{p,H}\) is the explicit transfer through the low-weight and zero-coefficient coordinates. It is precisely the missing elimination term. Positivity only says that the displayed combination is nonnegative; it gives no prime-uniform lower bound for it.

A sufficient actual arithmetic statement would therefore be a source-weighted coercivity estimate of the form
\[
\mathcal J_{p,H}(z)-\mathcal L_{p,H}(D_wz)
 \ge
 \kappa_{p,H}\,
 \operatorname{dist}(D_{w,A_H}z,\mathbb Rw_{A_H})^2
 -o(T)
\]
on the relevant source directions, with \(\kappa_{p,H}\) large enough to make the preceding quotient \(o(T)\). No such estimate follows from Jacobi orthogonality, the diagonal value, or the pointwise kernel bound alone.

## Precise failure of the present argument

The requested \(o(T)\) conclusion is not established because both required upper bounds remain open:

1. **Zero-coefficient debit**
   \[
   E_B=\langle c_{0,B},G_{0,BB}^{-1}c_{0,B}\rangle .
   \]
   The source entries are \(O(n^{-1/2})\), but there is no lower bound on the actual-prime \(B\)-block sufficient to control its inverse.

2. **Positive-kernel Schur debit**
   \[
   \langle d_A,Q_A^\dagger d_A\rangle .
   \]
   The signed Jacobi squared-difference expression controls the uneliminated high-weight block up to \(O(H^{-1})\), but the actual low-weight/zero-coordinate transfer \(\mathcal L_{p,H}\) can cancel the apparent high-weight coercivity. No source-weighted lower bound for the actual \(K_p\), actual \(U'(p)\), and actual \(w(p)\) is available.

The relaxed circulant construction only shows that positivity, diagonal information, and a positive interpolant do not imply the needed inverse bound. It is not a refutation for the actual ordinary-prime family. Conversely, the finite-prime identities and the forced low spectral window do not supply an asymptotic upper bound. Hence the correct status is **inconclusive**, not supported or refuted.

## Ranked next tests

### 1. Highest priority: actual signed-Jacobi transfer test

For the actual ordinary primes \(p\), compute or prove a source-specific bound for
\[
Q_H=
G_{0,A_HA_H}
-G_{0,A_HC}G_{0,CC}^{-1}G_{0,CA_H},
\qquad C=B\cup A_L,
\]
retaining the actual \(K_p\), \(U'(p)\), \(w(p)\), and physical mark \(0\). The decisive quantity is
\[
\langle e_H,Q_H^\dagger e_H\rangle ,
\]
not merely \(\lambda_{\min}(Q_H)\). A successful estimate
\[
\langle e_H,Q_H^\dagger e_H\rangle=o(T)
\]
together with \(E_L=o(T)\) would settle the positive-kernel Schur debit. Finite computations can diagnose the required cutoff \(H(p)\) and the size of \(\mathcal L_{p,H}\), but do not settle the asymptotic claim.

### 2. Zero-block inverse test

For the same actual primes and the same order of limits, establish directly
\[
\langle c_{0,B},G_{0,BB}^{-1}c_{0,B}\rangle=o(T).
\]
The most targeted route is an actual Fourier-minor/Cauchy–Binet or Jacobi-character estimate for the source-weighted resolvent, rather than a uniform lower bound on \(\lambda_{\min}(G_{0,BB})\). If \(B=\varnothing\) along a subfamily, this debit vanishes there; otherwise it must be controlled independently of the Schur estimate.
