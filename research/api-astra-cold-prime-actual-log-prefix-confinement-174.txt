## Result

I cannot certify the requested growing-prefix confinement from the supplied results. The correct status is **inconclusive, with a precise missing estimate**, not a refutation.

The family remains the actual physical one:

- eligible primes \(p\);
- \(n=(p-1)/2\);
- physical marked coordinate \(t_0=n\);
- both lifts;
- the original closed square \(Q\);
- true rows \(V_h\) and the actual map \(F_t(W)=K\Phi_t(W)\).

Set
\[
k_n=\left\lceil \frac47\log_2 n\right\rceil,\qquad
R_h=V_{h+1}-V_h.
\]
The required statement is a uniform fixed-margin bound such as
\[
\sup_{0\le j\le k_n}\|V_{602+j}-V_{602}\|_\infty
\le d-\eta
\]
for some \(d=0.025\) and \(\eta>0\), together with final clearance from the coordinate-disk boundary.

### What is established

At height \(602\), the supplied estimates give, uniformly on \(Q\) and on both lifts,

\[
\sup_i |V_{602,i}|<4.791,
\qquad
\sup_i|\Phi'_{t,i}(V_{602,i})|<0.518,
\]
and a coordinate disk of radius \(d=0.025\) on which the scalar derivative is bounded by
\[
q=0.525<1.
\]

They also establish genuine fixed-height information:

1. the actual transitions through height \(602\) have counting-norm contraction below approximately \(0.267\) over the two steps from the height-\(600\) increment;
2. the frozen height-\(602\) operator \(B_{602}=K D_{602}\) has
   \[
   \|B_{602}\|_{2\to2}<0.518;
   \]
3. its frozen entrywise powers satisfy a bound of the form
   \[
   \|(B_{602})^jK\|_{\max}
   \ll \min\{n^{-1/2}(1.066)^j,\;0.518^j\};
   \]
4. consequently, for a **fixed** frozen kernel, the relevant entrywise resolvent sum is \(O(n^{-0.455})\).

If the entire actual prefix were already known to stay in the disks, then the \(q\)-contraction would give
\[
\|R_{602+j}\|_{2,n}\le q^j\|R_{602}\|_{2,n},
\]
and, since
\[
q^{k_n}\sqrt n
=
n^{1/2+(4/7)\log_2 q}
=o(1)
\]
because \(q^8<2^{-7}\), the tail after the cutoff would be harmless.

### The remaining gap

The missing quantity is not the frozen \(B_{602}\), but the actual varying secant transfer
\[
R_{h+1}
=
K\,D_h^{\mathrm{sec}}R_h,
\qquad
D_h^{\mathrm{sec}}
=
\int_0^1
\operatorname{diag}
\Phi_t'\!\left(V_{h-1}+sR_{h-1}\right)\,ds,
\]
for \(h=602,\ldots,601+k_n\).

The available frozen estimates do not imply a bound for the products
\[
K D_{601+j}^{\mathrm{sec}}\cdots K D_{602}^{\mathrm{sec}},
\]
nor for their signed partial sums in coordinate supremum norm. In particular:

- the estimates \(\|B_{602}^jK\|_{\max}=O(n^{-0.455})\) concern one fixed kernel, not the moving kernels \(D_h^{\mathrm{sec}}\);
- the counting contraction alone gives only
  \[
  \|R_{602+j}\|_\infty
  \le \sqrt n\,q^j\|R_{602}\|_{2,n},
  \]
  which is useless at the beginning of the prefix when the known bound on \(\|R_{602}\|_{2,n}\) is merely \(O(1)\);
- the source-side prices \(<0.00008312\) and \(<0.00008861\) concern the first nonlinear source and one frozen transported step. They do not provide a uniform signed bound for the full varying-kernel orbit;
- the criterion in the logarithmic finite-tube reference assumes precisely the desired confinement and final clearance. It therefore cannot serve as an independent proof of them.

Thus no actual bound of the form
\[
\sup_{j\le k_n}\|V_{602+j}-V_{602}\|_\infty\le d-\eta
\]
has been verified for the original square, the physical \(t_0=n\), and both lifts.

This is a **failure of the presently supplied sufficient estimates**, not evidence that the actual family exits the tube.

## Ranked next tests

### 1. Highest priority: a moving-kernel signed transfer estimate

Prove, on the actual family and without assuming an invariant tube, a packet/character bound for the secant products
\[
T_j
=
K D_{601+j}^{\mathrm{sec}}\cdots K D_{602}^{\mathrm{sec}}
\]
through \(j=k_n\). A sufficient target would be a uniform estimate such as
\[
\|T_jR_{602}\|_\infty
\le C\rho^j
\quad(\rho<1),
\]
or, more flexibly,
\[
\left\|
\sum_{\ell<j}T_\ell R_{602}
\right\|_\infty
\le d-\eta
\]
for every \(j\le k_n\), with the final residual satisfying
\[
\sqrt n\,\|T_{k_n}R_{602}\|_{2,n}=o(1).
\]

The proof must retain the actual signed character cancellations, the physical marked-coordinate correction \(4n+z^2\), both lifts, and uniformity on \(Q\). Replacing every moving kernel by an unsigned frozen majorant would not settle the question.

### 2. Second priority: a bootstrap bridge from a short actual segment to the logarithmic cutoff

First obtain a genuinely signed bound for an initial segment, for example
\[
\max_{0\le j\le J}
\|V_{602+j}-V_{602}\|_\infty\le d-\eta_0
\]
with \(J\) growing with \(n\), not merely a fixed finite diagnostic. Then prove a perturbative comparison
\[
\|D_{601+j}^{\mathrm{sec}}-D_{602}\|_{\text{transfer}}
\le \delta_j
\]
whose cumulative effect on the frozen products is summable:
\[
\sum_{j\le k_n}\delta_j=o(1).
\]

The frozen height-\(602\) entrywise estimate could then control the remainder, while the initial signed bridge supplies the missing coordinate margin. This test is weaker than a full direct moving-kernel theorem but still requires a quantitative, prime-uniform growing segment; a fixed-depth computation would not be sufficient.
