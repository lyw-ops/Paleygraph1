# Review of the proposed height-600 cutoff comparison

The [returned draft](api-astra-overflow-124.txt) correctly computes that \(q^{k_S}\) with \(q=0.515\), \(\beta=0.467\) and \(k_S=\lceil\beta\log n/(2|\log q|)\rceil\) is only of order \(n^{-0.2335}\). An initial \(O(1)\) normalized residual and an assumed contraction through that many steps therefore do not by themselves imply \(o(n^{-0.2665})\). From the scalar geometric bound alone, a sufficient step count at a bounded initial residual is \((1-\beta)\log n/(2|\log q|)+\omega(1)\). This is a comparison of upper bounds.

The proposed construction misuses the cutoff. Round 122 derives \(k_S\) only **after** assuming the residual is already at the quadratic scale at the chosen moving starter. The cutoff certifies a frozen displacement prefix at that starter. It is neither an established prefix of that length at height 600 nor a limitation on the time allowed to reach a starter. Also \(0.515\) bounds a single actual outgoing secant at height 600. It gives neither a second certified adjacent step nor a contraction through \(k_S\). Thus the arithmetic mismatch supplies no obstruction to the actual trajectory and no necessary lower on any starter depth. At \(k_S\) the scalar bound would be sufficient with \(r_0=o(n^{-0.033})\) if the requisite continuation were separately proved.

The exceptional term from Round 118 for \(|F|=O(n^\beta)\) is \(O(n^{(1-\beta)/2}r)\), which is \(o(1)\) under the actual stated moving residual assumption. The factor \(n^{(1-\beta)/2}\) is missing from the draft's \(O(r)\).

The independently derived [varying-kernel transfer](cold-signed-time-kernel-transfer-124.md) retains the signed first insertion between two frozen coefficients. It gives an all-time quadratic estimate on genuinely changing products under the stated norm hypotheses. Its two frozen cutoffs shorten the signed test but do not construct the moving starter. The [completion receipt](api-astra-124-receipt.json) and raw draft preserve the response and its scope.
