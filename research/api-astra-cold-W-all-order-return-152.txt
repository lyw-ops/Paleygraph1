## Result

For the genuine \(C_1\) column, with \(t_0=n\), both physical lifts, and the translated \(P_{5000}\) norm, the two proposed coefficients are correct as **exposed field contributions**, provided \(a_k(t)\) and \(b_k(t)\) denote the actual Chebyshev coefficients of
\[
d_t(S)=\sum_{k\ {\rm odd}}a_k(t)C_k,\qquad
d_t'(S)=\sum_{k\ {\rm even}}b_k(t)C_k
\]
on the original square. Here \(S=s_nC_1\), \(s_n=2\sqrt{p/n}\), and \(\beta=\beta(t)\) is the actual complex coefficient of \(W\).

The two selected contributions are
\[
\boxed{\beta\,a_1\Bigl(b_0+\frac{b_2}{2}\Bigr)\,K(C_1F_4)}
\]
and
\[
\boxed{\frac{\beta b_0}{2}(a_1+a_3)\,K(WY)}.
\]

These formulas are valid up to the already established physical-mark and feedback corrections
\[
O_{P_{5000}}(n^{-1/2})
\]
uniformly on the original square, at both true heights and for both lifts.

They do **not** constitute the full all-order \(W\)-linear square.

## Derivation

Write the mixed \(W\)-linear square schematically as
\[
K\!\left[d_t(S)\,
 K\!\left(W\,d_t'(S)C_1\right)\right]
+
K\!\left[W\,d_t'(S)\,
 K\!\left(d_t(S)C_1\right)\right],
\]
with the common factor \(\beta\). The exact operator conventions produce the same two products as in the leading computation.

### 1. The \(K(C_1F_4)\) family

The first product contains
\[
d_t(S)\,K\!\left(W\,d_t'(S)C_1\right).
\]
To obtain the \(F_4\) label after the inner \(K\), one needs the \(C_1\)-component of
\[
d_t'(S)C_1.
\]
The relevant products are
\[
b_0C_1+\;b_2C_2C_1+\cdots,
\]
and
\[
C_2C_1=\frac{C_3+C_1}{2}.
\]
Hence the \(C_1\)-coefficient is
\[
b_0+\frac{b_2}{2}.
\]
The outer factor must contribute \(a_1C_1\). Since
\[
K(WC_1)=F_4+O_{P_{5000}}(n^{-1/2})
\]
after the exact feedback correction, this gives
\[
\beta a_1\Bigl(b_0+\frac{b_2}{2}\Bigr)K(C_1F_4).
\]

### 2. The \(K(WY)\) family

For the second product, the relevant part is
\[
W\,d_t'(S)\,K(d_t(S)C_1).
\]
The \(W\)-factor contributes \(b_0W\) to the selected family. The \(Y\)-component of \(K(d_t(S)C_1)\) comes from
\[
a_1C_1^2+a_3C_3C_1+\cdots.
\]
Using
\[
C_1^2=\frac{1+C_2}{2},\qquad
C_3C_1=\frac{C_4+C_2}{2},
\]
and \(K1=0\), \(K(C_2)=Y+O_{P_{5000}}(n^{-1/2})\), the \(Y\)-coefficient is
\[
\frac{a_1}{2}+\frac{a_3}{2}.
\]
Therefore the second exposed contribution is
\[
\frac{\beta b_0}{2}(a_1+a_3)\,K(WY).
\]

This also explains why the two coefficients are not simply \(\beta a_1b_0\): the \(b_2\) and \(a_3\) terms return to the same Jacobi mode through the Chebyshev product rule.

## What is and is not merged

The two displayed expressions are genuine same-label collections:

- \(K(C_1F_4)\) has the rank-four derived signature together with the Jacobi shift from \(C_1\).
- \(K(WY)\) has the rank-three \(W\)-signature and a nontrivial Legendre factor.

The rank-four, rank-three, and Legendre signatures described in the supplied arithmetic records distinguish these two families from one another. The known \(F_4\), \(W\), \(C_1\), and \(Y\) weight measures are preserved under both physical lifts. The punctual feedback terms are multiples of \(Ke_0\) and therefore have the usual \(O_{P_{5000}}(n^{-1/2})\) price.

However, this only excludes merging **among the four leading families**
\[
C_1F_4,\qquad YF_4,\qquad WY,\qquad W^2.
\]
It does not exclude coincidence with:

1. higher Chebyshev orders in the same square;
2. words containing two or more \(W\)-factors;
3. other input columns;
4. transported columns after the outer \(K\);
5. other centers or the genuine Picard residual.

Thus “same exposed field” is strictly weaker than “same irreducible label in the complete Taylor square.”

## Signed tails

The alpha-free \(W\)-linear part has the formal expansion
\[
\sum_{\substack{r\ {\rm odd}\\ s\ {\rm even}}}
\beta\,a_r b_s\,
\Bigl[
K\{C_rK(WC_sC_1)\}
+
K\{W C_sK(C_rC_1)\}
\Bigr],
\]
with the physical mark restored afterward.

The selected coefficients above are precisely the \((r,s)=(1,0),(1,2)\) returns in the first bracket and the \((r,s)=(1,0),(3,0)\) returns in the second bracket. All other pairs form the complementary tail. In particular, the first omitted terms include contributions from

- \(a_5b_0\),

as well as terms whose resulting labels are not \(C_1F_4\) or \(WY\).

The supplied analytic bounds do give convergence of this tail. On the nominated square one has the relevant strict scalar radius margin, with the existing \(U<1\) bound for the Jacobi argument and the all-order Legendre expansion controlled by the \(w<1\) estimate. Consequently the alpha-free \(W\)-linear series is absolutely summable before transport, and the physical-mark repair is termwise \(O(n^{-1/2})\) with a summable coefficient majorant.
