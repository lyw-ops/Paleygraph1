## Result

I cannot certify the \(0.472\) target from the supplied records. The existing \(0.6404\) computation is not a lower bound for the actual signed remainder operator, and no valid physical dual functional or coherent packet is specified in the records.

The precise unresolved object is, for each \(h\in\{601,602\}\),
\[
E_h=D_h-D^{\mathrm J},
\qquad
K E_h,
\]
where \(D_h\) is the actual diagonal derivative at the actual predictor \(V_h\), \(D^{\mathrm J}\) is the full pure Jacobi-seed derivative, and \(K\) is evaluated with the actual physical marking \(t_0=n=(p-1)/2\). The required assertion is
\[
\sup_{(a,b)\in Q}
 \|K E_h(a,b)\|_{P_{5000}}
<0.472-\delta
\]
for some \(\delta>0\), uniformly for all sufficiently large primes
\[
p\equiv1\pmod 4,\qquad n=(p-1)/2,
\]
with the prime limit taken after the fixed physical norm and fixed heights \(h=601,602\).

## What is established

The supplied records establish three relevant facts.

1. **The unsigned comparison fails.**  
   At \(a=2031/128\), the positive envelope formed from the linear and cubic orders gives an affine-limit charge \(>0.6404\). This proves only that this particular coefficientwise-positive majorant cannot certify \(0.472\).

2. **The analytic seed has already been separated.**  
   The full pure-seed derivative \(D^{\mathrm J}\), including all pure odd powers of \(S\), has a translated physical rate below \(0.5279\) by the recorded finite-order aggregation and geometric tail. Thus the remaining issue really is the signed remainder \(E_h\), not an omitted pure-seed term.

3. **Counting estimates are insufficient.**  
   The records give
   \[
   \|K E_h\|_{2\to2}<0.275,
   \]
   but the flat-entry example shows that a counting contraction does not produce a physical translated-price bound. Hence the \(0.275\) estimate cannot be converted into the required \(P_{5000}\)-estimate without additional physical support information.

## Why the \(0.6404\) number is not a signed lower bound

Write \(C_h=V_h-S\). The first remainder term is schematically
\[
d_t'(S)\,C_h,
\qquad
d_t'(S)=\frac{4t}{(4t+S^2)^{3/2}},
\]
and higher terms contain one or more occurrences of \(C_h\) mixed with powers of \(S\).

A valid signed calculation must perform the following operations in this order:

1. expand the actual \(C_h\) and the actual powers of \(S\) into Laurent-by-field terms;
2. identify terms with exactly the same Laurent exponent and the same underlying field;
3. add their signed coefficients;
4. only then take the physical price or a dual pairing.

The \(0.6404\) computation instead takes absolute values of the positive envelope before this aggregation. It therefore replaces, for a collection of coefficients \(c_{\lambda,\chi}\), the actual quantity
\[
\left|\sum_{\lambda,\chi\ \text{with same physical object}}
c_{\lambda,\chi}\right|
\]
by
\[
\sum_{\lambda,\chi}|c_{\lambda,\chi}|.
\]
This is an upper bound for the price of a chosen representation, not a lower bound for the norm of the signed operator. Numerical coincidence of boundary weights is also insufficient: the records correctly note that distinct Mellin fields with the same numerical weight are not automatically the same physical Laurent object.

Consequently, the current records neither prove nor disprove
\[
\|K E_h\|_{P_{5000}}<0.472.
\]

## Explicit coherent-packet test

The natural packet to test is the part of \(K E_h\) linear in the actual nonseed row \(C_h\):
\[
\mathcal L_h
=
K\left[
\sum_{\ell\ge0}
(-1)^\ell
\frac{\binom{2\ell+1}{\ell}}{4^\ell}
\frac{S^{2\ell}C_h}{(4t)^{\ell+3/2}}
\right],
\]
with the product interpreted in the actual Laurent-by-field algebra, including the physical marked coordinate \(t_0=n\). The finite truncation through \(\ell=13\) is the relevant first test; the recorded tail estimate beginning at order \(29\) is \(<0.0000407\) for the corresponding unsigned remainder tail.

For this packet, a valid positive price certificate would be obtained by exact aggregation of all equal Laurent-by-field terms. If the resulting folded coefficients are \(c_k(a,b)\), the physical translated price must be computed from the actual signed coefficients, for example through the exact column ratios
\[
\frac{(j-1)\nu+
 \sum_{k>0}2|c_k|
 \bigl(\pi(|j+k|)+\pi(|j-k|)\bigr)/2}
{\pi(j)},
\]
with the same fixed rational \(\pi\) and \(\Lambda=5000\) used in the seed calculation. The absolute values here occur only after coefficient aggregation.

The current material does not contain the required \(c_k(a,b)\) table for \(C_{601}\) or \(C_{602}\). Therefore this coherent-packet test cannot be completed from the supplied evidence.

## Precise failure

The proposed coherent-packet argument currently fails at the **field-identification step**, not at the tail estimate:

- the records provide unsigned boundary weights and rank/dimension bounds;
- they do not provide the actual signed Laurent expansion of \(C_h\) through the retained orders;
- without that expansion, one cannot determine which terms are identical physical objects;
- without those identifications, neither cancellation nor a bounded physical dual pairing can be certified.

The failure is therefore genuine and sharply localized. It does not show that the \(0.472\) claim is false.

## Ranked next tests

### 1. Exact signed aggregation of the linear packet — highest priority

For each \(h=601,602\):

1. compute the actual Laurent-by-field expansion of \(C_h\) through derivative order \(27\) (orders \(1,3,\ldots,27\));
2. multiply by the pure-seed powers \(S^{2\ell}\);
3. key terms by the full physical identity: Laurent exponent, field/character label, lift, and marked correction;
4. sum signed coefficients exactly or with outward Arb;
5. price the aggregated columns with the fixed \(P_{5000}\) and rational \(\pi\);
6. maximize over the original closed cold square.

**Decision criterion:**  
- a bound \(<0.472-\delta\) with explicit \(\delta>0\) supports the candidate, after adding the recorded \(<0.0000407\) tail and \(O_{5000}(n^{-1/2})\) physical-marking corrections;
- a single certified value \(>0.472\) for an actual bounded dual pairing refutes the candidate.

This is the narrowest arithmetic test because the linear term is expected to dominate and is exactly where the records identify the need for signed aggregation.

### 2. Physical dual LP on the aggregated finite support

If the first test does not close the estimate, construct the finite physical support of the aggregated order-\(\le27\) remainder and solve the dual price problem directly.

The dual must be defined on the actual physical translated coordinates, not on unsigned weights. Its norm constraint must be the dual of \(P_{5000}\), with the actual base mark \(t_0=n\), translated pair, and boundary transport included. Test separately the bulk and marked columns, then add the certified \(O_{5000}(n^{-1/2})\) correction uniformly as \(p\to\infty\).

This test can either produce:

- a bounded dual functional pairing \(>0.472\), which would refute the desired upper bound; or
- an exact finite-support upper certificate below \(0.472\), leaving only the already-controlled tail and asymptotic marking correction.

Until one of these two tests is performed, the \(0.472\) remainder estimate remains genuinely open.
