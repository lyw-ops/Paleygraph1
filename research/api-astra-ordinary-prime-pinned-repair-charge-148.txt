## Result

For the actual ordinary-prime family, one can prove a nontrivial **localized signed-spill estimate**, but not the required prime-uniform pointwise bound.

Let
\[
p\equiv1\pmod 4,\qquad n=\frac{p-1}{2},
\]
with the physical mark at the identity coordinate \(0\). Use the actual zero-start Picard orbit at fixed floor \(\tau>0\), and take a growing depth \(T=T(p)\) inside the established simultaneous marked window. For either sign \(\sigma\), write
\[
Z=\frac{X_{\sigma,T}}{M_T},\qquad
u=T^{-1/3},\qquad
d_0=0,\qquad d_i=(u-Z_i)_+\quad(i\ne0).
\]
All expectations below are normalized counting expectations
\[
\mathbb E f=\frac1n\sum_i f_i
\]
over the \(n\) physical coordinates. The Jacobi operator satisfies
\[
K^2=I-J,\qquad \|K\|_{2\to2}\le1.
\]

The previously established actual-orbit estimates give
\[
\mathbb E d^2=O_\tau(u^3+T^{-1})=O_\tau(T^{-1}).
\]
Therefore
\[
\mathbb E (Kd)^2
=\mathbb E d^2-(\mathbb E d)^2
\le \mathbb E d^2
=O_\tau(T^{-1}).
\]

Define the genuinely bad signed-spill set
\[
E_\sigma
=
\left\{i\ne0:\ \sigma(Kd)_i<-\frac u2\right\}.
\]
By normalized Chebyshev,
\[
\frac{|E_\sigma|}{n}
\le
\frac{4\,\mathbb E[(Kd)^2]}{u^2}
=
O_\tau\!\left(\frac{T^{-1}}{T^{-2/3}}\right)
=
O_\tau(T^{-1/3}).
\tag{A}
\]

Hence, for both signs and all sufficiently large \(T\),
\[
\sigma(Kd)_i\ge-\frac12T^{-1/3}
\]
outside a set of relative size \(O_\tau(T^{-1/3})\).

Using the exact pinned projection identity
\[
(Z+2L_\sigma d)_i
=
\max(Z_i,u)+\sigma(Kd)_i
-\mathbb E d
-\frac{2\ell_i\,\ell^{\mathsf T}d}{\ell_0},
\]
the final two terms are \(O_\tau(\mathbb E d)=O_\tau(T^{-2/3})\) uniformly on the bulk. Thus outside \(E_\sigma\),
\[
(Z+2L_\sigma d)_i
\ge
u-\frac u2-O_\tau(T^{-2/3})
>0.
\tag{B}
\]

So the exact one-reflection correction is positive on all but
\[
O_\tau(nT^{-1/3})
\]
bulk coordinates, while preserving:

- the actual physical mark \(0\);
- the exact mean;
- the \(\sigma\)-sector;
- inversion-evenness;
- the actual prime-specific projected average \(Z\).

This is a genuine global-spill estimate, not merely a bound on the local defect support. The original defect support itself satisfies
\[
|\{i:d_i>0\}|=O_\tau(nT^{-1/3}),
\]
but (A) controls the larger set created by Jacobi transport.

## What this does not prove

It does **not** prove the desired pointwise estimate
\[
\inf_{i\ne0}\sigma(Kd)_i\ge-\frac12T^{-1/3}.
\]
The \(L^2\)-contraction of \(K\) permits concentration of the signed spill on \(O(nT^{-1/3})\) sites, exactly the exceptional scale in (A). No ordinary-prime input has yet ruled out such concentration for the actual Picard defect.

Nor does (A) by itself give a global positive repair. The residual exceptional set is too large for the previously available small-support dual thresholds in general. In particular, the argument does not establish a same-mean, same-mark positive completion for every eligible ordinary prime.

## Prime arithmetic status

The estimate above is actually **all-prime**: it uses only the actual-orbit second-moment bounds and the contraction identity for \(K\). It therefore gives no lowering specific to ordinary primes.

The square obstruction remains compatible with this result. On eligible square orders, the signed terminal/hinge correlations are known to have positive macroscopic cost even though \(\mathbb E d=O_\tau(T^{-2/3})\). Thus small total defect mass does not imply small signed Jacobi image. For ordinary primes, the missing statement is precisely an arithmetic anti-concentration estimate for the actual support and amplitudes of \(d\), not a generic \(L^2\) estimate.

The order of limits remains:
\[
\text{fixed }\tau>0
\quad\longrightarrow\quad
p\to\infty\text{ through ordinary primes with }T=T(p)\to\infty
\quad\longrightarrow\quad
\tau\downarrow0.
\]
The present estimate is uniform in the first two stages only within the proved marked window and has constants depending on fixed \(\tau\).

## Evidence and hypotheses

The argument requires only the established actual-orbit facts:

1. \(Z=X_{\sigma,T}/M_T\) is the true projected average, not a surrogate mask.
2. \(d_0=0\) at the physical root.
3. \(\mathbb E d^2=O_\tau(T^{-1})\).
4. \(K^2=I-J\), hence
   \[
   \mathbb E(Kd)^2=\mathbb E d^2-(\mathbb E d)^2.
   \]
5. The pinned-projection remainder is \(O_\tau(\mathbb E d)\).
6. The marked time \(T\) and \(T+1\) lie in the simultaneous ordinary-prime marked window.

No claim is made that the finite checks or square examples determine the asymptotic ordinary-prime behavior.

## Remaining gap

A sufficient completion would follow from either of the following genuinely prime-specific statements:

- **Pointwise signed cancellation**
  \[
  \inf_{i\ne0}\sigma(Kd)_i\ge-\frac12u;
  \]
- **Exceptional-set repair**
  a prime-uniform theorem showing that the set \(E_\sigma\) in (A), together with its actual amplitudes, admits an exact mean- and mark-preserving correction with spill \(o(u)\).

Neither is presently proved. The known translated-mask and filtered-support bounds do not directly apply because the actual defect amplitudes are generated by the Picard endpoint and are not an arbitrary nonnegative row supported on a prescribed algebraic mask.

## Ranked next tests

### 1. Actual-support weighted Jacobi collision test — highest priority

For the true defect \(d\), compute or prove a bound for
\[
\mathcal C_\sigma(d)
=
\sum_{i\ne0}
\bigl((-\sigma Kd)_i\bigr)_+^2
\]
or, more sharply, for the weighted collision
\[
\mathbb E\!\left[F\,(-\sigma Kd)_+\right],
\]
where \(F\) is a true opposite-sector optimizer or a near-optimal positive row.

The target is
\[
\mathcal C_\sigma(d)=o_\tau(u^2)
\]
or
\[
\mathbb E[F(-\sigma Kd)_+]=o_\tau(1).
\]
This would upgrade the present \(O_\tau(u^2)\) bound to a genuinely useful prime-specific estimate and could feed either the exact reflection or the dual completion inequality.

### 2. Oriented terminal-mask test — second priority

Use the exact physical-root terminal field
\[
x_T=s\sqrt n\,v_T,\qquad s=-\sigma,
\]
and the oriented positive mask
\[
S^+=\{i\ne0:x_T(i)\ge cT,\ R_i\le\delta x_T(i)/T\}.
\]
Test whether ordinary-prime Jacobi correlations satisfy a uniform bound of the form
\[
\mathbb E\bigl[F\,x_T\,1_{S^+}\bigr]=o_\tau(T)
\]
for a true opposite optimizer \(F\), or equivalently whether the translated intersections of the actual mask have
\[
K_{S^+}(A)=o(a^2T/n)
\]
for a suitable near-cap support set \(A\).

This directly targets the signed terminal pair rather than the positive-part hinge and preserves the physical marking and orientation. A successful bound would exclude a fixed prime theta gap, even without proving the pointwise reflection inequality.
