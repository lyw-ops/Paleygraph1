## Verdict

The favorable-sign \(O(p)\) upper is incompatible with the stated near-critical actual branch, provided the Round-152 weighted-anchor flattening and the Round-154 wrong-sign estimate are used exactly as stated.

More precisely, along the true ordinary-prime sequence \(p\equiv1\pmod 4\), with \(p\to\infty\) and the coupled marked-window parameter \(T=T(p)\to\infty\), the actual retargeted deficit \(b\) selected in the Round-151 block satisfies
\[
b\ge0,\qquad b_0=0,\qquad
m_{\rm eff}=\frac{nr^2}{\mathbb E b^2}\sim \frac n{\sqrt p},
\qquad
\frac{\|L_\sigma b\|_{2,n}}r=o(1),
\]
where \(n=(p-1)/2\), \(r=\mathbb E b\), and \(\mathbb E,\|\cdot\|_{2,n}\) use divisor \(n\). The physical marked coordinate is \(0\), with
\[
z_*=(1+\delta)\frac nq,\qquad \delta=T^{-1/6},
\]
where \(q\) is the true opposite optimum.

For this actual \(b\), the favorable off-diagonal is not \(O(p)\); conditionally on the stated Round-152 and Round-154 estimates, it is superlinear:
\[
\frac{S_+}{p}\longrightarrow+\infty.
\]

The obstruction is the ordinary-prime multiplicative-correlation tail estimate for the actual reflected functions \(g_a\).

## 1. Exact physical expansion with the true anchor law

Put
\[
\nu_a=\frac{b_a}{nr},\qquad
f(t)=\sum_{a\in G}\nu_a\,a(t),
\]
where \(G=\widehat Q\), and \(f\) is zero off \(Q\). Thus \(f\) retains its actual complex phase.

Fix \(v\) with \(\chi(v)=\rho=-\sigma\), and define
\[
F_0(x)=f(1-vx).
\]
For a physical anchor \(a\in G\), put
\[
g_a(x)=F_0(x)\,\overline{a(1-vx)}
\]
on the contributing \(x\in Q\). For \(u\in Q\setminus\{1\}\), write
\[
C_a(u)=\sum_x F_0(ux)\overline{F_0(x)}\,
          \overline{a(r_u(x))},
\qquad
r_u(x)=\frac{1-vux}{1-vx}.
\]

Averaging over the **true** anchor law \(\nu\), not the uniform law on \(G\), gives exactly
\[
\mathcal M
=\mathbb E_{a\sim\nu}\sum_{u\in Q\setminus\{1\}}|C_a(u)|^2
=\sum_{u,x,y} A_u(x)\overline{A_u(y)}
   f\!\left(\frac{r_u(y)}{r_u(x)}\right),
\]
where
\[
A_u(x)=F_0(ux)\overline{F_0(x)}.
\]

The diagonal \(x=y\) has \(f(1)=1\). For \(x\ne y\),
\[
1-\frac{r_u(y)}{r_u(x)}
=
\frac{v(u-1)(y-x)}{(1-vy)(1-vux)}.
\]
All denominator factors are squares on the contributing set, hence
\[
\chi(1-t)=\rho\,\chi((u-1)(y-x)).
\]

Define \(S_\pm\) to be the off-diagonal contributions with
\[
\chi((u-1)(y-x))=\pm1.
\]
Then
\[
\mathcal M=D+S_++S_-,
\]
where \(D\) is the physical diagonal.

This is the required favorable/wrong sign split, with the original \(F_0\), \(f\), and true weights intact.

## 2. The diagonal is \(O(p)\)

By multiplicative Parseval,
\[
\sum_{y\in Q}|f(y)|^2
=n\sum_{a\in G}\nu_a^2
=\frac n{m_{\rm eff}}
\sim \sqrt p.
\]
The map \(x\mapsto1-vx\) is injective, so
\[
\|F_0\|_2^2\le \frac n{m_{\rm eff}}\sim\sqrt p.
\]
Therefore
\[
D
=\sum_{u\ne1,x}|F_0(ux)F_0(x)|^2
\le \|F_0\|_2^4
=O(p).
\]

This uses counting norms on the physical field; there is no divisor in this estimate.

## 3. The total true-anchor moment is superlinear

For each anchor \(a\), define
\[
D_{g_a}
=\sup_{s\ge1}\frac{s^2}{p}
 \bigl|\{u\ne1:|C_a(u)|\ge s\}\bigr|.
\]
Then directly
\[
\frac1p\sum_{u\ne1}|C_a(u)|^2\ge D_{g_a}.
\]

The ordinary-prime incidence estimate from Round 152 says that, for the reflected \(g_a\),
\[
L^2(1+D_{g_a})\log(2L(1+D_{g_a}))
\gg
\min(\eta_a^{-1/2},p^{1/4}),
\]
where
\[
L=\max\left(1,\frac{m_{\rm eff}}{m_0}\right),
\qquad m_0=\frac n{\sqrt p},
\]
and
\[
\eta_a
=\frac{\langle g_a,(I-\mathcal T)g_a\rangle}{\|g_a\|_2^2}.
\]

The actual affine reflection gives
\[
\eta_a=O\!\left(d_a+\frac L{\sqrt p}\right),
\]
with
\[
d_a=
\frac{\sum_j b_j^2(1-\rho k(j-a))}
     {\sum_jb_j^2}.
\]

The near-critical flattening statement for the actual \(b\) gives
\[
\mathbb E_{a\sim\nu}d_a=o(1).
\]
Hence \(d_a=o(1)\), and therefore \(\eta_a=o(1)\), in \(\nu\)-probability. Since \(L\to1\), the incidence estimate implies
\[
D_{g_a}\longrightarrow\infty
\qquad\text{in }\nu\text{-probability}.
\]
Consequently,
\[
\frac{\mathcal M}{p}
=
\mathbb E_{a\sim\nu}
 \frac1p\sum_{u\ne1}|C_a(u)|^2
\ge
\mathbb E_{a\sim\nu}D_{g_a}
\longrightarrow\infty.
\]

Thus the true weighted total moment is superlinear.

This is not a generic positive-energy argument: it uses the actual reflected \(g_a\), the ordinary-prime incidence estimate, and the true deficit-weighted anchor law.

## 4. The wrong-sign contribution is only \(O(p)\)

On the wrong sector,
\[
\chi((u-1)(y-x))=-1,
\]
equivalently \(\chi(1-t)=\sigma\). The exact pin gives
\[
f(t)=c+e(t)
\]
there, with
\[
c=O(p^{-1/2}),
\qquad
\|e\|_2
\le \frac{\|L_\sigma b\|_{2,n}}r
=O(n^{-1/3}T^{-2/9}).
\]

The Round-154 calculation retains the full complex values of \(A_u(x)\) and proves
\[
S_-=O(p)
+
O\!\left(p^{11/12}T^{-2/9}\right)
=O(p),
\]
because \(T\to\infty\) in the actual coupled limit. The \(c\)-part is controlled by the signed character mask and the additive convolution norm of \(\chi\); the \(e\)-part is controlled using the same actual block estimate.

Therefore
\[
S_+=\mathcal M-D-S_-.
\]
Since \(\mathcal M/p\to\infty\), while \(D=O(p)\) and \(S_-=O(p)\),
\[
\boxed{\displaystyle \frac{S_+}{p}\longrightarrow+\infty.}
\]

The aggregate \(S_+\) is real because it equals the favorable-sector contribution to a sum of squared correlations after subtracting the real diagonal. Thus this disproves not only an absolute \(O(p)\) bound but also a signed upper bound \(S_+\le Cp\).

## Named obstruction and its relaxed hypotheses

The obstruction is the ordinary-prime weighted multiplicative-correlation estimate
\[
L^2(1+D_g)\log(2L(1+D_g))
\gg
\min(\eta_g^{-1/2},p^{1/4}).
\]

For the contradiction, one does **not** need the full optimizer or the entire retargeted flow. It is enough to have, along ordinary primes \(p\equiv1\pmod4\):

1. \(b\ge0\), \(b_0=0\), with
   \[
   m_{\rm eff}/m_0\to1;
   \]
2. \(\|L_\sigma b\|_{2,n}/r=o(1)\);
3. true-anchor concentration
   \[
   \mathbb E_{a\sim\nu}d_a=o(1);
   \]
4. the physical wrong-sector estimate \(S_-=O(p)\).

No additive-energy assumption, pointwise correlation cap, uniform anchor law, or prime averaging over moduli is required.

Under these relaxed assumptions, the incidence estimate forces
\[
\mathbb E_{a\sim\nu}\sum_{u\ne1}|C_a(u)|^2\gg p\,\omega(p),
\qquad \omega(p)\to\infty,
\]
and the \(O(p)\) diagonal and wrong sector force the favorable off-diagonal to carry the same superlinear mass.
