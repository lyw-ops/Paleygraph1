# Review of the masked conditional trace response

The [API draft](api-astra-overflow-121.txt) gives the correct formal conditional decomposition (A). Several subsequent resource estimates use the wrong law. The coefficient is \(\xi_d(b)\pi_*(b+e)=q_*(b+e)g(b)g_-(b+d)/(n^2m\mu^2)\). There is no extra favored factor at \(b+e\). The small negative mass proved for the **reweighted** \(w_*\) is not a bound for the variation of \(\pi_*\). Since \(\xi_d\) is nonnegative and has mass one, the draft's proposed immediate bound by \(\|\pi_*\|_\infty=O(n^{-1/2})\) is valid without conditional uniformity.

The deleted-output term requires the uniform difference factor \(1/n\) as well as the output factor \(1/n\). After summing over both indices it factorizes into actual input Jacobi evaluations, with only a subcritical distinct correction. If any input sits at the removed output, its aggregate contribution is \(O(n^{-2})\), using its \(O(n^{-1/2})\) probability or signed mass, the factor \(k(0)=-1/\sqrt p\), and the deleted-output \(1/n\). In general the aggregate deleted term can be order \(n^{-1}\). Pointwise smallness in \((e,d)\) gives no necessary averaged cancellation, and the deleted term is already explicitly accounted for in the masked identity. The [local spectral calculation](masked-conditional-spectrum-121.md) gives the exact joint support of the genuine two-favored/one-opposite conditional law.

The [ordinary trajectory note](ordinary-slack-dissipation-121.md) handles actual arriving negative mass independently. It retains the actual old slack and the genuine initial capped row. The [completion receipt](api-astra-121-receipt.json) and returned draft preserve what was submitted. No prime-uniform upper for the full masked cubic or rate-free excess is established.

The [fixed-depth extension](early-ordinary-masked-cubic-121.md) uses the true difference law together with a second moment of the actual repair reweighting density. It removes the requirement that the repair source have a bounded supremum and allows a sufficiently large fixed ordinary repair depth. This is a new local lower under the critical cap-rate marked obstruction, independent of the draft's conditional estimate.
