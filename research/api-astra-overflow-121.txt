## New exact contraction and the remaining prime-specific bottleneck

Assume the reflected critical source \(q\) satisfies the accepted estimates
\[
\|q\|_\infty=O(1),\qquad \mathbb E_n(qg)\asymp n^{-1/2},\qquad
\mu\asymp n^{-1/2},
\]
and use the exact favored projection
\[
q_*=q-\Pi_-q,\qquad 
\pi_*(a)=\frac{q_*(a)}{n m}.
\]
Let
\[
\xi_d(b)=n\omega_b\omega^-_{b+d},
\]
so that \(\xi_d\) is a probability distribution for every \(d\). For distinct shifts, write \(e=a-b\), \(d=c-b\). The output-masked conditional contraction is exactly
\[
\begin{aligned}
\mathscr C(e,d)
&:=\sum_b \xi_d(b)\pi_*(b+e)
 \left[
 T_3(e,0,d)
 -\frac1n k(-b-e)k(-b)k(-b-d)
 \right]  \\
&=T_3(e,0,d)\,R(e,d)-\frac1n D(e,d), \tag{A}
\end{aligned}
\]
where
\[
R(e,d):=\sum_b\xi_d(b)\pi_*(b+e),
\]
and
\[
D(e,d):=\sum_b\xi_d(b)\pi_*(b+e)
 k(-b-e)k(-b)k(-b-d).
\]

This is the required full-input formula: the opposite-factor singleton remains in \(\xi_d\), and the deleted output term is retained inside the same conditional average.

### Quantified bound that is available uniformly in \(d\)

The favored projection has total mass one. Moreover, the accepted projection estimate gives
\[
\|\pi_*\|_{\mathrm{TV}}
=\sum_a|\pi_*(a)|
\le 1+O(n^{-1/4}). \tag{B}
\]
Indeed, the negative mass of the projected reweighting is \(O(n^{-1/4})\), while its signed total mass is exactly one.

Since \(|k|\le1\) and \(\xi_d\) is a probability,
\[
|D(e,d)|
\le \sum_b\xi_d(b)|\pi_*(b+e)|
\le \|\pi_*\|_\infty
\]
is not the right bound, because the conditional translate need not be uniform. The unconditional bound that follows directly from (B) is
\[
|D(e,d)|\le \|\pi_*\|_{\mathrm{TV}}
\le 1+O(n^{-1/4}), \tag{C}
\]
and therefore
\[
\boxed{\left|
\frac1n D(e,d)\right|
\le \frac{1+O(n^{-1/4})}{n}.} \tag{D}
\]

Thus the output deletion is at the exact critical scale \(n^{-1}\). Neither the exact uniformity of the difference variable \(d\), nor the small negative mass of \(\pi_*\), improves (D) to \(o(n^{-1})\).

The exceptional values \(b\in\{0,-e,-d\}\) do not repair this. At such a value one factor is \(k(0)=-p^{-1/2}\), so their contribution is smaller provided one has a pointwise conditional-mass estimate. From the normalized factor bounds,
\[
\omega_b,\omega^-_b=O(n^{-1/2}),\qquad
\pi_*(b+e)=O(n^{-1/2}),
\]
one obtains only
\[
\sum_{b\in\{0,-e,-d\}}\xi_d(b)|\pi_*(b+e)|
=O(n^{-1/2}),
\]
hence an exceptional contribution
\[
O(n^{-1}p^{-1/2}n^{-1/2})=O(n^{-3/2})
\]
after the outside \(1/n\). The critical part of \(D(e,d)\) therefore comes from the nonexceptional \(b\), where all three \(k\)-factors have ordinary size \(O(1)\).

### Why prime arithmetic has not yet supplied the needed gain

For constant weights in \(b\), the nonexceptional sum
\[
\sum_b k(-b-e)k(-b)k(-b-d)
\]
is a complete reciprocal/Jacobi correlation and is accessible to Deligne-type estimates. The actual coefficient is instead
\[
r_{e,d}(b):=\xi_d(b)\pi_*(b+e)
=\frac{q_*(b+e)\,g(b+e)\,g(b)\,g_-(b+d)}
 {n^2\mu^2\,\mathbb E_n(qg)}
\]
(with the displayed normalization understood in the normalized \(n\)-mean convention). It is a translated product of three true stationary rows and the projected source. The exact law
\[
\sum_b\xi_d(b)=1
\]
controls only the mass of \(r_{e,d}\); it gives no cancellation against the reciprocal triple.

In particular, even the strongest immediate consequence,
\[
|D(e,d)|\le 1+O(n^{-1/4}),
\]
leaves a possible contribution \(c/n\) with \(c\neq0\). A bound such as
\[
D(e,d)=o(1)
\quad\text{uniformly or after the actual }(e,d)\text{-average}
\]
would be sufficient, but it does not follow from uniformity in \(d\), from \(\|\Pi_-q\|_{2,n}=O(n^{-1/2})\), or from Hasse bounds alone.

The exact arithmetic target is therefore the weighted estimate
\[
\sum_{e\ne0,d\ne0,e\ne d}
\sum_b \xi_d(b)\pi_*(b+e)
 k(-b-e)k(-b)k(-b-d)
=o(1), \tag{E}
\]
with the same conditional weights as in (A). This is a genuinely stronger statement than any fixed-\(d\) estimate for \(T_3(e,0,d)\).

## Conclusion

The proposed \(o(n^{-1})\) upper remains unproved. More precisely:

* The full-input, output-masked contraction has the exact decomposition (A).
* The deleted-output term is bounded uniformly only by \(n^{-1}+O(n^{-5/4})\), see (D).
* The three exceptional input locations contribute \(O(n^{-3/2})\), so they are not the obstruction.
* The unresolved obstruction is the nonexceptional weighted reciprocal correlation (E), where the conditional weight is the actual product \(\xi_d(b)\pi_*(b+e)\).
* Therefore the accepted prime-uniform Legendre bound for the unmasked \(T_3\) or the exact uniformity of \(d\) does not settle the masked full-input cubic.

### Ranked next tests

1. **Weighted sheaf/Fourier test.** Expand \(r_{e,d}(b)\) in positional Fourier modes and determine whether its nonconstant modes have an \(o(1)\) correlation with the reciprocal triple. A bound of \(O(p^{-1/2})\) for the low modes plus an \(L^2\) tail estimate would prove (E).

2. **Exact finite-prime diagnostic.** For actual stationary rows, compute \(D(e,d)\) and its \((e,d)\)-average separately from \(T_3(e,0,d)R(e,d)\). The relevant failure signature is a nonzero limiting average of \(D\), not merely a large individual \(T_3\).
