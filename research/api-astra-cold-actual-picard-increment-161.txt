## Result: the signed increment is identifiable, but no sufficient bound is currently certified

Let \(p\equiv1\pmod 4\) run through the original eligible prime family, put
\[
n=\frac{p-1}{2},\qquad t_0=n,
\]
and retain the original complex square \(Q\), both physical lifts \(\ell\in\{+,-\}\), and the translated \(P_{5000}\)-norm.

For each lift write \(V_h^\ell(t)\) for the genuine height-\(h\) Picard orbit and
\[
D_h^\ell(t)=\operatorname{diag}\!\left(\Phi_t'(V_h^\ell(t))\right),\qquad
A_h^\ell=I-KD_h^\ell,\qquad
Z_h^\ell=(A_h^\ell)^{-1}K.
\]
The fixed-center terms cancel in the genuine comparison, so
\[
J_{602}^\ell-J_{601}^\ell
   =V_{602}^\ell-V_{601}^\ell
   =:\delta_{601}^\ell .
\tag{1}
\]

This is an identity on the actual orbit, not a fixed-height formal residual.

### Exact signed transport identity

Let
\[
\delta_h^\ell=V_{h+1}^\ell-V_h^\ell .
\]
Since \(V_{h+1}^\ell=K\Phi_t(V_h^\ell)\), Taylor's formula between \(V_{600}^\ell\) and \(V_{601}^\ell\) gives
\[
\delta_{601}^\ell
 =KD_{600}^\ell\delta_{600}^\ell+N_{600}^\ell,
\tag{2}
\]
where the nonlinear remainder is the signed vector
\[
N_{600,i}^\ell
 =
 K_i\!\left[
 \Phi_t(V_{601}^\ell)-\Phi_t(V_{600}^\ell)
 -\Phi_t'(V_{600}^\ell)\delta_{600}^\ell
 \right].
\]
Equivalently, coordinatewise,
\[
N_{600}^\ell
 =
 K\left[
 \int_0^1(1-s)
 \Phi_t''\!\left(V_{600}^\ell+s\delta_{600}^\ell\right)
 \bigl(\delta_{600}^\ell\bigr)^2\,ds
 \right].
\tag{3}
\]

Thus the input transported by the outer operator \(Z_{602}^\ell\) is not a same-row derivative bound. It is the signed combination
\[
\delta_{601}^\ell
 =
 KD_{600}^\ell\delta_{600}^\ell+N_{600}^\ell,
\]
and hence
\[
Z_{602}^\ell(J_{602}^\ell-J_{601}^\ell)
 =
 Z_{602}^\ell KD_{600}^\ell\delta_{600}^\ell
 +Z_{602}^\ell N_{600}^\ell .
\tag{4}
\]
The intervening multiplier is therefore \(D_{600}^\ell\), and the two lifts must be carried separately through both \(D_{600}^\ell\) and the nonlinear remainder \(N_{600}^\ell\). Replacing \(D_{600}^\ell\) by \(D_{601}^\ell\), or pricing only \(Z_{602}^\ell\), does not estimate (4).

A second exact form, useful when comparing the two outer resolvents, is
\[
Z_{602}^\ell-Z_{601}^\ell
 =
 Z_{602}^\ell K(D_{602}^\ell-D_{601}^\ell)Z_{601}^\ell .
\tag{5}
\]
Consequently every signed comparison of the two actual heights contains the insertion
\[
Z_{602}^\ell K(D_{602}^\ell-D_{601}^\ell)Z_{601}^\ell
\]
together with the transported input (4). This is the required resolvent bridge; a same-row bound on \(D_{601}^\ell\) alone does not control it.

## Quantitative nonlinear remainder

Assume, uniformly for \(t\in Q\) and both lifts, that the physical sheets remain in the established coordinate disks and
\[
\sup |\Phi_t''|\le C_{\rm curv}.
\]
Then
\[
\|N_{600}^\ell\|_{1,n}
 \le \frac{C_{\rm curv}}2
       \|\delta_{600}^\ell\|_{2,n}^{\,2}.
\tag{6}
\]

The available translated resolvent estimate at height \(601\), and the same estimate required at height \(602\), have the form
\[
\max_{i,j}|Z_h^\ell{}_{ij}|
 \le C_Z n^{-\beta},
 \qquad \beta=0.467
\tag{7}
\]
for sufficiently large eligible primes. Since normalized first norm carries a factor \(n\) when multiplied by a maximal-entry bound,
\[
\|Z_h^\ell N_{600}^\ell\|_\infty
 \le
 \frac{C_ZC_{\rm curv}}2\,
 n^{1-\beta}
 \|\delta_{600}^\ell\|_{2,n}^{\,2}.
\tag{8}
\]

More generally, if the frozen-orbit hypothesis is written with
\[
r_{600}:=\sup_{t\in Q,\ell}\|V_{601}^\ell-V_{600}^\ell\|_{2,n},
\]
then
\[
\sup_{t\in Q,\ell}
\|Z_h^\ell N_{600}^\ell\|_\infty
 =O\!\left(n^{1-\beta}r_{600}^{\,2}\right).
\tag{9}
\]
This is the available nonlinear transfer scale. It is conditional on a bound for the actual height-600 increment. The cited frozen-orbit result explicitly notes that the required residual scale is not yet controlled as \(n\to\infty\).

The same issue appears in the height insertion. If
\[
e_{601}^\ell:=D_{602}^\ell-D_{601}^\ell,
\]
then the curvature bound gives only
\[
\|e_{601}^\ell\|_{2,n}
 \le C_{\rm curv}\,
 \|V_{602}^\ell-V_{601}^\ell\|_{2,n},
\tag{10}
\]
and therefore the quantitative resolvent bridge requires an actual bound on the height increment, not merely the derivative estimate at height \(601\).

## Margin

The best retained positive comparison is currently
\[
\|A_{602}A_{601}-B_3^2\|_{P_{5000}}
 <0.66348896,
\]
whereas the required bound is
\[
0.51961239.
\]
The unresolved margin is therefore
\[
0.66348896-0.51961239
 =0.14387657.
\tag{11}
\]

The genuine signed increment would need to produce a certified saving exceeding \(0.14387657\), after adding the nonlinear transfer error from (9), the two-lift discrepancy, the marked-coordinate correction, and the analytic tail. The existing scalar-unit saving, \(>2.9\cdot10^{-7}\), is many orders of magnitude too small and does not address (11).

## Precise failure

No asymptotically sufficient signed estimate has been proved.

The exact obstruction is not the absence of an algebraic identity: (1)–(5) provide the relevant identities. The missing bound is a uniform estimate, over the original prime family, \(t\in Q\), and both lifts, for at least one of
\[
\|Z_{602}^\ell KD_{600}^\ell\delta_{600}^\ell\|_{P_{5000}},
\qquad
\|Z_{602}^\ell N_{600}^\ell\|_{P_{5000}},
\]
with enough signed cancellation to save \(0.14387657\). In particular:

1. the actual size and transported signed profile of \(\delta_{600}^\ell\) and \(\delta_{601}^\ell\) are not bounded in the required physical norm;
2. the remainder estimate \(O(n^{1-\beta}r_{600}^2)\) is conditional because \(r_{600}\) has no verified asymptotic rate;
3. the dependence of both lifts on \(h\) has not been incorporated into a common signed label transport;
4. the positive \(H_h\)-degree comparison deliberately separates the true Picard residuals, so it cannot exploit (1).

Accordingly, the proposed mission is **inconclusive**, not supported or refuted.

## Ranked next tests

### 1. Highest priority: exact signed two-height transport replay

Construct the finite-prefix signed label representation of
\[
Z_{602}^\ell KD_{600}^\ell\delta_{600}^\ell
 +Z_{602}^\ell N_{600}^\ell
\]
for \(\ell=+,-\), retaining the original Jacobi and Legendre labels, physical \(t_0=n\), both lift maps, and the translated \(P_{5000}\)-price. Check whether the resulting lower signed saving exceeds
\[
0.14387657+\varepsilon_{\rm nl},
\]
where \(\varepsilon_{\rm nl}\) is the independently charged nonlinear remainder. This directly tests the only mechanism capable of overcoming the current gap.

### 2. Second priority: certify the actual increment scale

Prove a uniform bound for
\[
r_{600}
 =\sup_{t\in Q,\ell}
 \|V_{601}^\ell(t)-V_{600}^\ell(t)\|_{2,n}
\]
and the analogous \(601\to602\) increment, strong enough that
\[
C_ZC_{\rm curv}n^{1-\beta}r_{600}^{\,2}
\]
is below a specified fraction of \(0.14387657\). If no such rate holds, the proposed nonlinear transfer cannot close the gap even if the signed first-order identity is favorable.
