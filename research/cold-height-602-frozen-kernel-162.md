# A frozen kernel estimate at the genuine height 602

Use the genuine physical predictors on the original closed complex square of [Round 130](cold-combined-height-130.md), with \(t_0=n\) and both lifts. Let \(D_h=\operatorname{diag}\Phi_t'(V_h)\), and keep the translated price \(P_{5000}\). The following estimates hold for all sufficiently large eligible primes, uniformly over that square and both lifts.

## Genuine height transfer

The unshifted first-secant price in [Round 126](cold-actual-first-insertion-126.md#a-second-genuine-outgoing-counting-step), before taking its coordinate consequence, gives actual packet price at most \(1.074(0.011)<0.012\) for \(E=V_{602}-V_{601}\). It has the same base pair of boundary characters. Expand the two endpoints using the decomposition of \(V_{600}\), then add each actual increment. Their dimension budgets are bounded by \(M=4.823\), and both contain the same designated Jacobi seed of squared dimension mass at least 8. At the mark the derivative uses the actual physical radicand, and its discrepancy from the bulk comparison is handled by the exceptional base coordinate after transport.

Put \(N=4(2031/128-2^{-17})\), \(x=M^2/N\), and \(\varepsilon=0.012\). Apply the telescoped odd-word estimate and the common seed-pair saving of [Round 128](cold-actual-quadratic-128.md#first-moment-price-of-the-actual-derivative-difference) to the true \(D_{602}-D_{601}\). The translated multiplier mass and first moment are bounded by
\[
 m_0\le\frac{\varepsilon}{\sqrt N}(1-x)^{-3/2}<0.002988,\qquad
 m_1\le\frac{\varepsilon}{\sqrt N}(1+2x-12/N)(1-x)^{-5/2}<0.007281.
\]
The first uses the product dimensions. In the second, pairing the non-increment factors subtracts at least 8 from the first-moment budget of each common Jacobi-seed pair, precisely as in Round 128. On a translated input the off-base first moment is multiplied by at most \(m_0\); the base cost grows by at most \(m_0\) times its old cost plus \(2m_1\) times its dimension. The physical bad-character and distinct marked derivative corrections contribute \(O_{5000}(n^{-1/2})\) after \(K\), as in the cited estimates. Add the genuine height-601 rate \(<1.048\) to get
\[
 \|KD_{602}\|_{P_{5000}}<1.066.
\]
The actual two endpoint segments of Round 126 have curvature below \(0.248\). The coordinate derivative bound at 601 of Round 128 and \(\|E\|_\infty<0.012\) imply \(\|KD_{602}\|_{2\to2}\le\|D_{602}\|_\infty<0.518\).

For a fixed frozen exponent \(j\), the transported column \(Ke_i\) starts with price \(O(n^{-1/2})\) uniformly in all physical columns, while its counting norm is at most one. Consequently
\[
 \big\|(KD_{602})^jK\big\|_{\max}
 \ll \min\{n^{-1/2}(1.066)^j,\ (0.518)^j}.
\]
Use \(\min(X,Y)\le X^{91/100}Y^{9/100}\). The exact rational inequality \((1.066)^{91}(0.518)^9<1\) yields
\[
 \sum_{j\ge0}\big\|(KD_{602})^jK\big\|_{\max}=O(n^{-0.455}).
\]
The squared scalar comparisons in the mass and moment formulas are rational. The same estimate bounds the frozen resolvent entrywise for the Neumann parameter in the closed unit disk and all its shifted tails. This weaker exponent at 602 is proved using the same actual column classes. This is an entrywise sum and supplies no translated operator bound for the full inverse.

## A translated bound for the Taylor term after its first transport

Write \(R=V_{601}-V_{600}\), \(r=\Phi_t(V_{600}+R)-\Phi_t(V_{600})-D_{600}R\). On the bulk, with \(M'=4.811\) and \(x'=(M')^2/N\), expand
\[
 r=R^2\int_0^1(1-s)(4t)^{-1/2}
            \bigl(1+(V_{600}+sR)^2/(4t)\bigr)^{-3/2}\,ds .
\]
The coefficient magnitudes of the last series are \(b_l=(2l+1)\binom{2l}{l}/4^l\). Every word has \(2l+2\) negative factors, including the two actual \(R\)'s of price at most \(0.011\). Their product before transport has rank bounded by \((2l+2)\) times its product dimension, by the boundary first-moment argument of Round 126. The \(K\) twist kills the punctual term and yields a base negative output with price at most twice that rank. The integral of \(1-s\) cancels this factor two. The bad base characters and replacement of the bulk formula by the actual first and second values at the mark cost \(O(n^{-1/2}(0.011)^2)\) after \(K\), uniformly by the convergent dimension sum. Indeed the actual marked curvature is \(O(n^{-1/2})\) because its radicand is \(4n+z^2\) on bounded \(z\).

Using \(\sum_l(l+1)b_l(x')^l=(1+x'/2)(1-x')^{-5/2}\), the leading translated price of the unshifted input is
\[
 \|Kr\|_{P_{5000}}
 \le \frac{2(0.011)^2}{\sqrt N}(1+x'/2)(1-x')^{-5/2}+O(n^{-1/2})
 <0.000115
\]
eventually. The base class has no off-base price. This bounds the actual Taylor term before the outer frozen inverse; it does not price \((I-KD_h)^{-1}Kr\) in the translated norm, or imply confinement. The different expression \(Z_hKr=(I-KD_h)^{-1}K^2r\) contains the direct coordinate term of [Round 161](cold-picard-increment-correction-161.md). The new frozen entrywise bound controls the smoothed part of this latter expression at height 602 at the scale \(n^{1-0.455}\|r\|_{1,n}\), which needs a separate residual rate for a useful decay.
