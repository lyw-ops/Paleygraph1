## Report

### Checked conclusion

I cannot certify the proposed \(p^{-1/2}\) phase-average upper from the supplied record. The current material establishes only a conditional reduction, not the required prime-family estimate.

The relevant asymptotic remains:

- \(p\to\infty\) through ordinary primes, with \(n=(p-1)/2\);
- fixed floor \(\tau>0\);
- an admissible marked time \(T=T(p)\to\infty\), with \(T+1\) in the simultaneous marked window;
- the physical root and both physical roots in the two-coordinate first-spill formulation deleted, as well as the diagonal;
- normalized expectations \(\mathbb E=n^{-1}\sum_{i\in Q}\);
- a selected **true** optimizer \(F\in U_{-\sigma}\), not an arbitrary point of the relaxed cone and not a supremum over the opposite face;
- only after the \(p\)-limit is obtained is \(\tau\downarrow0\) taken.

For the first reflected deficit \(d_{\rm mid}\), the actual signed cost is
\[
w=-\sigma 1_S K^\circ d_{\rm mid},\qquad
S=\{i\in I:b_i^{(0)}\ge b_*\}.
\]
Under a fixed gap, the cited argument gives, for every true opposite optimum \(F\),
\[
\mathbb E(Fw)\ge g-b_*-o(1),
\]
with the \(o(1)\) uniform in the true face. The comparison with the positive cost \(w_+\) is also valid:
\[
\sup_F\mathbb E\bigl(F(w_+-w)\bigr)
=O_\tau\!\left(T^{-1/12}+T^{-1/6}
+n^{-1/2}T^{-2/3}\right).
\]
Thus it is enough to control one actual face minimum. What is not proved is an upper bound tending to zero for that selected minimum.

### What the ordinary-prime route would need

The Astra review records the strongest currently available arithmetic reduction: after lifting to the full field and separating degeneracies, the relevant weighted quantity is a product of three complete Jacobi sums, with an \(O(p)\) degeneracy contribution. Individual Jacobi bounds give only
\[
O(p^{3/2})
\]
before phase averaging. The desired conclusion requires an additional cancellation of one square-root factor, schematically
\[
\sum_{\text{actual weighted phases}}
J_1(\cdot)J_2(\cdot)J_3(\cdot)
=O(p),
\]
so that division by the natural \(p^{3/2}\) scale gives
\[
O(p^{-1/2}).
\]

That estimate is not present in the record. In particular:

1. The complete-factorization statement does not identify the exact weighted phase sum, its phase variables, or its coefficients on the true selected face.
2. The Weil bound controls each complete Jacobi factor but gives no cancellation between their phases.
3. The filtered-support result applies to exact cone rows and gives directional support exclusion, but it does not give the required \(o(1)\) bound for
   \[
   \min_{F\in\mathcal R_{\rm true}}\mathbb E(Fw)
   \]
   on the full high receiver.
4. Replacing the selected true optimizer by an arbitrary dual-feasible point would not settle the question: the mission specifically requires a true optimum in \(U_{-\sigma}\).

Therefore the proposed ordinary-prime high-face selection is presently **inconclusive**, with a precise missing estimate rather than a refutation.

## Remaining gap

The missing statement should be formulated with the actual face selection and normalization, not as an absolute supremum:

> For ordinary primes \(p\), fixed \(\tau>0\), and an admissible \(T(p)\), construct or select a true \(F_p\in\mathcal R_{\rm true}\subset U_{-\sigma}\) such that
> \[
> \mathbb E\!\left[
> F_p\,(-\sigma 1_S K^\circ d_{\rm mid})
> \right]=o(1),
> \]
> with the limits taken first as \(p\to\infty\), then \(\tau\downarrow0\).

A sufficient arithmetic formulation is a prime-uniform \(O(p)\) bound for the exact weighted three-Jacobi phase sum, including the \(O(p)\) degenerate contribution and all errors induced by the marked-time truncation. No such bound is currently proved.
