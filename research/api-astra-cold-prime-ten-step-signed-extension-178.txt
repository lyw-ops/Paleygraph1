## Result

The supplied estimates support the **fixed ten-step actual prefix**
\[
V_{602},V_{603},\ldots,V_{612}
\]
inside the seed-centered coordinate polydisk of radius \(3/8\), but they do **not** yet prove the required growing-prefix or logarithmic-cutoff statement.

The family is the actual eligible-prime family used in the records, with the original square \(Q\), both lifts, physical marking \(t_0=n\), and the true height-\(602\) center \(V_{602}\). No frozen or artificially shifted orbit is being substituted.

### 1. Tube contraction

On each coordinate disk
\[
|z_i-V_{602,i}|\le \frac38,
\]
the stated scalar estimates give
\[
\|KD\|_{2\to2}\le q=0.545
\]
in the counting second norm. The marked coordinate is included with \(t_0=n\); its physical radicand has the same positive-sheet margin. The numerical cutoff check is valid:
\[
0.545^8=0.00778\ldots<2^{-7}=0.0078125.
\]

Thus the radius-\(3/8\) tube is a valid contractive physical tube for the actual map, provided the actual orbit remains in it.

### 2. Ten actual displacements

Let
\[
U_j=V_{602+j}-V_{602}.
\]
Using the unshifted genuine base-pair price \(P_{5000}\), the actual outgoing source satisfies
\[
P_{5000}(U_1)<0.01272.
\]
For every subsequent step whose input remains in the radius-\(3/8\) tube, the retained nonlinear power gives
\[
P_{5000}(U_{j+1})
 \le 0.01272+1.066P_{5000}(U_j)
       +1.22P_{5000}(U_j)^2.
\tag{1}
\]

The rounded-up values from (1), in units of \(10^{-5}\), are
\[
1272,\ 2648,\ 4181,\ 5943,\ 8039,\ 10631,\ 13984,\ 18565,\ 25268,\ 35998.
\]
Since \(P_{5000}\) dominates coordinate supremum on these packets,
\[
\|U_{10}\|_\infty\le P_{5000}(U_{10})<0.35998<0.375.
\]
Consequently all ten actual displacements, and hence the actual rows through \(V_{612}\), remain in the radius-\(3/8\) tube. The endpoint has explicit fixed-depth clearance
\[
\frac38-0.35998=0.01502.
\]

This is a valid finite-prime-family asymptotic statement: for each of the ten fixed steps, the vanishing bad-character and physical-mark corrections are absorbed for all sufficiently large eligible \(n\). It is not a uniform statement for \(j\) growing with \(n\).

### 3. What is not proved

The ten-step estimate does **not** imply the requested growing actual prefix through
\[
602+\left\lceil \frac47\log_2 n\right\rceil.
\]
The reason is that the recurrence has no uniform continuation margin: at the terminal certified value \(0.35998\), one more application of (1) is already much larger than \(3/8\). Thus the finite sequence cannot be iterated to logarithmic depth by the displayed unsigned recurrence.

Likewise, the claimed sufficient bridge
\[
\|R_{611}\|_{2,\mathrm{unnorm}}
 <0.0556556
 =\left(\frac38-0.25268\right)(1-0.545)
\tag{2}
\]
has not been derived. The ten-step bound gives \(P_{5000}(U_{10})<0.35998\), not the unnormalized counting-second-norm estimate (2). A conversion from the packet price to (2) is not automatic and would in any event require a signed residual/source estimate, not merely the unsigned displacement bound.

The obstruction in the full coordinate norm,
\[
\|KD_{602}\|_{\infty\to\infty}\asymp\sqrt n,
\]
does not refute a signed propagation argument in the counting norm. It only rules out proving the continuation by full coordinate-supremum contraction. Derivative transfer also remains separate.

## Conclusion

**Supported conclusion:** the true actual prefix through \(V_{612}\) fits in the exact seed-centered radius-\(3/8\) disk with fixed terminal clearance \(0.01502\), uniformly for sufficiently large eligible primes, under the stated genuine-source and nonlinear estimates.

**Unresolved conclusion:** the logarithmically growing actual prefix, or the alternative unnormalized \(R_{611}\) bridge with fixed slack, is not proved. The order of limits remains:

1. fix the ten heights \(602\le m\le612\);
2. take sufficiently large eligible \(n\);
3. only afterward seek a depth \(j=j(n)\asymp\log n\).

The existing argument does not justify interchanging steps 1–2 with a depth growing in \(n\).

## Ranked next tests

1. **Direct signed \(R_{611}\) test — highest priority.**  
   In the unnormalized counting second norm, compute/prove
   \[
   \|R_{611}\|_{2,\mathrm{unnorm}}
   \le 0.0556556-\delta
   \]
   for some fixed \(\delta>0\), retaining the physical mark \(t_0=n\) and both lifts. This immediately supplies the stated first-exit bridge once the \(q=0.545\) tube estimate is accepted.

2. **Signed partial-sum propagation test.**  
   For
   \[
   B=KD_{602},\qquad
   L_j=\sum_{\ell<j}B^\ell K,
   \]
   establish a signed price bound with fixed clearance through the required logarithmic cutoff, rather than summing absolute packet prices. The target is a bound on the signed aggregate source
   \[
   S_k=\sum_{j<k}B^jR_{602}
   \]
   staying below \(3/8-\eta\), together with the shifted-resolvent bound needed for the nonlinear remainder. This is the route that could prove the genuinely growing actual prefix without relying on (2).
