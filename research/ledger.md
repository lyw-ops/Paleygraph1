# Research ledger

## Target

Prove or refute the ordinary theta asymptotic $\vartheta(\overline{H_p})/\sqrt{(p-1)/2}\to1$ through all primes $p\equiv1\pmod4$, where $H_p$ is the subgraph induced on the nonzero squares. [ETH statement](https://randomstrasse101.math.ethz.ch/posts/PaleyGraph/) · [Public attempt record](https://jig.so/p/7) · [Open-problem index](https://clopen.solutions/OP-00041/)
