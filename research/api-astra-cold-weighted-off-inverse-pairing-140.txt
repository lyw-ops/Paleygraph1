## Result: inconclusive; the required strict-margin estimate is not established

I cannot certify that the two signed rational pullbacks price the off-inverse part below either target. The available identities give exact cancellation and pointwise/entrywise square-root bounds, but they do not give the required translated physical norm bound for the actual weighted packets.

The statement concerns the fixed actual family

- primes \(p\) in the previously specified eligible-prime family, with \(p\to\infty\);
- \(G=\mathbb F_p^{\times 2}\), \(n=|G|\);
- height \(h=601\) and the pure Jacobi seed;
- the original closed square;
- the actual marked rows, including \(t_0=n\), both boundary pairs, and the actual trace lifts;
- the physical translated packet norm \(P_{5000}\), with the exceptional-lift and marked corrections retained.

The desired eventual bounds are

\[
\|{\cal S}_{\mathrm{off}}\|_{P_{5000}}<0.703184
\]

for the square difference, or

\[
\|{\cal T}_{\mathrm{off}}\|_{P_{5000}}<0.268468
\]

for the anticommutator, with a separate inverse-line contribution and a strict residual margin.

### 1. Exact signed pairing

For \(c=xy\neq1\), the actual square-difference kernel is

\[
{\cal S}(x,y)=
\frac{s(x)}2\sum_{z\in G}
\bigl(s(z)+s(c/z)\bigr)
\bigl(d(x/z)d(z/y)-j(x/z)j(z/y)\bigr).
\]

For the anticommutator it is

\[
{\cal T}(x,y)=
s(x)\sum_{z\in G}
\bigl(s(z)+s(c/z)\bigr)e(x/z)j(z/y).
\]

Thus the opposite-sign intermediate positions cancel exactly. On \(c=1\), however, the two signs agree for every \(z\in G\), so this mechanism supplies no saving. That inverse line must remain separate.

The exact rational parametrization is

\[
z_c(r)=\frac{r^2-c}{r^2-1},
\]

and, for a weight \(F\),

\[
\begin{aligned}
\sum_{z\in G}(s(z)+s(c/z))F(z)
={}&s(c)F(1)\\
&+\frac{s(c)}2
\sum_{\substack{r\in\mathbb F_p\\r^2\notin\{1,c\}}}
\bigl(\chi(r^2-1)+\chi(r^2-c)\bigr)
F(z_c(r)).
\end{aligned}
\]

This identity is applicable to the actual weights

\[
F(z)=d(x/z)d(z/y)-j(x/z)j(z/y)
\]

and

\[
F(z)=e(x/z)j(z/y).
\]

Consequently, the off-inverse contribution is exactly the sum of two signed quadratic pullbacks of the actual convolution weights.

### 2. What is proved

The existing evidence establishes the following.

1. **Exact half-mask cancellation.**  
   For \(c\neq1\), the nonzero sign mask has approximately half the positions, with exact squared norm

   \[
   2n-4-a_p(c),
   \]

   where \(a_p(c)\) is the Legendre trace. This gives uniform \(n/2+O(\sqrt p)\) support size.

2. **Entrywise arithmetic control.**  
   The actual derivative rows admit trace expansions

   \[
   d_i(a)=\sum_u c_u t_u(a)+e_i(a),
   \qquad
   \sum_u |c_u|r_u=O(1),
   \qquad
   \|e_i\|_\infty=O(p^{-1}).
   \]

   For individual trace pairs, the intermediate \(z\)-sum has a nontrivial quadratic inertia and is \(O(r_ur_v/\sqrt p)\). This gives the previously recorded uniform position-kernel estimate

   \[
   |(KD_1KD_2)(x,y)|\ll p^{-1/2}
   \]

   for \(y\neq1\).

3. **Inverse-line counting control.**  
   The \(xy=1\) part has counting norm \(O(p^{-1/2})\), because it is supported on one inverse-line entry per row. This does not imply a small \(P_{5000}\)-price: on translated packets the inverse-line operator is a position multiplier, and its physical cost has not been bounded.

4. **Actual marking and height data.**  
   The height-transfer argument gives the actual \(601\to602\) cost \(<0.017\) in \(P_{5000}\), with the same marking. The resummed remainder estimate gives, separately,

   \[
   \|KE_h\|_{P_{5000}}<0.666,
   \]

   but that is a positive comparison for the remainder and is not an estimate for the present signed off-inverse transport. The positive comparison is known to have a rate exceeding \(0.570\), so repricing alone cannot supply the missing signed improvement.

### 3. Precise remaining gap

The missing assertion would have to be a uniform translated-packet estimate of the form

\[
\left\|
\frac{s(x)}2
\sum_{r}
\bigl(\chi(r^2-1)+\chi(r^2-c)\bigr)
F_{x,y}(z_c(r))
\right\|_{P_{5000},\,c\neq1}
\leq \rho_{\mathrm{off}}+o(1),
\]

where \(F_{x,y}\) is one of the two actual weighted products above, and where \(\rho_{\mathrm{off}}\) is small enough that, after the separately priced inverse line and marking errors, the total is below \(0.703184\) or \(0.268468\).

No such estimate is currently available. In particular:

- the half-mask count does not control the correlation of the mask with \(F_{x,y}\);
- pointwise Weil bounds do not control the translated packet coefficients;
- the sheaf estimate for each fixed trace pair is an entrywise \(O(p^{-1/2})\) statement, not a bound after the physical packet supremum and the actual marked trace-lift summation;
- the inverse-line counting estimate cannot be substituted for a physical inverse-line price;
- the positive resummed comparison deliberately discards the signed identifications needed here.

The **first weighted approximation that fails** is therefore the replacement

\[
\sum_z (s(z)+s(c/z))F_{x,y}(z)
\quad\leadsto\quad
\text{“half the unsigned mass of }F_{x,y}\text{.”}
\]

This replacement is valid for support counting but not for the physical weighted norm: the actual pullbacks \(F_{x,y}(z_c(r))\) may correlate with either quadratic branch. No strict numerical slack can be claimed from the present records.

## Conclusion

The mission is **inconclusive**, not refuted. Exact signed cancellation off the inverse line is proved, and the actual trace expansions and entrywise cohomological bounds are compatible with a strict physical saving. But the decisive weighted rational-pullback estimate, including the actual marked rows and the separate inverse-line price, has not been proved. Hence neither the square threshold \(0.703184\) nor the anticommutator threshold \(0.268468\) is currently certified.
