## Result

I do not obtain the required contraction estimate. The available records establish the two-center estimate, but they do not provide a signed price for the remaining actual centers and Picard residual.

Let \(\mathcal P\) denote the same sufficiently large eligible-prime family used in the cited records. For each \(n\) along \(\mathcal P\), use the original closed marked square, the exact physical marking \(t_0=n\), both boundary lifts, and the \(P_{5000}\) norm. Fix \(h=601,602\), and write
\[
A_h=K\,\operatorname{diag}\bigl(d_{t_i}(V_{h,i})\bigr),
\]
with
\[
B_2=K\,\operatorname{diag}\bigl(d_{t_i}(S_i+\alpha Y_i)\bigr).
\]

The accepted two-center result gives, eventually along \(\mathcal P\),
\[
\|B_2\|_{P_{5000}}<0.644,\qquad
\|B_2^2\|_{P_{5000}}<0.415.
\]

It also gives
\[
\|A_h-B\|_{P_{5000}}<0.550,
\qquad
\|B_2-B\|_{P_{5000}}<0.0092,
\]
where \(B\) is the Jacobi-plus-linear-Legendre operator. Therefore the only currently justified triangle estimate is
\[
\boxed{\|A_h-B_2\|_{P_{5000}}<0.5592.}
\]

This does not meet the required \(0.356\) one-step error.

## Consequence for the paired product

Using
\[
A_{602}A_{601}-B_2^2
=(A_{602}-B_2)A_{601}
+B_2(A_{601}-B_2)
\]
and the established full-row bound \(\|A_{601}\|_{P_{5000}}<1.048\), one gets only
\[
\begin{aligned}
\|A_{602}A_{601}-B_2^2\|_{P_{5000}}
&<0.5592(1.048)+0.644(0.5592)\\
&=0.9461664.
\end{aligned}
\]
This is substantially larger than the required \(0.585\).

The same-row reduction does not close the gap either. The accepted height-transfer estimate
\[
\|A_{602}-A_{601}\|_{P_{5000}}<0.017
\]
would reduce the paired target to the stated same-row requirement
\[
\|A_{601}^2-B_2^2\|_{P_{5000}}<0.585-0.017\cdot1.048
=0.567184.
\]
But the available triangle estimate gives only
\[
\|A_{601}^2-B_2^2\|_{P_{5000}}
\le
\|A_{601}-B_2\|(\|A_{601}\|+\|B_2\|)
<0.5592(1.048+0.644)
=0.9461664.
\]

Thus neither the \(0.356\) step criterion nor the \(0.585\) paired criterion is proved.

## What is genuinely established

The pure Legendre contribution is not the obstruction. The exact \(B_2\) representation includes all Legendre powers in the two-center subrow, and the bound
\[
\|B_2-B\|_{P_{5000}}<0.0092
\]
is already available with the correct physical marking and both lifts.

The unresolved part is
\[
A_h-B_2,
\]
which contains the other actual centers and the actual Picard residual. Existing bounds such as
\[
\|O_h\|<0.408
\]
and the positive prices from the resummed remainder are absolute envelopes. They cannot be converted into a signed lower or cancellation estimate. In particular, the positive-envelope obstructions from the earlier rounds do not refute \(A_h\) or \(A_h-B_2\), because they discard the signs and identifications of the actual finite-field operator.

The previously separated first-order bounds also do not close the requested budget: the center and residual estimates are positive comparisons, while the needed improvement requires either an actual signed collection or a marked exact representation in which every pure-Legendre nonseed word is removed before taking absolute values.

All conclusions here are eventual statements along the fixed eligible-prime family, with the physical floor \(t_0=n\) and the original square. No claim is made for arbitrary primes, and no limit in \(h\) is being taken. Nonlinear invariance and the physical half-step remain separate obligations even if the derivative comparison is later closed.

## Precise failure

The missing assertion is one of the following:

\[
\|A_h-B_2\|_{P_{5000}}<0.356
\quad(h=601,602),
\]
or
\[
\|A_{602}A_{601}-B_2^2\|_{P_{5000}}<0.585,
\]
or, after using the height-transfer debit,
\[
\|A_{601}^2-B_2^2\|_{P_{5000}}<0.567184.
\]

None follows from the currently recorded positive prices. The numerical gap is not a rounding issue: the triangle bound is \(0.5592\), leaving at least \(0.2032\) of excess in the one-step criterion, and the corresponding square estimate is \(0.9461664\).
