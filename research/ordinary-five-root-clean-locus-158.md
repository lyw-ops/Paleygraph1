# Clean five-root locus and dense-edge corrections

Continue the notation and the actual weights of [Round 157](ordinary-five-root-trivial-slices-157.md). The three remaining boundary monodromy resonances are \(\beta C\chi=1,\ AD\chi=\alpha=1,\ (\beta A C D)^{-1}\chi=1\); the last is \(V=V'\). On \(\beta C\chi=1\), substitute \(z=y t\) and sum in \(y\ne0\). With \(w=t(y+1)/(t-1)\) on the complete portion, the exact expression is
\[
 \mathscr K=D(-1)J(D,A)J(\beta/D,\alpha)-J(\beta,\chi).
\]
If one of the two product factors is principal, there are three independent equalities among the five original weights. For \(J(D,A)\), \(D=A=1\) and \(\beta C\chi=1\) fix \(T,U',V'\) in terms of \(U,V\). For \(J(\beta/D,\alpha)\), \(\beta=D,\alpha=1\) and the same resonance fix \(T,U',V'\). The outer principal event is impossible on this slice because \(L=(\beta C)^{-1}=\chi\).

Reflect both coordinates around \(-1/2\) to exchange the first dense edge with \(AD\chi=1\). The two possible principal pairs in the displayed product impose, in the original variables, respectively \(U=U'=V=V'\), or \(U=U', V'=V\chi, T=V\chi^q\). Outer principal now has \(i=0\) and is the two constraints \(U=U',V=V'\). Invert both coordinates for the infinity edge \(V=V'\). Then the corresponding two possible product-principal sets are \(V=V',\,U'=U\chi,\,T=U\chi^{q+1}\), or \(U=U'=V=V'\). Outer principal again requires \(i=0\) and two constraints. Thus on each edge the generic contribution is \(O(p\sqrt p\,\kappa)=O(p)\). The outer-principal part with kernel \(O(p)\) costs \(O(p^2\kappa^2)=O(p)\), and any principal product factor with kernel \(O(p^{3/2})\) costs \(O(p^{5/2}\kappa^3)=O(p)\). The earlier six root slices and these three edges are jointly removable.

Outside them \(U_0=(\mathbb P^1\setminus\{0,-1,\infty\})^2\setminus\Delta\) is the affine surface supporting the rank-one tame Kummer sheaf of the kernel. Blow up \((0,0),(-1,-1),(\infty,\infty)\) in \(\mathbb P^1\times\mathbb P^1\) to get a smooth simple-normal-crossing compactification. The six vertical and horizontal inertias are the finite-root and infinity characters, the diagonal is \(\chi\), and the exceptional three are the dense-edge products. Every boundary inertia is nontrivial on the clean locus. The tame normal-crossing local cohomology has zero boundary stalks, so ordinary and compactly supported cohomology coincide. Affine vanishing and duality concentrate them in degree two. Tame Euler characteristic equality gives dimension \((-1)^2-(-1)=2\); apply the proof of [Sawin–Forey–Fresán–Kowalski, Proposition 7.9](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2101.00635) to this smooth tame compactification. Deligne weights give \(|\mathscr K|\le2p\). In fact both degree-two eigenvalues have modulus \(p\). The inverse-character sheaf is also clean, so the duality pairing is between these two compactly supported groups with target \(\overline{\mathbb Q}_\ell(-2)\). The weight upper on the dual gives the reverse modulus inequality. For outer principal after this cleanup use probability \(O(\kappa^2)\); other outer degeneracy factors are bounded by one. In the assembled favorable sum, the last sign lift \(s=0\) remains the three-Jacobi average of Round 153; the \(s=1\) part is now a normalized rank-two trace times a regular Jacobi phase, with all removed terms \(O(p)\). The pin relation at the end of Round 157 compares the two assembled terms up to \(O(p)\). An \(O(p^{-1/2})\) signed weighted upper on their common contribution after normalization by \(p^{3/2}\) is still open.
