## Result: a genuine \(F_4\) coincidence exists, but no certified budget-closing gain follows

I retain the stated arithmetic family: sufficiently large eligible primes
\[
p\equiv1\pmod 4,\qquad n=(p-1)/2,
\]
with the separately available prime-square members treated only when their corresponding finite-field replay is invoked. I retain the original physical marking
\[
t_0=n,\qquad t_i=t\ (i\ne0),
\]
both physical lifts, and the complete translated \(P_{5000}\) norm. All estimates below are intended uniformly on the nominated closed square, with the finite Taylor/arithmetic collection performed first and \(n\to\infty\) taken afterward.

### 1. The concrete genuine label

The natural choice is the rank-four arithmetic label \(F_4\). The exact arithmetic identity is
\[
K(C_1W)
 =
F_4-\frac{n(1+q+h_q)}{2q^2}\,K e_0,
\]
where \(C_1\) and \(W\) are the actual lift fields and \(h_q\) is the rank-three punctual convolution term. Equivalently,
\[
F_4=K(C_1W)+
\frac{n(1+q+h_q)}{2q^2}\,K e_0. \tag{1}
\]

This is an actual label identity, not merely equality of positive weight measures. It is valid on the square-restricted arithmetic family, including the bad compact characters and the two physical lifts. The correction coefficient is \(O(1)\), since \(|h_q|\le 2q\), but its translated \(P_{5000}\)-price is \(O(n^{-1/2})\) because it is supported on the punctual column \(K e_0\).

The weighted \(F_4\) fiber measure is explicitly
\[
D_{F_4}
 =\frac74\delta_0
 +\frac12(\delta_{-1}+\delta_1)
 +\frac12(\delta_{-2}+\delta_2)
 +\frac18(\delta_{-4}+\delta_4). \tag{2}
\]
Thus \(F_4\) is not a new disjoint positive packet: after transport it overlaps the \(C_1W\) packet, up to the punctual correction in (1).

### 2. The relevant two-vertex transport

At the two actual vertices \(t=601,602\), write
\[
A_h=K\,\mathrm{diag}\bigl(d_{t_i}(V_{h,i})\bigr),
\qquad
V_h=S+\alpha Y+\beta W+\gamma F_4+H_h,
\]
where \(H_h\) contains the other fixed centers and the actual Picard residual. The physical \(t_0=n\) row is retained in both vertices.

The exact two-height decomposition remains
\[
A_{602}A_{601}-B_3^2
 =
E_{602}A_{601}+B_3E_{601},
\qquad E_h=A_h-B_3.
\]
The positive comparison gives only
\[
\|A_{602}A_{601}-B_3^2\|_{P_{5000}}<0.66381,
\]
while the targets are \(0.51961239\) for the direct route and \(0.50179639\) for the same-row route with transfer.

The \(F_4\)-dependent square contribution is contained in the genuinely signed \(W\)-linear return
\[
JM+MJ,
\]
with
\[
J=K\operatorname{diag}(d_t(S)),\qquad
M=K\operatorname{diag}(\beta W\,d_t'(S)).
\]
The exact exposed tuples include
\[
\beta a_1\Bigl(b_0+\frac{b_2}{2}\Bigr)K(C_1F_4)
\]
and
\[
\frac{\beta b_0(a_1+a_3)}2K(WY).
\]
The first is the requested actual \(F_4\) contribution. Substituting (1) into it gives
\[
K(C_1F_4)
 =
K\!\left(C_1K(C_1W)\right)
+
\frac{n(1+q+h_q)}{2q^2}K(C_1K e_0). \tag{3}
\]
The first term in (3) lies in the same transported arithmetic class as the \(C_1W\) return. The second is a marked/punctual correction and must be retained, not discarded; its coefficient is \(O(1)\), while its translated price is \(O(n^{-1/2})\).

The existing all-order replay proves that, after collecting the signed arithmetic tuples and excluding the two exposed tuples, the canonical translated price is below
\[
0.00768,
\]
with relative modulus bounds \(0.837\) and \(0.863\) for the two exposed whole-order coefficients. It also proves the complete absolute estimate
\[
\|JM+MJ\|_{P_{5000}}<0.05956,
\]
and a \(C_1\)-column price below \(0.04938\).

### 3. What is actually established

The established arithmetic fact is therefore:

> \(F_4\) has a real transported overlap with the \(C_1W\) class at both vertices, with an explicit punctual correction and an \(O(n^{-1/2})\) physical-column repair.

This refutes the possibility that all fixed other-center labels are arithmetically disjoint from the \(W\)-return class.

However, it does **not** yet establish a signed gain beyond the positive two-height composition. The current replay still takes separate moduli at the point where the coefficient of the \(F_4\) contribution must be combined with the coefficient of the matching \(C_1W\) contribution. In particular, it does not certify that the combined coefficient has smaller modulus than the sum of the two moduli after:

1. the \(K\)-transport at the inner vertex;
2. the second \(K\)-transport at the outer vertex;
3. the two lift averages;
4. the \(t_0=n\) marked row;
5. the punctual correction in (1);
6. the complementary \(F_4\), Picard, and Taylor-tail terms.

Consequently there is presently no checked inequality of the form
\[
\|A_{602}A_{601}-B_3^2\|_{P_{5000}}<0.51961239
\]
or
\[
\|A_{601}^2-B_3^2\|_{P_{5000}}<0.50179639.
\]

Nor does the existing \(0.05956\) bound imply such an improvement: it is an absolute norm estimate and does not retain the relative phase between the matched \(F_4\) and \(C_1W\) coefficients.

### Precise remaining gap

The missing statement is a **two-vertex, signature-by-signature signed collection**. For every matched transported signature generated by
\[
K(C_1F_4)
\quad\text{and}\quad
K(C_1K(C_1W)),
\]
one must compute the actual complex coefficient, including the punctual term from (1), before taking modulus. The complementary translated packet must then be priced positively in the full \(P_{5000}\) norm.

Until that calculation is done, the \(F_4\) coincidence is evidence for a possible gain, not a certified gain. The nonlinear invariant-ball and half-step arguments remain open as well.
