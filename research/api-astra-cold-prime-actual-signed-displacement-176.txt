## Result: the signed-propagation obligation is presently unproved

For the actual prime family \(p=2n+1\) (eligible primes, \(n\to\infty\)), with physical mark \(t_0=n\), both lifts, and the original complex square \(Q\), the supplied estimates verify only a finite unsigned prefix. They do **not** prove summably small actual signed increments through the growing cutoff.

Let
\[
F_t(W)=K\Phi_t(W),\qquad B_h=K D_h,\qquad
D_h=\operatorname{diag}\Phi_t'(V_h),
\]
and use \(P_{5000}\) for the unshifted packet price. At the true height \(602\), the verified estimates are
\[
P_{5000}(R_{602})<0.01272,
\]
and, for \(U_j=V_{602+j}-V_{602}\),
\[
P_{5000}(U_{j+1})
 \le P_{5000}(R_{602})
 +1.066\,P_{5000}(U_j)
 +P_{5000}(U_j)^2
\]
for the fixed steps covered by the finite-tube argument. Consequently,
\[
P_{5000}(U_1)<0.01272,\quad
P_{5000}(U_2)<0.02645,\quad
P_{5000}(U_3)<0.04162.
\]
Thus \(V_{603},V_{604},V_{605}\) are inside the radius-\(0.05\) coordinate tube around \(V_{602}\), with the stated finite clearance.

However, applying the same unsigned recurrence once more gives only
\[
\begin{aligned}
P_{5000}(U_4)
&\le 0.01272+1.066(0.04162)+(0.04162)^2\\
&<0.0589,
\end{aligned}
\]
which exceeds \(0.05\). This is a concrete failure of the available unsigned sufficient bound, not a refutation of the actual signed orbit.

### Why the fixed-height contraction does not close the gap

On the radius-\(0.05\) tube around \(V_{602}\), the scalar derivative is bounded by
\[
q=0.535<1
\]
in the normalized counting/second norm. This gives
\[
\|R_{602+j}\|_{2,n}\le q^j\|R_{602}\|_{2,n}
\]
provided the orbit has already remained in the tube. It does not by itself give coordinate confinement, because
\[
\|x\|_\infty\le \sqrt n\,\|x\|_{2,n}.
\]
At the growing cutoff
\[
k_n=\left\lceil \frac47\log_2 n\right\rceil,
\]
the \(q^{k_n}\) decay is useful only after a signed transport or partial-sum estimate removes the potentially fatal \(\sqrt n\) conversion. The full coordinate operator cannot supply this: at the actual physical height \(602\),
\[
\|K D_{602}\|_{\infty\to\infty}\asymp\sqrt n,
\]
uniformly on the original square for sufficiently large eligible primes.

Therefore the required missing input is genuinely signed. In the notation of the frozen-orbit argument, one needs bounds of the form
\[
L_j=\sum_{\ell<j}B_{602}^{\ell}K,
\qquad
S_k=\sum_{j<k}B_{602}^{j}R_{602},
\]
with coordinate clearance through the relevant logarithmic cutoffs. Absolute entrywise summation or the packet price \(P_{5000}\) does not establish these bounds.

## Status of the \(V_{605}\) source test

The separate counting test at \(V_{605}\) remains completely open from the supplied evidence. In particular, no estimate has been supplied for the required **unnormalized counting norm**
\[
\|\text{source at }V_{605}\|_{\mathrm{count}}
<0.0038967
\]
with fixed slack.

This cannot be inferred from the normalized second norm, from the three verified \(P_{5000}\)-prices, or from a signed rate below one. The norms have different dimension factors, and the full \(\ell^\infty\) derivative obstruction rules out the most direct conversion.

## What is proved and what remains

**Proved, uniformly for sufficiently large eligible primes \(p=2n+1\):**

1. The actual physical rows \(V_{602},\ldots,V_{605}\) remain in the radius-\(0.05\) tube around \(V_{602}\).
2. The fixed-height scalar map has counting-norm contraction factor \(q=0.535\) on that tube.
3. The first three actual displacements have the stated strict packet-price bounds.
4. The frozen \(V_{602}\) estimates and entrywise resolvent bounds do not imply a translated full inverse or signed orbit confinement.

**Not proved:**

1. Signed partial-sum control through the growing cutoff.
2. A uniform coordinate bound on the total signed displacement.
3. The \(V_{605}\) unnormalized source threshold \(0.0038967\).
4. Any derivative-transfer statement; that remains a separate task.

The order of limits also matters: the finite three-step estimates are uniform as \(n\to\infty\), whereas the desired continuation requires a prefix whose length grows like \(\log n\). A fixed-depth check therefore cannot settle the asymptotic target.

## Ranked next tests

### 1. Direct \(V_{605}\) unnormalized-source test — highest priority

For the actual \(V_{605}\), with \(t_0=n\), both lifts, and the original square \(Q\), compute or interval-bound the designated signed source in the **unnormalized counting norm**. Prove
\[
\sup_{Q,\ \text{both lifts}}
\|\text{source at }V_{605}\|_{\mathrm{count}}
\le 0.0038967-\delta
\]
for some fixed \(\delta>0\), uniformly for all sufficiently large eligible primes.

This is the explicitly identified gateway quantity and is the shortest possible route to an unconditional bridge if the associated signed transport estimate is available.

### 2. Signed partial sums at the true fixed base

At \(V_{602}\) or \(V_{605}\), evaluate with interval control
\[
L_j=\sum_{\ell<j}(K D_h)^\ell K
\]
and
\[
S_k=\sum_{j<k}(K D_h)^jR_h
\]
through the two logarithmic cutoffs required by the signed propagation lemma. Establish, with fixed \(\eta>0\),
\[
\sup_{k\le c\log n}\|S_k\|_\infty
\le 0.05-\eta,
\]
and a corresponding signed bound on \(B^jZ\) or \(L_j\).

This directly tests the actual cancellation left open by the \(\ell^\infty\)-derivative obstruction. Failure would identify an obstruction specific to the signed proposal; success would provide the needed finite-to-growing transport.
