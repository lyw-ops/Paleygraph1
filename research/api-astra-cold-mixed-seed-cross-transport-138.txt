## Result

No new certified signed cancellation is established from the supplied records. The exact joint algebra is

\[
A_h:=KD_h,\qquad J:=KD^{\mathrm J},\qquad R_h:=A_h-J=K(D_h-D^{\mathrm J}),
\]

and therefore

\[
\boxed{R_{602}J+JR_{601}
=K E_{602}K D^{\mathrm J}+K D^{\mathrm J}K E_{601}},
\qquad E_h:=D_h-D^{\mathrm J}.
\]

Equivalently,

\[
R_{602}J+JR_{601}
=A_{602}J+JA_{601}-2J^2,
\]

and

\[
A_{602}A_{601}-J^2
=R_{602}R_{601}+\bigl(R_{602}J+JR_{601}\bigr).
\]

Thus the proposed reduction is correct: the already available remainder-product price

\[
\|R_{602}R_{601}\|_{P_{5000}}<0.443556
\]

would imply the full two-step bound \(<0.721\) if one could prove the **joint signed physical price**

\[
\boxed{\|R_{602}J+JR_{601}\|_{P_{5000}}<0.277444.}
\]

No such bound is presently proved.

## Actual setting and hypotheses

The scope remains the original closed cold square

\[
Q=\left\{z:\left|z-\frac{2031}{128}\right|\le 2^{-17}\right\},
\]

in the physical translated price \(P_{5000}\), for the actual fully marked rows \(h=601,602\). The prime family is the eligible sufficiently large primes

\[
p\equiv1\pmod 4,\qquad n=\frac{p-1}{2},
\]

with the physical marking

\[
t_0=n,\qquad t_i=t\quad(i\ne0),
\]

and with both translated boundary pairs retained. The operators are the genuine finite-field operators, not independent positive envelopes.

The established pure-seed and remainder prices are, eventually and uniformly on \(Q\),

\[
\|J\|_{P_{5000}}<0.528,
\]

\[
\|R_h\|_{P_{5000}}<0.666,\qquad h=601,602.
\]

These are separate physical prices after retaining the actual \(K\), both marked fibers, and the exceptional-lift corrections.

## What the current bounds actually give

If the two cross terms are priced separately, then

\[
\|R_{602}J+JR_{601}\|_{P_{5000}}
\le
\|R_{602}\|\,\|J\|+\|J\|\,\|R_{601}\|
<2(0.666)(0.528)=0.703296.
\]

This is strictly above \(0.277444\). Combining it with the remainder product gives only

\[
\|A_{602}A_{601}-J^2\|_{P_{5000}}
<0.443556+0.703296
=1.146852,
\]

which is not useful for the \(0.721\) target.

The earlier \(1.049616\) estimate is also valid but concerns the differently decomposed expression

\[
R_{602}A_{601}+JR_{601},
\]

where the first factor is charged using the full \(A_{601}\) rate \(<1.048\). It does not price the desired mixed pair.

## Structural simplification and cancellation test

Because \(D^{\mathrm J}\), \(E_{601}\), and \(E_{602}\) are diagonal,

\[
R_{602}J+JR_{601}
=
K\Bigl(E_{602}KD^{\mathrm J}+D^{\mathrm J}KE_{601}\Bigr).
\]

This is the useful form for a joint transport calculation: the intermediate physical \(K\) is preserved, and the two terms should be collected before taking absolute values.

However, the supplied material does not establish any of the identities that would force cancellation, such as

\[
E_{602}KD^{\mathrm J}\approx -D^{\mathrm J}KE_{601},
\]

nor an exact row-exchange or centered-row involution identifying the two diagonal fields with opposite signs. The facts that \(K\) is symmetric and \(D_h,D^{\mathrm J}\) are diagonal only give

\[
(K E_{602}K D^{\mathrm J})^{*}
=D^{\mathrm J}K E_{602}K,
\]

which is not the second term \(K D^{\mathrm J}K E_{601}\). Thus symmetry of \(K\) alone does not produce antisymmetry of the mixed pair.

Likewise, the existing Laurent resummations collect powers of the Jacobi seed \(S\) or repeated seed factors inside a **single** nonseed word. They do not identify the nonseed fields from rows \(601\) and \(602\). Numerical coincidence of boundary weights is insufficient: the corresponding Mellin objects and physical lifts must be equal, or must be related by a proved signed transport identity.

## Evidence supporting the possibility of a new estimate

There is one genuine opening. The existing positive comparisons discard cancellation between nonseed objects, and their obstructions therefore do not refute the signed target. In particular:

- the positive remainder comparison already has a lower witness above the \(0.472\) one-step budget, but this is only a lower obstruction for that unsigned comparison;
- the positive two-step derivative comparison has an expanding finite witness, but again only for the chosen positive majorant;
- the pure-seed Laurent coefficients are already collected with signs before modulus, showing that analogous collection is technically possible when the underlying field word is actually the same.

The missing step is therefore a **cross-row signed identity**, not a better choice of the existing positive price.

## Remaining gap

The current record proves neither:

1. a strict physical bound
   \[
   \|R_{602}J+JR_{601}\|_{P_{5000}}<0.277444,
   \]
   nor

2. directly,
   \[
   \|A_{602}A_{601}-J^2\|_{P_{5000}}<0.721.
   \]

It also does not prove the required half-step and invariant-ball margins. The available counting estimates,

\[
\|KD^{\mathrm J}\|_{2\to2}<0.335,\qquad
\|KE_h\|_{2\to2}<0.275,\qquad
\|KD_{601}\|_{2\to2}<0.5145,
\]

give useful Hilbert-space contraction information but do not imply a physical \(P_{5000}\)-packet bound. In particular, they do not provide growing-depth confinement.

The limit order must remain explicit: the quoted fixed-row estimates are eventual as \(p\to\infty\), uniformly on the fixed square \(Q\). A growing-depth conclusion would require first obtaining uniform physical confinement and half-step control at fixed depth, then selecting a depth \(r=r(n)\to\infty\) as \(n\to\infty\). No interchange of these limits is currently justified.

This is the highest-value test because only a genuine field-level identification can overcome the \(0.703296\) separate-price bound.

### 2. Joint finite-depth physical replay with certified tails

Price the jointly collected output in \(P_{5000}\), then add an explicit analytic tail and exceptional-lift correction uniformly for sufficiently large \(p\equiv1\pmod4\). A finite-prime success would be diagnostic only; the required result is an eventual uniform bound below \(0.277444\), followed separately by half-step, invariant-ball, and \(r(n)\to\infty\) confinement estimates.
