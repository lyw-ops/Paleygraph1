# A signed binary response invisible to global adjoints

At the actual finite-field seed, an order-one bulk mixed response of the exact marked activation can be invisible to a growing family of global linear moments of total real dimension $d=o(n)$. The perturbations are real at real floors, holomorphic on a common disk about $t_*=2031/128$, and uniformly small. This tests the map at its seed. The perturbations need not be directions generated by its Picard histories.

## A transported column orthogonal to the probes

Use counting Euclidean geometry, $e_i$ the unit coordinate, $J=11^T/n$, $E$ the orthogonal projection onto inversion-even rows, and $K$ the physical real symmetric Paley convolution. It commutes with inversion, has $K^2=I-J$, and satisfies $|K_{ij}|\le\sqrt q/n$. Include $e_0,Ke_0$ in addition to the chosen input tests and the $K$-transforms of chosen output tests. Include real and imaginary parts of complex tests. Let $P$ project onto the span of their even parts, of real dimension $d$. The space can vary with the prime. Set $z_i=EK e_i$. Then
$$
 \sum_i\|P z_i\|_2^2=\mathrm{tr}(P K^2)\le d .
$$
There are $n-2$ coordinates other than the two inversion-fixed ones. Pick one with squared projection at most $d/(n-2)$ and put $h=(I-P)z_i$. This real even signed row is invisible to every included input test, and $Kh$ to every included output test. Both vanish at the mark. Moreover
$$
 \|h\|_\infty\le\sqrt q/n+\sqrt{d/(n-2)},\qquad
 \|h\|_2\le1,\qquad
 (Kh)_i=\|h\|_2^2\ge\tfrac12-\tfrac1n-\tfrac{d}{n-2}.
$$
Indeed $\langle e_i,EK^2e_i\rangle=1/2-1/n$. The selection can be made at half the bulk coordinates by doubling the projection cost, constructing a separate row for each. The mark itself has its own explicit dual column $Ke_0$.

## Exact mixed response on a common disk

Use the true seed $S=2\sqrt n K e_0$, of absolute value at most $2\sqrt{q/n}$. Let $F_t(x)=\sqrt{4t+x^2}$ on the bulk and use the actual $n$-floor at the mark. There are even directions $u,v$, zero at the mark, such that
$$
 \tfrac14\{F_t(S+u+v)-F_t(S+u-v)-F_t(S-u+v)+F_t(S-u-v)\}=h
$$
on $|t-t_*|<1/4$ for all sufficiently large primes. The directions are holomorphic there, uniformly $O(\sqrt{\|h\|_\infty})$, and real at real floors. The constant affine correction in the physical update disappears in this difference. The transported response is $Kh$ throughout the disk. A parameter-dependent probe family is included uniformly whenever its values lie in the indicated fixed span.

At each bulk coordinate set $b=|h|$, $f=F_t(S)$, $a=f+2b$, and
$$
 2u=a\sqrt{4b(f+b)/(a^2-S^2)},\qquad v=\operatorname{sgn}(h)u .
$$
Use positive roots on the real interval and zero at $b=0$. The denominator is $4t+4bf+4b^2$, uniformly separated from zero, as are $f$ and $f+b$, on the stated disk once $\|h\|_\infty=o(1)$. The roots extend there with uniform bounds. With $r=2u$, the formula gives $r^2=a^2(a^2-4t-S^2)/(a^2-S^2)$. Squaring gives $F_t(S\pm r)=a\pm Sr/a$ by real positivity for small $b$ and analytic continuation. This proves the mixed identity. The marked coordinate evaluates four identical terms.

The leading signed mixed Taylor coefficient $F_t''(S)uv$ differs from $h$ in counting $L^2$ by $O(\|h\|_\infty)$ uniformly on the disk. The cubic terms cancel by central symmetry. The coordinate error is $O(b^2)$ by the uniform fourth derivative bound in a fixed neighborhood of the bounded seed, and $\|b\|_2\le1$. The post-$K$ error in every coordinate is therefore also $O(\|h\|_\infty)$. The obstruction is already present at binary order, with all higher arities controlled for these examples.

## Mean-zero physical input space

The four perturbed inputs can also be required to be in the linear image of $K$. Reserve two disjoint inversion-even bulk sets of sizes comparable to $n^{7/8}$, and include their coordinate evaluations in the input test space. The resulting dimension $d'=d+O(n^{7/8})$ is still $o(n)$, so the previous lower bound with $d'$ still tends to $1/2$. The row $h$ vanishes on both sets. Before correcting, $\sum_j |u_j|+\sum_j|v_j|=O(n^{3/4})$ uniformly on the disk, by the coordinate bound $O(\sqrt{|h_j|})$, Hölder and $\|h\|_2\le1$. On the first set assign $u$ the constant $-\sum_j u_j$ divided by its size, leaving $v=0$ there. On the second set do the same for $v$, leaving $u=0$. Both corrections are holomorphic and uniformly $O(n^{-1/8})$. They make both directions mean zero. The mixed central difference and its binary term vanish identically on each reserve since one direction is zero. Thus the exact response, the binary approximation and the fixed mark all persist. Since $S$ already has mean zero and $K^2=I-J$, all four inputs belong to the even mean-zero image of $K$. Reality on the real axis is preserved.

## Ranked tests

1. Test the actual signed mixed remainder against translated columns $EK e_i$, including the supremum over bulk $i$, after separating the marked correction. Low-dimensional Jacobi tests and the mark can all be exactly right while a bulk coordinate is large. Any deduction from fewer moments needs an additional restriction on the realized joint histories.
2. Seek approximation of the actual residual by a controlled trace-field span in **counting** $L^2$ on the overlap. An error $\epsilon$ costs at most $\epsilon$ at every missing column with no loss in $n$. Normalized $L^2=o(1)$ allows all the rows constructed above.

Even with the mean-zero constraint, the four inputs need not be reached by the physical Picard recursion. The construction rules out uniform bulk recovery for general signed mixed directions at the true seed. The physical marked branch and its possible continuation remain undecided.
