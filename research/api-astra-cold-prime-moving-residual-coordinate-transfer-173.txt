## Result

A genuine moving starter is available formally, but the existing estimates do **not** yet prove the required moving-depth coordinate bridge on the original square.

Keep throughout:

\[
t_0=n=\frac{p-1}{2},
\]

the original closed complex square \(Q\), both physical lifts, and the actual map

\[
F_t(W)=K\Phi_t(W),
\]

where the marked coordinate uses the physical radicand \(4n+W_0^2\), while the other coordinates use \(4t+W_i^2\).

Let \(V_m\) denote the actual cold Picard orbit. The genuine physical residual at height \(m\) is

\[
\mathcal R_m(t):=F_t(V_m)-V_m=V_{m+1}-V_m.
\]

This is not an inverse-defined correction.

### Candidate moving starter

Take

\[
m=m(p):=602+\left\lceil \frac12\log n\right\rceil,
\qquad
\widehat V_p:=V_{m(p)}.
\]

The proposed residual is therefore the actual next orbit increment

\[
\widehat{\mathcal R}_p
=
F_t(\widehat V_p)-\widehat V_p
=
V_{m(p)+1}-V_{m(p)}.
\]

The order of limits is: for each eligible prime \(p\), form \(m(p)\), prove estimates uniformly on \(Q\) and on both lifts, and only then let \(p\to\infty\).

## What the existing counting estimates would give

Suppose, in addition to the fixed-height estimates already recorded, that the actual orbit remains in a common coordinate tube on which

\[
\sup_{W\text{ in the tube}}\|K\Phi_t'(W)\|_{2\to2}\le q
\]

with, for example, \(q=0.520\). The height-602 curvature calculation is compatible with this numerically: starting from the quoted \(0.51358\) bound, the displayed curvature \(0.248\) and radius \(0.011+0.012/2\) give

\[
0.51358+0.248(0.011+0.012/2)
<0.5169<0.520.
\]

Under this additional tube hypothesis, the normalized counting residual satisfies

\[
\|\mathcal R_{602+k}\|_{2,n}
\le q^k\|\mathcal R_{602}\|_{2,n}.
\]

Since \(m(p)-602=\lceil \frac12\log n\rceil\),

\[
\|\widehat{\mathcal R}_p\|_{2,n}
\ll q^{\frac12\log n}
=
n^{\frac12\log q}.
\]

For \(q=0.520\),

\[
\gamma:=-\frac12\log(0.520)>0.326,
\]

so

\[
\|\widehat{\mathcal R}_p\|_{2,n}=O(n^{-\gamma}),
\qquad \gamma>0.326.
\]

This is enough for the quadratic residual condition in the existing continuation bridge with \(\beta=0.455\), because

\[
n^{1-\beta}\|\widehat{\mathcal R}_p\|_{2,n}^{\,2}
\ll
n^{0.545-2\gamma}
=
O(n^{-0.107}),
\]

which tends to zero.

Thus the arithmetic choice \(m(p)=602+\lceil \frac12\log n\rceil\) is sufficient **if** one has a uniform moving-height contraction tube and the corresponding shifted resolvent estimate.

## Physical coordinate norm remains unresolved

The preceding estimate is only in the normalized counting norm. It does not imply a decaying coordinate residual. The trivial conversion gives merely

\[
\|\widehat{\mathcal R}_p\|_\infty
\le \sqrt n\,\|\widehat{\mathcal R}_p\|_{2,n}
=
O(n^{1/2-\gamma}),
\]

which with \(\gamma>0.326\) is \(O(n^{0.174})\), not \(o(1)\).

Therefore:

- **Counting residual:** conditionally decays at a rate sufficient for the quadratic continuation term.
- **Physical coordinate residual:** not proved to decay; the trivial norm comparison is inadequate.
- **Translated norm:** not proved at the moving starter. The existing \(P_{5000}\) estimates are at fixed heights and do not supply a uniform bound for \(V_{m(p)}\).
- **Physical marking:** the marked coordinate remains governed by \(4n+W_0^2\). The already quoted \(O(n^{-1/2})\) marked/bad-character repairs are harmless at fixed height, but their uniform accumulation through \(m(p)=O(\log n)\) steps has not been established. A bound of \(O((\log n)n^{-1/2})\) would still be harmless, but it is not currently recorded.

## Why the existing bridge cannot yet be applied

The bridge requires, at the moving starter, analogues of

\[
\sup_{k\ge0}\|B_p^k Z_p\|_{\max}
\le C n^{-\beta},
\qquad
B_p=K\Phi_t'(\widehat V_p),
\]

together with coordinate confinement of the signed linear sums

\[
S_{p,k}:=\sum_{j<k}B_p^j\widehat{\mathcal R}_p.
\]

The available height-602 result proves only

\[
\sum_{j\ge0}
\|(KD_{602})^jK\|_{\max}
=O(n^{-0.455})
\]

for the frozen derivative at \(V_{602}\). It does **not** prove the same estimate uniformly for

\[
D_{m(p)}=\operatorname{diag}\Phi_t'(V_{m(p)}).
\]

Nor does the fixed-height source price for the mixed seed packets produce outer-power decay in \(j\). The one-step translated estimates at height 601 or 602 therefore cannot be iterated to depth \(m(p)\).

Consequently, the proposed moving starter is a valid **conditional construction**, but the requested genuine moving-prime confinement theorem is not proved by the current record.

## Precise remaining gap

One needs a theorem uniform for all heights

\[
602\le h\le 602+\left\lceil \frac12\log n\right\rceil
\]

that simultaneously supplies:

1. a common physical coordinate tube containing \(V_h(Q)\) on both lifts;
2. a counting contraction \(q<1\) for \(K\Phi_t'(V_h)\);
3. a translated or entrywise shifted-resolvent bound
   \[
   \sup_{h\le 602+\lceil\frac12\log n\rceil}
   \sup_{j\ge0}
   \|(K D_h)^jK\|_{\max}
   \ll n^{-\beta};
   \]
4. uniform control of the marked-coordinate corrections with \(t_0=n\);
5. a coordinate bound for the signed partial sums \(S_{p,k}\), not merely a counting bound.

Without item 3, the existing \(O(n^{-0.455})\) bridge cannot be transferred from \(V_{602}\) to the moving starter. Without item 5, even the decaying counting residual does not imply physical coordinate confinement.

## Ranked next tests

### 1. Highest priority: moving-height resolvent replay

For each

\[
h=602,\ldots,602+\left\lceil \frac12\log n\right\rceil,
\]

replay the actual two-lift translated-column argument with \(V_h\) in place of \(V_{602}\). The decisive target is a uniform estimate such as

\[
\sup_h\sup_j
\|(K D_h)^jK\|_{\max}
\ll n^{-0.44}
\]

together with a uniform \(P_{5000}\) rate below one. This directly tests the missing hypothesis of the bridge.

### 2. Second priority: finite moving-orbit coordinate test

Using the actual physical recursion, propagate \(V_{602}\) through \(m(p)-602=O(\log n)\) steps while retaining separately:

\[
\|V_{h+1}-V_h\|_{2,n},
\qquad
\|V_{h+1}-V_h\|_\infty,
\qquad
\|V_{h+1}-V_h\|_{P_{5000}}.
\]

The test should also retain the marked \(4n+W_0^2\) term rather than replacing it by the bulk \(4t+W_i^2\). A successful bound of the form

\[
\sup_{h\le m(p)}
\|V_h-V_{602}\|_\infty<d-\eta
\]

would establish the physical tube needed for the first test. A failure would give a quantitative obstruction to this specific moving starter, without settling other possible starters.
