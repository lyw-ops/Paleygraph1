## Report: exact reduced dual remains unbounded at the required scale

Fix \(0<\tau\) sufficiently small, an ordinary sign \(s\in\{\pm1\}\), and primes
\[
p\equiv1\pmod 4,\qquad p\to\infty,\qquad n=(p-1)/2.
\]
The physical mark is the distinguished coordinate \(0\). Let \(G=\sqrt n\,g_T\), where \(g_T\) is the actual fixed-\(\tau\), zero-start activation increment, and choose \(T=T(p)\to\infty\) with \(T+1\) in the proved simultaneous marked window.

For the ordinary mean-one optimum on sign \(s\), the exact reduced dual is
\[
L(G)=
\max\left\{
\mathbb EZ-\frac{q_s}{n}Z_0:
Z\in U_{-s},\quad Z_i\le G_i\ (i\in I)
\right\}, \tag{1}
\]
where \(q_s\) is the physical root value of the sign-\(s\) optimum and
\[
I=\{i\ne0:R_i=0\text{ for every true opposite optimum }R\in\mathcal R_{-s}\}.
\]
The constraints are only on this common zero set \(I\), not on the full bulk.

### Checked result

I do not obtain a prime-uniform \(o(T)\) upper bound for (1). The available prime autocorrelation estimates do not control the exact reduced dual, because they apply to nonnegative feasible opposite rows, whereas \(Z\in U_{-s}\) is generally signed and is constrained only on \(I\). In particular:

1. The Fejér and quotient-character profiles from the opposite-autocorrelation argument price repeated differences of nonnegative rows.
2. A dual certificate may have large positive and negative parts away from \(I\); those sites impose no inequalities in (1).
3. The reduction from full-bulk constraints to \(I\) is exact, so replacing \(I\) by a synthetic support or by a finite LP support would not prove the stated theorem.
4. The square obstruction is irrelevant to the requested prime family: it shows that no all-order argument can work, but it does not produce a prime obstruction.

The energy estimate \(\mathbb E G^2=O_\tau(T)\) also does not suffice. It controls a signed certificate only after an additional norm or support estimate, and no such estimate is currently available uniformly for the actual common zero set \(I\).

### A precise new sufficient boundary comparison

There is, however, an exact interpolation criterion that would prove the desired bound.

Let
\[
d:=\mathbf1-q_s e_0,
\]
so that for every \(Z\in U_{-s}\),
\[
\left\langle d,Z\right\rangle
=\mathbb EZ-\frac{q_s}{n}Z_0.
\]
Suppose there exist nonnegative weights \(a_i\ge0\), supported on \(I\), such that
\[
P_{U_{-s}}a=P_{U_{-s}}d, \tag{2}
\]
where \(P_{U_{-s}}\) is orthogonal projection under normalized counting measure. Equivalently,
\[
\sum_{i\in I}a_i Z_i
=
\mathbb EZ-\frac{q_s}{n}Z_0
\qquad (Z\in U_{-s}). \tag{3}
\]
Then every feasible \(Z\) in (1) satisfies
\[
\mathbb EZ-\frac{q_s}{n}Z_0
=\sum_{i\in I}a_iZ_i
\le \sum_{i\in I}a_iG_i. \tag{4}
\]
Therefore the prime-uniform estimate
\[
\sum_{i\in I}a_iG_i=o(T) \tag{5}
\]
at a selectable \(T=T(p)\) would imply
\[
L(G)=o(T).
\]

This is a genuine boundary comparison for the exact reduced dual. It preserves the physical mark, the actual common zero set \(I\), and the order of limits:
\[
\text{fixed }\tau
\quad\longrightarrow\quad
p\to\infty\text{ through }p\equiv1\pmod4
\quad\longrightarrow\quad
\tau\downarrow0.
\]
The unresolved issue is existence of nonnegative interpolating weights \(a\) satisfying (2), together with the weighted terminal estimate (5). Neither follows from the currently proved character-arc or autocorrelation bounds.

A useful variant is to seek approximate interpolation:
\[
\left\|P_{U_{-s}}a-P_{U_{-s}}d\right\|_{U_{-s}^*}
=o(T^{-1})
\]
with \(\sum_{i\in I}a_iG_i=o(T)\) and a prime-uniform bound on the residual action against admissible \(Z\). This would still suffice, but requires a norm bound on the admissible dual certificates that is presently missing.

## Evidence and remaining gap

The exact duality proof ensures that no omitted bulk coordinate can be used to evade (1): a true opposite optimizer positive on the omitted complement can be subtracted without changing the objective. Thus the correct arithmetic target is genuinely the common zero set \(I\).

Conversely, the existing prime-specific autocorrelation inequality
\[
n\sum_{h\ne0}C_q(h)C_r(h)
=1-nL_qL_r
\]
controls correlations of nonnegative feasible rows \(q,r\). It does not imply (2), because interpolation requires a nonnegative measure on \(I\) whose \(U_{-s}\)-projection equals the signed representer \(d\). This is a different, stronger statement involving the actual face \(I\), not merely the ambient prime Fourier geometry.

Hence the current status is **inconclusive**, not refuted: the exact reduced dual is identified, and (2)–(5) give a precise sufficient route, but the required prime-uniform boundary comparison is open.

## Ranked next tests

### 1. Exact character-interpolation LP on the actual common zero set \(I\)

For each eligible prime \(p\), compute or characterize the cone
\[
\mathcal C_I
=\{P_{U_{-s}}a:a_i\ge0,\ \operatorname{supp}(a)\subseteq I\}.
\]
Test whether \(P_{U_{-s}}d\in\mathcal C_I\), first exactly at moderate primes and then through a prime-uniform structural proof. If membership holds, estimate the minimizing weighted cost
\[
\inf\left\{\sum_{i\in I}a_iG_i:
a\ge0,\ \operatorname{supp}(a)\subseteq I,\ 
P_{U_{-s}}a=P_{U_{-s}}d\right\}. \tag{6}
\]
This is the most direct test because it uses the actual face, the actual physical mark, and the exact mean-minus-mark functional.

### 2. Prime-specific level-set control for signed dual certificates

For admissible \(Z\in U_{-s}\), study
\[
A_\lambda(Z)=\{i\in I:Z_i>\lambda T\}.
\]
Combine the pointwise constraint \(Z_i\le G_i\), the energy bound for \(G\), and prime character/interpolation restrictions on \(U_{-s}\) to prove that every such level set either has negligible weighted mass or forces a large dual norm cost. A sufficient target is
\[
\sup_{\substack{Z\in U_{-s}\\ Z_i\le G_i\ (i\in I)}}
\left(\mathbb EZ-\frac{q_s}{n}Z_0\right)
\le o(T).
\]
This should be attempted after the interpolation test, since failure of (2) would identify the precise signed residual that the level-set argument must control.
