## Result: not supported; the proposed completion remains unproved

For the actual prime family
\[
q=p\equiv1\pmod 4,\qquad n=(q-1)/2,
\]
with physical mark \(0\), weights
\[
w_0=1,\qquad w_i=\tau/n\quad(i\ne0),\qquad W=1+(n-1)\tau/n,
\]
and counting norm \(\|\,\cdot\,\|_2\), the available results do **not** produce a sector-preserving completion inside the proved marked window.

The relevant order of limits is:

1. fix \(0<\tau<1\);
2. take primes \(q\to\infty\) through the simultaneous marked window;
3. only then send \(\tau\downarrow0\).

The certified arithmetic window is
\[
T(q)\le \log_2^*q-\log_2^*\log_2^*q-O_{\tau,B}(1),
\]
with marked cancellation
\[
\max_{t\le T(q)}|v_{t,0}|=O_{\tau,B}(T(q)^{-B})
\]
for the actual zero-start orbit
\[
v^{(0)}=0,\qquad v^{(t+1)}=K\sqrt{(v^{(t)})^2+4w}.
\]

Within this window, the projected averages \(X_{\sigma,T}\) have the required sector and mean, and their raw marked coordinates satisfy
\[
X_{\sigma,T}(0)=1+o(1).
\]
The Picard budget is
\[
nM_T^2\le B_{n,T},\qquad
B_{n,T}\to1+\tau+1/T
\]
in the prime-first order.

### Why the known completion does not fit

The explicit pinned reflection preserves sector, mean, and the physical mark exactly, but its sufficient positivity condition requires
\[
T\ge C_\tau n
\]
with an explicit \(C_\tau>0\). This is incompatible with the proved arithmetic window, since
\[
T(q)=o(n).
\]
The unmodified-prefix positivity argument is even weaker: direct positivity for every prefix is only proved at
\[
T=O_\tau(n\log n),
\]
while selected positive prefixes occur at \(O_\tau(n)\).

Thus the existing positive completion is valid only after the marked window has expired. It does not establish the requested fixed-\(\tau\), prime-uniform completion with additional mean \(o(n^{-1/2})\).

### What is genuinely obstructed

Two restricted repair classes are ruled out, but neither obstruction rules out an arbitrary signed sector correction.

1. **Doubly stochastic repairs.**  
   If \(M\) is doubly stochastic, preserves the relevant sector, and preserves the marked coordinate, then for the actual prime orbit in the window the mark is the unique macroscopic maximum. This forces \(Me_0=e_0\). The sector and orbit are then fixed by \(M\), so
   \[
   MX_{\sigma,T}=X_{\sigma,T}.
   \]
   Such a map cannot remove any negative coordinate.

2. **The seven pinned arithmetic directions.**  
   For the affine representative with scaled mean \(G\), scaled mark \(a\), and coefficients \(c\in\mathbb R^7\), the pinned-corner support estimate gives, on a positive fraction of bulk sites,
   \[
   R_{\sigma,q}(i)
   \le G-(\sqrt2-\varepsilon)a
      -(1/4-\varepsilon)\|c\|_1+\varepsilon.
   \]
   In the marked window,
   \[
   G=\sqrt n\,M_T\le\sqrt{1+\tau}+o(1),\qquad a=X_{\sigma,T}(0)=1+o(1).
   \]
   For fixed \(\tau<1\),
   \[
   \sqrt2-\sqrt{1+\tau}>0.
   \]
   Hence no correction confined to those seven pinned directions can make the actual projected average nonnegative while retaining \(o(1)\) scaled mark loss and \(o(n^{-1/2})\) extra mean.

These are precise failures of two proposed repair mechanisms. They are **not** a no-go theorem for the full cone
\[
V_\sigma\cap\{r:r(0)=0\},
\]
so the unrestricted signed-completion question remains open.

## Exact remaining gap

The missing object is an endpoint-dependent signed correction for the actual row \(X_{\sigma,T}\), not an ordinary theta optimization and not a fixed source ansatz.

Let
\[
\ell_\sigma=P_\sigma e_0,\qquad
k_\sigma=1-\frac{\ell_\sigma}{(\ell_\sigma)_0}.
\]
Then \(k_\sigma\) is sector-compatible, has \(k_\sigma(0)=0\), and has mean one. The exact completion cost is
\[
\delta_{\sigma}(q,T)
=
\inf\left\{
\delta\ge0:
\begin{array}{l}
\exists r\in V_\sigma,\quad r(0)=0,\\[2mm]
X_{\sigma,T}+r+\delta k_\sigma\ge0
\end{array}
\right\}.
\]
The target required by the mission is
\[
\delta_{\sigma}(q,T)=o(n^{-1/2})
\]
uniformly for primes in the proved growing window, while retaining
\[
X_{\sigma,T}(0)=1+o(1).
\]

Neither the pinned-reflection estimate nor the seven-direction support calculation bounds this full-cone quantity. The stochastic result says only that a positive doubly stochastic repair cannot help.

## Ranked next tests

### 1. Highest priority: actual endpoint-dependent signed-reserve LP

For each actual prime \(q\equiv1\pmod4\) and each certified \(T\), solve the exact finite-dimensional problem above using the true \(X_{\sigma,T}\), the exact projector \(P_\sigma\), and the physical mark \(0\).

Its dual is especially informative. A nonnegative dual weight \(\lambda\) satisfying
\[
\lambda\perp\{r\in V_\sigma:r(0)=0\}
\]
gives
\[
\delta_\sigma(q,T)
\ge
-\frac{\langle\lambda,X_{\sigma,T}\rangle}
        {\langle\lambda,k_\sigma\rangle}
\quad
\text{whenever }\langle\lambda,k_\sigma\rangle>0.
\]
The first test should be nonstatic: allow \(\lambda\) to depend on the actual endpoint \(v_T,v_{T+1}\), not merely on the seven fixed arithmetic sources.

A successful result would need an explicit bound
\[
\delta_\sigma(q,T)\le C(\tau)\,o(n^{-1/2})
\]
with \(C(\tau)\) independent of \(q\) and \(T\) throughout the certified window. Finite feasibility alone would not settle the asymptotic claim.

### 2. Second priority: prove or refute a full-cone weighted endpoint inequality

Establish a prime-uniform inequality controlling the full signed correction by the actual endpoint defect, for example in the form
\[
\delta_\sigma(q,T)
\le
C(\tau)\,\mathcal D_{\sigma,T},
\]
where \(\mathcal D_{\sigma,T}\) is computed from the actual endpoint difference
\[
\frac{P_\sigma(v_{T+1}-v_1)}{2T}
\]
and the actual negative part of the raw averaged row, with no replacement by an ordinary sector-row optimization.

The required conclusion would be
\[
\mathcal D_{\sigma,T}=o(n^{-1/2})
\]
in the prime-first order. The constant \(C(\tau)\) must be explicit and independent of the unknown prime growth; otherwise this is only a fixed-depth diagonal argument.

At present, the available endpoint estimates give only \(L^2\)-scale control such as \(O(T^{-1/2})\) after normalization. That is far too weak when \(T\) is restricted to the log-star window, and no full-cone signed reserve estimate converts it into the required \(o(n^{-1/2})\) mean cost.

**Conclusion:** the mission is presently inconclusive, with a precise failure of the known repair mechanisms rather than a refutation of all sector-preserving signed completions.
