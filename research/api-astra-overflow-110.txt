### New consequence: the core balance is a near-extremal character-sum requirement, not an automatic contradiction

Assume the standard Paley normalization
\[
K_{xy}=\frac{\chi(x-y)}{\sqrt p},\qquad K\mathbf 1=0,\qquad K^2=I-J_0,
\]
with normalized inner product \(\langle u,v\rangle_p=p^{-1}\sum_xu_xv_x\). Write \(c=|C|=a\sqrt p\), where \(a\) is bounded above and below by positive constants, and decompose
\[
\bar h=1_C\bar h+r,\qquad r=1_{C^c}\bar h.
\]
The persistent-core estimates give, for fixed \(\eta\) and sufficiently small \(\kappa\),
\[
\bar h_x\ge s\quad(x\in C),\qquad
\|r\|_{2,p}\le \rho\,p^{-1/4},
\]
where
\[
\rho=O\!\left(\sqrt{\eta+C_\eta\sqrt\kappa+p^{-1/2}}\right).
\]

Taking \(v=1_C\) in (2), and using
\[
\|1_C\|_{2,p}=\sqrt{a}\,p^{-1/4},
\]
gives the explicit lower bound
\[
\epsilon\langle 1_C,K1_C\bar h\rangle_p
\ge
\left(sa-\sqrt a\,\rho\right)p^{-1/2}
+O(p^{-1}).                                      \tag{A}
\]
The term \(\bar m\,|C|/p\) is only \(O(p^{-1})\), while the exterior contribution satisfies
\[
|\langle 1_C,Kr\rangle_p|
 \le \sqrt a\,\rho\,p^{-1/2}.
\]
Thus the low exterior is genuinely present at the same \(p^{-1/2}\) transport scale as the core term. It may be made a small fraction of the core contribution by taking \(\eta,\kappa\) small, but it cannot simply be deleted.

Since
\[
\langle 1_C,K1_C\bar h\rangle_p
=\frac1{p\sqrt p}
 \sum_{x\in C}\sum_{y\in\mathbb F_p}\chi(x-y)\bar h_y,
\]
(A) implies the checkable prime-specific requirement
\[
\sum_{x\in C}\sum_y\chi(x-y)\bar h_y
\ge
\frac{p}{\epsilon}\left(sa-\sqrt a\,\rho\right)-O(\sqrt p).       \tag{B}
\]

This is a substantial requirement: the Paley spectral bound gives, after removing constants,
\[
\left|\sum_{x,y}\chi(x-y)\,u_xv_y\right|
\le \sqrt p\,\|u\|_{\ell^2}\|v\|_{\ell^2}.
\]
For \(u=1_C\) and \(v=\bar h-\bar m\mathbf1\), the right side is \(O(p)\). Hence (B) asks for a fixed positive proportion of the maximal spectral correlation whenever
\[
sa>\sqrt a\,\rho.
\]
The persistent balance therefore forces the weighted pair \((C,\bar h-\bar m\mathbf1)\) to be near-extremal for the Paley character operator.

### Precise remaining arithmetic condition

Define the spectral defect
\[
\mathcal D_C(\bar h)
=
\|1_C\|_{2,p}^2\,
 \|\bar h-\bar m\mathbf1\|_{2,p}^2
-
\langle 1_C,K\bar h\rangle_p^2.
\]
A sufficient prime-specific obstruction would be a uniform estimate
\[
\mathcal D_C(\bar h)\ge \delta p^{-1}                         \tag{C}
\]
for every admissible persistent \(C\) and every nonnegative \(\bar h\) obeying the carrier bounds, with some \(\delta>0\) depending only on the fixed obstruction parameters. Combined with (A), (C) would exclude the persistent core when the lower bound in (A) lies within \(\delta^{1/2}p^{-1/2}\) of the spectral maximum.

However, (C) does **not** follow from support size, positivity, or abstract reflection geometry. The sets \(C\) are adaptive subsets of \(\mathbb F_p\), not intervals or fixed low-complexity sets, so ordinary Weil bounds do not apply. The usual bilinear character estimate is exactly the spectral bound above and leaves room for (B).

### Delimiting example for abstract reflection geometry

In a general Hilbert-space reflection model, let
\[
e=\frac{1_C}{\|1_C\|},\qquad
q=\frac{\bar h-\bar m\mathbf1}
        {\|\bar h-\bar m\mathbf1\|}.
\]
An orthogonal involution can be chosen to interchange \(e\) and \(q\), while acting arbitrarily on their orthogonal complement. For that reflection,
\[
\langle 1_C,K\bar h\rangle
=
\|1_C\|\,\|\bar h-\bar m\mathbf1\|,
\]
so the spectral defect is zero and the signed balance can hold exactly after choosing the scalar parameters. Positivity of \(\bar h\) and a small exterior component do not prevent this construction. Therefore no contradiction can follow from the reflection identities alone; one needs a genuine arithmetic non-extremality statement such as (C), or a structural theorem showing that Paley-near-extremizers cannot have a persistent positive core of size \(\asymp\sqrt p\).

### Status and ranked next tests

1. **Highest priority:** test the defect \(\mathcal D_C(\bar h)\) numerically on actual repair cores for primes \(p\), retaining the full exterior \(r\). The decisive scale is \(p^{-1}\), not merely the sign of the core correlation.

2. **Second:** impose and verify an additional structure condition on \(C\) (for example, bounded additive energy or interval-like localization), then seek a character-sum improvement over the spectral bound. Without such a condition, the current argument gives the near-extremal requirement (B) but does not exclude the obstruction.
