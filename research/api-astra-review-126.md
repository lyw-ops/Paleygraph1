The [returned draft](api-astra-overflow-126.txt) gives a valid conditional power comparison. If both columns have dimension cost \(O(n^{-1/2}\alpha^\ell)\) at the required powers and the signed derivative difference has compatible odd-word cost \(O(\rho_E)\), then the per-term estimates sum to \(O(n^{-\beta})\) at the stated quadratic displacement scale under the displayed inequality (7). For a varying insertion time one needs the uniform per-term envelope in \((u,v,i)\). An estimate on the summed entries for each frozen time alone does not by itself prove the absolute sum for the varying time ordering.

An odd derivative difference may be a sum of odd products of negative fields rather than a sum of negative simple fields. The rank and boundary estimates must cover the chosen representation. For the actual first difference this is done by the linear-in-arity word estimate in the [height-600 arithmetic](cold-actual-first-insertion-126.md). Finite interpolation proves no rank lower bound or general obstruction for the genuine growing orbit.

The moving-starter implication in the raw verdict presupposes several of the inputs it is intended to obtain. Common sheets, confinement, a small genuine residual, and a compatible frozen summability bound at the same height remain separate requirements. Its finite-height numerical comparison does not decide any of them. The actual fixed-height calculation bounds all frozen powers of the first signed insertion and supplies the next actual counting step, while the moving starter and a common cold continuation through primes remain open.
