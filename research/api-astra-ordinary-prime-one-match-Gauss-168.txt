## Result

I do not obtain the required \(O(1)\) signed average for the one-match class. The exact reduction is clear, and it isolates a genuine remaining source-weighted \(3+3\) spectral estimate. Existing cap, physical-slope, and positive-energy estimates do not imply that estimate.

All statements below are for the selected ordinary-prime family \(p\to\infty\) in the cited rounds, with the actual law \(\nu_p\), actual lifted character law \(\omega_p\), actual nonzero physical mark \(v_p\), and fixed physical thresholds \(a,b>0\). No averaging over \(v_p\), the prime family, or the square lifts is being introduced. The two lifts \(i,i'\in\{0,1\}\) remain explicit.

Write
\[
m=p-1,\qquad \kappa_p=\|\omega_p\|_\infty\ll p^{-1/2},
\qquad \ell_p=\omega_p-\omega_p*\omega_p .
\]
With counting norms,
\[
\|\ell_p\|_1\le 2,\qquad \|\ell_p\|_\infty\le 2\kappa_p.
\]

The zero-match \(4+4\) class remains separate and is not controlled by the argument below.

## Exact one-match decomposition

At fixed \(h=\psi'/\psi\), the plus labels are
\[
\{\alpha,\delta,\bar\beta'/h,\bar\gamma'/h\},
\]
and the minus labels are
\[
\{\bar\beta,\bar\gamma,\alpha'/h,\delta'/h\}.
\]

The within-copy matches are excluded on the regular set. Thus the eight possible cross-copy matches are the only one-match subclasses. Consider the representative match
\[
\alpha=\alpha'/h,
\qquad\text{equivalently}\qquad
\alpha'=h\alpha.
\]

After cancelling this pair, the residual \(3+3\) labels are
\[
A_M=(\delta,\bar\beta'/h,\bar\gamma'/h),
\qquad
B_M=(\bar\beta,\bar\gamma,\delta'/h).
\]

Let \(E_M\) denote the event that this is the only cross-copy match. The exact normalized residual correlation is
\[
\mathcal R_3(A;B)
=
\sum_{\psi}
\prod_{j=1}^3g_{A_j/\psi}\,
\overline{g_{B_j/\psi}}.
\]

On \(E_M\), the two residual multisets are disjoint, so the exact Gauss expansion from Round 167 gives
\[
\boxed{
\mathcal R_3(A;B)
=
\frac{m}{p^3}
\prod_{j=1}^3G(A_j\bar B_j)
\sum_{\substack{t_1t_2t_3=1\\ t_j\neq1}}
\prod_{j=1}^3
A_j(t_j)\,
\overline{A_j\bar B_j}(t_j-1).
}
\tag{1}
\]

The fixed phase is not discarded. For \(i,i'\in\{0,1\}\),
\[
C_i(\alpha,\beta,\gamma,\delta)
=
(\beta\gamma)(-1)\,
\overline{g_{\chi^i\alpha\gamma}}\,
g_{\alpha\beta\gamma\delta}\,
\overline{
g_{\chi^i\beta\delta}\,
g_{\gamma\delta}\,
g_{\alpha\beta}
},
\]
and the one-match summand contains
\[
C_i(\alpha,\beta,\gamma,\delta)\,
\overline{C_{i'}(\alpha',\beta',\gamma',\delta')}.
\]

Therefore the exact representative one-match average is
\[
\begin{aligned}
\mathcal A_{M}^{i,i'}
={}&
\sum_{h}\ell_p(h)
\sum_{\alpha,\beta,\gamma,\delta}
\sum_{\beta',\gamma',\delta'}
\omega_p(\alpha)\omega_p(\beta)\omega_p(\gamma)\omega_p(\delta)
\\
&\quad\cdot
\omega_p(h\alpha)\omega_p(\beta')\omega_p(\gamma')\omega_p(\delta')
\,
1_{E_M}
\\
&\quad\cdot
C_i(\alpha,\beta,\gamma,\delta)
\overline{C_{i'}(h\alpha,\beta',\gamma',\delta')}
\,
\mathcal R_3(A_M;B_M).
\end{aligned}
\tag{2}
\]

The full one-match class is the sum of (2) over the eight match equations and \(i,i'\in\{0,1\}\), with the usual \(1/4\) lift factor if both outer lifts are averaged uniformly.

## What the cap actually pays

For this match define
\[
q_p(h)=\sum_{\alpha}\omega_p(\alpha)\omega_p(h\alpha).
\]
Then
\[
q_p(h)\le \kappa_p,\qquad \sum_hq_p(h)=1.
\]

Consequently, for any pointwise bound
\[
|\mathcal R_3(A_M;B_M)|\le B_p,
\]
the absolute estimate from the actual source law gives only
\[
|\mathcal A_M^{i,i'}|
\le
\sum_h|\ell_p(h)|q_p(h)B_p
\le
\kappa_p\|\ell_p\|_1 B_p
\le 2\kappa_p B_p.
\tag{3}
\]

The true quadratic-form prefactor is
\[
\frac{p^3}{m}\asymp p^2.
\]
Hence the target \(O(p^{3/2})\) requires
\[
\boxed{
\mathcal A_M^{i,i'}=O(\kappa_p)=O(p^{-1/2}),
}
\tag{4}
\]
or, equivalently, an \(O(1)\) bound after extracting the single match cap \(\kappa_p\).

A pointwise \(O(1)\) estimate for \(\mathcal R_3\) would suffice. The exact formula (1), however, does not provide such a bound. The residual two-dimensional character sum can have the generic bounded-conductor square-root scale, and the currently available estimates do not show uniform \(O(1)\) cancellation after the \(C_i\overline{C_{i'}}\), actual \(\omega_p\), and \(\ell_p\) averages are inserted.

Even a generic pointwise \(O(p^{1/2})\) estimate would give only
\[
|\mathcal A_M^{i,i'}|=O(\kappa_p p^{1/2})=O(1),
\]
which becomes \(O(p^2)\) after multiplication by \(p^3/m\), one factor \(p^{1/2}\) above the target.

Thus the needed estimate is genuinely signed and source-weighted:
\[
\boxed{
\left|
\sum_h\ell_p(h)\,
\mathbb E_{\omega_p^{\otimes 8}}
\left[
1_{E_M}\,
C_i\overline{C_{i'}}\,
\mathcal R_3(A_M;B_M)
\mid \alpha'=h\alpha
\right]
q_p(h)
\right|
\ll \kappa_p .
}
\tag{5}
\]

No such estimate is established in the cited rounds.

## Why the existing physical estimates do not close this

The physical mark remains
\[
A_x=1-v_px,\qquad C_x=1-v_pu x,
\]
with the actual square masks, actual slope set, and actual amplitudes \(f_p\). The positive completion and the shifted-layer energy estimates control physical counts and positive squared masses. They do not control the phase-weighted source kernel in (2).

In particular:

1. The one-match constraint only gives \(q_p(h)\le\kappa_p\).  
   It does not provide a second \(\kappa_p\) factor.

2. \(\|\ell_p\|_1\le2\) is not enough to force cancellation.  
   The identity
   \[
   \ell_p=\omega_p-\omega_p*\omega_p
   \]
   would help only after proving that the \(h\)-dependence of the completed \(3+3\) kernel has suitable centered spectrum.

3. The sign-selective completion results show that a uniform convolution-norm shortcut is unavailable on the genuine critical sequence: the relevant actual slope filter can have norm of order \(\sqrt p\), not \(O(1)\).

4. The two outer lifts cannot be merged before this step.  
   The factors \(C_i\overline{C_{i'}}\) remain in (2), and their dependence on \(\alpha,\beta,\gamma,\delta\) is part of the cancellation problem.

The known two-or-more-match bounds and frequency-deletion bounds remain valid and already cost only \(O(p^{3/2})\). They do not improve (5).

## Zero-match class

The zero-match class has four residual factors on each side:
\[
\mathcal R_4(A;B)
=
\sum_\psi\prod_{j=1}^4g_{A_j/\psi}
\overline{g_{B_j/\psi}}.
\]

There is no match probability \(q_p(h)\le\kappa_p\). Therefore its required normalized estimate is stronger:
\[
\left|
\sum_h\ell_p(h)\,
\mathbb E_{\omega_p^{\otimes8}}
\left[
1_{E_0}\,
C_i\overline{C_{i'}}\,
\mathcal R_4(A;B)
\right]
\right|
\ll p^{-1/2}.
\tag{6}
\]

Nothing in the one-match reduction proves (6), and it would be incorrect to fold the zero-match \(4+4\) class into the one-match argument.

## Conclusion

**Status: inconclusive, with a precise remaining gap.**

The actual one-match contribution reduces exactly to eight source-weighted \(3+3\) phase subclasses, each retaining:

- the actual \(\omega_p\)-draws;
- the fixed ratio phases \(C_i\overline{C_{i'}}\);
- both outer square lifts \(i,i'\);
- the exact physical marking \(v_p\) through the source law and physical completion;
- the signed weight \(\ell_p(h)\);
- the exact completed residual correlation (1).

The cap pays one factor \(\kappa_p\), but the required residual signed average is still \(O(1)\). Existing results only give a potentially \(O(p^{1/2})\) residual scale, which is insufficient by one square root. The independent zero-match \(4+4\) estimate remains completely open at its stronger \(O(p^{-1/2})\) normalized scale.
