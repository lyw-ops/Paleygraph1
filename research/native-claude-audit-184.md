**SRM – SE in detail.**

1. **The common quantity is \(\Gamma_2=\sum_{x\ne1}g(x)^2=n\,\mathcal E(\nu)-1\)**, which on (NC) equals \(\sqrt p\,(\mathcal E(S)/|S|^3+o(1))\) by the accepted Round 183 Proposition 1.4. SE studies it; SRM uses it as an input.
2. **Lemma Y is proved only in SRM, and it is not new mathematics.** \(X\le R_*M_1\le\Gamma_2M_1\le(G-1)^2\Gamma_2\) is Cauchy–Schwarz plus the Young bound \(E^\times(h)\le\|h\|_1^2\|h\|_2^2\) that Round 182 already recorded as the light-level barrier (SRM says so itself). SE does not prove, cite or need Lemma Y. Calling it a lemma "shared with the support-energy call" means: it is the bridge through which an SE outcome would reach SRM. It is not a lemma both calls prove.
3. **The bridge carries only power rates, and the SE question as posed is qualitative.**
   - SE asks whether \(\mathcal E(S)=o(|S|^3)\). Through Lemma Y that gives only \(X=o(p^{3/2})\), which moves no exponent: the one-match use needs \(\Gamma_2=O(p^{\gamma})\) with \(\gamma<3/8\) (by SRM's corrected threshold), the X target needs \(\gamma\le1/1280\).
   - Even a power rate \(\mathcal E(S)=O(|S|^3p^{-\delta})\) does not transfer as stated, because the identity \(\Gamma_2/\sqrt p=\mathcal E(S)/|S|^3+o(1)\) has an unquantified \(o(1)\). The transferable statement is a power bound on the exact \(\Gamma_2=n\mathcal E(\nu)-1\) of the law, \(\gamma<3/8\), i.e. \(\delta>1/8\) relative to \(\sqrt p\).
   - So: SE \(\Rightarrow\) SRM only through a stronger, quantitative SE outcome that neither report proves. SRM \(\not\Rightarrow\) SE: Lemma Y is not reversible (SRM's \(g_H\) remark), and nothing in SRM bounds \(\Gamma_2\).
4. **SE's Theorem 2 does not feed \(X\) at any useful scale.** Theorem 2 is a statement about the multiplicative Fourier coefficients \(\widehat g(a)=n\lambda_a\) of \(g\), i.e. about the difference law \(\lambda\) of \(\nu\), at level \(\tfrac12\) with no power. \(X=E_Q(h)\) is a multiplicative energy of the *additive shift* \(h(z)=g(1-z)\); its Fourier coefficients are Jacobi-weighted sums \(\sum_d\lambda_dJ(\psi^d,\cdot)\), on which a bound \(\lambda_a\le(\tfrac12+o(1))\lambda_0\) gives nothing beyond Lemma S2. The only route from Theorem 2 to \(X\) is Corollary 3(i) \(\Rightarrow\Gamma_2\le(\tfrac12+o(1))\sqrt p\Rightarrow X\le(\tfrac12+o(1))p^{3/2}\) via Lemma Y, which is the Round 183 cap and weaker than the verified \(X=O(p^{34/23})\). The mass of \(g\) on \(\Gamma_*=H_*^\perp\) (item 3) is vacuous when \(H_*=\{0\}\) and otherwise places \(g\) on a subgroup of order \(\gg\sqrt p\), for which no shifted-subgroup energy bound below \(p^{3/2}\) is invoked by either report. SE says the same ("no shared estimate in this report"); I agree.
5. **A common blocking configuration, found independently from both sides.**
   - SE (Proposition 4, (Sub\(_C\)), Criterion C2): the energy configurations it cannot exclude need a multiplicative subgroup \(B\), or a multiplicative Bohr set / geometric progression \(\{\theta^i:i\in I\}\), of size \(\asymp\sqrt p\), on which \(\chi(1-y)=\rho\) for almost every \(y\).
   - SRM (Proposition 5 and §7): the square-field control is the exact field-general model of the same thing (\(B=\mathbb F_r^\times\), \(\chi(1-y)\equiv1\)), and the X-version prototype blocking configuration is a positive-mass part of \(g\) on a geometric progression of length \(\asymp\sqrt p\) inside \(T_\rho\) — SE's "progression case".
   - The two reports need **different estimates** on this configuration: SE needs non-concentration of the shifted character sum \(\sum_{y\in P}\chi(1-y)\le(1-c)|P|\); SRM needs the shifted-progression energy \(E^\times(1-P)=O(|P|^{2+o(1)})\). Neither is proved. For the **subgroup** case they also diverge: SRM's Proposition 4 (Round 151 rich ratios) already makes a subgroup piece of size \(\asymp\sqrt p\) harmless for \(X\) (\(E^\times\ll p\log p\)), while for SE it is exactly the open (Sub\(_C\)). So the overlap is the **progression/Bohr case**, where both are open.
   - This is a shared *configuration*, not a shared estimate. If Round 185 dispatches work on it, one `cand:` entry naming the configuration with both target estimates would be appropriate.
6. **Common accepted inputs** (not overlaps in the dispatch sense): the Round 20 flattening, Round 183 Propositions 1.1, 1.2, 1.4, and the Round 151 coset-mass exclusion are used by both. Both re-prove nothing the other proves. SE re-proves the Round 183 cap \(\tfrac12\) (Corollary 3), which duplicates an accepted Round 183 result, not SRM.

**Would this have changed dispatch?** No for the choice of calls. It should have changed the SE mission's framing: its "if yes" branch ("relaxes every operator target by \(p^{\delta/2}\)") is only reachable with a quantified power rate on the exact \(\Gamma_2\), and a qualitative \(o(|S|^3)\) answer could not have moved any ordinary exponent. That was visible before dispatch from Round 182's Young bound and Round 183 Proposition 1.4.

### After unblinding

Opened afterwards: `coordination/round-184-inflight.json`, `coordination/shared-obligations/round-184-before.json` and `coordination/drafts/round-184-dependency-assessment.json`.

- The coordinator's pre-dispatch comparison (`comparison_status: assessed`, `known_shared: []`, no `cand:` IDs per call) dispatched the two ordinary calls as independent. It states that "\(\sum g^2\) enters \(X\) only through the diagonal term, which is \(O(G\sum g^2)=O(p)\) and harmless at both scales".
- **That sentence is incomplete.** \(\sum g^2\) also enters \(X\) as an *upper bound* through the Young inequality \(X\le E^\times(h)\le(G-1)^2\Gamma_2\) (SRM's Lemma Y; Round 182 already had it as the light-level barrier, and Round 183's (G2) is the same mechanism for the first mass). So a power bound on \(\Gamma_2\) is a sufficient condition for both SRM targets. The coordinator's main conclusion stands for the questions *as posed*: a qualitative answer to SE (\(o(|S|^3)\)) implies nothing for \(X\), and a bound on \(X\) decides nothing about \(\mathcal E(S)\).
- **My classification is unchanged: SRM – SE is shared-input**, one-directional (SE \(\Rightarrow\) SRM only at the power rate \(\Gamma_2=O(p^{\gamma})\), \(\gamma<3/8\)), plus a common blocking configuration (geometric progression / multiplicative Bohr set of size \(\asymp\sqrt p\) with \(\chi(1-x)\) nearly constant), on which the two calls need different estimates. Not duplicated. Both cold pairs are independent, as the coordinator recorded.
- **Dispatch effect:** same calls. The SE mission would have been sharper had it asked for a power rate on the exact \(n\mathcal E(\nu)-1\), with the threshold \(\gamma<3/8\) (after SRM's Proposition 1) stated. Visible before dispatch: yes, from Round 182's Young bound and Round 183 (G2). The common configuration was not visible before dispatch; it is a Round 184 output of both reports.
- The SE report honoured the coordinator's request to state its structural input on the actual \(g\) (Theorem 2); SRM stated its shared lemma (Lemma Y). The request worked as intended.
