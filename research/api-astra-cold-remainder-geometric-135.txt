## Result: the target remains unproved; no genuine signed obstruction was found

Fix the actual prime family
\[
p\equiv1\pmod 4,\qquad n=(p-1)/2,
\]
with \(p\to\infty\) through eligible primes. Keep the original closed cold square \(Q\), the actual marked floor
\[
t_0=n,
\]
the frozen rows \(h=601,602\), and the translated physical price \(P_{5000}\). Let \(V_h\) be the genuine fully marked predictor and \(S=2\sqrt n\,Ke_0\) the Jacobi seed. The required estimate is
\[
\sup_{(t_i)\in Q}\|K(D_h-D^{\mathrm J})\|_{P_{5000}}
<0.472-\varepsilon
\]
for some \(\varepsilon>0\), uniformly for both \(h=601,602\), once \(p\) is sufficiently large.

I do not obtain this estimate, nor a valid signed lower bound disproving it.

### What the geometric/arithmetic calculation does establish

The relevant signed transport calculation gives the following fixed-depth fact.

For compositions whose two predictor inputs are represented by the verified negative Mellin objects, the physical position kernel has entries
\[
O(p^{-1/2})
\]
uniformly on \(Q\), including:

- the seed–seed term;
- all terms linear in each of the two predictors;
- higher odd derivative words;
- the marked coefficient corrections;
- bad-lift and boundary corrections.

The reason is that, after inverse Fourier transformation, each position entry is a signed character sum. The quadratic twist \(s(y)=\chi(1-y)\) supplies odd inertia at \(y=1\), eliminating a geometrically trivial quotient. The resulting complete sums have weight at most \(-1\) and therefore size \(O(p^{-1/2})\), with constants controlled by the verified dimension budget below \(4.8\). This remains valid after integrating along the secant segments between the actual predictors.

Thus one obtains a genuine signed physical-kernel estimate for the frozen two-step compositions, of the form
\[
\max_{x,z\ne1}
\left|
\bigl(KD_{602}KD_{601}\bigr)_{x,z}^{\mathrm{pos}}
\right|
=O(p^{-1/2}),
\]
and similarly for the relevant secant operators.

This is not yet a \(P_{5000}\)-operator estimate. The physical price is sensitive to the translated packet boundary columns and to coherent accumulation across many \(O(p^{-1/2})\) entries. No summation argument converting the signed entry estimate into a bound below \(0.472\) was supplied.

### Why the existing \(0.528\) estimate does not settle the remainder

The full Jacobi-seed derivative \(KD^{\mathrm J}\) has an independently checked translated rate below \(0.528\), and its two-step rate below \(0.279\). This prices the pure seed derivative, not
\[

\]
The difference contains the nonlinear predictor displacement
\[
V_h-S
\]
inside the odd derivative expansion. The cited seed estimate does not control these mixed words in the physical price.

Likewise, the positive retained-block witness
\[

only proves that the particular **nonnegative majorant**
\[

has two-step rate greater than \(1.073296\) in every positive price. It is not a signed lower bound for the actual operator. Cancellation in the actual character sums can invalidate the comparison. Therefore this is an obstruction to the envelope route, not an obstruction to the exact physical remainder target.

The auxiliary rank-one operator example shows the same logical issue: counting contraction and small position entries do not by themselves price a physical packet. It is not an example drawn from the actual \(D_h-D^{\mathrm J}\).

### Precise remaining gap

A complete proof must perform signed transport for the actual remainder
\[
E_h=D_h-D^{\mathrm J}
\]
at each of \(h=601,602\), with:

1. the actual \(V_h\), not merely its positive envelope;
2. the actual marked floor \(t_0=n\);
3. all mixed seed/non-seed words in the odd derivative expansion;
4. bad-lift and base-boundary corrections;
5. the translated pair and physical packet columns;
6. a tail bound after the retained signed transport;
7. a final \(P_{5000}\)-price estimate strictly below \(0.472\).

The current signed character-sum estimate controls individual position entries but does not control the weighted packet transport. Conversely, the available positive packet comparison gives a lower bound only for a majorant, not for the actual signed operator.

Hence the checked conclusion is:

\[
\boxed{\text{No proof of }\|K(D_h-D^{\mathrm J})\|_{P_{5000}}<0.472
\text{ and no true signed obstruction are currently established.}}
\]

The order of limits remains the required one: first obtain bounds uniform on the fixed square \(Q\) and both fixed rows \(h=601,602\), then take \(p\to\infty\) through \(p\equiv1\pmod4\). A finite-prime replay alone would not settle the asymptotic assertion.
