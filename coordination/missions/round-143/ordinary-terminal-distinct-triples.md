## Question
Can the genuine opposite optimal face give a subquadratic signed distinct-index sum on ordinary primes?

## Why this call is worth making
Coincidence and exceptional-mark terms are proved lower order. A prime gap would force quadratic growth and force concentration on the actual terminal high set for every optimizer.

## Current proof obligation
Prove a one-sided upper o(T^2) of the signed distinct triple at selectable true optimum and growing marked time on ordinary primes, or the weaker first-moment test. Use a fixed positive floor first and take it down last.

## Scope
Use the actual p=1 mod 4 family, zero-start trajectory, fixed tau then tau down to zero, and selectable times T growing with T+1 in the simultaneous marked window. Use true opposite mean-one optima at each sign and preserve signs in the triple sum.

## How the result changes the next decision
- If supported: Prove a prime-uniform signed upper o(T^2) at a selectable optimizer and time on each sign, or the weaker sufficient weighted activation bound o(T).
- If refuted: Give a genuinely arithmetic obstruction to a specific estimation route or an actual prime subsequence obstruction, with scope and sign.
- If inconclusive: Derive an exact arithmetic reduction of the signed sum to a support-sensitive statistic or prove a nontrivial bound using the exact face minimization.
