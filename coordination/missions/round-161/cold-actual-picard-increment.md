Prove a useful signed estimate on the genuine Picard increment or a nonlinear invariant with a quantitative resolvent bridge.

## Scope
Keep both actual heights, original square, physical t0=n and both lifts. A symbolic equality or a fixed-height residual second norm without transfer does not imply an invariant.

## How the result changes the next decision

- If refuted: Show why a specified actual increment representation or proposed mixed norm cannot achieve the required rate.
- If inconclusive: Verify one actual nontrivial signed transport identity and its true remainder, or a new quantified local inequality.
