## Question
Can a weighted affine-incidence argument control a growing portion of the actual favorable slope fibers?

## Why this call is worth making
The projective family shares a fixed point and both transported factors depend on the affine slope and intercept. Bounded total fiber weight is already O(p).

## Current proof obligation
Prove a signed estimate for growing favorable weighted fibers retaining the transported weights. For the earlier route obtain an adaptive cost bound on the actual optimal face.

## Scope
Use actual nonnegative b near critical on the selected short block, complex f, true weights and the explicit chart. Supply a new upper; the conditional lower under a hypothetical sequence is an accepted input.

## How the result changes the next decision
- If supported: Prove O(p) for all favorable slopes or give a prime-only growing weighted subfamily bound with errors at O(p).
- If refuted: Identify a precise obstruction for a specified weighted incidence lemma, preserving logical scope.
- If inconclusive: Give a new inequality for the actual high slope-mass fibers or an exact decomposition which identifies a genuinely smaller unresolved term.
