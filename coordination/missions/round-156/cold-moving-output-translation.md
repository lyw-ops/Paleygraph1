## Question
Can a true omitted arithmetic family with output shift depending on canonical input weight produce signed gain at large width?

## Why this call is worth making
The fixed-return R155 gains oppose on C1 only and decay. A global budget needs gains that survive translation or a different confinement proof.

## Current proof obligation
Find a genuinely translated overlap with moving output shifts or alternative confinement. The whole signed difference, invariant ball and half-step remain open.

## Scope
Keep both actual heights, both physical lifts, common square and floor, t0=n and actual coefficient. Work on a family growing with j and price the result on actual translated columns, with exceptional and analytic tails stated.

## How the result changes the next decision
- If supported: Give a provable substantial signed affine-limit gain, or a rigorous true gain on an unbounded translated family toward the direct target.
- If refuted: Prove exact noncoincidence or constructive reinforcement in a specified translated family.
- If inconclusive: Check one growing-width label mechanism and the necessary extra terms for a full rate.
