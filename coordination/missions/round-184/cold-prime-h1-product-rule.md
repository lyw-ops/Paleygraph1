## Question
In the two-fibre calculus, what is the exact per-simple dimension-price (A) ratio of F -> K(Phi''_t(S) h_1 F), where h_1 = 2 sqrt t sum_k b'_k K(J^k) is the first Picard piece of h = V_602 - S, and does it leave room under beta_max = 0.19359 for h - h_1; and does (b1*) A <= P_5000 hold on the unshifted class, with the O(n^(-1/2)) corrections of Lemma 4(ii) in A?

## Why this call is worth making
Round 183 priced the seed multiplier exactly with its Taylor terms and moved the whole budget to the non-seed h-linear term in the dimension price, with the sharp requirement A(h) < 0.6179 at the generic product ratio rho_0 = 2.17565; the h_1 simples have exactly known weights and the generic word rule is the only crude step left.

## Current proof obligation
A(h) < 0.6179 uniformly on Q, both lifts, for h = V_602 - S, or a sharper product rule than rho_0 = 2.17565 for the actual non-seed simples (decisive: the transported fields K(J^(2i)) of the first Picard piece h_1); plus (b1*) A <= P_5000 on the unshifted class, the O(n^(-1/2)) corrections in A, and (H1)/(H2) for A. Uniform complex stationary domain and derivative transfer remain separate.

## Scope
True cold orbit on the original closed square, physical t0=n, frozen-base identity at V_602, compact model with the Round 57/80/126 weight calculus for tensor products of two-fibre simples (stated as a hypothesis where not proved). Use Lemmas 1-5 and Proposition 3 of research/native-claude-cold-prime-seed-multiplier-power-rate-183.md as accepted in Round 183 (with the audit's corrections). Cite accepted statements by round; re-prove anything taken from an unaccepted draft.

## How the result changes the next decision
- If supported: A certified h_1 ratio leaving A(h - h_1) <~ 0.19 feasible keeps the A-price route alive, with a certified A(h - h_1) as the remaining input to a power rate below 1 - C(1 - kappa*_A).
- If refuted: A ratio >= 2 - o(1) on the h_1 simples retires the per-simple A route, and the cold route returns to Criterion D or sup transfer.
- If inconclusive: Return the exact ratio for the K(J^(+-2)) part and the remaining budget.
