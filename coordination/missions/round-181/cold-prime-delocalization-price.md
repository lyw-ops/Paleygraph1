## Question
Is there an n-independent delocalization bound for the actual increments R_{602+k} along the confined prefix — for example a signed, cross-vertex collected sup-dominating price of the secant operator on the 3/8 polydisk about V_602 with rate below 0.966, or a fixed-height compact coset supremum with sup transfer — or a coordinate that defeats Criterion D?

## Why this call is worth making
Round 180 proved the normalized R_602 lies in (0, 9.07e-209) and that confinement holds iff increments stay delocalized; Criterion D (n-independent sup/RMS ratio with growth gamma, q*gamma<1) is sufficient. The audit showed the moving-predictor no-go is only heuristic and that Criterion D is not necessary.

## Current proof obligation
Prove n-independent delocalization along the confined prefix, e.g. a signed collected secant price with rate below 0.966 on the 3/8 polydisk or a compact coset supremum with sup transfer; or locate a coordinate that defeats it. Uniform complex stationary domain and derivative transfer remain separate.

## Scope
Genuine nine-center start, both lifts, physical t0=n, original closed square, actual K Phi_t, coordinate sup norm. Inherited fixed-height compact transfer may not be used at heights growing with n without proof. Distinguish (a) proofs for all large eligible primes, (b) conditional criteria, (c) numerics (state start row, primes, precision). Prefer exact or outward-rounded arithmetic for any constant used in a proof.

## How the result changes the next decision
- If supported: Proves the Round 173 finite-prefix test and common stationary continuation on the interior of the original square; derivative transfer becomes the next cold obligation.
- If refuted: A proved coordinate concentration at some height 602+k with k<k_n refutes Criterion D (not confinement itself) and directs the cold route to signed cumulative sums.
- If inconclusive: Return the exact price norm or coset quantity that is not controlled, with verified bounds on the controlled parts.
