## Question
Can the one-match weighted complete 3+3 Gauss class be controlled at the genuine covariance scale O(p^1.5)?

## Why this call is worth making
All classes with at least two matches are now paid at target scale, and restoring omitted frequencies also costs at most that scale. A single match gives a cap factor but still needs signed averaging.

## Current proof obligation
Bound the one-match and zero-match true weighted covariances, or prove a fixed-gap exclusion through actual centered source-weighted spectrum.

## Scope
Use actual omega and ell, fixed ratio phase factors, both outer square lifts and the exact prime cap. Condition on one specified match without replacing the remaining source law. Zero-match is a separate obligation.

## How the result changes the next decision
- If supported: Prove the actual one-match O(p^1.5) contribution, or a stronger sufficient prime estimate covering more of the covariance.
- If refuted: Prove a scoped obstruction to a stated one-match or weighted hypergeometric cancellation mechanism.
- If inconclusive: Give a new actual exact phase subclass or a quantitative average improvement on the completed 3+3 class.
