## Question
Can a regular rank-two phase transform yield cancellation with the actual five independent base draws and coupled ratios?

## Why this call is worth making
The regular two-variable kernel is a rank-two pure trace with no unresolved boundary degeneracies. Its geometry alone gives p pointwise.

## Current proof obligation
Connect the prime-only slope exclusion or convolution smoothing to moderate actual amplitudes or a real coupled upper on the marked arc. Pointwise trace geometry does not control the true weight law.

## Scope
Use both slope and outer lifts, the exact true weight laws, actual sign, and the nine regular exclusions. Compare with genuine convolution smoothing only after deriving a correct weighting relation.

## How the result changes the next decision
- If supported: Obtain a sufficient p^-1/2 signed saving, or a true sub-block whose weighted absolute contribution is O(p).
- If refuted: Prove a named transform or resonant regime cannot give such a bound.
- If inconclusive: Derive an exact useful trace correlation, determinant normalization or higher moment with a checked relation to the actual weights.
