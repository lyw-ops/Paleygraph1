## Question
Can the signed endpoint defect of the true projected Picard average be repaired within an already proved growing prime marked window?

## Why this call is worth making
The constructive proof only needs balanced marks and the true mean budget. A feasible correction at this depth avoids the separate ordinary tail and clipped window estimates.

## Current proof obligation
At fixed small positive floor, find an explicit sector-preserving worst-site completion with raw marks preserved up to o(1) and additional mean o(n^-1/2) within the actual marked arithmetic window. Do not pass from a fixed-depth completion to an uncontrolled diagonal or use the long polynomial horizon alone.

## Scope
At every fixed sufficiently small positive tau, work with actual zero-start averaged rows for primes p=1 mod4 and floor tau/n. Use T(p) tending to infinity inside the proved simultaneous marked window. Keep sector, raw marks up to o(1), and additional mean o(n^-1/2). Then tau tends to zero. Squares are controls only.

## How the result changes the next decision
- If supported: Construct an explicit positive completion and uniform cost bound at a genuinely compatible depth.
- If refuted: Give a precise signed-repair obstruction in this window, with all prime hypotheses, or distinguish the failed restricted cone from actual completion.
- If inconclusive: Provide the smallest specific weighted endpoint deficit inequality that would couple the two depths, with no unknown growth hidden in constants.
