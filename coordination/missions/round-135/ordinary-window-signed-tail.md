## Scope
Use the actual prime ordinary optimizer, fixed cap at the mark and complementary positive factors with disjoint bulk support. Either upper must be prime uniform in the stated fixed-window order. Avoid positing full optimizer L2 without proof.

## How the result changes the next decision
