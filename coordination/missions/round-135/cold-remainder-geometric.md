## Question
Can direct geometric relative pricing prove or obstruct the very same remainder norm below 0.472?

## Why this call is worth making
A shorter arithmetic argument can distinguish a defect of the coefficient envelope from a genuine limitation of the remainder estimate.

## Current proof obligation
Bound the actual nonlinear remainder in physical price at the same frozen heights and square. A separate remainder estimate would suffice at the two center rows; the invariant and half-step sheet margins would still need proof.

## Scope
Use exactly the same actual h=601,602, Q, t0=n and P5000 as the envelope route. Work by signed transport of mixed word weights, relative seed powers or a genuine packet witness. Counting norm and position flatness do not price this norm.

## How the result changes the next decision
- If supported: Prove the physical operator bound independently of numerical coefficient scanning.
- If refuted: Identify an exact sign, rank or packet obstruction at these true assumptions and distinguish it from a majorant-only failure.
- If inconclusive: Give a checked reduction and the first nontrivial discriminating signed transport test.

## Shared proof obligations

- `cand:cold-pure-seed-remainder-472` — For all sufficiently large primes p=1 mod 4, uniformly on the original closed cold square and for h=601 and h=602, the actual physical translated P5000 operator norm of K(D_h-D^J) is strictly below 0.472 with a field-uniform gap. D^J is the full derivative at the Jacobi seed using the actual marked floor n.
