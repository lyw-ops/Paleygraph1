## Question
Can exact Fourier and overlap identities of the four-ratio law yield cancellation against the single clean trace?

## Why this call is worth making
All lifts now reduce to one signed regular bound. The law is far from uniform and from its smoothed versions, so a genuine transfer must be trace-specific.

## Current proof obligation
Control the remaining low-to-middle and aggregate weighted contributions at O(p). Generic replacement by a smoothed or uniform law is rigorously obstructed.

## Scope
Use the true five draws, one pointwise clean rank-two lift with all exclusions at O(p), and the exact four-variable pushforward. Do not replace the actual underlying law by independent ratio marginals.

## How the result changes the next decision
- If supported: Prove the needed p^-1/2 normalized signed real estimate or a new O(p) sub-block.
- If refuted: Prove an exact failure of a specific transform, overlap, or conditional Cauchy route.
- If inconclusive: Derive a usable trace correlation or spectral decomposition that retains the correct overlap weights and normalization.
