## Question
Can the actual zero-coefficient block outside the small-size range have a sufficiently small directional inverse cost and Schur leakage?

## Why this call is worth making
The small-block bound eliminates part of the problem; larger actual blocks can still matter. A true basis extension of the positive support may change the separate debits without changing the full inverse.

## Current proof obligation
Control larger actual directional blocks and the low Schur source, or the regular actual weighted covariance needed for the moderate slice.

## Scope
Use genuine positive support, actual zero columns and pure marked compression. Preserve a full-span basis, actual c0 and signed Schur term. Any transfer needs exact scale at low eigenvalues and one admissible growing T.

## How the result changes the next decision
- If supported: Prove an actual-prime O(1) or o(T) zero-block cost and sufficient Schur control or an equivalent fixed-gap exclusion.
- If refuted: Prove a precise obstruction to a specified high-weight or basis restriction mechanism, with actual scope.
- If inconclusive: Bound a genuinely new directional low window, an actual source of zero coefficients, or a basis-dependent leakage statistic with a rigorously identified remaining test.
