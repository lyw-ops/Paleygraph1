## Question
Can the actual deficit-weighted affine fourth moment be bounded by O(p) in the near-critical prime branch?

## Why this call is worth making
The near-critical deficit forces divergence divided by p. The diagonal and uniform-anchor bound are exact, leaving a specified off-diagonal five-factor sum.

## Current proof obligation
Prove a true prime estimate for the deficit-weighted moment, or a prime upper for the full high first-spill face maximum. Even exclusion of the critical case leaves the diffuse regime.

## Scope
Use ordinary p=1 mod 4, the genuine R150 retargeted orbit and same R151 block, L_sigma b/E b tending to zero and m_eff/m0 tending to one. Maintain all roots, the fixed floor, and the actual normalized anchor weights. No assumption of a coset template.

## How the result changes the next decision
- If supported: Prove the weighted O(p) bound and exclude the critical actual branch.
- If refuted: Identify an exact prime obstruction to the proposed off-diagonal bound on an explicitly specified relaxed model or actual geometry.
- If inconclusive: Derive a useful factorization of the complete shifted Jacobi sum, quantify its exceptional coincidences, or obtain a bound on a specific weighted contribution.
