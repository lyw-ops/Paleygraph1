## Question
Can the true weighted affine difference distribution improve the full energy beyond the max-vertical argument?

## Why this call is worth making
High-level vertical occupancies are quantitatively smaller, and the previous use of a generic maximum leaves an explicit O(p^(11/4-1/1280)) energy. The inverse chart Y=r/(1-b) makes differences structurally analyzable.

## Current proof obligation
Control the still genuinely coupled full diagonal to O(p^3/2), or the goal-scale one-match and separate zero-match bounds, with the original weights.

## Scope
Use the actual coefficients g(Y)g(D), b-square, endpoints and all levels. For right differences (a,c) use r-prime=a r, b-prime=b+c r and Y-prime=aY/(1-cY) to expose real weighted gain or apply a phase-sensitive analogue. Do not assert an unweighted counterexample is arithmetic. Keep zero match separate.

## How the result changes the next decision
- If supported: Prove O(p^2) for the true weighted energy or a sufficient one-match bound.
- If refuted: Give a precise obstruction to the specified weighted difference or convolution approach.
- If inconclusive: Improve a true energy exponent or prove a target-scale nontrivial difference class.
