## Question
Can the actual regular same-pole or genuinely distinct-pole physical couples reach the needed full diagonal scale?

## Why this call is worth making
Two-label intersections and same-pole character degeneracies are paid. The fixed-b row and per-line weights have power savings but still give O(p^(2-1/640)) for entire common-pole physical collisions.

## Current proof obligation
Control the still genuinely coupled full diagonal to O(p^3/2), or the goal-scale one-match and separate zero-match bounds, with the original weights.

## Scope
Keep true four factors g(A),g(C),g(Y),g(D), w(s), mark and b-square. Try to exploit C=(A-r)/(1-r) and the forced pole r/(1-r) parameter, or treat distinct-pole correlations by a weighted incidence with both inputs. Do not subtract physical and character cuts twice.

## How the result changes the next decision
- If supported: Prove the full sufficient O(p^3/2) weighted diagonal, or handle a major unresolved class at target scale.
- If refuted: Give a precise arithmetic obstruction to a named actual coupling or signed estimate.
- If inconclusive: Give a prime-uniform power gain on the regular common-pole or distinct-pole block with all actual hypotheses.
