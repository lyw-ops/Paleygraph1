## Question
Can either high-overlap equal-u collisions or distinct-u collisions be bounded at O(p^1.5) on a genuine fixed moderate slope band?

## Why this call is worth making
All shared complete endpoints have a finite-multiplicity charge under the target. The equal-u low-overlap part also costs O(p^1.5).

## Current proof obligation
Bound both centered costs on true zero columns or otherwise exclude fixed gaps, and bound the remaining disjoint fixed-moderate collisions.

## Scope
Use genuine physical (u,z) graph, original mask and true f; state fixed amplitude thresholds and band. Subtract the identity slope; separate actual shared endpoints and equal-u versus different-u. No unshifted energy substitution.

## How the result changes the next decision
- If supported: Prove the full desired collision bound or close one of its two remaining explicit parts at target scale.
- If refuted: Prove a precise obstruction to a stated incidence or multiplicity shortcut, with actual or relaxed scope.
- If inconclusive: Derive a new two-copy weighted inequality beyond the pointwise-and-total product bound.
