## Question
Can the actual zero-coefficient inverse debit and positive-kernel Schur source cost both be controlled by o(T), or an equally sufficient bound?

## Why this call is worth making
Centering removes the genuine physical mark within the pure Gram and exhibits a strictly positive kernel on the complementary support. Positivity alone has a relaxed obstruction, so Jacobi arithmetic is required.

## Current proof obligation
Bound both centered costs on true zero columns or otherwise exclude fixed gaps, and bound the remaining disjoint fixed-moderate collisions.

## Scope
Use the actual independent zero columns, G0 and c0, the complementary coefficients and genuine prime signs, and an admissible growing T. Retain both Schur debit terms, signed edge weights, high versus low w cutoff and actual support.

## How the result changes the next decision
- If supported: Prove both needed source-weighted bounds or one equally sufficient actual prime fixed-gap exclusion.
- If refuted: Give a precise obstruction to a specified cut-plus-transfer or signed Jacobi lower mechanism.
- If inconclusive: Prove a quantified coercivity or leakage estimate for one of the two real source debits, keeping the other explicit.
