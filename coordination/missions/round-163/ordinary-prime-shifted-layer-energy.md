## Question
Can the off-diagonal multiplicative energy of the two true high reflected layers be bounded at O(p^(1+sigma/2)) for one remaining slope exponent sigma<1?

## Why this call is worth making
The actual physical counts already cover sigma>=1 and sparse first mass. The nontrivial Mellin spectrum is the first remaining concrete excess on a moderate slice.

## Current proof obligation
Prove an actual-prime lower ratio omega(1/T) or an equivalent gap exclusion. Bound nontrivial shifted-layer energy for a moderate actual slope exponent.

## Scope
Use actual reflected high-level sets X,Y for the chosen v, with actual f, physical signs and slope cutoff. Retain the true level constraints. State whether all shifts or only stabilizing or sign-restricted shifts are controlled.

## How the result changes the next decision
- If supported: Give a new O(p) block through energy or a sufficiently strong signed variant with all five weights retained.
- If refuted: Identify a precise obstruction to a stated energetic criterion or a non-genuine approximation.
- If inconclusive: Bound a genuine high contribution, e.g. the count or energy of actual stabilizing dilates, or gain quantitatively in the remaining exponent.
