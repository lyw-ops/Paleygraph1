## Question
Can the actual Fourier-minor mass omitting the constant row dominate 1/T on the genuine Jacobi zero sets?

## Why this call is worth making
The sector and the same minors are explicit, and the row translations preserve squared determinant size. A count free of determinant weights or a generic positive-semidefinite argument cannot close the estimate.

## Current proof obligation
Prove an actual-prime lower ratio omega(1/T) or an equivalent gap exclusion. Bound nontrivial shifted-layer energy for a moderate actual slope exponent.

## Scope
Use the actual sector Omega and actual maximal independent spatial columns U prime from the least-energy optimizer. Keep the sign, physical mark, possible high rank and one admissible growing T. A quantified signed leakage exclusion is also valid.

## How the result changes the next decision
- If supported: Prove a genuine weighted lower ratio omega(1/T), or a stronger actual prime fixed-gap exclusion.
- If refuted: Give a precise obstruction to a stated orbit, determinant, or signed leakage shortcut, retaining assumptions and whether models are prime.
- If inconclusive: Prove a useful exact prime-sensitive relation quantifying high-rank weights or isolating the translate orbits that keep the constant row.
