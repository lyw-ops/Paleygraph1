## Question
Can a genuinely prime-sensitive bound control a specified dyadic portion of the actual first reflected spill?

## Why this call is worth making
The initial nonnegative source d and adaptive first mask have an exact two-coordinate pairing. Its geometry differs from that of later relaxed deficits.

## Current proof obligation
Exclude the actual simultaneous prime constraints, including diffuse and low-energy regimes, with original optimizer weight. The separate first reflected pairing still needs a prime-uniform upper.

## Scope
Use exact y0=Z+2Ld, adaptive threshold max(Z_i,u) with exact mean and pin corrections, true optimizer and its complementary zero set, nonroot distinct pair, one growing marked T and a selectable cutoff H=o(T). F and R may be chosen to make the actual bound strongest.

## How the result changes the next decision
- If supported: Prove a prime-uniform o(1) bound on the actual signed two-coordinate pairing for selectable T and weights.
- If refuted: Prove a precise obstacle to a specified dyadic or spectral analysis, preserving its actual or relaxed scope.
- If inconclusive: Find a true dyadic region with a strict quantitative bound and identify the remaining range without replacing the actual adaptive threshold by equality with a fixed mask.
