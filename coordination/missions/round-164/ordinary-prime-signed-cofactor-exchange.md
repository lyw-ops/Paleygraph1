## Question
Can the actual signed Jacobi mass of one-row cofactor replacements give a weighted Fourier-minor deficit above 1/T?

## Why this call is worth making
Whole-row translates cannot exchange any high-rank actual row set, and the macroscopic Gram is necessarily non-scalar. Single-row exchange retains nonzero freedom and has an exact signed mass formula.

## Current proof obligation
Control the actual signed cofactor deficit or leakage, and the full remaining moderate critical block with the true slope weight.

## Scope
Use the actual unmarked zero columns, constant row, real signs, full squared minors and one admissible growing T. Cofactor trace alone is tautological. The actual signed leakage alternative must keep mean and norm scales.

## How the result changes the next decision
- If supported: Prove an aggregate signed replacement lower above (k/T) times the marked weight, or a comparable actual-prime fixed-gap exclusion.
- If refuted: Prove a precise mathematical limitation of a named cofactor simplification, with actual versus relaxed scope.
- If inconclusive: Derive an exact additional constraint depending on true optimizer zero columns that could force the cofactor mass to remain outside the constant row.
