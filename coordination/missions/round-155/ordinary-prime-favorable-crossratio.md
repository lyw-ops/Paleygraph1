This exports a proposal; it does not launch an agent or record a past dispatch.

## Question
Can the favorable-sign off-diagonal of the actual weighted Möbius cross-ratio moment be bounded above by O(p)?

## Why this call is worth making
The actual pinned short block and Legendre splitting remove the opposite sign and diagonal at O(p). A positive contribution of superlinear size must now come from this one sign.

## Current proof obligation
Prove the signed favorable off-diagonal upper O(p) under the same true weights. Separately the original high first-spill optimum lacks a prime upper.

## Scope
Use true ordinary primes, actual retargeted b with m_eff~n/sqrt(p), true anchor law, quantitative pin and actual f including its phase. Retain the same u,x,y summation and favorable sign. Track any restriction or excluded tuples at the O(p) scale.

## How the result changes the next decision
- If supported: Prove an O(p) signed upper with an explicit prime-only ingredient or another incompatible bound on this same actual branch.
- If refuted: Prove an obstruction for a named incidence or weighted trace estimate and give its exact relaxed assumptions.
- If inconclusive: Isolate a strict subfamily of sign configurations at an unconditional O(p) cost and give the new residual estimate.
