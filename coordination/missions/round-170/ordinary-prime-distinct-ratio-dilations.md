## Question
Can the distinct-z twisted physical correlations at a common slope be bounded by O(p^5/2) after the actual outer weight?

## Why this call is worth making
The exact physical test has no unresolved Gauss phases, pays all z=z-prime terms, and converts the square condition to a slope mask.

## Current proof obligation
Bound the coupled distinct ratios at common slope with the genuine weights, and independently the zero-match term, or prove a fixed-gap exclusion through a true Jacobi bound on centered optimizer energy.

## Scope
Use actual g, w=g(1-g), lambda_z=(1-z)/(1-sz), both square slope masks, and the common affine parameter 1-s. Price the distinct-z terms. A physical or arithmetic argument is allowed. Zero-match is separate.

## How the result changes the next decision
- If supported: Prove the target-scale distinct-ratio bound and thus the one-match contribution.
- If refuted: Prove an actual obstruction to a specified distinct-ratio mechanism or a scoped counterexample for strictly weaker inputs.
- If inconclusive: Give a new quantitative gain on a precise off-diagonal subclass or reduce to a smaller genuine arithmetic estimate.
