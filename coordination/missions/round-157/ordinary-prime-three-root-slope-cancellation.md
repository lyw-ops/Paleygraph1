This exports a proposal; it does not launch an agent or record a past dispatch.

## Question
Can the separated three-root kernel yield a new ordinary-prime bound for a growing set of favorable slopes?

## Why this call is worth making
The signed physical lift costs O(p), and the per-u uniform convolution norm is rigorously too large.

## Current proof obligation
Find a coupled ordinary-prime upper on the same law, via a many-slope slice of the complete kernel or one of the frequency signs. The earlier adaptive face upper remains open.

## Scope
Keep the complete u Jacobi average and three-root z kernel with actual lifted weights and a third genuine slope factor. Keep the correct favorable sign and exact normalized errors.

## How the result changes the next decision
- If supported: Prove a genuinely signed O(p) bound expending no unsupported independence assumption, or a serviceable upper on a nontrivial many-slope block.
- If refuted: Prove that a specific weaker complete-kernel bound cannot serve, stating the precise degeneracies or scope.
- If inconclusive: Give an exact new meaningful block decomposition or an ordinary-prime joint trace estimate with quantitative errors.
