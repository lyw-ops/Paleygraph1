## Question
Can one bound a nonzero additive frequency half of the favorable form, using the actual law nu_a M_a and the factor 1±k(a)?

## Why this call is worth making
Both signs now have an exact positive spectral representation up to O(p). This may connect the original mark and pin with the same structured high-correlation anchors.

## Current proof obligation
Find a coupled ordinary-prime upper on the same law, via a many-slope slice of the complete kernel or one of the frequency signs. The earlier adaptive face upper remains open.

## Scope
Use the original actual selected b, full short-block sign and quantitative pin, both physical signs, and true ν. Distinguish k(a) from within-set k(a-b) and isolate all error terms.

## How the result changes the next decision
- If supported: Obtain an O(p) bound on at least one sign with genuine prime arithmetic, or a strengthened joint law constraint that advances the original upper.
- If refuted: Demonstrate an exact failure of a specified localization argument on the correct law.
- If inconclusive: Derive a new concrete regularity or high-anchor correlation relation linked to actual mark with checked scaling.
