## Question
Can genuine prime arithmetic bound the weighted signed deleted tail of a true ordinary optimizer?

## Why this call is worth making
Any cutoff L=o(T) now gives the terminal first moment for free; the only remaining debit for this construction is a one-sided correlation with the actual projected score.

## Current proof obligation
Establish the prime-uniform one-sided deleted-tail or near-cap terminal estimate at genuine growing marked times. No estimate on optimizer support alone substitutes for the joint cost.

## Scope
Use all ordinary primes p=1 mod 4, fixed positive tau, the actual zero-start trajectory, and selectable T tending to infinity with T+1 in the simultaneous window. Seek the bound for both signs or the genuinely larger optimum sign at each p. Use the true optimizer and exact Xi and take tau down after the prime limit.

## How the result changes the next decision
- If supported: Prove E[D Xi]>=-o(1) uniformly with D=1_bulk(F-L)_+ at some L=o(T).
- If refuted: Prove a scoped arithmetic obstruction to the choice of cutoff or the minimization within the true face, keeping the square control separate.
- If inconclusive: Give a prime-sensitive bound for the joint F-weighted negative level sets of Xi, or an exact signed decomposition that removes one actual problematic tail range.
