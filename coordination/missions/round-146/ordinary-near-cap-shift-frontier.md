## Question
Can a true ordinary prime optimizer supply one near-cap shift with sublinear actual terminal cost?

## Why this call is worth making
For positive convolution both normalized costs are nonnegative, and its best asymptotic choice is equivalent to a single symmetric shift orbit.

## Current proof obligation
Establish the prime-uniform one-sided deleted-tail or near-cap terminal estimate at genuine growing marked times. No estimate on optimizer support alone substitutes for the joint cost.

## Scope
Use actual even F and G, mark left at zero, true q_s and shifts in the cyclic frequency group. Preserve fixed tau, all ordinary primes and selectable growing marked times. Target the larger sign or both signs. This task does not clip the true row.

## How the result changes the next decision
- If supported: Find a shift with root loss o(sqrt n) and true bulk pair cost o(T), uniformly on the eligible prime family.
- If refuted: Show a mathematically precise obstacle on the near-cap set tied to the prime arithmetic rather than the trivial singleton-cap case alone.
- If inconclusive: Use complementary orthogonality and a genuinely prime arithmetic estimate to constrain the shift cost on the near-cap set, with a precise remaining uniform bound.
