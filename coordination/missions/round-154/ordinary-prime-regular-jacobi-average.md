## Question
Can the fully regular product of three normalized Jacobi factors be bounded under the actual near-critical deficit weights?

## Why this call is worth making
The true moment reduces to a weighted phase average. All degenerate and fixed algebraic relations cost only O(p), so controlling their sizes cannot settle the critical branch.

## Current proof obligation
Prove the actual p^(-1/2) phase average upper, or a prime-only restriction for the regular weighted law. The direct critical-mask route is unchanged and the true first-spill optimum still lacks an all-prime upper.

## Scope
Use true nonnegative retargeted b, physical mark, near-critical m_eff~n/sqrt(p), vanishing relative pinned projection and the exact actual weight w. Both third-factor lifts, actual within-set phase deficit and the order of prime and floor limits matter.

## How the result changes the next decision
- If supported: Prove a real signed average upper O(p^-1/2), or another ordinary-prime restriction that contradicts the critical lower on the same regular law.
- If refuted: Prove a specific obstruction to a proposed phase argument with relaxed or square scope stated.
- If inconclusive: Obtain a quantitative decomposition of the regular phase or a genuine weighted saving relative to the complete individual sizes.
