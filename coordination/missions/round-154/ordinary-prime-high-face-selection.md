## Question
Can the original high first-spill cost be made small by a true optimal-face selection on ordinary primes?

## Why this call is worth making
A minimum over the opposite optimal face suffices. Density and global F squared caps alone force a time requirement outside the fixed-gap setting.

## Current proof obligation
Prove the actual p^(-1/2) phase average upper, or a prime-only restriction for the regular weighted law. The direct critical-mask route is unchanged and the true first-spill optimum still lacks an all-prime upper.

## Scope
Use the original first reflection, actual middle d, entire high receiver, exact common complementary zero set, true signs, both roots and diagonal excluded, fixed floor and one selectable marked T.

## How the result changes the next decision
- If supported: Produce a true minimizing or feasible opposite optimum with o(1) signed cost, or bound the actual secondary dual maximum.
- If refuted: Identify an exact obstacle for a specified face selection or phase approximation, without treating an arbitrary point of the cone as a true optimum.
- If inconclusive: Prove a new prime bound for a specified receiver/source subset with errors that sum to o(1).
