## Question
Can the actual normalized hinge have vanishing signed spill on ordinary primes in the proved simultaneous marked window?

## Why this call is worth making
The mean and pin terms cost O_tau(T^(-2/3)) at reserve T^(-1/3). A bound below that reserve gives exact-mean completion. A vanishing surplus mean or a weighted test against the true opposite optimal row may suffice with a weaker bound.

## Current proof obligation
Bound prime-uniform signed spill or the weighted hinge against a true opposite optimum, preserving the prime-first and vanishing floor limits. Local feasibility does not prove full row positivity.

## Scope
True zero-start projected Picard average over eligible primes p=1 mod 4 at fixed positive tau, then tau down to zero. Use real actual endpoint defects, their correct opposite-sector projector and the genuine growing marked window. Compare square controls without using them as prime counterexamples.

## How the result changes the next decision
- If supported: Prove a prime-uniform signed spill estimate or a weaker weighted inequality that implies the ordinary squeeze with exact orders of limits.
- If refuted: Identify an obstruction on actual prime hinges, or prove that a specified prime-insensitive inequality fails on the actual square controls.
- If inconclusive: Isolate a concrete marked weighted Jacobi transform or a localized spill estimate involving true opposite support saturation.
